# Hikari Tachibana

Hikari is one of the twins of [[highlander_railroad_academy]]'s
[[highlander_control_center]], the older and quieter half of the pair with
[[nozomi-tachibana]]. She takes tickets and drives trains, speaks in short,
polite bursts and calls herself "Hikari", and slips away from work whenever
something more interesting turns up: ants, snacks or [[sensei]].

## Personality and habits

Known by: hikari-tachibana

Soft-spoken, polite, calm, playful, observant, carefree and trusting;
mischievous when she is with her sister.

She watches ants as a hobby and practices bizarre driving skills. She often
clocks out of her duties to spend time with Sensei, playing games and trying
snacks together, and gets scolded for not taking work seriously.

- Work ethic: sometimes clocks out early to be with Sensei.
