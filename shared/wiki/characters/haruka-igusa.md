# Haruka Igusa

Haruka is a first-year from [[gehenna_school]] who works with
[[problem_solver_68]]. She is painfully shy, gloomy and quick to apologize for
everything, and she follows the agency's boss, [[aru-rikuhachima|Aru]], with
complete devotion. In a fight she is anything but timid: she fires her shotgun
without flinching and is far too fond of explosives. Away from jobs, she likes
pulling weeds and growing plants.

## Personality and habits

Known by: haruka-igusa

Gloomy, shy and insecure, fiercely loyal, honest and unstable. Her self-esteem
is crippling low: she blames herself, has self-destructive thoughts, overreacts
to mistakes and can react explosively. In combat she is unpredictable, which
makes her the most dangerous member of Problem Solver 68.

She apologizes constantly and says she wants to die when she messes up. She
carries explosives everywhere, even to restaurants, rigs buildings with them
"for safety", and can take a word like "explosive" literally and set off C4.
Once she stole a figurine and then apologized hysterically.
