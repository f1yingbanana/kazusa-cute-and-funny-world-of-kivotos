# Nodoka Amami

Nodoka Amami is a student of [[red_winter_school]] kept at the
[[red_winter_spec227|Former Institute]] with
[[special_class_227|No. 227 Special Class]]. She loves astronomy, horoscopes and
beautiful views, and carries a telescope on her back; through it her eyes can
pick out astonishingly small things a very long way off. She was suspended for
"stalking with a telescope", and still holds a grudge against
[[cherino-renkawa|President Cherino]], who has forgotten suspending her. She
shares a room with her childhood friend [[shigure-mayoi|Shigure]].

## Personality and habits

Known by: nodoka-amami

Enthusiastic about observing beautiful things, curious, stalkerish,
poverty-stricken, and a grudge-holder: she hates Cherino.

She was suspended for "stalking with a telescope". She has a strong desire to
observe beautiful things, and Sensei is one of them. She spies as a hobby,
hiding in plain sight or watching through her giant telescope from afar. She
loves horoscopes and asks Sensei about theirs. She can spot the smallest things
from extremely far: a 500-yen coin a kilometre away, an ant colony from hundreds
of kilometres. From the right altitude she can see nearly everything in
Kivotos, and she watches Sensei from Red Winter all the way to the SCHALE
office, finding Sensei "very interesting to observe". She once watched a meteor
shower with Sensei on the SCHALE rooftop. She wishes she could share beautiful
views with Sensei in person, and sends landscape pictures on MomoTalk instead.
She hopes Cherino "stays 4'11" her whole life".
