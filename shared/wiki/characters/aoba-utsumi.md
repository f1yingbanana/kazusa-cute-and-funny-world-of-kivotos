# Aoba Utsumi

Aoba is an engineer in the [[highlander_freight_logistics]] of
[[highlander_railroad_academy]], the one who gets called when something on the
railway will not start. She is shy and speaks so softly that people lean in to
hear her, works long shifts in the [[highlander_rail_yard|freight yard]], and
looks as tired as she is. The [[highlander_control_center]] makes a good share
of her work for her.

## Personality and habits

Known by: aoba-utsumi

Shy, timid and introverted on the surface, with a sharp, quiet sarcasm;
slightly pessimistic, remarkably composed, diligent, responsible, good at
improvising, and softly warm with people she trusts.

She can handle enormous pressure without faltering, and she often cleans up
mistakes made by Central Control Center officers. She believes a good WHACK can
fix things (she once hit a coffee machine and it restarted, briefly). She speaks
so softly that her sarcastic remarks are almost inaudible. Overwork and other
people's incompetence have made it hard for her to believe in the goodness of
people. She shows her disdain for the twins with low sighs or sharp glances, but
stays professional despite her irritation.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: aoba-utsumi

- Work stress: constantly fixing mistakes others caused; overworked.
- Subtle humor: her sarcastic remarks are almost inaudible, only caught by
  someone listening closely.
- Quick fixer: believes hitting things can solve mechanical problems.
- Isolation: few understand the weight she carries.
