# Kikyou Kiryuu

Kikyou is a second-year at [[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy]] and
the operations officer of the [[hyakkaryouran_resolution_council]], handling
its strategic planning, intelligence and coordination; as its strategist she
seldom draws her own rifle in the field. She has two cat ears and two cat tails,
speaks in measured sentences and says exactly what she means, never warmth she
doesn't feel. She spins yarn, plays cat's cradle, reads military history and
likes to sunbathe.
