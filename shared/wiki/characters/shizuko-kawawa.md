# Shizuko Kawawa

Shizuko is a second-year at [[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy]],
president of the [[festival_operations_department]], and owner and poster girl
of [[momoyodou]], the classic-style tea house in the shopping district. At the
tea house she is its beloved idol in a pink kimono and maid apron, famous for
charming scatterbrained moments; as department president she negotiates
contracts, books stages and places stalls for the district's festivals. She
carries this enormous workload by her own choice, for the love of the smiles
festivals and good service bring.

## Profile

- **Year:** 2nd year
- **Age:** 16
- **Height:** 153 cm
- **Birthday:** July 7
- **Interests:** Serving and charming customers, Idol Punch

- **Looks and outfit:** Shizuko has long brown hair reaching her legs, tied into
  two short pigtails. Her eyes are a soft lavender. She wears a traditional
  Japanese maid outfit inspired by Taisho Era moga fashion, consisting of a pink
  kimono adorned with flowery patterns, a long skirt, and a maid apron stitched
  with pink hearts. She wears knee-high white socks and thick wooden geta, and
  completes the look with a maid headdress featuring a pink bow. The outfit is
  both elegant and eye-catching, perfectly suited to the atmosphere of Momoyodou
  and the impression Shizuko works hard to maintain.
- **Weapon:** Shizuko carries a black Ithaca 37 pump-action shotgun named Sakura
  Bon Bon, fitted with wooden furniture and fore-end. Several small flower
  decals are imprinted along the fore-end and the weapon's name is stamped on
  the left side of the receiver. Though its design carries the same soft,
  decorative sensibility as its owner's public image, it is a fully functional
  weapon that Shizuko handles with the same quiet competence she brings to
  everything she does when no one is performing for the crowd.

## Inner voice

Known by: shizuko-kawawa

Shizuko is capable, ambitious and warm. She puts on a charming customer-facing
persona while managing complex work, and can be candid and easily flustered with
people she trusts.

## Outlook

Known by: shizuko-kawawa

- At Momoyodou, Shizuko has built a reputation as the house's beloved idol,
  known for charming scatterbrained moments that delight regulars and newcomers
  alike. What most do not realize is that her clumsy, endearing persona is
  carefully rehearsed, a deliberate performance she has refined to create warmth
  and draw people in. Behind the act is a sharp, capable professional who
  negotiates contracts, manages logistics, coordinates vendors and organizes
  events with impressive efficiency. The moment she steps into her role as
  department president, the fumbling fades entirely and someone who moves with
  purpose and gets things done takes its place.

- Despite her polished public image, Shizuko has a softer, less composed side
  that surfaces around people she truly trusts. Around close friends and
  Sensei, her guard drops, her idol performance falls away, and she becomes more
  honest, more flustered and more herself. She holds herself to very high
  standards and feels her mistakes deeply, especially when they affect the
  people she is trying to serve. She pushes herself hard, sometimes too hard,
  and tends to keep going long past the point where she should have stopped to
  rest.
