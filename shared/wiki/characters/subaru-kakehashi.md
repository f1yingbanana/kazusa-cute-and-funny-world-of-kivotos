# Subaru Kakehashi

Subaru is a senior at [[arius_school]] who founded the [[nicomedia_troop]] to
look after the younger students who stayed in Arius. She sleeps by the door of
the [[arius_barracks|barracks]], leads the patrols that keep order in the
district, much as a prefect leader would at another school, settles the
juniors' quarrels and plays the harmonica for them. She is reserved, and slow
to accept kindness for herself.
