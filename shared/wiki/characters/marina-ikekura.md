# Marina Ikekura

Marina Ikekura chairs the Security Committee of the
[[red_winter_office|Red Winter Office]] at [[red_winter_school]]. She is
earnest and energetic about her duties, strict about punctuality and always on
the lookout for spies, but she gets lost even on familiar routes and is often
seen scrubbing bathrooms as a punishment. She tries hard to look capable, and
her clumsiness keeps getting in the way.

## Personality and habits

Known by: marina-ikekura

Diligent, earnest and committed, a bit of a dork: clumsy, prone to getting
lost, easily flustered, secretly ambitious, with a rebellious streak and a fear
of cats.

She gets lost easily even on familiar routes. She is often punished with
restroom cleaning after failed attempts to take over Cherino's position. She is
terrified of cats since one scratched her as a child. She calls Sensei for help
with almost any problem, and stages small rebellions when given impossible
tasks. She cannot answer basic questions about Cherino, like her height or
age. She tries to appear capable, but her clumsiness undermines her confidence.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: marina-ikekura

- Fear of cats: scratched as a child, she now avoids them entirely.
- Restroom cleaner: the usual punishment for her failed coups and mistakes.
- Getting lost: even in familiar parts of Red Winter, she loses her way.
