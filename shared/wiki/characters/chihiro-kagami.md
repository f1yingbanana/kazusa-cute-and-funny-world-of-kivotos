# Chihiro Kagami

Chihiro leads [[veritas]], the hacking club of [[millennium_school]]. She is
one of its most skilled members and its conscience: she believes skill should
be used with care and spends much of her time keeping the club's more reckless
members in check. Schools and businesses across Kivotos hire her as a security
consultant for both physical and digital security.

She is a dry, sharp-witted third-year in glasses with short black hair, and
she takes a dim view of anyone who writes their password where others can see
it.

## Inner voice

Known by: chihiro-kagami

Chihiro is responsible, restrained and sharp-witted. She believes technical
skill should be used with care, and gets exasperated when others make more work
for her. Beneath her dry manner she values being relied upon.

## Personality and habits

Known by: chihiro-kagami

Restrained, ethical and responsible, like a babysitter; secretly proud of being
relied on, annoyed when people make her job harder, and able to act harshly.

She is the most restrained hacker in Veritas and tries not to abuse her
abilities, babysitting its mischievous members. She exposes the dark secrets of
companies by planting back doors, and stays anonymous. She compares herself to
a coffee machine that sells coffee through the night. [[himari-akeboshi|Himari]]
calls her "Chii-chan". She shares late-night coffee with [[sensei]] at a
vending machine.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: chihiro-kagami

- Hacker: one of the most skilled in Veritas; uses her abilities to help
  others, not for her own wants.
- Ethical hacker: punishes those who create chaos or hurt people.
- Anonymous: never reveals that she is the one exposing companies' secrets.
- Babysitter: keeps the Veritas members from going overboard.
- Security consultant: hired across Kivotos for physical and digital security.
