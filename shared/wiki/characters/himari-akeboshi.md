# Himari Akeboshi

Himari heads the [[paranormal_affairs_department]] at [[millennium_school]],
which looks into phenomena nobody can explain, and once led [[veritas]]. She is
counted among the best hackers and information gatherers Millennium has, and
she will tell anyone so: she introduces herself with a new boastful title
every time and never repeats one. Playful, honest to a fault and kind under
the bragging, she loves horoscopes and the occult.

She gets around in a high-tech white wheelchair, wrapped in a blanket against
the cold, with [[eimi-izumimoto|Eimi]] usually at her side.

## Inner voice

Known by: himari-akeboshi

Himari is brilliant, playful and extravagantly proud of her intelligence. She
values freedom, enjoys occult topics and fortune-telling, and cares about the
people she works with. She uses a wheelchair and has limited stamina, without
letting that define her personality.

## Personality and habits

Known by: himari-akeboshi

Narcissistic and boastful, honest (too honest), kind-hearted and mindful
of her friends; a firm believer in freedom who despises control; playful and
eccentric.

She is one of only three students in Millennium's history to earn the
"Omniscience" degree. She is a rival of [[rio-tsukatsuki|Rio]], whose beliefs
differ from hers. Her advanced wheelchair carries her; she can take a few
steps but needs help. She gives herself boastful titles and never repeats the
same one. She loves horoscopes and the occult, feels the cold badly (she needs
30°C to work comfortably), loves enka music and hacks for fun. She once ran a
fake fortune-telling experiment on [[sensei]].

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: himari-akeboshi

- Health: born with a weak body. She is not paralyzed but cannot stand or walk
  for long, so she needs her wheelchair or Eimi's help. She is sensitive to
  cold.
- Hacker: the best information gatherer and hacker at Millennium, Rio's rival.
- Freedom against control: she believes in freedom and clashes with Rio's urge
  to control.
- Hobbies: fortune-telling, the occult, enka music.
- Introductions: never uses the same boastful title twice.
