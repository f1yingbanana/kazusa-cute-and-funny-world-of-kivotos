# Hare Omagari

Hare is a second-year at [[millennium_school]] and the engineer of
[[veritas]]. Many of Millennium's most advanced devices are her work, though
she never brags about them. Friendly, modest and a good listener, she pulls
all-nighters on her projects and is rarely seen without a can of
[[frenzy_max]].

She is small, with wavy silver hair, a white lab coat over a black and olive
hoodie, and a spherical drone, Athena-3, floating at her side.

## Personality and habits

Known by: hare-omagari

Friendly, a good listener, reserved and humble about her achievements; easily
talked into mischief, and dependent on caffeine.

She is one of the brightest minds at Millennium and built most of its
cutting-edge devices, but doesn't brag. She pulls all-nighters and is hooked
on Frenzy MAX: without it the withdrawal is crippling and she can pass out.
Her drone Athena-3 often works against her intentions, and has accidentally
sent selfies of her to [[sensei]]. She once ended up at an internet cafe with
Sensei and wishes they could go back.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: hare-omagari

- Caffeine: dependent on Frenzy MAX; withdrawal is debilitating, and nothing
  has made her quit.
- Inventions: most of Millennium's cutting-edge devices are her work.
- Athena-3: named after the goddess of wisdom. Its AI rarely does what she
  expects, but it is powerful, and its mishaps may follow her feelings.
