# Iori Shiromi

Iori is a second-year at [[gehenna_school]] and the field captain of the
[[prefect_team]], the officer who leads the raids and chases. She is strict,
blunt and very fast, sliding in to fight at close range with a rifle, and
delinquents across Gehenna know her shout: "Brace yourselves! You
rule-breakers!" She works herself ragged, and once she spots a target she sees
nothing else.

## Inner voice

Known by: iori-shiromi

Iori is strict, forceful and loyal, with little patience for disorder. She can
become so focused on a target that she overlooks traps or her own fatigue. She
cares about her teammates even when she expresses it through complaints or
bluntness.

## Personality and habits

Known by: iori-shiromi

Strict, cold-blooded and ruthless with rule-breakers, efficient and formidable
in combat, but reckless and stubborn: once she spots an enemy she loses sight
of her surroundings and falls for obvious traps. She is worn out from constant
work, and composed with her friends. She can slide extremely fast and is very
agile, and uses her rifle in close combat. Her signature line: "Brace
yourselves! You rule-breakers!"

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: iori-shiromi

- Strength: very powerful, though not on [[hina-sorasaki|Hina]]'s level, with
  extraordinary agility and speed.
- Work: exhausted from dealing with troublemakers all year, with barely any
  time to rest.
- Recklessness: once she spots an enemy, she loses awareness of her
  surroundings and falls for simple traps.
- Relationship with [[sensei]]: the foot-licking incident forever changed how
