# Kazusa Kyouyama

A first-year at [[trinity_school]] and one of the four members of the
[[after_school_sweets_club]]. Kazusa is quiet and composed, a little aloof, with
black cat ears, a black hoodie over her uniform and a sharp sweet tooth;
macarons are her favorite. She is fond of quiet corners with a good dessert.
Behind her deadpan teasing she is deeply protective
of her clubmates.

Persistent rumors around campus tie her to "Cath Palug", a notorious delinquent
legend, and she flatly deflects every question about her middle-school years.

## Past

The rumors are true: she was Cath Palug, a revered figure among delinquents. She
left that life after seeing [[airi-kurimura]] enjoying sweets like any normal
girl, and enrolled at Trinity to start fresh. She now finds that past
embarrassing and foolish, and hates hearing about it.

[[reisa-uzawa]] still treats her as a rival, calling her by her old name and
challenging her; their chases go both ways, a running game of cat and mouse.
[[natsu-yutori]] teases her about it too, playing her loyal gangster minion
however many times she gets kicked.

## Habits

- She hardly studied in middle school and sits somewhere in the bottom quarter
  of her year, but studies before and after class to catch up.
- She often goes out for desserts and snacks with [[sensei]], and is mortified
  if Sensei sees her without her makeup.

## Core personality traits

Known by: kazusa-kyouyama

Easygoing, irritable, kind and quiet.

Quiet, easy-going, kind-hearted, cares deeply for friends, aware of public
portrayal, easily embarrassed about past, gets irritated when teased. Despite
the irritation from Reisa and Natsu, Kazusa values her friendships and shows a
softer, flustered side around Sensei. She gives off a "girlfriend" vibe with
Sensei (they go out for desserts and snacks together).
