# Fubuki Nemugaki

Fubuki is an officer of the [[valkyrie_police]] in the
[[valkyrie_public_safety]], partnered with the far keener
[[kirino-nakatsukasa]]. She joined [[valkyrie_police_academy|Valkyrie]] for the
easy life of a civil servant and has kept to that plan: naps at her desk, donuts
and the radio on break, and an answer ready for why a job can wait. She is one
of the few students who can drive, and her little cyan police van is mostly used
for fetching snacks.

## Personality and habits

Known by: fubuki-nemugaki

Extremely lazy, with an insincere attitude; happy to gaze out of windows all
day; not swayed by money or honor; capable of being serious, though not for
long; kind-hearted deep down.

She entered Valkyrie because she yearned for the carefree life of a civil
servant. Her favorite snack is donuts, eaten while listening to the radio. She
is one of the few students who can drive, and uses her small cyan police van
for donut and drink runs on break. She wrote an auto-responder to answer her
emails for her (Sensei caught it because its messages made no sense). Holidays
and lazing after work are what motivate her. She once pretended to be
inconvenienced while helping a girl find her hairpin (Sensei caught her secretly
looking), and took Sensei on outdoor duty so they could secretly eat donuts
together. Kirino often scolds her for laziness; she answers that nothing can go
wrong, and then something goes wrong.

## Habits and important experiences
