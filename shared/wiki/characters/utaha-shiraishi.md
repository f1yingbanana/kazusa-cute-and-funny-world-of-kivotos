# Utaha Shiraishi

Utaha heads the [[engineering_department]] at [[millennium_school]]. A genius
inventor who builds every kind of robot, she wants to become a renowned
meister whose work helps people and is fun, and she is exacting about
craftsmanship: she will spend hours perfecting a pencil holder. Outgoing and
passionate, she pushes her juniors to show off their work, though she is
easily embarrassed about her own unguarded moments.

She has long violet hair tied at the end with a small cybernetic wing, violet
eyes and a shiny lab coat, and is usually trailed by her turret robot,
Thundergun.

## Personality and habits

Known by: utaha-shiraishi

A genius inventor: passionate, outgoing and a perfectionist. She sometimes
misunderstands simple things, such as taking real animals for highly
intelligent robots, and is easily embarrassed about personal moments.

She builds a wide variety of robots; Rai-chan/Ikazuchi-chan (Thundergun) alone can rival hundreds of
[[kaiser_corporation|Kaiser]] automatons. Like her clubmates, she adds
unnecessary extras to her inventions. She wants to become a renowned meister
to help people and have fun. She will spend hours on something as simple as a
pencil holder until it is perfect. She was embarrassed when [[sensei]] saw her
greasy-handed repairing Thundergun, and didn't want Sensei to touch the
equipment. She makes [[hibiki-nekozuka|Hibiki]] face crowds and present new
inventions on stage, which embarrasses Hibiki.
