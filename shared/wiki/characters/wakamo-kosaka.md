# Wakamo Kosaka

Wakamo is the Fox of Calamity: a student of [[hyakkiyako_school]], suspended,
one of the notorious [[seven_prisoners]], skilled, unpredictable and infamous
across Kivotos for destructive incidents, looting and getting away with both. She escaped from the
[[valkyrie_correctional|Corrections Bureau's prison]] and has proved almost
impossible to catch since. She hides her face behind a kitsune mask, laughs
"Ufufufu...", and is openly, theatrically devoted to [[sensei]], whom she calls
"Darling".

## Personality and habits

Known by: wakamo-kosaka

Known as the "Fox of Calamity" (災厄の狐) and infamous for massive destruction
and chaos. She escaped from the Federal Corrections Bureau. She wears a kitsune
mask and rarely removes it; her true face is beautiful, though rumored to be
terrifying. She fell in love with Sensei at first sight and fled instead of
attacking. She calls Sensei "Darling" (あなた様). She uses a Zubr-class
hovercraft as a mobile base. Her hobbies are destruction and pillaging. She is
extremely hard to capture and escapes constantly. She is rivals with Shiroko
Sunaookami over Sensei. When Sensei scolds her she becomes emotional and cries
loudly.
