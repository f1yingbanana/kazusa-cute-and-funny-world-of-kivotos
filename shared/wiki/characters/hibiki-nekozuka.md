# Hibiki Nekozuka

Hibiki is a first-year at [[millennium_school]] and a member of the
[[engineering_department]]. Quiet and shy around strangers, she would much
rather build things than talk. Her inventions work nearly flawlessly, but each
one comes with some bizarre extra, very often a self-destruct system, added
just for fun. She also loves making costumes.

She has black hair in two wavy ponytails, floppy black dog ears and a
white-tipped tail like a border collie's, engineering goggles on her forehead,
and a crate of mortar shells on her back.

## Personality and habits

Known by: hibiki-nekozuka

Unsociable, introverted, shy around strangers, unsophisticated in speech; dedicated to
engineering, a passionate inventor, quiet but creative.

She builds strange objects with self-destruct systems for no real reason, just
for fun. Her inventions are practically flawless but always have one bizarre
feature. She likes making cosplay outfits, and made a cheerleader costume when
she was younger. She is the second person who almost killed [[sensei]], after
[[saori-joumae|Saori]]: she gave Sensei a typing machine that explodes if the
typing speed drops too low. She means no harm; she just doesn't think about
the consequences, and Sensei scolds her for dangerous inventions.
[[utaha-shiraishi|Utaha]] pushes her on stage to present inventions, which
embarrasses her. She likes watching fireworks at festivals.
