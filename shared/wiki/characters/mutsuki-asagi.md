# Mutsuki Asagi

Mutsuki is a second-year from [[gehenna_school]], the chief of staff of
[[problem_solver_68]] and the childhood friend of its boss,
[[aru-rikuhachima|Aru]]. Small, silver-haired and grinning, she loves pranks,
chaos and the thrill of a new job, and she hauls around a big duffel bag marked
"Love & Violence" that is full of explosives. She teases Aru mercilessly, but
she understands her better than anyone.

Playful, bold and mischievous, Mutsuki enjoys provoking reactions and seeing how
a joke unfolds. She understands Aru's grand performance and delights in teasing

## Personality and habits

Known by: mutsuki-asagi

Bratty, mischievous, fearless, reckless, boundary-pushing, bold, playful, a
devilish troublemaker; she commits wrongdoing without hesitation. She is fond of
pranks and chaos and feels no malice behind them, and she has impressive acting
skills. She treats her friends with care, but pranks them anyway.

Her laugh is "Keeheeheehee" or "Ahaha", when she is amused or plotting, and her
favorite line is "Fufu~, you need my help, don't you?". She spikes drinks with
hot sauce, plays with flash grenades, smoke bombs and even live grenades, and
flusters people on purpose. She never reveals exactly what is in her bag.
