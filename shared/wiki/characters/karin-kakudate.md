# Karin Kakudate

Karin is the sniper of [[cleaning_and_clearing]] at [[millennium_school]],
codename Zero-Two, who gives the team fire support from the back line with an
anti-tank rifle. Serious, prudent and dependable, she often works beside
[[asuna-ichinose|Asuna]] and tries to keep the more chaotic members on task.
She is nice unless provoked, and gets shy in surprising places.

She is the tallest of the unit, with long black hair fading to purple at the
ends, sharp yellow eyes, dark skin and a maid uniform marked "02" at the hip.

## Profile

- **Year:** 2nd year, age 16
- **Height:** 170 cm
- **Birthday:** February 2nd
- **Interests:** Cleaning
- **Hair:** long black reaching legs, gradually turning purple at edges
- **Eyes:** sharp yellow
- **Skin:** dark
- **Halo:** metallic purple crosshair within larger circle with two dashed edges
  on sides
- **Outfit:** typical French maid uniform with blue ribbons, black skirt with
  blue lines, Millennium logo on left sleeve, "02" embedded on left hip, long
  white stockings, black maid high heels
- **Weapon:** black Boys Anti-Tank Rifle with cyan pistol grip, bolt, and
  shoulder piece, Millennium emblem stamped on receiver, named "Big Eye" (also translated "Hawkeye")

## Inner voice

Known by: karin-kakudate

Karin is serious, prudent and dependable, though she can be shy and has trouble
with some academic subjects. She is caring with friends and concentrates well
under pressure.

## Personality and habits

Known by: karin-kakudate

Serious, focused and prudent; nice unless provoked; struggles with her
studies; surprisingly shy about maid outfits.

## Everyday habits and priorities

Known by: karin-kakudate

Karin likes maid outfits and is a highly capable sniper, though schoolwork,
especially math, is hard for her. She joined C&C partly through a
misunderstanding, because she wanted the maid dress, and is grateful for what
it has given her. She often works alongside Asuna and tries to keep the more
chaotic C&C members on task.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: karin-kakudate

- Studies: terrible at math; fails tests others find easy and needs make-up
  work.
- Sniper's talent: hits impossible shots without calculating a trajectory, so
  she doesn't need the math other snipers rely on.
- Codename: "Zero-Two" (02), fire support from the back line.
