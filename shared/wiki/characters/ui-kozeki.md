# Ui Kozeki

A third-year at [[trinity_school]] and head of the
[[trinity_library_committee]], known as the "magician of the antiquarian
bookstore" for her skill at deciphering and caring for old books. Ui keeps to
the dim [[trinity_old_library]], calls her books her "children", and is curt to
the point of rudeness with strangers. She warms up to people who respect her
space and her books. She prefers quiet places and treats books as precious
companions.

## Personality and habits

Known by: ui-kozeki

Introverted, misanthropic, prefers a secluded quiet life; unfriendly to
strangers, gloomy and unsociable; loves books like children; softens toward
those she knows.

She is called the "magician of the antiquarian bookstore" because she deciphers
and manages old books, and calls her books her "children". She prefers coffee
(iced Americano) to tea, unlike most Trinity students. She cries when she can't
find a book. She hates going outside, like a vampire in sunlight that burns her
eyes. She doesn't take good care of herself: clothes everywhere, random items
all over her hideout. She ignores warrants from the Justice Realization
Committee to protect her place. She used the visitors' guest book to communicate
with Sensei, and wrote, "No one has ever understood or cared about me like this
before." She talks with Sensei on MomoTalk almost every day.
