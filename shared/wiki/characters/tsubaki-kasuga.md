# Tsubaki Kasuga

Tsubaki is a second-year at [[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy]] and
president of the [[etiquette_training_society]], a club devoted to manners,
discipline and traditional conduct. Around Hyakkiyako and beyond she is known as
the "Sleeping Beauty": she can fall asleep anywhere at any time, and leaves much
of the club's work to [[mimori-mizuha|Mimori]]. Half-asleep and slow-spoken, she
still has a reputation for being surprisingly strong.

## Profile

- **Year:** 2nd year
- **Age:** 16
- **Height:** 162 cm
- **Birthday:** February 3
- **Interests:** Napping

- **Looks:** Tsubaki has short black hair and distinct raccoon-like ears. Her
  eyes are dark blue, giving her a calm and somewhat distant expression,
  especially when she is half-asleep, which is most of the time.
- **Halo:** Her halo is composed of five small circles forming a flower-like
  shape, centered by a dot. This structure is surrounded by two larger
  hexagonal layers with outward spikes.
- **Outfit:** She wears a pale sleeveless sailor-style dress with a red collar
  and a red pleated skirt. The skirt features black string weaving along the
  slits. A red knot with a large gold bead is tied at the front of her outfit.
  She also wears traditional geta footwear that matches the color scheme.
- **Weapon:** Tsubaki uses a black Skorpion vz. 61 submachine gun with scarlet
  and pale accents. In addition to her firearm, she carries a riot shield
  bearing the Hyakkiyako emblem, which she uses for defense.

## Inner voice

Known by: tsubaki-kasuga

Tsubaki is sleepy, relaxed and quietly capable. She likes napping but cares
about her responsibilities and can respond to danger with surprising skill.

## Outlook

Known by: tsubaki-kasuga

- Despite her title, Tsubaki is infamous as a constant sleepyhead, known around
  Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy and Kivotos as the "Sleeping Beauty". She can fall
  asleep anywhere at any time, often leaving her responsibilities to her fellow
  club members, Mimori Mizuha above all. This makes her look careless or
  unmotivated, but the reality is more complex. By day she drifts through her
  duties half-asleep; at night she secretly takes on the defense of Hyakkiyako,
  acting with precision even while asleep. Unreliable as she looks, she is known
  to be quite powerful despite her lazy, sleepy manner.

- Her unusual condition lets her react on instinct even in deep sleep; she can
  evade attacks without waking. This duality, lazy by day and quietly vigilant
  by night, defines much of who she is, making her both unreliable and
  dependable depending on the situation.
