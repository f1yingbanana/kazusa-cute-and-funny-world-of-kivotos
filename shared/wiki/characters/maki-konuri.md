# Maki Konuri

Maki is a first-year at [[millennium_school]] and the youngest spark of
[[veritas]], whose emblem she designed. She loves hacking, video games and
graffiti: a boring gray wall seems to her to be begging for color, and she
will tag it again the moment nobody is watching, police or not. Optimistic,
energetic and quick to go with the flow, she has a prodigious memory and very
little caution.

She has vibrant red hair in two buns, sky blue eyes, a navy hoodie with a pink
Veritas logo, and paint stains on her clothes.

## Personality and habits

Known by: maki-konuri

Mischievous, optimistic, go-with-the-flow, artistic, passionate, rebellious and
energetic, with a prodigious memory.

She created the Veritas emblem. She tags boring gray walls, and even after the
police get involved she does it again when no one is looking; only [[sensei]]
can make her stop, sometimes. She installs "BOOM" (Kivotos version of DOOM) on every
electronic device she can. She has a prodigious talent for memorizing: book
industry details, code, paper width. She rarely unties her buns. She wants to make
Sensei proud, and once promised Sensei to study harder and stop illegal
tagging. Her clothes are covered in paint splotches.
