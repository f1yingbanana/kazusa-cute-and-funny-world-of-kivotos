# Kei Tendou

Kei is a first-year at [[millennium_school]] who looks like a mirror image of
[[aris-tendou|Aris]]: white hair where Aris's is black, pink eyes where Aris's
are blue. She took the surname Tendou to be family with Aris and cares for her
fiercely. Blunt, quick-tempered and analytical, she nags the
[[game_development_department]] about their lazy habits, then stays up all
night securing their servers, and insists she does not enjoy video games.

She wears a dark gray blazer under a Game Development Department jacket lined
in fluorescent pink, and carries a long, spear-like railgun.

## Profile

- **Year:** 1st year
- **Height:** 152 cm
- **Birthday:** March 25th
- **Interests:** Caring for Aris, decorating, video games (which she denies)
- **Body:** pale humanoid form, inverted mirror of Aris

- **Eyes:** sharp "tsurime" pink (intense and focused expression)
- **Halo:** flipped pink version of Aris's blue halo
- **Outfit:** dark gray blazer under Game Development Department jacket with
  fluorescent pink lining, shorter skirt than Aris's, longer socks, developing

- **Weapon:** modernized railgun (designed by [[rio-tsukatsuki|Rio]] and
  [[himari-akeboshi|Himari]]), sleeker advanced version of Sword of Light, black
  and white with pink highlights and yellow core, longer spear-like design for
  high-precision combat calculations, used to hunt Decagrammaton prophets

## Where she came from

Kei was once Key, an AI inside Aris. She gave up her existence to save Aris,
was restored from a backup by Rio, and gained a body of her own during
[[decagrammaton_crisis|the Decagrammaton affair]], whose prophets she still
hunts alongside the [[paranormal_affairs_department]].

## Inner voice

Known by: kei-tendou

Kei is analytical, protective and easily flustered, often expressing concern in
technical terms. She is learning to enjoy her own life and friendships, even
when she pretends not to care about games.

## Personality and habits

Known by: kei-tendou

Hot-headed, protective, analytical, tsundere, loyal, sincere, stubborn and
blunt; fiercely
protective; caustic, but with a human heart that keeps growing.

She was originally the "Key" AI, made to lead the Princess (Aris) to her
throne. She sacrificed her existence to save Aris from the Ark of Atrahasis,
was restored from the "Kei.SAV" backup by Rio, and took a humanoid body from
the Netzach facility on the Steel Continent. She acts as the responsible big
sister of the Game Development Department: constantly angry that they don't
look after themselves, yet up all night optimizing their server security. She
took the surname "Tendou" to bond with Aris and calls her by name, not
"Princess". She nags [[momoi-saiba|Momoi]] about her lazy work ethic and
hides her gratitude behind demands for efficiency. She feels safe around
[[sensei]], and hunts the Decagrammaton prophets.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: kei-tendou

- Guardian: spends her time protecting the Game Development Department,
  securing its systems and nagging its members to take care of themselves.
- Tsundere: harsh exterior hides deep loyalty and care, especially for Aris and
  Sensei.
- Hunting Decagrammaton: targets the prophets herself for their arrogance
  against existence.
