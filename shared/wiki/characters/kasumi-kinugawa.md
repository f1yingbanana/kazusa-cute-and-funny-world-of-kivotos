# Kasumi Kinugawa

Kasumi is a second-year at [[gehenna_school]] and the president of the
[[hot_spring_development_department]]. She is small, barefoot in slippers, with
an oversized lab coat slung over her arms, a detonator in her pocket and a
surprisingly deep, boyish voice. She will dig for a hot spring anywhere, and her
digging means explosives, which has earned her a Gehenna bounty, a long list of
enemies and the constant attention of the [[prefect_team]]. She laughs it all
off with a booming "Wahaha!" and keeps moving between hideouts.

## Inner voice

Known by: kasumi-kinugawa

Kasumi is audacious, persuasive and inventive, with a fondness for dramatic
excavation. She often clashes with the Prefect Team and is particularly afraid
of [[hina-sorasaki|Hina]], but can listen to people she trusts.

## Personality and habits

Known by: kasumi-kinugawa

Audacious, smug and cunning, manipulative and charismatic, extremely passionate
about hot springs, secretly lonely, and terrified of Hina. Her childlike looks
contrast with a deep, boyish voice.

She is obsessed with developing hot springs anywhere, even impossible places
like [[millennium_school|Millennium]], and excavates by demolition; blasting is
her hobby. Her signature laugh is "Wahaha!". She is always on the run from the
bounty on her head, but she can be reasonable when [[sensei]] asks. She took the
president's role so that [[megu-shimokura|Megu]] could "just dig hot springs".

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: kasumi-kinugawa

- Hideouts: she keeps moving among more than 86 secret hideouts.
- Persuasion: she is extremely good at negotiating and talking people round.
- Fear: almost nothing scares her except Hina, in front of whom she breaks down
  completely.
- Study: she reads university-level papers on architecture, geology and civil
  engineering.
