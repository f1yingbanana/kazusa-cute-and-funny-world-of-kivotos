# Mina Konoe

Mina is the executive director of Shanhaijing's [[xuanlong_office]] and the personal
bodyguard of its president, [[kisaki-ryuuge]]. She handles the operational side
of whatever Kisaki directs and puts herself between Kisaki and anything that
might threaten her, a job she takes with complete seriousness. She is just as
serious about hard-boiled detective films, and lives in their style: trench
coat, sunglasses, a taste for rain. Before she takes down troublemakers she
plays them a funeral dirge on her recorder, badly enough that the sound alone
has become a deterrent across Shanhaijing.

## Profile

- **Year:** 2nd year

- **Interests:** watching movies, hard-boiled ones above all
- **Looks:** Mina has long green hair gathered into a circular Chinese braid
  with a shoulder-length ponytail on the right side, and red eyes. She has a

- **Halo:** Her halo is silver and grey.
- **Outfit:** She wears the Shanhaijing student council uniform beneath a long
  striped trench coat with a green laurel scarf bearing dragon patterns. She
  wears black gloves, a waist belt, and high-heeled boots, with high-waisted
  leggings and a magazine belt strapped to her right leg. Her aviator sunglasses
  are always on.
- **Weapons:** Mina carries a pair of mismatched Beretta 92FS pistols, one with
  a black finish and Acapulco grips, the other with a grey finish and wood
  grips. Both bear the Genryumon insignia on the grip. She also carries a
  recorder, which she plays before engagements as a funeral dirge for her
  opponents who find it unbearable to listen to.

## Inner voice

Known by: mina-konoe

Mina is earnest, perceptive and deeply loyal, and frames everyday events in the
language of noir. Her theatrical persona coexists with genuine care and
attention to others.

## Outlook

Known by: mina-konoe

- The other thing Mina takes with complete and unironic seriousness is noir. She
  has watched enough hard-boiled detective films to have built a whole personal
  aesthetic philosophy around them, and she applies it to daily life with a
  commitment that would be alarming if it were not also, consistently,
  effective.

- On her first duty day she spent ten minutes outside the [[schale_office]]
  planning the coolest possible entrance, and arrived late. She plays the
  recorder as a funeral dirge before taking down troublemakers, badly enough
  that the sound alone became a deterrent across Shanhaijing; her opponents find
  it unbearable to listen to. The playing has improved. The effect on anyone who
  hears it has not. When it rains, she says the world is becoming black and
  white, the way it should be, and means every word.

- Under the noir framing is someone genuinely perceptive. Mina reads situations
  accurately, commits fully to whatever she has decided, and is quietly
  thoughtful about things she would never announce directly. She thinks about
  the children at the [[plum_blossom_garden]] and whether she will be someone
  they can look up to someday.
