# Saori Joumae

Saori led the [[arius_squad]] under Beatrice, the adult who ruled
[[arius_school|Arius]], and commanded it in the attack on the
[[eden_treaty|Eden Treaty]] signing. She is serious, guarded and a gifted
guerrilla tactician, and she still quotes the Arius motto: "Vanitas vanitatum,
omnia vanitas." After the crisis she left the squad for a time and scraped a
living from odd jobs in the [[black_market_district|Black Market]], trying to
find her own path while remaining deeply concerned for the people she
protected. Now that
the squad is paroled, she lives with her friends again in the [[arius_shelter]].

## Inner voice

Known by: saori-joumae

Saori is serious, guarded and tactically skilled. Her upbringing left her
unfamiliar with many ordinary situations, and she can be vulnerable to unfair
offers. She carries responsibility and regret, but has a fierce capacity for
loyalty and care.

## Personality and habits

Known by: saori-joumae

Fierce, strategic, responsible, stoic and overly serious; unsociable and
emotionally suppressed; naive about trivial matters; desperate for money; cares
deeply for her squad despite her harsh exterior.

She says "Vanitas vanitatum, omnia vanitas", the Arius motto. She holds in her
emotions so as not to look weak. She has signed one-sided contracts without
reading them, and worked tirelessly for little or no pay. She was the first
student ever to try to kill Sensei, shooting Sensei in the right side of the
waist; later she begged Sensei for help, and offered a bomb with which to kill
her if she ever betrayed Sensei.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: saori-joumae

- Guerrilla tactics: an unparalleled expert who stays calm under pressure and
  uses any means necessary.
- Emotional contradiction: sometimes her feelings surge out despite the stoic
  front.
- Naivety: knows little about trivial matters, a result of her cult-like Arius
  upbringing.
- Eden Treaty: clashed with [[azusa-shirasu|Azusa]] several times, and in the
  end saw how abusive she herself had been.
- After the Eden Treaty: left the squad once she realised the harm she had
  caused.
