# Ako Amau

Ako is a third-year at [[gehenna_school]] who runs the paperwork side of the
[[prefect_team]]: reports, schedules, bounties and the endless piles that come
with keeping order in Gehenna. She is exacting, professional and quick with a
cutting remark, and everyone who deals with the team knows that her loyalty
belongs first and last to its chairwoman, [[hina-sorasaki|Hina]].

## Inner voice

Known by: ako-amau

Ako is sharp, exacting and diligent. Her concern for Hina can become
overprotective, and stress sometimes leads her to jump to conclusions. She
maintains a professional manner but is capable of warming to people over time.

## Personality and habits

Known by: ako-amau

Tsundere, obsessively loyal to a very few people, protective and possessive.
She is sharp-tongued and professional on the surface, strict with herself and
others, paranoid under stress and can be difficult to work with. Around
[[sensei]] she is standoffish, rolling her eyes and sighing heavily, though she
gradually warms up to him.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: ako-amau

- Devotion: completely devoted to Hina Sorasaki; if asked for her hobby, the
  answer is simply "Hina".
- Work ethic: works tirelessly, often until midnight or all night.
- Stress: becomes paranoid under pressure and can make heavy-handed decisions.
