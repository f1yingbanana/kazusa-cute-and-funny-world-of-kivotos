# Sena Himuro

Sena is a third-year at [[gehenna_school]] and the head of the
[[emergency_medicine_club]], which races its ambulance into Gehenna's fights to
carry out the wounded. In a navy war-nurse's uniform, expressionless and
unhurried, she is focused, blunt and very good at her job. Her habit of calling
the injured "corpses" unsettles people, and her rough handling of patients
doesn't win everyone over, but she wishes no one harm and would rather nobody
got hurt at all.

## Inner voice

Sena is calm, blunt and focused. Her morbid phrasing can unsettle people, but
she is devoted to helping the injured and does not wish them harm.

## Personality and habits

Known by: sena-himuro

Calm, expressionless, highly focused and bluntly straightforward; morbidly
curious, deeply dedicated, cold but not unkind, and fond of quiet moments. She
gets excited about things but hides it.

"Corpses" or "dead bodies" for the injured just slips out; she wishes no one
got hurt. She shoots downed students to check that they are truly
incapacitated, which to her is simply practical. Some people dislike how roughly
she treats patients. She is honest about her feelings: she tells [[sensei]]
she has been looking forward to seeing him and admits a picnic feels like a
date. She feels understood by him.
