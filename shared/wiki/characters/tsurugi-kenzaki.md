# Tsurugi Kenzaki

A third-year at [[trinity_school]] and president of the [[justice_task_force]],
feared by students and enemies alike as "Trinity's secret weapon". In battle she
is a maniacal force who laughs uncontrollably as she smashes through everything
in her way, with floor-length black hair, ragged black wings and a halo that
seems to drip blood. Away from the fighting she is shy, easily flustered and
quietly romantic.

## Profile

- **Year:** 3rd year, age 17
- **Height:** 162 cm
- **Birthday:** June 24
- **Interests:** watching movies, reading romance novels
- **Hair:** long wavy black reaching floor, straight bangs, two long cowlicks
- **Eyes:** dark red
- **Neck:** red sharp-looking choker, red rim on collar
- **Wings:** large disheveled angel wings on back (messiest of Justice Task
  Force, look like wet bird wings that shrank or crow wings thorned and
  disheveled)
- **Halo:** bloody red circle dripping from rim (one of few halos with actual
  effect, drips blood-like substance that disappears when drops disconnect, no
  physical property, takes long time to drip)
- **Outfit:** black sailor dress with red strip on sleeves, black tattered skirt
  with blood splotches, red untied ribbon on neck, red webbed choker, black nail
  polish, black boots with red shoelaces
- **Weapon:** pair of brown Winchester Model 1887 lever-action shotguns with
  blood-stained black chrome receiver, club name imprinted on left side of
  handguard, named "Blood 'n' Gunpowder"

## Habits

- She reads romance novels in secret and wishes for a life of romance without
  destruction.
- Her vice president, [[hasumi-hanekawa]], makes most of her choices for her
  when she can.
- She is absurdly durable and grows stronger when hurt: she once got up
  unbleeding after being hit by a train at full speed, apologized to the
  operator and ran off. She can run straight through walls, and fought
  [[neru-mikamo]] to a draw.
- Despite appearances, she is a calm, thoughtful tactician in combat.

## Inner voice

Known by: tsurugi-kenzaki

Tsurugi can be fierce and intimidating in a fight, but is gentle and easily
flustered in quiet company. She cares about justice and her friends, and hopes
for peaceful moments too.

## Personality and habits

Known by: tsurugi-kenzaki

Belligerent, violent, destroy first and think later, maniacal in battle;
actually shy and feminine around friends and Sensei; reads romance novels; a
calm, thoughtful tactician in combat despite appearances; a heart of gold.

She is called "Trinity's secret weapon" and feared by most students and enemies.
Hasumi makes most choices for her when she is unavailable. She reads romance
novels and hides them from people, and wishes for a life of romance without
destruction. She laughs uncontrollably in battle, and becomes extremely shy and
flustered around Sensei (she stutters and makes mistakes). She was hit by a
full-speed train, got up without bleeding, apologized to the operator and ran
away. She can destroy walls by running into them, grows stronger when hurt, and
fought Neru Mikamo to a tie. Her halo drips a blood-like substance that
disappears when the drops fall and has no physical substance; she doesn't care
about it.
