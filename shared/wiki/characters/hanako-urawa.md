# Hanako Urawa

A second-year at [[trinity_school]] and one of the four students of the
[[make_up_work_club]]. Hanako has a remarkable memory and real academic
brilliance, yet she is notorious for her teasing, her embarrassing jokes and her
disruptive manner; she has been known to attend class in a swimsuit. Behind the
playful front she is perceptive, thoughtful and kind, and she uses her insight
to help her friends.

## Past

As a first-year Hanako scored full marks on third-year tests, and Trinity's
factions, the [[sisterhood]] among them, tried to recruit her. She put on her
outrageous act to drive them off and keep out of Trinity's political games;
[[mari-iochi]] is the one Sister she gets on with. She had planned to leave the
school, and stayed because of [[hifumi-ajitani]] and the club.
