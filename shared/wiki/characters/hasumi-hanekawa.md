# Hasumi Hanekawa

A third-year at [[trinity_school]] and vice president of the
[[justice_task_force]], who handles much of its planning and formal business.
Hasumi is tall and composed, with long black hair, dark red eyes and black wings
big enough to cover a person, and she carries an ornate gold-trimmed rifle. She
takes her duties and her ideals of justice seriously, and makes little secret of
her distrust of [[gehenna_school]].

## Profile

- **Year:** 3rd year, age 17
- **Height:** 179 cm
- **Birthday:** December 12
- **Interests:** reading, people-watching
- **Hair:** long black reaching waist with loose bangs
- **Eyes:** dark red
- **Wings:** large black angel wings on back (so big they could cover someone
  entirely)
- **Halo:** four red sharp daggers surrounding it, four-point star fills inner
  portion of ring
- **Outfit:** black sailor dress with red strip on torn pleated skirt and
  sleeves, Trinity logo on left sleeve, red tie, black ballet shoes with
  stockings, black gloves, thigh-high stockings
- **Weapon:** black Pattern 1914 Enfield bolt-action battle rifle with gold
  ornaments decorated all over, gold Trinity insignia on stock, named
  "Impalement"

## Habits

- She has a fierce sweet tooth and orders desserts by the dozen, while
  constantly worrying about her weight. She says she might have joined the
  [[after_school_sweets_club]] had she not joined the Task Force.
- She makes many of the Task Force's decisions for its president,
  [[tsurugi-kenzaki]].
- She was one of the first four students who helped [[sensei]] reclaim
  [[schale]] when they arrived in Kivotos.
- She likes quiet time alone in the park at sunset before club duties.

be prejudiced against Gehenna, though working with students from other schools
can challenge her assumptions. She likes sweets and often struggles to reconcile
that with her concerns about dieting.
