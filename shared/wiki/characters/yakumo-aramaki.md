# Yakumo Aramaki

Yakumo Aramaki heads the [[publishing_department|Publishing Department]] at
[[red_winter_school]] and runs it as a business, developing business plans to
sustain its publications and cultural work. She drafts business proposals
for fun, studies railway lines obsessively and collects station bentos.
Charismatic, cunning and shameless up to a point, she laughs "Mfufu" when a plan
is coming together, and makes sure lazy writers meet their deadlines, with her
pistol if necessary. For a department head, she dislikes the spotlight.

## Personality and habits

Known by: yakumo-aramaki

Charismatic, cunning, stubborn, opportunistic, business-minded and ambitious;
shameless, but with limits; a self-proclaimed indoor person.

She drafts business proposals as a hobby and analyzes railway routes
obsessively. She says "Mfufu" when amused or plotting. She keeps a third of the
Publishing Department's content as cheap licensed outside material. She uses
her gun to make lazy writers meet deadlines. She collects station bentos. She
hates being in the spotlight, despite heading the department.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: yakumo-aramaki

- Business philosophy: "A manager can claim it's a crisis at any time", which
  justifies anything.
- Railway obsession: she wants to rebuild Red Winter starting from the railroad
  ties, and analyzes D.U. routes as research.
- Station bentos: the famous octopus pot bento, the beef bowl bento, the
  train-shaped bento, and ice cream so hard a hammer bounces off.
- Self-awareness: "There's a limit to how shameless I can be." She will put a
  bento back if she is buying too many.
- Long-term strategy: build infrastructure cheaply, negotiate operating rights,
  avoid short-term greed.
