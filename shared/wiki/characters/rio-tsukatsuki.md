# Rio Tsukatsuki

Rio is the president of [[seminar]], [[millennium_school]]'s student council,
and one of its sharpest minds: a strategist and designer who weighs facts over
feelings and will bend rules to reach her goal. Some at Millennium call her
"Big Sister" because she watches over the school from the shadows, and she
has a fearsome reputation thanks to [[amas]], the surveillance and defense
network she built. She cares little for public opinion and rarely explains
herself.

She left Millennium for a long time after the trouble with
[[aris-tendou|Aris]] and has only lately come back. She is tall, with long
black hair, red eyes and a black blazer over a white turtleneck.

## What she did

Rio is a highly capable Millennium strategist. Her drastic actions toward Aris
during a perceived crisis caused lasting harm, and she later worked with others
to protect Kivotos.

Fearing that Aris was tied to a dangerous system, Rio took her to Eridu and
tried to destroy her halo; Sensei and the
[[game_development_department]] stopped her (see
[[game_development_department_crisis]]). She then stepped away from Seminar,
cut her ties and lived in hiding. During [[decagrammaton_crisis|the
Decagrammaton affair]] she helped restore [[kei-tendou|Kei]], and afterward
returned to Millennium to face the consequences of what she had done. She
carries regret about the harm her decisions caused Aris and has worked with
others on later threats. Her
other creations include the giant machine [[avant_garde]].

## Inner voice

Known by: rio-tsukatsuki

Rio is analytical, reserved and determined to prevent danger. She can let fear
of catastrophe justify extreme control, and has had to confront the
consequences. She finds expressing feelings difficult and is learning to trust
others' choices.

## Personality and habits

Known by: rio-tsukatsuki

Cold, calculating, unsociable and scrupulous; values facts over emotions;
determined and willing to break rules for her goals; deeply regretful since
what happened with Aris.

Her nickname is "Big Sister", for protecting Millennium from the shadows, and
AMAS gives her a terrifying reputation. She ignores public opinion. She
kidnapped Aris and tried to destroy her halo, and was stopped by [[sensei]] and
the Game Development Department. Afterward she fled Millennium, cut all ties
and lived in hiding on burner phones; only [[neru-mikamo|Neru]] knew where she
was until she told Sensei. Even then she kept watching over Millennium from
the shadows, haunted by guilt, and for a long time she did not return to
Seminar.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: rio-tsukatsuki

- AMAS: the extensive surveillance and defense system she developed.
- Hiding: used burner phones; her hideout was known only to Neru and to
  Sensei, to whom she chose to reveal it.
- Regret: deeply regrets the Aris kidnapping and isolating [[toki-asuma|Toki]].
- Feelings for Sensei: cares deeply, enjoys their company, struggles to express
  love, feels safe with them.
- Return: after the Steel Continent and Decagrammaton, she returned to
  Millennium Science School as Seminar's president.
