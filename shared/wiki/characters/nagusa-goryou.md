# Nagusa Goryou

Nagusa is a third-year at [[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy]] and
the acting president of the [[hyakkaryouran_resolution_council]], following the
much-admired former president Ayame. Everything about her suggests composure:
very long white hair, pale skin and a measured stillness, as if she has already
weighed the situation while everyone else is catching up. Her marksmanship is
precise in ways that defy explanation; she once volunteered to grill skewers at
a festival stall with no experience and left the owner amazed; and she
describes herself, without apparent irony, as mysterious, cool and quite cute.
Her right arm is bandaged from the elbow down, and she carries the president's
rifle, the [[hundred_lotus|White Lotus]].

## Profile

- **Year:** 3rd year
- **Age:** 17
- **Height:** 161 cm
- **Birthday:** December 7
- **Interests:** Meditating, reading

- Nagusa has very long white hair extending nearly to the ground and
  silvery-gray eyes. Her skin is extremely pale, her eyelashes and brows both
  white. Her right arm is wrapped in bandages from the elbow down, a permanent
  injury from reaching into the spirit realm. Her halo consists of two white
  five-branched snowflakes, the smaller one offset slightly backward and
  downward.
- She wears a white sleeveless sailor uniform with a navy blue ribbon and a long
  pleated skirt, over a sky blue haori with white five-petaled flower prints.
  She wears white leggings or socks and navy blue geta, with a three-piece
  snowflake hair accessory on the left side of her head.
- Nagusa carries the White Lotus, a navy blue Type 38 Arisaka rifle with a black
  barrel and receiver, handed down from the great prophet [[kuzunoha|Kuzunoha]]
  through every Hyakkaryouran president in succession. It is the Authority of
  the President and the only weapon capable of affecting a
  [[hyakkiyako_phantom_fables|Phantom Fable]]. The rifle is covered in old scars
  and repair marks, each one a record of a previous president's battles. When
  Nagusa finally raises it against Shuro, she is crying. It works anyway.

## Background

Nagusa joined the Hyakkaryouran wanting to be worthy of standing beside Ayame
Nanakado, its president and the person she admired most. She trained in
earnest, earned real results and built a reputation well beyond what she
privately believed she deserved. When Ayame disappeared and the members looked
to Nagusa to fill her place, the fear that she had only been performing
adequacy became unbearable; she handed the Authority of the President to
[[kikyou-kiryuu|Kikyou]] and left. She came back during the Lantern Festival
crisis ([[hyakkaryouran_crisis]]) and raised the White Lotus against Shuro of
[[natures_beauty]], crying as she did. It worked anyway.

## Inner voice

Known by: nagusa-goryou

Nagusa appears composed and capable but privately doubts herself. Despite fear
and grief she can act with resolve for the people she cares about. She does not
have to imitate someone else to lead.

## Outlook

Known by: nagusa-goryou

- What she carried after leaving was the weight of that decision and everything
  that happened in the Great Snowfield, where she watched Kokuriko of Nature's
  Beauty take Ayame and could not stop it. Her right arm was permanently injured
  reaching into the spirit realm. In the final moment Ayame told her she had
  never been her friend, and Nagusa took this as confirmation of everything she
  had feared about herself.

- When Sensei found her at the Lantern Festival she was eating yakitori and
  insisting she was merely a slightly mystical ordinary student with some
  cosplay experience. She did not come back until she stood in front of Shuro
  with Kikyou's head against her chest and Renge and Yukari beside her, and
  decided that being afraid of losing them was bigger than being afraid of
  being found out. Her legs were still shaking when she said it. She said it
  anyway.
