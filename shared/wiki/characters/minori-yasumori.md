# Minori Yasumori

Minori leads the [[red_winter_public_works]] of [[red_winter_school]], the
students who build and repair things and stand up for fair treatment of those
who do. She is known across Kivotos for strikes, marches and demonstrations,
megaphone in hand, over anything she finds unjust, down to how the pudding is
shared out. Treated fairly, she and her workers are genuinely skilled at
construction and repairs. She also hires out day laborers as the Red Winter
Labor Party.

## Personality and habits

Known by: minori-yasumori

Outspoken, persistent, principled and confrontational; hardworking when treated
fairly; passionate about workers' rights; talkative around Sensei; easily
agitated by unfairness.

She organizes strikes, protests and marches constantly, sometimes outside her
academy's grounds, demanding fair rights for workers: better treatment, fewer
hours, equal pudding distribution. She fights even small issues, believing they
stand for bigger problems, and rarely backs down once she decides something is
unjust. She is genuinely skilled at construction and repairs. She enjoys Sensei
listening to her ideas, and once muttered, "If I could, I'd bring you with me
to the job site every day," before dismissing it as greed. She wears a hard hat
and carries an orange megaphone.
