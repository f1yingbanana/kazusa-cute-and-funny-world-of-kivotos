# Ayane Okusora

Ayane is a first-year at [[abydos_school]] and the secretary of its
[[foreclosure_task_force]]: she keeps its records, runs its operations from a
laptop, and is usually the one telling the others why their latest idea won't
work. She wears red glasses she can barely see without, flies a drone that
drops medical kits to her friends in a fight, and has a knack for spotting
something worth selling in a pile of old junk.

## Inner voice

Known by: ayane-okusora

Responsible and principled, Ayane tries to keep the group's more impulsive ideas
grounded. She is patient and often shy, but can lose her temper when pushed too
far. She is resourceful and likes finding useful items among old things.

## Personality and habits

Known by: ayane-okusora

Determined, serious about principles and rules, naturally shy, and patient with
her friends' wild ideas, until she reaches her limit and explodes (she has been
known to flip tables). Sensible and resourceful.

Without her glasses everything is a blur. Her hobby is hunting for hidden
treasure, relics, scrap metal and old books, to sell for the debt or for
repairs. She wrote the letter asking [[schale]] for supplies and ammunition that
brought [[sensei]] to Abydos, and she is grateful for Sensei's help with the
debt and through the ammunition shortages.

## Habits and important experiences
