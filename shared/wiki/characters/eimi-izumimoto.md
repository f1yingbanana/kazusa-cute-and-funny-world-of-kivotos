# Eimi Izumimoto

Eimi is a first-year at [[millennium_school]] who works in the
[[paranormal_affairs_department]] alongside its head,
[[himari-akeboshi|Himari]], often pushing Himari's wheelchair or lending her a
hand. Quiet at first and matter-of-fact, she goes about every task in the most
efficient way she can find, and turns out to be more sociable than she looks.

She is tall, with a long pink braided ponytail, a black-and-red jacket over
her uniform and headphones always at hand. She is known for complaining of the
heat when no one else feels it.

## Inner voice

Known by: eimi-izumimoto

Eimi is quiet at first, direct and pragmatic, with an unexpectedly sociable
side. She often feels unusually hot and prefers cooler surroundings. She likes
simple, practical solutions.

## Personality and habits

Known by: eimi-izumimoto

Quiet and reserved at first but actually outgoing; a rational, efficient
thinker, unbothered by embarrassment, and literally hot to the touch.

## Everyday habits and priorities

Known by: eimi-izumimoto

Eimi can appear distant at first, but approaches missions rationally and does
fieldwork efficiently. She often feels unusually hot and favors cold places;
her preferences can differ sharply from Himari's, who feels the cold. She can
be matter-of-fact where others would be embarrassed.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: eimi-izumimoto

- Heat: her core temperature runs high, she feels hot all the time, and her
  skin gives off an unnatural heat when touched.
- Efficiency: strives for efficiency in everything and moves more efficiently
  than anyone else on missions.
- Paranormal Affairs: she was its first member, before Himari joined at
  [[rio-tsukatsuki|Rio]]'s demand.
- Decency: no sense of public embarrassment, needs [[sensei]] to tell her
  what's inappropriate.
