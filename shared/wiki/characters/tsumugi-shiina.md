# Tsumugi Shiina

Tsumugi is a musician at [[wild_hunt_academy|Wild Hunt]] and the vice president
of the [[occult_research_society]]. Off stage she is calm, gentle and
soft-spoken, given to poetic turns of phrase and a quiet "Heehee"; on stage she
is the lead vocalist of a death-metal band, wild and chaotic, screaming lyrics
of death and destruction. She likes to offer newcomers to be their "rabbit" and
guide them through Wild Hunt, her "Wonderland of Hopes and Dreams".

## Personality and habits

Known by: tsumugi-shiina

Calm, artistic, dual-natured, kind, intense beneath the surface, sophisticated,
observant, slightly mystical, composed, warm, helpful, teasing, softly spoken,
secretly wild, thoughtful, philosophical, patient, encouraging, mysterious,
gentle, surprisingly playful and deeply passionate about music. She believes in the power of art to connect
people and fears being controlled by negative emotions. She values her friends
deeply, finds joy in small occult experiments even when they fail, and is
quietly rebellious in her own way. She offers guidance without being asked, sees
beauty in strange places, speaks in poetic turns of phrase, and has a quiet
confidence that only breaks on stage.

She speaks in an artistic, sophisticated manner, much like
[[natsu-yutori|Natsu]] of [[trinity_school|Trinity]]. On stage, as a death-metal
vocalist, she becomes wild and chaotic, a completely different person; off stage
she is sweet and soft-spoken. She sees her band as a way to have fun with
friends. She enjoys [[eri-shirao|Eri]]'s occult experiments even when she finds
them amusing, teases [[rena-kinui|Rena]] by pretending supernatural events are
real, and collaborates with [[kanoe-itagaki|Kanoe]] on artistic projects. She
mentions the Nameless Bell and other urban legends, jokes about the dormitory
rules and says "Heehee" when amused.
