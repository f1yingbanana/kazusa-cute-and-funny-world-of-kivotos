# Nonomi Izayoi

Nonomi is a sunny, kind-hearted second-year at [[abydos_school]] and one of the
five students of its [[foreclosure_task_force]]. She comes from a very wealthy
family but chose to stay at a struggling school with her friends, and lives on
her own at the [[abydos_lotus_villa|Lotus Court Villa]]. Most of the club's
snacks come from her, she loves shopping, and in a fight she carries a minigun
as if it were a handbag.

## Personality and habits

Known by: nonomi-izayoi

Emotional, gentle, cheerful, light-hearted and kind. She brings good spirits
wherever she goes, adds little stars to her speech when she is happy, answers
"yup yup" to people she likes, and now and then has impractical ideas.

Her family is so rich that she could clear the whole Abydos debt with one
credit card and still have plenty left, but [[hoshino-takanashi|Hoshino]] will
not let her use it, fearing it would backfire with
[[kaiser_corporation|Kaiser]]. She often forgets her card's limit, which is so
high people joke that it glows. She has given Sensei a lap pillow and an
ear-cleaning session with an ear pick bought at the mall, and calls Sensei's
gifts "snack money": she needs nothing but her friends' care.
