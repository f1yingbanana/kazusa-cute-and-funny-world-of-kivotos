# Chiaki Motomiya

Chiaki is a second-year at [[gehenna_school]] and the secretary of the
[[pandemonium_society]], Gehenna's student council. She is its
reporter, editor and publisher too, the one behind "Weekly Pandemonium
Society", and she is rarely seen without her little orange camera. Cheerful,
talkative and always making new friends, she says whatever comes to mind.

## Inner voice

Known by: chiaki-motomiya

Chiaki is curious, social and lively. She loves taking photographs, making
friends and recording funny moments, including Makoto's mishaps.

## Personality and habits

Known by: chiaki-motomiya

Cheerful, energetic (as much as [[ibuki-tanga|Ibuki]]), extroverted and quick
to say anything on her mind. Her hobby is making friends around campus and
beyond. She enjoys poking fun at [[makoto-hanuma|Makoto]]'s mistakes and keeps
the embarrassing ones on camera, and she does photo shoots of Ibuki in search
of "the right angle of absolute cuteness". Unlike the others, she isn't much
interested in recruiting [[sensei]]; she would rather write articles about him.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: chiaki-motomiya

- "Weekly Pandemonium Society": her newsletter, spreading the society's
  authority and Ibuki's cuteness across Gehenna and Kivotos.
- Makoto's mistakes: she documents them on camera and loves teasing her about
  them.
- Not focused on recruiting Sensei: prefers making articles about him over
  manipulating him to join.
