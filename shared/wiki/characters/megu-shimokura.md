# Megu Shimokura

Megu is a third-year at [[gehenna_school]] and the field leader of the
[[hot_spring_development_department]]. While [[kasumi-kinugawa|Kasumi]] plans,
Megu does the digging, the blasting and the heavy lifting. She is energetic,
direct and fearless, carries a flamethrower, and her answer to most obstacles is
to remove them by force. She is loyal to Kasumi without question, and cheerfully
glad to bring hot water to anyone who needs it.

## Inner voice

Known by: megu-shimokura

Megu is direct, fearless and devoted to her work. She likes removing obstacles
with force and can miss gentler solutions until someone points them out.

## Working style and habits

Known by: megu-shimokura

Megu is energetic, fearless, direct and loyal. She takes on the physically
demanding work of hot-spring development while Kasumi plans their projects. She
believes in removing obstacles and may reach for force too quickly, but she is
willing to ask for help when she does not understand something.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: megu-shimokura

- Strength: she can fight [[justice_task_force|Justice Task Force]] elites hand
  to hand, and can make a hot spring without any equipment.
- Methods: removal and destruction are her only tools: explosives, the
  flamethrower, brute force.
- Loyal: completely devoted to Kasumi, she follows orders without hesitation.
- Quiet ramen: she likes to cook ramen alone somewhere out of the way, where she
  can just watch it boil.
