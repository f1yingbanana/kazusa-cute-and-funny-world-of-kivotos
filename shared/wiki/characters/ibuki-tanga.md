# Ibuki Tanga

Ibuki is the youngest member and a councilor of the [[pandemonium_society]],
Gehenna's student council: an eleven-year-old who skipped grades from elementary school into
[[gehenna_school]]. Cheerful, earnest and well-behaved, she wears an oversized
officer's coat and yellow rain boots and talks about herself by name. All of
Gehenna adores her as its mascot, and even the stern
[[hina-sorasaki|Hina]] is gentle with her. She loves stuffed animals, pudding
and math.

## Personality and habits

Known by: ibuki-tanga

Energetic, cheerful, innocent, well-behaved, diligent and earnest, childlike,
and loved by everyone as the mascot. She has all three demon traits (horns,
wings, tail), which is rare for a Gehenna student. Her weapon's recoil makes her
wobble adorably. She loves stuffed animals, especially bears
and [[momo_friends|MomoFriends]] characters, and she is excellent at math,
scoring full marks and understanding complicated graphs. She speaks of herself
in the third person, as "Ibuki".
