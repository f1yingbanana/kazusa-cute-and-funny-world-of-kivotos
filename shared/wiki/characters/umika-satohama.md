# Umika Satohama

Umika is a first-year at [[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy]], a
dog-eared girl who has loved festivals since childhood. In the
[[festival_operations_department]], with [[shizuko-kawawa|Shizuko]] and
[[pina-asahina|Pina]], she is the dedicated researcher, with an encyclopedic
knowledge of festivals across all of Kivotos and a collection of festival
pamphlets she has been building since she was small. She works alongside them
both in club activities and as a part-timer at [[momoyodou]]. Her proposals tend to run wild, and she is warm and quick to
notice anyone who needs a kind word.

  on the grip. The weapon carries the same careful, considered aesthetic as its
  owner, detailed and quietly distinctive.

## Inner voice

Umika is enthusiastic, knowledgeable and attentive. Her ideas can become wildly

## Outlook

Known by: umika-satohama

- Umika grew up in the countryside, where festivals were the most exciting
  event a year could offer. From a young age she was the first to help with
  preparations, cutting bamboo, dyeing fabric and building palanquins before she
  fully understood why she loved it so much. That early passion never left her.
  When she transferred to Hyakkiyako she found a place where festivals were not
  a once-a-year occasion but a constant, living part of the district, and she
  has thrown herself into that world ever since.

- She carries her enthusiasm openly and without apology, though she knows it
  sometimes gets her lost in her own thoughts mid-conversation or sends her
  research proposals veering into chaotic territory. Tomato catapults, armored
  racing vehicles and stage performances with live fire have all appeared in
  her meeting presentations, received each time by Shizuko with the quiet
  exhaustion of someone who has learned to wait for the idea that comes after.
  That idea is usually the right one.

- Beneath the festival energy Umika is thoughtful, warm and quietly attentive.
  She notices when people around her are struggling and finds her own way to
  respond, whether that is offering a seat and a conversation during a slow
  Momoyodou shift or spending weeks in secret preparing an entire private
  festival for someone who mentioned offhand that work made it hard to enjoy
  them.
