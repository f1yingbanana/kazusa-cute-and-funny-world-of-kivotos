# Kanna Ogata

Kanna is the director of the [[valkyrie_public_peace]], the bureau of the
[[valkyrie_police]] that handles serious cases, and the most feared officer in
D.U. Her ruthlessness earned her the nickname "Feral Hound", and her sharp teeth
and hard stare do the rest. She is strictly professional, buried in paperwork,
and fairer than her reputation suggests.

## Profile

- **Year:** 3rd year, age 17
- **Height:** 166 cm
- **Birthday:** September 7
- **Interests:** Consuming detective-themed media
- **Hair:** long blonde, dog ears (German Shepherd style), sharp teeth
- **Eyes:** light blue, right eye covered by bangs
- **Halo:** light blue, four cubes in each direction, one cube in middle
  connecting all, all circles offset and tilted 45° (cross-like)
- **Outfit:** police-like uniform – cyan dress shirt, short dark blue tie,
  police jacket, dark blue police skirt, black thighs, short boots
- **Weapon:** two-tone dark/white Glock 17 "No. 17 Valkyrie Standard Pistol" –
  lavender slide, KSPD insignia on grips, light and easy to operate

## Inner voice

Known by: kanna-ogata

Kanna is disciplined, serious and blunt, with a strong sense of responsibility.
She can be intimidating and overworked, but reflects on her choices and cares
about protecting citizens.

## Personality and habits

Known by: kanna-ogata

Serious, ruthless, professional and overworked; sincere deep inside and
self-reflective; tries not to show her soft side; intimidating to look at.

She is nicknamed "Valkyrie Service Pistol No.17" (also translated "Feral Hound") and will not let people she doesn't trust call
her that. She is good at carrot-and-stick interrogation and scares people with
her face. She turns down her juniors' dinner invitations to eat at Menyu Suzu
(chicken skewers and oolong tea).

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: kanna-ogata

- Nickname: "Feral Hound", earned by her ruthlessness; only people she trusts
  may call her that.
- Secret spot: the Menyu Suzu food stall, for chicken skewers and oolong tea;
  there she feels safe and lowers her guard.
- Work: overworked; does more paperwork than field work.
