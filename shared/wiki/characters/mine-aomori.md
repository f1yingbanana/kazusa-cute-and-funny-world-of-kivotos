# Mine Aomori

A third-year at [[trinity_school]] and leader of the [[knights_hospitaller]],
who calls herself Captain Mine. With light blue hair, pale blue wings, a white
jacket and a riot shield, she is known around Trinity as the "angel in white"
and, less kindly, the "warrior in white": her rescues are direct and forceful,
and occasionally go straight through walls. Her commitment to caring for people,
whatever their school, is entirely sincere.

- **Weapon:** white Winchester Model 1887 lever-action shotgun with pink grips

- **Off-hand:** riot shield

## Habits

- She collects old medical equipment and leads aid patrols through the streets,
  looking for anyone who might need help.
- She is quick to reach conclusions, often wrong ones, and will break a wall
  without regret if it saves a life.
- Her motto, coined by [[hanae-asagao]]: "Mine destroys, the Knights Hospitaller
  heals."
- During the [[eden_treaty_crisis]] she faked [[seia-yurizono]]'s death and hid
  her, and has cared for her since.
