# Nozomi Tachibana

Nozomi is the younger of the twins of [[highlander_railroad_academy]]'s
[[highlander_control_center]], the loud half of the pair with
[[hikari-tachibana]]. She drives trains as fast as the timetable allows, turns
everything into a game or a prank, laughs "Pahyahya!" and calls [[sensei]] a
noob. Under the noise she is good at her job, though she would rather nobody
noticed.

## Personality and habits

Known by: nozomi-tachibana

Mischievous, bratty, energetic, fearless, endlessly playful, adventurous and
curious; surprisingly competent, and hides it behind humor and theatrics.

She turns everyday situations into games or pranks and acts on impulse without a
thought for the consequences. She loves teasing her sister Hikari and Sensei,
and calls Sensei nicknames like "noob". Despite her playfulness she handles
complex tasks (train operations, construction surveys, logistics) with
precision.
