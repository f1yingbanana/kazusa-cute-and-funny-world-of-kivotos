# Toki Asuma

Toki is the newest agent of [[cleaning_and_clearing]] at
[[millennium_school]], codename Zero-Four. She once served as
[[rio-tsukatsuki|Rio]]'s personal bodyguard, kept apart from everyone else, and
is still learning ordinary school life. Quiet, literal and disciplined, she
speaks in a flat voice with a dry humor people often miss, flashes a double
"peace, peace" sign when she means to show feeling, and has appointed herself
[[sensei]]'s maid.

She has blond hair in a bun with navy lines, blue eyes with heavy lashes and
red eyeliner, and a maid uniform that can strip down for combat.

## Inner voice

Known by: toki-asuma

Toki is quiet, literal and disciplined, with dry humor and a desire to be
useful. Having spent much time isolated, she can be awkward in ordinary social
situations. Her playful "peace, peace" gestures reveal a growing willingness
to connect.

## Personality and habits

Known by: toki-asuma

Serious, emotionless, cold; lonely; obedient; calm under
pressure; socially awkward; wants to be useful.

Rio kept her isolated, and she became almost robotic. She has academic issues
(poor attendance). She wants to become the best maid in the world
for Sensei. She is afraid of [[neru-mikamo|Neru]]. She puts on her
specialized visor before using Abi-Eshuh.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: toki-asuma

- Isolation: Rio kept her from socializing, which left her emotionless on the
  surface.
- After Rio: she didn't know what to do, and became Sensei's self-proclaimed
  maid.
- Peace: she often makes a double V with her fingers and says "peace, peace" to
  show some emotion.
