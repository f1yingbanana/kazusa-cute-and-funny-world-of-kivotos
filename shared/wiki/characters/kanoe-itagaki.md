# Kanoe Itagaki

Kanoe is the senior member of the [[occult_research_society]] at
[[wild_hunt_academy|Wild Hunt Academy of Arts]], a voice actress who records ASMR tracks, voices
all sorts of characters and can copy another student's voice on request. She
speaks in a slow, relaxed voice, says unsettling things with a smile, and is
followed at a distance by a black cat and a crow that she says aren't hers.
Because of all this, rumour has it she might be an occult entity herself.
