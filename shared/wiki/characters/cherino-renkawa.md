# Cherino Renkawa

Cherino Renkawa is the president of [[red_winter_school]] and head of its
student council, the [[red_winter_office|Red Winter Office]]. She is tiny,
theatrical and spoiled, demands absolute obedience, and enforces it with a
single word: "Purge!" She wears a fake mustache as the symbol of her power and
loves [[pudding_in_kivotos|pudding]] above almost everything. Coups topple her
nearly every week, yet she always ends up back in charge.

## Profile

- **Year:** third year
- **Height:** 128 cm
- **Birthday:** October 27
- **Interests:** doling out demerits, purges, snowball fights
- **Hair:** long white reaching waist, pigtails tied with black ribbons
- **Hat:** white Red Winter Soldier hat with iconic badge on front
- **Mustache:** fake moustache (worn always)
- **Outfit:** white military-style coat with fur trim, red linings, golden
  buttons, brooch with red ribbon, black suit underneath, white shorts, white
  gloves, black stockings, white winter boots, black sling bag, red randoseru
  bag with moustache keychain
- **Halo:** sky blue cogwheel shape with smaller circle in center and two
  polygonal pegs on sides
- **Weapon:** black Nagant M1895 revolver named "EChistka" with red linings on
  barrel, cylinder, and handle (doesn't need to fire; shouting "Purge!" is
  enough in Red Winter)

## Inner voice

## Personality and habits

Known by: cherino-renkawa

Commanding, spoiled, confident, meticulous, playful and childish. She is
obsessed with control, expects absolute obedience, holds grudges, forgets whom
she has suspended, and hides her feelings behind her authority.

She wears a fake mustache, believing it is the symbol of power. She is
overthrown almost every week and somehow keeps her position. She loves pudding
and puts it before almost everything, and hates bitter things like coffee and
bell peppers. She calls Sensei "Comrade" instead of Sensei. "Purge!" is her
command, and she doesn't even need to fire. She forgot that she suspended
[[nodoka-amami|Nodoka]], which infuriates Nodoka. She organizes snowball fights
to reinforce her dominance. She is small, and her authority is absolute anyway.
