# Neru Mikamo

Neru leads [[cleaning_and_clearing]], [[millennium_school]]'s maid unit, under
the codename "Double O", a symbol of victory. She claims a perfect mission
record and is counted among the two strongest fighters at Millennium. Blunt,
hot-tempered and always ready for a fight, she charges into any challenge,
cares little for collateral damage, and respects people who stand their
ground. Never mention her height.

She is small, with short vermilion hair and sharp red eyes, and wears a black
sukajan jacket embroidered with red cherry blossoms over a short maid uniform,
a chained pair of submachine guns at hand.

## Profile

- **Year:** 3rd year, age 17
- **Height:** 146 cm, the shortest in C&C, 22 cm shorter than Akane
- **Birthday:** August 17th
- **Interests:** Winning
- **Looks:** Neru's short vermillion hair has a long crooked cowlick. She ties
  part of it into a back knot, with a braid secured by two Millennium tags on
  the left. A French maid headdress sits on her head. Her sharp red eyes match
  her fierce personality. Her halo is a black circle with dashed edges inside a

  sukajan jacket decorated with red sakura blossoms, over a short French maid
  uniform with a loose blue ribbon. Red and black floppy boots and black nail
  polish finish the outfit.
- **Weapon:** a pair of black SIG Sauer MPX submachine guns linked by a chain,
  named "Twin Dragons". The guns have a dandelion grip and stock, and a dragon
  decal on the receiver.
