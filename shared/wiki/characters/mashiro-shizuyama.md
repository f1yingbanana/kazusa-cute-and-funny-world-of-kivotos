quiet and sincere and rarely makes small talk, until the subject turns to
justice, which she pursues with total seriousness.

## Personality and habits

Known by: mashiro-shizuyama

Sincere, quiet but not shy, zealous about justice, determined and professional;
she acts first when justice is at risk.

She carries a massive anti-materiel rifle bigger than her body. She rarely
chats, but becomes talkative when justice is the topic. She joined the Justice
Task Force to spread justice and discipline, and is still learning about
justice. She doesn't share Hasumi's pure hatred of Gehenna: she dislikes them,
but cares only whether an individual threatens justice. She asks Sensei for help
with bugs during sniper duty, debates justice with Sensei on the rooftop, and
enjoys talking about justice with them.
