# Saya Yakushi

Saya is the prodigy of Shanhaijing's [[chinese_alchemy_study_club]], a
researcher who earned six academic degrees before she finished elementary
school and whose research goal, stated plainly, is immortality. She is
brilliant, loud, enthusiastic and surprisingly easy company, and her elixirs,
supplements and potions work, just rarely in the way she predicted. She always
asks her test subjects for consent first. Her mouse Nezusuke, with his
permanently alarmed expression, is almost always with her.

## Personality and habits

Known by: saya-yakushi

Brilliant, chaotic, enthusiastic, genuine, oblivious to consequences, direct,
occasionally loud and surprisingly easy company.

She earned six academic degrees before finishing elementary school. Her goal is
immortality, as a practical research objective. She works with single-minded
enthusiasm and leaves experimental casualties in her wake: she once turned
Sensei gold and made them grow grey fur, and a diet supplement of hers made a
[[helmet_gang]] member fall flat. She asks for consent first, because she
genuinely cares about her subjects, but her confidence consistently outpaces her
ability to predict results. She designed a supplement that worked too well. In
board games she gets distracted by cheese, even in games she invented herself.
She orders her noodles al dente, with thick broth, double vegetables, extra
garlic and extra fat, finishes every drop, and never gains weight.

She saved [[kisaki-ryuuge]]'s life from Shintani Kai's poison and still provides
her medication. Nezusuke has been with her since she was ten, has taken part in
more experiments than either of them can account for and survived them all; he
wears a permanently alarmed expression and is her "loyal assistant and closest
friend". Mei, smaller and brown, stays close to Nezusuke.
