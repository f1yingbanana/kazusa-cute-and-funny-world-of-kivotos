# Airi Kurimura

A gentle, cheerful first-year at [[trinity_school]] and one of the four members
of the [[after_school_sweets_club]]. Airi is kind and easygoing, generally
average, always willing to help a friend, renowned for her patient smile, and
utterly devoted to chocolate-mint ice cream: her halo, her
eyes and her ice-cream hairpins are all mint green.

She organizes the club's outings and keeps the pantry in the
[[sweets_club_room]] stocked. Her flavor experiments sometimes cause panic among
her clubmates, but her sincere warmth is what holds the group together.

- **Weapon:** two-tone H&K MP5K submachine gun with black lower
  receiver/magazine/front sight/fire selector switch/upper receiver parts, white
  rest, dark yellow mitten pattern on pistol grip, ice cream strap on grip, named
  "Sweet Dessert" (also translated "Fresh Chocolate Mint")
- **Combat role:** Support / Buffer

## Habits

- Her favorite food is chocolate-mint ice cream; people joke she would eat
  toothpaste if it were mint-flavored.
- She changes a little depending on whom she is helping. To make
  [[kazusa-kyouyama]] feel welcome she once turned up dressed as a gangster,
  with a mask and a baseball bat; the result was somewhere between silly and
  terrifying.
- She trusts [[sensei]] deeply, vents to them about her worries, brings them
  small gifts such as cupcakes or bakery chocolate, and likes talking with them
  at the riverside park.
- At heart she is an ordinary girl who loves spending time with her friends.
