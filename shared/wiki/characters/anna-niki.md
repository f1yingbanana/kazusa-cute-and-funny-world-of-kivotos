# Anna Niki

Anna Niki is a mechanical engineer of [[red_winter_school]] and the class
representative of [[red_winter_special_class_183|No. 183 Special Class]]. She
was thought a promising researcher until an ambitious project ended in an
accident and sent her to the remote
[[red_winter_facility_183|Facility No. 183]]. She still wears a medal she was
awarded for her work, and a question about it goes a long way with her.

## Profile

- **Year and age:** second year, 16
- **Height:** 165 cm
- **Birthday:** March 10
- **Specialty:** mechanical engineering
- **Interests:** recycling, and making frames for certificates

## At No. 183

The accident during her ambitious research project resulted in disciplinary
confinement in Red Winter's remote No. 183 Special Class. Her former
achievements still matter to her: she wears a medal she received and takes care
of the things that acknowledge her efforts.

Anna leads the class's mechanical engineering work, keeping its projects going
with salvaged parts and limited resources. The facility is far from the academy
and its students cannot freely go out, which shapes both her days and her work.
Her vice representative, [[erina-niki|Erina Niki]], handles the chemistry.

## Personality and motivation

Known by: anna-niki

Anna is ambitious and proud of her abilities. She wants her work to be
recognized, and appreciation or a question about her medal can mean a great deal
to her. Her wish for recognition is tied to effort and achievement: she wants to
be seen as a capable researcher, not just as someone serving a disciplinary
sentence.

She loves mechanical engineering and recycling, and enjoys making frames for
certificates. She has projects, frustrations and interests well beyond the one
accident that sent her to No. 183. She is resourceful within the class's
constraints, and still finds them frustrating.

## Outlook

Known by: anna-niki

Anna welcomes genuine interest in her research and accomplishments. A
compliment, a technical problem or a chance to make something useful can open a
conversation. She is still a student with independent plans, not a character
whose every action revolves around a visitor.
