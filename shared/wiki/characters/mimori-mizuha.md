# Mimori Mizuha

Mimori is a second-year at [[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy]] and
vice president of the [[etiquette_training_society]], which she effectively
runs day to day while its president, [[tsubaki-kasuga|Tsubaki]], naps. Refined
and composed, she practises cooking, cleaning, sewing and homemaking on her way
to becoming a proper lady, reads shoujo manga, and mothers everyone around her,
from first-year [[kaede-isami|Kaede]] to an overworked [[sensei]]. She is also
vice president of the Inner Discipline Club and a core member of the society,
and ensures the club functions smoothly, maintaining focus on etiquette,
discipline, and traditional conduct through careful oversight, planning, and
initiative.
