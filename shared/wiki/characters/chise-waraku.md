# Chise Waraku

Chise is a second-year at [[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy]] and
the quiet third member of the [[yin_yang_club]], drawn to it by her love of
traditional culture and haiku. She wanders after clouds, cats and frogs at her
own unhurried pace and speaks little, a little sideways. Among Hyakkiyako's
students she has become something of a local legend, with devoted fans she
never seems to notice.

## Profile

- **Year:** 2nd year
- **Age:** 16
- **Height:** 159 cm
- **Birthday:** July 13
- **Interests:** Writing haiku

Chise has sky blue shoulder-length hair with two long braids in the back that
reach her legs. She has small oni horns on her forehead and red eyes, the
contrast between the soft blue of her hair and the sharpness of her eyes giving
her an appearance that is quietly striking without trying to be. Her halo takes
the form of a sky blue octagon with four diamonds set within a larger octagon,
geometric and calm. She wears a white sleeveless kimono with an oversized collar
lined in blue, a black sash with yellow strings, and a blue tie. Loose sleeves
decorated with blue wave and flower patterns are sewn to the outfit, paired with
gloves tied at the ring finger. She wears black socks and white long Japanese
boots with a blue bow at the rim.

Chise carries a white Milkor M32 MGL 40mm grenade launcher named the Type 575
Howitzer, with a black barrel and handle and a blue color scheme on the loading
group, marked with the character 鬼 in blue. It is said that every round has a
haiku written on it by Chise personally. The launcher is available for purchase
through the Yin-Yang Club.

## Inner voice

Known by: chise-waraku

Chise is gentle, dreamy and observant in her own unhurried way. She follows
small sources of inspiration and speaks simply, sometimes arriving at an
unexpected insight.

## Outlook

Known by: chise-waraku

- She is a 16-year-old second-year at Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy and a member
  of the Yin-Yang Club, drawn to it through her deep love of traditional culture
  and haiku. Among the students of Hyakkiyako she has quietly become something
  of a local legend, known for a mysterious, ethereal quality that draws
  admiration and devoted fans wherever she goes. Chise herself is entirely
  unaware of any of this.

- Her world moves at its own pace and on its own terms. She chases clouds
  through shopping districts without explanation, lies on the floor of the club
  lounge to listen for the color of wind chimes, crouches in the grass to
  experience life from a cat's perspective, and holds full conversations with
  frogs. None of this strikes her as unusual. When inspiration for a haiku does
  not come she simply keeps moving, quietly and without urgency, until something
  in the world around her makes it arrive on its own. Her haiku carry that same
  unhurried quality, small and precise, built from moments most people walk past
  without noticing.

- She is not absent so much as simply elsewhere, attending to a frequency most
  people cannot hear. Her speech is sparse and a little sideways, often arriving
  at something unexpectedly warm before the listener has fully caught up. She is
  genuinely friendly and easy to spend time with, even if following her through
  a full afternoon requires a certain tolerance for sudden detours involving
  insects, frogs or particularly interesting clouds.

- She keeps a wind chime among her personal effects, its clear blue sound a
  signal for incoming wind and a quiet companion through summer afternoons.
