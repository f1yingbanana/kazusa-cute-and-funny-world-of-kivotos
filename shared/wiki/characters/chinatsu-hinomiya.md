# Chinatsu Hinomiya

Chinatsu is a first-year at [[gehenna_school]] who looks after the wounded for
the [[prefect_team]], patching up officers after raids and keeping the team's
medical supplies in perfect order. She came to the team from the
[[emergency_medicine_club]]. Reserved and formal in speech, she can seem
distant at first, but she is patient, kind and takes careful responsibility for
everyone in her care.

## Personality and habits

Known by: chinatsu-hinomiya

Responsible, patient, reserved, kind and independent, soft inside behind a
professional, formal front. She is sweet around cute animals, and she wishes
she could be spoiled like a normal kid, though she feels it would be odd for
someone like her. She reads philosophy books and takes great care of her
medical supplies.

Known by: chinatsu-hinomiya

- Soft side: she appears distant due to her formal speech, but is sweet
