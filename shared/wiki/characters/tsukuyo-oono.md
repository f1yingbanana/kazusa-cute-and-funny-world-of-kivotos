# Tsukuyo Oono

Tsukuyo is a first-year at [[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy]]
and, at 180 cm, impossible to miss, which is the last thing she wants. She is
timid, soft-spoken and apologetic, and when nervous she hides, often by
disguising herself as a tree. In the [[ninjutsu_research_department]], with
[[michiru-chidori|Michiru]] and [[izuna-kuda|Izuna]], she is slowly learning
ninjutsu and slowly opening up. The department is a small club dedicated to
studying and promoting ninjutsu throughout Kivotos. Although she is still
inexperienced with ninja techniques, Tsukuyo works hard alongside her clubmates
to learn more about the art and slowly grow into a capable ninja; she wants to become a real ninja so she can
help the people around her.

- **Interests:** Disguising herself as a tree, hiding

- Tsukuyo has waist-length dark purple hair with a yellow inner layer, styled

- Her halo glows a pale pink and resembles an upside-down sakura flower. The
  center of the halo is formed by five small oval shapes, while each petal
  contains a diamond shape inside it. Between each petal are five curved diamond
  decorations.
- Tsukuyo wears a short white dress shirt that exposes her waist, paired with a
  light purple cardigan. Around her neck she wears a long magenta scarf
  decorated with flower patterns that drapes down to her thighs. Her lower
  outfit consists of a black pleated skirt that extends below her calves with an
  opening along the left side that exposes her entire leg. Her left thigh is
  wrapped in bandages and she also wears a black cloth around her hip.
- Tsukuyo carries a white Orita M1941 submachine gun. The weapon features
  lavender receiver furniture and muzzle components with a black stock, while
  gold floral pattern decals decorate the handguard and stock.

## Outlook

Known by: tsukuyo-oono

- At first glance, Tsukuyo seems extremely timid and quiet. Her unusual height
  has made her stand out since childhood, and because she was often mocked for
  it when she was younger, she grew deeply insecure about it. She became shy,
  withdrawn and hesitant to approach others. She often worries that her presence
  inconveniences the people around her, sometimes even believing that simply
  standing next to someone could block their view or cause them trouble.

- Despite these insecurities, Tsukuyo truly wants to improve herself. Since
  joining the Ninjutsu Research Department she has slowly opened up, thanks to
  the encouragement and companionship of her clubmates. She still struggles with
  confidence, but she tries her best to stay positive in club activities and
  works steadily toward her dream of becoming a real ninja.

- Her motivations are simple but sincere. She wishes to become a full-fledged
  ninja not for fame or recognition but to support and protect the people
  around her. She believes that if she improves enough, she will be able to
  properly help her friends in the club and fulfill requests like a true ninja.

- Shy as she is, once Tsukuyo sets her mind on something she can show
  surprising determination. Whether it is practicing camouflage, studying ninja
  books or tracking down a lost pet, she throws herself fully into it. Her
  earnestness often leads to clumsy or embarrassing situations, but she rarely
  gives up once she commits to improving herself.
