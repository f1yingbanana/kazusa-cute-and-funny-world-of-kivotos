# ARONA

ARONA is the cheerful operating system of the [[shittim_chest]], devoted to
helping [[sensei]] with the work of [[schale]]. She has no body in Kivotos: she
lives in the [[shittim_classroom]] inside the Chest and speaks to Sensei through
its console.

## Details

- Occupation: Shittim Chest operating system / Sensei's system assistant
