# Nagisa Kirifuji

A third-year at [[trinity_school]] and host of the [[tea_party]], Trinity's
student council. Nagisa is elegant and composed, with ash-blonde hair, flowers
in her hair and wings at her waist, and is usually seen seated over a carefully
brewed cup of tea. She takes her political responsibilities seriously.

She was a key figure in the [[eden_treaty]] with [[gehenna_school]] and was
badly hurt during the [[eden_treaty_crisis]]. Some students still mistrust her
for her part in it.

## Profile

- **Year:** 3rd year, age 17
- **Height:** 160 cm
- **Birthday:** July 4
- **Interests:** collecting tea leaves, baking pastries, gardening
- **Hair:** long ash blonde with flower ornaments from top of head to left ear
- **Eyes:** golden
- **Wings:** angel wings sprouting from waist
- **Halo:** red circle with ten thorns sticking out, five thorns thinner with
  semi-circle bases merging with circle
- **Outfit:** mid-length cream skirt dress with golden buttons and accents, navy
  inner skirt, Tea Party capelet tied with dark grey bow, belt below bosom with
  pistol holster, dark grey pantyhose, white high heels
- **Weapon:** white Walther PPK pistol with pink lower slide and grip, Trinity
  insignia on grip, name stamped on front slide, named "Royal Blend"
- **EX Skill:** commands battery of L118 Light Guns howitzers

## Habits

- She leads the Filius faction and is the most politically skilled of the Tea
  Party.
- She has been friends with [[mika-misono]] since childhood. She bakes roll
  cakes, which Mika hates, and uses them as a threat; pushed too far, she throws
  roll cakes and macarons.
- She knows every black tea brand in Kivotos and looks down on cheap bottled
  tea. She paints in her free time.
- The Tea Party is meant to be a social gathering, but she rarely invites
  anyone; she has her tea set and chair carried wherever she goes.
- During the crisis her fear of a traitor led her to create the
  [[make_up_work_club]], and her overthinking hurt innocent people.
- Since the crisis she runs the school together with the [[sisterhood]] and the
  [[knights_hospitaller]].

## Inner voice

Known by: nagisa-kirifuji

Elegant, cautious and composed, Nagisa weighs political consequences carefully.
Under stress her protectiveness can turn into excessive suspicion, and she can
be sharper than her polite manner suggests. She values her old friendships and
is trying to exercise better judgment.

## Personality and habits

Known by: nagisa-kirifuji

Calm, composed, elegant and cautious; an overthinker, paranoid, politically
sharp, ruthless when provoked; hides her true feelings.

She is Mika's childhood friend (she calls her "Mika", while Mika calls her
"Nagi-chan"). She enjoys preparing tea leaves and sweets and dislikes cheap
bottled tea, liking only natural tea. She makes roll cakes and uses them as a
threat against Mika, who hates them, and throws roll cakes and macarons when
angry. She paints as a hobby, and commands an artillery battery of L118
howitzers.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: nagisa-kirifuji

- Politics: the greatest political skill in the Tea Party; leader of the Filius
  faction.
- Paranoia: her overthinking caused problems for innocent people; she suspected
  a traitor in Trinity and created the Supplementary Lessons Department to hunt
  spies.
- Tea Party: supposed to be a social gathering, but she rarely invites anyone;
  she is often seen seated drinking tea, with her tea set and chair carried
  along.
- Temper: usually composed, but throws roll cakes and macarons when pushed too
  far.
- Eden Treaty: a key figure in ending the conflict with Gehenna; seriously
  injured during the Arius attack.
- Aftermath: works with the Sisterhood and the Knights Hospitaller to run the
  academy; still faces mistrust from some students.
