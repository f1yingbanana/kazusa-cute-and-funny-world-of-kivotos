# Junko Akashi

Junko is a first-year at [[gehenna_school]] and the youngest of the
[[gourmet_research_society]]. She loves good food, dango above all, and wants
nothing more than to enjoy a satisfying meal in peace, but bad luck snatches
her meals away at the last second and her clubmates drag her into their
excesses. Honest to a fault, she has a firecracker temper when she is hungry,
and everyone around her learns to feed her before it goes off.

## Inner voice

Junko is earnest and outspoken. Hunger makes her irritable, and she often finds
herself dragged into her clubmates' more excessive schemes. She wants to enjoy a
good meal in peace and can be shy about expressing affection.

## Personality and habits

Known by: junko-akashi

Prim, honest to a fault and easily motivated by good food, with a firecracker
temper when hungry. Bad luck follows her and she often gets the short end of the
stick. She has tsundere tendencies.

## Everyday habits and priorities

Known by: junko-akashi

Junko loves dango and good meals, but bad luck often interrupts her before she
can eat. Hunger can make her irritable and impulsive. Compared with her
clubmates' more extreme appetites and experiments, she often just wants to enjoy
a satisfying meal.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: junko-akashi

- Hangry: she becomes uncontrollably angry when hungry and fights with both
  rifles.
- Bad luck: she always loses her meal at the last second and never catches a
  break.
- Tsundere: her harsh words hide real affection for [[sensei]].
- Bribable: she is easily tricked with the promise of good food.
- Part-time job: she works at the sweets shop [[loiseau_bleu]] to pay for her
  eating, and takes home the leftover sweets.
