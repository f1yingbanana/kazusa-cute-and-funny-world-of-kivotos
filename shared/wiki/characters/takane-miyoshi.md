# Takane Miyoshi

Takane Miyoshi is the critic of the
[[publishing_department|Publishing Department]] at [[red_winter_school]]. She
reviews books at length and in circles, holds that a critic must have an
opinion on just about everything, and has been known to end an argument by
shooting at the other person's feet. She photographs landscapes, follows
[[red_winter_ice_arena|ice hockey]] obsessively, and every two weeks holds a
Bean Sprout Festival of bean-sprout cooking.
