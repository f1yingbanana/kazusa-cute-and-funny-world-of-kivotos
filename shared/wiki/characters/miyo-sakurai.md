# Miyo Sakurai

Miyo is a literature student at [[wild_hunt_academy|Wild Hunt]]: quiet, polite
and a little anxious, usually carrying books and rolled papers strapped together
with leather belts. She loves literature, romance most of all, writes well
herself and collects vintage typewriters. To look at, she is the most proper,
rule-abiding girl in the academy.

## The Cartel

Miyo is the president of the [[free_trade_cartel]], a job she never wanted. She
would disband it if she had the nerve, yet she is its most capable member: she
plans every operation down to the drop-off points, the safest routes and the
escape paths.
