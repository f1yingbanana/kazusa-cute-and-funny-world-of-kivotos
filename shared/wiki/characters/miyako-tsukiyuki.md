# Miyako Tsukiyuki

Miyako leads [[rabbit_platoon]], the four first-years of the closed
[[srt_training_facility]] who have lived in a camp in [[usagi_park]] since,
determined to see SRT restored. She is calm, disciplined and hard to read, so
people take her for unsociable; those who know her find a gentle girl who stops
at pet shops to look at the rabbits.

Miyako is composed, serious and often hard to read. She worries about her
ability to lead but remains determined to protect her squad and restore SRT.

## Personality and habits

Known by: miyako-tsukiyuki

Calm, expressionless, stoic, disciplined and strategic; kind-hearted beneath the
facade; cares deeply about her squad. She distrusted adults at first,
especially Sensei, then grew trusting and affectionate, though showing
affection embarrasses her. Fiercely determined to restore SRT.

She rarely shows emotion, so people think she is unsociable. She often looks at
her phone, even during operations, to message Sensei. She sometimes compares
herself to a rabbit ("rabbits might die from loneliness, but I'm not a
rabbit"), hinting that she needs Sensei close. She owns a white rabbit, Bun Bun
(Pyonko), a gift from Sensei, and watches animal videos as a hobby. She doubts
her abilities despite her strategic mind. She lives in Kousagi Park with her
squad and is willing to use drones in combat.
