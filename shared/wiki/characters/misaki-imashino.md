# Misaki Imashino

Misaki of the [[arius_squad]] from [[arius_school]] says little, and what she
says is blunt. She claims to have no interest in anything, food included, and
on the surface she seems cold and worn out. Yet for all her complaining she
never really pulls away from people, and she stays close to the friends who
care about her.
