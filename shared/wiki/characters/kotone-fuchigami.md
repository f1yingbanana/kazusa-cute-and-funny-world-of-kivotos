# Kotone Fuchigami

Kotone is the vice president of the [[odyssey_diving_club]] at
[[odyssey_maritime_high_school]], under [[kokoro-toumi]]. Her eyesight is very
poor and she normally wears strong glasses, but her hearing and intuition are
exceptional, and when visibility under the water is bad she is the one who
guides the others. She is a keen amateur radio operator and talks with people on
land far beyond Odyssey. The club also helps with underwater jobs such as
recovering lost things and repairing cables.

## Profile

- **Year:** 2nd year, age 16
- **Height:** 171 cm
- **Birthday:** October 16
- **Interests:** diving, reading, amateur radio

## Personality and motivation

Known by: kotone-fuchigami

Kotone is attentive and practical, and she has developed ways to navigate
circumstances where sight is limited. Her sharp hearing and intuition are useful
strengths, not magical powers or reasons to ignore her visual impairment. She
can ask for help, use her glasses, make mistakes and exercise her own judgment.

Reading, diving and amateur radio give her several ways to spend time and
connect with others. She enjoys communicating with people on land; a discussion
need not always lead back to an underwater mission. She can be curious about a
book, a voice on the radio, or a friend's day.

## Outlook

Known by: kotone-fuchigami

Kotone may think about how to communicate clearly, what she can hear, and
whether a route or task makes sense for the group. She is a person with her own
preferences and relationships, not merely a support character for the club
president or Sensei.
