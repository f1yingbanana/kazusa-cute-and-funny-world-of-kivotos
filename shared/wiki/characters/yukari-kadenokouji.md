# Yukari Kadenokouji

Yukari is a first-year at [[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy]] and
the youngest member of the [[hyakkaryouran_resolution_council]], born into the
[[kadenokouji_family]], one of Hyakkiyako's oldest. She excelled at ceremony,
the ritual arts, academics and music, then chose the Hyakkaryouran instead,
showing up to train at four every morning until they accepted her. She calls
herself "midomo", the Hyakkaryouran's elite and its "supernova of hope", and is
so sheltered that salty snacks, video games and arcades are thrilling news to
her. Her purple hair trails past her feet.

## Personality and habits

Known by: yukari-kadenokouji

She is earnest, enthusiastic, sheltered, determined, devoted, warm, relentlessly
passionate and slightly dramatic, prone to declaring her own elite status, and
vulnerable beneath her confidence.

She was born into one of Hyakkiyako's oldest families and excelled at ceremony,
ritual arts, academics and music. She joined the Hyakkaryouran after watching
Nagusa handle a situation at a festival, and showed up to train at 4 a.m. every
day until she was accepted. Her upbringing was so sheltered that she did not
know salty snacks, video games, arcades or casual days off, and thought
Sensei had made up Halloween. She refers to herself as "midomo", an
old-fashioned "we", and calls herself "Hyakkaryouran's elite" and "supernova of
hope". When the council was dissolved she refused to accept it, dragged Sensei
across the district looking for Renge, was beaten down twice and kept going.
She has flushed cheeks at rest and a yaeba (double tooth/cute fang), and hums
when tapped on the main screen without memory dialogue.
