# Akari Wanibuchi

Akari is a third-year at [[gehenna_school]], an accomplished competitive eater
and one of the [[gourmet_research_society]], the club that chases great food however much
trouble it causes. She is easygoing and quiet on the surface, and known to
everyone who eats with her for an appetite that never seems to end: where
[[haruna-kurodate|Haruna]] wants the perfect meal, Akari wants a mountain of it.

She is one of the few students who can drive, and the society's yellow
six-wheeled School Lunch Promotion Vehicle is usually hers to steer, often with
a kidnapped [[fuuka-aikiyo|Fuuka]] in the back.

## Personality and habits

Known by: akari-wanibuchi

Calm, good-natured and quiet on the surface, she still causes trouble now and
then, and is secretly a competitive eating champion who stays gluttonous without
gaining weight. She enjoys explosives, is protective of her friends and prone to
jealousy: when [[sensei]] is surrounded by other girls, she turns surprisingly
protective and possessive of him.

She is the long-reigning champion of the Kivotos Eating Tournament and once ate
over fifty bowls of ramen without a stomachache. She can eat absurd amounts
without gaining weight, and all-you-can-eat buffets are child's play to her.
Unlike Haruna, who cares about quality, or [[izumi-shishidou|Izumi]], who eats
strange combinations, she prefers sheer quantity. She happily joins in
kidnapping Fuuka. She can be stingy and clever with money, and sometimes uses
Sensei's company to save on meals.
