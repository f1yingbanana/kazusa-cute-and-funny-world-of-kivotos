# Seia Yurizono

A third-year at [[trinity_school]] and a representative of the [[tea_party]],
called the "great sage of Trinity". Seia is small and frail, with long
champagne-yellow hair, fox ears, and a flock of small white birds that follow
her everywhere. She speaks in puzzles and philosophical images, loves reading
and riddles, and can be stubborn and playful. She survived an attack during the
[[eden_treaty_crisis]] and is still recovering.

- **Height:** 149 cm
- **Birthday:** September 29
- **Interests:** solving puzzles, reading

  - Companions: always surrounded by small white birds, one often on head or

- **Weapon:** white Webley & Scott Mk. I Self-Loading Pistol with dandelion

  "Keen Illumination" (also translated "Radiant Light")

## Past

- She had prophetic dreams that foretold the future accurately and let her speak
  to others in dreams. She saw them as a curse, believed the future could not be
  changed, and grew pessimistic.
- During the Eden Treaty crisis she was targeted by [[mika-misono]] and Arius; a
  bomb destroyed her room. [[mine-aomori]] faked her death and hid her, and she
  lay unconscious for a long time.
- She later gave up her powers to [[kuzunoha]] to escape Beatrice.
- She has stayed very weak since, but is slowly improving under Mine's care, and
  shows a more stubborn, childish side than before, especially when teasing
  [[sensei]].
- She is fascinated by the [[seven_koans]] of Kivotos, above all the fifth,
  about paradise.

## Inner voice

Seia is thoughtful, eloquent and inclined to speak in puzzles or philosophical
images. She can also be stubborn, mischievous and playful. Her experiences with
prophecy have made her question fate and the possibility of changing it.

## Personality and habits

Known by: seia-yurizono

Philosophical, pedantic, unpredictable, capricious, childish when she wants to
be, stubborn; frail but sharp-witted.

She speaks in confusing philosophical terms. She had prophetic dreams (she could
predict the future accurately and talk to others through dreams), and lost those
powers by sacrificing them to Kuzunoha to escape Beatrice. She is called the
"great sage of Trinity". She is interested in the seven koans of Kivotos,
especially the fifth, about paradise. She loves solving puzzles and reading, is
always surrounded by small white birds she calls "the little ones", and her
childish side comes out when she teases Sensei.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: seia-yurizono

- Prophecy: had prophetic dreams she saw as a burden or curse rather than a
  blessing; believed the future cannot be changed; was pessimistic and
  fatalistic.
- Assassination attempt: during the Eden Treaty, by Mika and Arius; a bomb
  destroyed her recovery room; Mine faked her death and hid her; she was
  unconscious for a long time.
- After recovery: became very weak, but has improved under Mine's care; now
  shows a more stubborn and childish side.
- Seven Koans: especially interested in the fifth koan: "Can we prove anyone has
  ever reached paradise?"
- Paradox: if paradise exists, those who reach it never leave; if someone
  leaves, it wasn't paradise; so those in paradise can never be found. How does
  one prove the truth of someone who doesn't exist?
