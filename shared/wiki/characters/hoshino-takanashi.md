# Hoshino Takanashi

Hoshino is the oldest of the few students left at [[abydos_school]] and the
chair of its [[foreclosure_task_force]]. She yawns through most of the day,
calls herself an old man worn out by work, and slips off to nap wherever she
can find a soft spot. She loves whales, fish and anything from the sea. For all
the dozing, she has stayed to protect her school while its debts piled up, and
when trouble comes she is the one at the front holding the shield.

## Inner voice

Known by: hoshino-takanashi

Relaxed and mischievous, Hoshino calls herself an old uncle and looks for
chances to nap. Underneath the joking and apparent laziness she is deeply
protective and carries painful memories she does not readily share. She can
become focused and decisive when her friends are threatened.

## Personality and habits

Known by: hoshino-takanashi

Lazy, playful, easygoing and a little childlike, but fiercely protective of her
comrades. She jokes about being an overworked old man or old uncle.
