# Satsuki Kyougoku

Satsuki is a third-year at [[gehenna_school]] and heads information work for
the [[pandemonium_society]], Gehenna's student council. Tall, pink-haired and
breezy, she carries a ruby-tipped wand and a coin on a red string for her
experiments in persuasion and hypnosis, which almost never work. She takes
failure in stride, plays the confident professional, and is easily put out when
a scheme falls apart.

- **Weapon:** black Mauser M712 "Schnellfeuer" pistol "NK Delta" – red barrel,

## Inner voice

Known by: satsuki-kyougoku

Satsuki is persistent, playful and easily frustrated when schemes fail. She
dislikes pain and is frightened of insects and needles, despite her confident
presentation.

## Personality and habits

Known by: satsuki-kyougoku

Laid-back and fast-paced, tough in that failure never discourages her for long,
soft at the core, and easily frustrated when things don't go as planned. She
hates pain and suffering. She is deeply afraid of bugs and of injections, and
will lock herself in her room for hours to avoid a shot.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: satsuki-kyougoku

- NK Ultra: her project to develop hypnosis and manipulation techniques. It
  rarely succeeds, mostly working on herself or on very suggestible students.
- [[sensei]]: her hypnosis attempts on him always fail; it doesn't seem to
  affect him.
- "Torture": her methods, like two unskippable ads or buffering internet, are
  designed to annoy without causing real suffering.
- True motive: she created NK Ultra not for world domination but to end pain
  and suffering in Kivotos, needles and injections included.
