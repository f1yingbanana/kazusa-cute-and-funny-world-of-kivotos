# Kaede Isami

Kaede is a first-year at [[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy]] who
transferred from the countryside, where she grew up a wildly mischievous girl
catching beetles and roaming the fields. Feeling small beside the elegant
members of the [[etiquette_training_society]], she joined it out of admiration
for her upperclassmen [[tsubaki-kasuga|Tsubaki]] and [[mimori-mizuha|Mimori]] and now works
loudly and sincerely at becoming a proper lady, with chaotic results. Her
pranks are, she says, on hiatus. Outside training she is a devoted Mushiqueen
card battler and still jumps at any chance to go beetle-catching.

- Appearance Kaede has dark purple shoulder-length hair styled into two neat
  buns with longer strands framing her face and side-swept bangs. A small

  removes them. Her eyes are a warm yellow and she has dark purple raccoon ears
  matching her hair. Her halo takes the form of a five-petaled pink lotus flower
  with an empty star at its center and five symmetrical circles adorning each
  petal.
- Kaede wears a traditional Japanese hakui robe with detached miko sleeves and a
  red side-less pleated skirt, the upper body covering the open sides. A large
  bow sits behind her waist with sashes visible and neatly tied. She wears white
  thigh-length tights with red-trimmed edges and a small decorative red bow at
  each end, and black Japanese okobo sandals with small lotus patterns drawn on
  them.
- Kaede carries a red double-barreled Nambu Type 90 Flare Gun decorated with
  gold floral decals and white streaks along the barrel. A small flower keychain
  is attached to the grip. The weapon's vivid coloring and floral detailing make
  it unmistakably hers. Despite its decorative appearance it is a genuine piece
  of equipment that Kaede maintains carefully, treating it as something precious
  that deserves to be looked after properly.

## Outlook

Known by: kaede-isami

- Arriving in the city, Kaede felt small and out of place beside the composed
  members of the Inner Discipline Club, and that feeling lit a determination in
  her that has never faded.

- She throws herself into training with boundless energy and complete
  sincerity, whether it means eating all her green peppers, practicing club
  one-liners at the playground or attempting to brew coffee for Sensei. The
  results rarely go to plan and chaos tends to follow her everywhere, yet none
  of it discourages her for long. She bounces back from every stumble with the
  same loud, infectious energy she started with. Beneath that exterior, Kaede
  carries a genuinely tender heart. When she fails she feels it deeply,
  sometimes to the point of tears, and the gap between where she is and where
  she wants to be weighs on her more than she usually lets on. Outside of
  training she is a devoted Mushiqueen card battler who treats every match as a
  test of heart and strategy, keeps a steel chest of personal treasures, and
  jumps at any opportunity to go beetle-catching.
