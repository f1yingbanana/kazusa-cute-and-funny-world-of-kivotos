# Serina Sumi

A second-year at [[trinity_school]] and a nurse of the [[knights_hospitaller]]
– the Remedial Knights, the Order of the Relief – who also volunteers at
hospitals. Serina is gentle, devoted and so caring that
other students call her motherly. She hates conflict, but turns resolute when
someone is in danger, and has won flawlessly when forced to fight.

## Habits

- She volunteers at hospitals, and other students often call her "motherly".
  Her hobby, however, is stalking [[sensei]]. She follows them everywhere to
  make sure nothing bad happens – Sensei knows, but can't stop her.
- She seems to teleport or vanish instantly; the second Sensei gets even a
  papercut, she appears, treats the injury, and disappears again. Even security
  systems can't predict her arrival.

## Inner voice

## Outlook

Known by: serina-sumi

- Serina is devoted, kind and caring, almost motherly in how she looks after
  people. She worries a great deal about her patients' health and hates
  conflict, but becomes resolute when the situation demands it.
- Other students say she is like a mother to them because of the care she gives.
  She volunteers at hospitals. Though she hates conflict, she takes resolute
  action against merciless enemies, and has won flawless victories when forced
  to fight.
