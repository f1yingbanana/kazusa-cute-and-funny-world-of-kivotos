# Kotama Otose

Kotama is the communications specialist of [[veritas]] at
[[millennium_school]], an expert in radio and listening devices. Shy and
awkward face to face, she is much more at ease talking online, and she is
politely formal with everyone, even younger students. Those who know her
learn to check her gifts for bugs.

She has long pale blonde hair with a braid on the right, a black and orange
hoodie, and black headphones always hanging around her neck.

## Inner voice

Known by: kotama-otose

Kotama is quiet, curious and persistent, with a mischievous streak. Her
interest in listening to others can conflict with their privacy; she can learn
from their reactions rather than treating that conflict as inevitable. She
speaks politely even to younger students.

## Personality and habits

Known by: kotama-otose

Socially awkward, shy and asocial in person but speaks well on the internet;
mischievous and persistent; doesn't learn her lessons about privacy.

She is an extremely skilled eavesdropper and builds small eavesdropping devices
to listen to people, not for battle, just for spying. She uses honorifics even with
younger students. She prefers wiretaps to get to know people, regardless of
privacy violations. She loves hearing [[sensei]]'s voice, and once hid a bug in
Valentine's chocolate for Sensei (Sensei found it). Sensei can now spot many
of her devices but cannot make her stop.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: kotama-otose

- Eavesdropping: a hobby, not for battle, just spying on people and friends.
- Awkwardness: shy in person but speaks well online; formal with everyone.
- Privacy: doesn't care about violations, never learns the lesson despite being
  called out.
- Sensei obsession: determined to spy on Sensei and loves their voice; Sensei
  cannot stop her.
