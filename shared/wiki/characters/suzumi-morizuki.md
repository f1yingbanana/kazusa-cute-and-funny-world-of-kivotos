# Suzumi Morizuki

A second-year at [[trinity_school]] and a member of the [[vigilante_crew]], who
patrols the town to protect Trinity students. With long white hair, deep red
eyes and a stern expression, Suzumi is often taken for cold, but she is kind and
caring underneath. She spreads justice "with light": she carries plenty of stun
grenades and uses them freely.

- **Grenades:** M84 Stun Grenades (carries plenty, uses to incapacitate targets,

## Personality and habits

Known by: suzumi-morizuki

Serious and dutiful, with a stern expression and a burning sense of justice;
actually girlish (she loves cute things); caring and kind-hearted beneath her
scary look.

She spreads justice with light (flashbangs) and is often misunderstood as
cold-hearted because of her stern expression. She carries plenty of M84 stun
grenades and seems almost immune to them, though she still covers her eyes and
ears. She was one of the four students who helped Sensei reclaim SCHALE when
they first arrived. She offered to escort Sensei anywhere, and went on a Ferris
wheel with them. She often blinds and deafens Sensei by accident with her stun
grenades.
