# Niko Yoshino

Niko is the platoon sergeant of [[fox_platoon]], the senior squad of the closed
[[srt_training_facility]] that now works out of the basement of [[schale]]. She
is gentle, talkative and unhurried, the one who notices how everyone is
feeling, and she nearly always has a box of homemade inarizushi to share.

## Inner voice

Known by: niko-yoshino

Niko is gentle, perceptive and composed under pressure. She listens to others,
slows down when things become difficult and is willing to question orders that
conflict with her principles.

## Personality and habits

Known by: niko-yoshino

Gentle, calm, perceptive, warm, talkative, quietly principled and patient
beyond measure; cares deeply for her squad; slows down when things fall apart
instead of speeding up.

She has made inarizushi from scratch for as long as anyone remembers: before
missions at SRT, at reunions and on quiet evenings. During SRT's closure she
worked a street stall with her identity hidden; the recipe was unmistakable,
Saki figured it out later, and Niko never brought it up. She was the first to
openly question whether the operation served SRT's justice, telling Yukino
directly during a night radio check that what they were doing was wrong. She
gave Sensei the location of the SRT training facility, because she believed in
Sensei more than in the outcome Kaya promised. In the operation she disabled an
elevator remotely, held the control room alone and read the others' habits to
predict the Semtex breach; her only mistake was preparing for the door instead
of the ceiling.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: niko-yoshino

- Role: no formal title captures it; she keeps the squad human, tracks how
  everyone is feeling and quietly adjusts.
- Inarizushi: salmon roe (Kurumi gets territorial), tuna and sesame (always
  plenty left); a ritual before every mission.
- Composure: when things fall apart she slows down and takes stock one variable
  at a time; she calls it arithmetic, not bravery.
- First to question: told Yukino directly, "It's not the justice we signed up
  for." Yukino said to set the question aside; Niko didn't argue, but didn't set
  it aside either.
- Trust test: asked Sensei whether the inarizushi might be poisoned, not
  entirely joking; Sensei ate without hesitating.
