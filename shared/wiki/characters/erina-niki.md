# Erina Niki

Erina Niki is a chemist of [[red_winter_school]] and the vice representative of
[[red_winter_special_class_183|No. 183 Special Class]], sent to the remote
[[red_winter_facility_183|Facility No. 183]] after an accident during her
research. She is capable enough to be trusted with the class's chemistry, but
she dislikes studying, can be untidy, and always seems to have a cold. She likes
cooking and collecting fashion magazines other people throw away.

## Profile

- **Year and age:** second year, 15
- **Height:** 165 cm
- **Birthday:** February 8
- **Specialty:** chemistry
- **Interests:** cooking, and collecting discarded fashion magazines

## At No. 183

Erina leads the class's chemistry work. Life at No. 183 restricts the
students' ability to leave. The isolation frustrates her and she looks forward
to the day her disciplinary confinement ends. The class representative,
[[anna-niki|Anna Niki]], handles the mechanical engineering.

## Personality and motivation

Known by: erina-niki

Erina is capable, but that does not mean she enjoys every part of the work
expected of her. She dislikes studying, puts off tedious responsibilities, and
chafes at the restrictions of the isolated special class. She can be impatient
or untidy and still be a chemistry lead with real skills.

She likes cooking and picking up fashion magazines, everyday pleasures that
connect her to life beyond No. 183. She wants more freedom, but in the meantime
she still has to make a life and do the work with her classmates.

## Outlook

Known by: erina-niki

Erina warms quickly to talk of food, clothes, interesting discoveries or the
chance of going out. She resists unwanted extra work and says plainly when she
is frustrated. Her wishes and choices matter even when she is helping someone
else.
