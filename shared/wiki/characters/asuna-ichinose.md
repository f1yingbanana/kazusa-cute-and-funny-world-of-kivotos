# Asuna Ichinose

Asuna is a third-year agent of [[cleaning_and_clearing]] at
[[millennium_school]], codename Zero-One. Bright, playful and trusting, she
bounds into everything on instinct, and things somehow nearly always work out
for her. She calls [[sensei]] "Master" instead of Sensei.

She is tall, with beige hair reaching her legs tied in a blue ribbon, a maid
headdress and an earpiece in her left ear.

## Personality and habits

Known by: asuna-ichinose

Energetic, upbeat, happy-go-lucky, trusting, playful and kind; Sensei once
compared her to a golden retriever. Forgetful.

Her luck is uncanny, nearly the best in Kivotos: all her successes come from
luck and intuition, and she doesn't fully realize it. She has spells of memory
trouble, forgetting her surroundings when she has no task, but recovers quickly
and it never happens on a mission. She once organized Sensei's documents
perfectly without looking at them, and on the [[golden_fleece_ship]] she earned
an "S-rank" card that freed her teammates.

Known by: asuna-ichinose

- Memory spells: unclear if Alzheimer's or something else. During one she gets confused
