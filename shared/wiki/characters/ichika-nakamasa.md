# Ichika Nakamasa

A second-year at [[trinity_school]] and an officer of the
[[justice_task_force]], who works closely with its rank-and-file members and
keeps operations on track. Ichika is easygoing, cordial and polite, even with
students from other schools, and talks with her eyes almost closed, so people
often think she is asleep. She takes her duties seriously all the same.

- **Weapon:** black Enfield EM-2 experimental bullpup assault rifle with red
  furniture

## Habits

- She is the one charged with keeping the president, [[tsurugi-kenzaki]], from
  running wild, with limited success.
- Problems seem to find her: she often points out what is going wrong, but
  things fall apart before she can step in.
- She learns new things remarkably fast: she picked up the guitar on a whim, and
  came second in her first surfing competition.

## Personality and habits

Known by: ichika-nakamasa

Easygoing, laid-back, calm and formal; takes her duties seriously and is cordial
with other schools. Her amiability is the result of continuous effort. Pushed to
her absolute limit, she becomes destructive.

She keeps her eyes almost closed when talking. She can see perfectly, the reason
is unknown, nobody brings it up, and she is often mistaken for being asleep;
fully open, her eyes are a bright blueish grey. She learns fast: her first time
on a guitar, her first time surfing she took second place in a competition. Her
destructive side rivals Tsurugi's, though it only comes out when she is pressed
to the absolute limit; she once took down Iori Shiromi with ease. She works hard
to keep that side contained, since most who have seen it ended up in hospital,
and only Sensei is safe from it. She often says what is wrong, but the situation
deteriorates before she can intervene. She is responsible for keeping Tsurugi
from going loose, with limited success, and is close with the rank and file of
her club.
