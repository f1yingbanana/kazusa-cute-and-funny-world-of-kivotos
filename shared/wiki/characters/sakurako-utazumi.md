# Sakurako Utazumi

A third-year at [[trinity_school]] and leader of the [[sisterhood]], who spends
most of her time at [[trinity_sisterhood]]. Sakurako is kind, sincere and a
patient listener, but somewhat old-fashioned, and her expressions are easily
misread: her smile has been known to send chills down spines and make small
children cry, though she means no harm at all.

  rails, Manticore Arms Curved Buttpad, golden bird as front sight, named
  "Purifying Touch" (also translated "Purification Weaver")

## Habits

- The Sisterhood she leads offers spiritual guidance and support to students.
- She knows little of current trends and feels left out by the other Sisters;
  she once tried performing as an idol, and people only ran away.
- She asks [[sensei]] to reassure the Sisters when something bad happens.
- She has clashed politically with [[nagisa-kirifuji]] and [[mine-aomori]].

## Personality and habits

Known by: sakurako-utazumi

Kind-hearted, helpful, old-fashioned, willing to listen, easily misunderstood;
fears being left out.

She spends most of her time in the cathedral, so she doesn't know current trends
and feels left out. Her smile scares people, sending chills down spines and
making children cry; she never shows ill will, but her expressions make people
think otherwise. She once took part as an idol, which only made people run away.
She wears a latex suit for heavy battles. She asks Sensei to reassure the Sisters when bad things happen.
