# Makoto Hanuma

Makoto is a third-year at [[gehenna_school]] and the president of the
[[pandemonium_society]], the student council that rules Gehenna, at least on
paper. She styles herself "the Great Makoto", champions Gehenna's spirit of
freedom and chaos, and spends lavishly on grand plans that tend to backfire.
Her favorite targets are [[hina-sorasaki|Hina]] and the [[prefect_team]], whom
she mocks with mass-printed posters, and her favorite person is
[[ibuki-tanga|Ibuki]], whom she spoils shamelessly. She walks with guards and
laughs "Kihehe!".

## Inner voice

Known by: makoto-hanuma

Makoto is theatrical, proud and impulsive, with grand plans that often go wrong.
She recovers quickly from setbacks and loves displaying her authority. Her
affection for Ibuki brings out a genuinely protective side.

## Personality and habits

Known by: makoto-hanuma

Immature, dimwitted, tyrannical and strong-willed. She puts her own passions
first, bounces back from failures quickly and is deeply committed to her
ideology of chaos and freedom. Her mouth often runs ahead of her brain, and for
all her wealth and power people see her as a dork.

She loves spoiling Ibuki with pudding, toys and praise. She enjoys teasing and
bullying Hina and the Prefect Team, mass-producing posters that mock them. She
buys out entire rows of shops and walks with guards to show her authority.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: makoto-hanuma

- Ideology: pure chaos and freedom, set against the Prefect Team's mission of
  discipline.
- Showing off: she buys out shops, has dishes Ibuki loves made for her, and
  bought huge hangars to store anti-Prefect Team posters.
- Desperation: she gets desperate when things don't go her way.
- Weapon irony: named "The Honored One" – fitting name, but gun not fitting for
  its user.
- Allergies: she is surprisingly allergic to cats.
- [[sensei]]: she wants them as the society's partner and has shown them its
  printing office, its resort business, its merchandise and the planned Makoto
  Tower.
