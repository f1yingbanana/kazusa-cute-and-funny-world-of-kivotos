# Shun Sunohara

Shun is an instructor at Shanhaijing's [[plum_blossom_garden]], where she cares
for and teaches the youngest children alongside her younger sister,
[[kokona-sunohara]]. Tall, gentle and patient, she rarely raises her voice; she
sings for the children, puts on puppet shows and collects toys for them, and
students across Shanhaijing trust her. She has been an instructor for a very
long time, and she is very sensitive about anything that suggests she is old.
Her age is a closely kept secret.
