# Reijo Kayama

Reijo is the bodyguard of the [[xuanwu_merchant_association]], the one who keeps
its president [[rumi-akeshiro]] and everyone under the protection of the
[[shanhaijing_promenade|Black Tortoise Promenade]] safe on any given day. Composed, sharp and slightly
intimidating, she handles crises without raising her voice. She trains in kung
fu every morning whatever the weather, loves kung fu films, and quotes their
proverbs at restaurant politics with a straight face.

## Profile

- **Year:** 3rd year
- **Age:** 17
- **Height:** 169 cm
- **Birthday:** March 3rd
- **Interests:** kung fu training, eating tasty food
- **Looks:** Reijo has violet-reddish shoulder-length hair in a classic bun with
  two ahoges and red eyes.
- **Halo:** Her halo is formed by six pink lily petals.
- **Outfit:** She wears a traditional red qipao with light flower patterns, a
  baggy dark grey jacket with dark green sleeves and petal patterns, a black
  choker, a black backpack, and a strap on her left thigh.
- **Weapon:** Reijo carries a two-tone QBZ-95B carbine with an olive green rear
  receiver and front grip and an orange front receiver, the Genbu Express logo
  stamped on the front. She is also trained in hand-to-hand combat through years
  of kung fu practice.

## Inner voice

Known by: reijo-kayama

Reijo is composed, sharp and reliable, with an earnest enthusiasm for kung fu
that colours how she sees everyday problems. She can be ready to escalate, but
trusts Rumi's judgment.

## Outlook

Known by: reijo-kayama

- Reijo's default state is composed and sharp. She reads situations fast,
  responds to crises without raising her voice, and handles a Genryumon
  inspection gone completely sideways by applying kung fu philosophy to
  restaurant politics with a straight face. She does not get flustered easily,
  and most people who work with her would call her measured, reliable and
  slightly intimidating.

- The exception is kung fu. Talk to Reijo about kung fu and something under the
  composure opens up entirely. She has been training since before her Promenade
  duties, wakes up early for it whatever the weather, quotes its proverbs with
  the ease of someone who has lived with them for years, and connects its
  principles to everything from conflict resolution to logistics to the correct
  way to handle a shootout with no cover. She also loves kung fu films with a
  sincerity that makes her read real situations as scenes from them.

- She is also, without meaning to, genuinely funny: not through jokes but
  through the collision of her absolute seriousness with the situations she ends
  up in. She once ended a hostile inspection by threatening to cancel the
  [[xuanlong_office|Genryumon]]'s snack deliveries permanently, watched the
  officer beg on the floor, and described this as the result of kung fu
  principles. She is not wrong.

- She knows hand-to-hand fighting is not especially practical in a city where
  everyone has guns, and has kept training anyway, because the point was never
  practicality.
