# Akane Murokasa

Akane is an agent of [[cleaning_and_clearing]], [[millennium_school]]'s maid
unit, under the codename Zero-Three. She handles infiltration and demolition,
and is called the "Sweeper Master" for clearing out enemy lines with a great
deal of explosives.

Away from a mission she is soft-spoken, polite and caring: a beige-haired maid
in glasses who keeps her teammates organized and makes sure [[neru-mikamo]]
does her work instead of slipping off to play games.

- **Outfit:** typical French maid uniform with blue tie, white gloves, long

- **Weapon:** black Welrod Mk. I bolt-action pistol with light blue strips on
  barrel and breech handle, named "Silent Solution"
- **Secondary:** large suitcase containing explosive charges (C4, dynamite,
  etc.)

## Inner voice

Known by: akane-murokasa

Akane is gentle, caring and composed, with a practical side and an unexpected
fondness for explosives. She often looks after others and tries to keep Neru
focused, though her own choices can be surprisingly bold.

## Personality and habits

Known by: akane-murokasa

Caring, matronly, mature and gentle, with a quirky side; openly affectionate,
and now and then careless with explosives.

She carries bombs the way others carry candy, and once blew up the main room
at [[schale]] without thinking. She acts as [[sensei]]'s personal maid
(cleaning, supplies, housekeeping) and as Neru's babysitter, keeping her from
dodging work to play games. Like Asuna, she sometimes calls Sensei "Master".
She says openly that she misses Sensei and wishes to see them, and once fell
asleep in Sensei's bed from exhaustion.

## Outlook

Known by: akane-murokasa

Akane takes pride in supporting her C&C teammates and can show a playful side
while working with them.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: akane-murokasa

- Codename: "Zero-Three" (03), an essential employee at "cleaning up".
