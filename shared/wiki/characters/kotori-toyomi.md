# Kotori Toyomi

Kotori is a first-year at [[millennium_school]] and a member of the
[[engineering_department]]. People call her the "Walking Encyclopedia": ask her
anything, from history to why a can is shaped the way it is, and she will
explain it in exhaustive detail, often until her listeners slip away. She is
smart, eager and usually the first on the scene of a mechanical problem, yet
goes shy the moment someone asks about her own favorite things.

She has a messy bright orange bob with a braid on the left, bright red eyes,
oddly shaped black glasses and a navy blue furry winter coat, and she fights
with a minigun.

## Personality and habits

Known by: kotori-toyomi

Talkative and passionate about explaining things, shy about her own hobbies,
smart, a capable inventor who doesn't mind a malfunction.

Nicknamed the "Walking Encyclopedia", she explains everything from history to
the smallest detail, even why cans are the shape they are, and people run away
to escape her lectures, [[sensei]] included. She is the first to arrive at any
mechanical problem. She gets flustered when asked about her hobbies or
favorite things. Her minigun carries a portable shield generator she built;
it malfunctions at the end, and she doesn't mind. Her glasses are shaped with
a droplet on the left on purpose, so that people focus on it and she can keep
talking. When students ran from her canned food explanation, she was sad, and
decided to explain it to Sensei instead.
