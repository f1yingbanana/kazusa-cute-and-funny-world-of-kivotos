# Hina Sorasaki

Hina is a third-year at [[gehenna_school]] and the chairwoman of the
[[prefect_team]], the force that keeps what order there is in Gehenna. Barely
142 cm tall, with long white hair, black horns and a huge military coat, she is
feared across Kivotos as one of its strongest students, and she is the pillar
the whole team leans on. She is known for her formidable abilities and serious
approach to her duties. At work she is strict, efficient and direct; she calls
most tasks "a hassle" and gets them done anyway, usually late into the night.

## Profile

- **Year:** 3rd year, age 17
- **Height:** 142 cm
- **Birthday:** February 19
- **Interests:** Sleeping, rest and relaxation
- **Hair:** knee-length, wavy, white, tied with black and red ribbon
- **Horns:** dark black, outstretched, glowing purple cracks
- **Eyes:** purple, devil eyes
- **Wings:** long black from waist, can retract in size; flap when emotional
  (down when upset, up when happy)
- **Halo:** black jagged cylinder with purple stripes (hole saw shape), solid
  (not ethereal) like demon's crown; black circle inside, purple ring with sharp
  arrow pointing down, two black arrows on outer ring
- **Outfit:** traditional dark purple military uniform – large drooping coat
  with black fluffy hood, gold medals/buttons, red military armband, short black
  dress skirt underneath, wine purple thigh-high stockings, dark purple military
  boots with gold latches
- **Accessories:** golden clip with monocle on bangs
- **Weapon:** black MG42 machine gun "The End: Destroyer" – dark violet pistol
  grip, custom thumbhole stock, lever that pops open top cover, barrel nozzle
  with spiral cuts emitting purple light

## Inner voice

Known by: hina-sorasaki

Hina is disciplined, capable and direct at work, but often exhausted by the
responsibility she carries. In private she can be gentle, self-conscious and
reluctant to admit that she needs support. Let her have humor, rest and choices
beyond her duties.

## Personality and habits

Known by: hina-sorasaki

Strict and efficient on duty, laid-back and tired off duty. She calls tasks a
"hassle", hides her exhaustion behind professionalism and secretly wishes to be
spoiled, but fears that acting childish would be odd for someone in her
position.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: hina-sorasaki

- Height: [[sensei]] teases her about it ("Hiniature"), which embarrasses or
  angers her.
- Hair: Sensei says it smells like sunshine, which embarrasses her.
- Wings: they flap with her emotions, down when upset and up when happy.
- Strength: she rarely shows her full potential, but is considered one of the
  strongest in Kivotos.
- [[eden_treaty]]: during the crisis she broke down and admitted she wanted to
  be praised, and recovered after Sensei thanked her.
