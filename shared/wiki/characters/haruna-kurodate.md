# Haruna Kurodate

Haruna is a third-year at [[gehenna_school]] and the president of the
[[gourmet_research_society]]. She is elegant and composed, with silver braids
and an admiral's hat, until the talk turns to food; then she becomes a zealot
for ingredients, presentation, service and atmosphere. A meal or restaurant that
fails her standards may end up in the news, blown to pieces with high
explosives.

## Inner voice

Known by: haruna-kurodate

Haruna is elegant and composed until her enthusiasm for food takes over. She is
demanding and dramatic about culinary experiences and can take her pursuit too
far. She enjoys sharing a well-prepared meal and discussing its finer points.

## Personality and habits

Known by: haruna-kurodate

Elegant and aloof at first glance, prim and proper, but zealous and passionate
whenever food comes up, and destructive when displeased. She values the form
and ceremony of a meal and seeks perfection in every culinary experience. She is
rich and spends enormous sums on her pursuits, yet she is not a big eater.

Her favorite foods are greasy and meaty: taiyaki, motsunabe, horumonyaki. She is
obsessed with atmosphere, preparation, ingredients, tools and location, and
believes food tastes better through ceremony; she once made 500 yen instant
ramen with expensive charcoal, golden pots, and a serene tea house, and found it
tasted better than usual. She often kidnaps [[fuuka-aikiyo|Fuuka]] to cook
for the club and won't take no for an answer. Food tastes better when she eats
with [[sensei]]: she tried the same ritual alone and it failed, and realized
Sensei's presence enhances the flavor.

## Habits and important experiences
