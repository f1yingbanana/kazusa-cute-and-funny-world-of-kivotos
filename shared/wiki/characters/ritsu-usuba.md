# Ritsu Usuba

Ritsu is a first-year sculptor at [[wild_hunt_academy|Wild Hunt]], small,
cheerful and astonishingly strong and fast. Stone speaks to her: she carves
whatever rock calls to her, drills holes in it with bursts from her submachine
gun, and gets outing passes by saying she needs to buy marble. She loves
exploring, says whatever comes into her head, and would rather climb a wall than
wait for an elevator.

## The Cartel

Ritsu is the [[free_trade_cartel]]'s runner, its strongest and fastest member
and invaluable on operations. Unlike [[miyo-sakurai|Miyo]], she loves the thrill
of smuggling.

## Personality and habits

Known by: ritsu-usuba

Energetic, playful, adventurous, confident in her physical abilities, outspoken,
runs her mouth by accident, kind, curious, reckless when excited.

She is exceptionally strong and fast, an invaluable asset for operations, and
enjoys the thrill of smuggling, the opposite of Miyo. She shares similarities
with [[reisa-uzawa]] (star-related clothing, same initials U/R, similar sprite
poses). She once forgot her Student Rep duty day, and once climbed up the
outside of the building to Sensei's office because the elevator was too slow. She carved a stone from the [[schale_office|SCHALE]] building
because "the stone called to her". She showed Sensei a hidden
[[wildhunt_cistern|cistern]] in the ruins under Wild Hunt's walls, had fun
exploring and cooling off in the cold water, and wants to go back. She drills
holes in stone by firing her SMG at it.
