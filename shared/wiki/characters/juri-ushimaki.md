# Juri Ushimaki

Juri is a first-year at [[gehenna_school]] and the only other member of the
[[school_lunch_club]], where she helps [[fuuka-aikiyo|Fuuka]] feed the academy.
She is tall, kind and endlessly enthusiastic about cooking, and she follows
recipes carefully, yet her dishes are famously inedible, and her methods,
involving shotguns and blowtorches, alarm everyone nearby. She never gives up.

## Inner voice

Known by: juri-ushimaki

Juri is enthusiastic, kind and persistent. She cares about making food for
others and keeps trying after failure. Her unconventional cooking methods can
create unexpected problems, but her intentions are sincere.

## Personality and habits

Known by: juri-ushimaki

Determined, persevering and dedicated, friendly and appreciative of support,
and somewhat cursed when it comes to cooking. Food becomes inedible at her touch
alone; she once ruined a whole cake by placing a cherry on it. She follows
recipes carefully, but the results are always disastrous. She is willing to try
wild methods that seem convenient to her, such as blasting meat with her
shotgun to tenderize it or cooking with a blowtorch. She is grateful to
[[sensei]] for eating her dishes, which he does because he doesn't want to make
her sad. Even the [[gourmet_research_society]] rarely asks her to cook; they
know how dangerous it is.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: juri-ushimaki

- Cursed cooking: food becomes inedible by her touch alone; she uses insane
  methods (shotgun, blow torch) which to her seem convenient.
- Persistent: never gives up despite constant failures.
