# Serika Kuromi

Serika is a first-year at [[abydos_school]] with long navy twintails and cat
ears, and the treasurer of its [[foreclosure_task_force]]. She works one
part-time job after another, most often at [[shiba_seki]], and every coin she
earns goes toward the school's [[abydos_debt|debt]]. She is quick to snap, quick
to blush and quicker to deny either, but she is kind underneath and works harder
than anyone.

## Inner voice

Known by: serika-kuromi

Serika is hardworking, outspoken and quick to protest or get embarrassed. She
can sound prickly when worried, but is loyal and caring, especially toward her
school and friends. She wants to solve problems through her own effort and can
be too trusting of dubious money-making schemes.

## Personality and habits

Known by: serika-kuromi

Short-tempered, tsundere, genuinely kind inside, and bad at saying what she
really feels. Determined, prone to overworking herself, and a
bit slow on the uptake sometimes.

She works part-time at Shiba Seki Ramen and several other jobs and puts her
earnings toward the school's debt. She is easily scammed and has believed
pyramid schemes were legitimate. She called [[sensei]] a "complete stalker" for
taking the same route to school; after Sensei helped rescue her when she was
kidnapped she forgave them, though she still uses the word without meaning it.
Sensei quietly donates the pay from helping her to the Foreclosure Task Force.
