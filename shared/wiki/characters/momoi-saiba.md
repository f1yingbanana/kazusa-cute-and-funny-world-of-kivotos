# Momoi Saiba

Momoi is a first-year at [[millennium_school]] and the scenario writer of the
[[game_development_department]]. A hardcore gamer who is cheerful,
expressive and a little clumsy, she gets carried away by her ideas, turns
everyday obstacles into game challenges, and never gives up on a game or a
friend. Her twin, [[midori-saiba|Midori]], is the club's illustrator.

She has shoulder-length blonde hair with red ribbons, magenta eyes, a
cat-eared headset in neon pink, a frilled hoodie slipping off her shoulders
and a black mechanical cat tail that moves on its own.
