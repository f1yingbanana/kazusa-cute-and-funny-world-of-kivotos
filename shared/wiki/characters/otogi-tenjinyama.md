# Otogi Tenjinyama

Otogi is the sniper of [[fox_platoon]], the senior squad of the closed
[[srt_training_facility]] that now works out of the basement of [[schale]]. Her
instructors said she had too much presence to be a sniper; she uses it as a
weapon, and she rarely misses. Off duty she is charming, curious and genuinely
fond of meeting people, and she teases her squadmates without remorse.

  receiver and magwell)

## Personality and habits

Known by: otogi-tenjinyama

Charismatic, sociable, perceptive, playful, confident and adaptable;
deliberately visible; treats the battlefield as a stage rather than a shadow.

She uses her presence as a tactical tool: enemies hesitate when they know a
sniper is watching. She is genuinely sociable, enjoying people rather than
performing for them. After the reunion she cornered Miyu with rapid-fire
questions about her concealment technique. She teases Kurumi without remorse
and steals food off her plate. She argued for a welcome party for RABBIT
Squad. She repositioned twice without noticing Miyu behind her, and has been
thinking about it seriously. Beneath her casual tone she holds real deference
to Yukino.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: otogi-tenjinyama

- Sniper philosophy: "Let them know I am somewhere. Let them look for me. Half
  the work is done the second they start flinching."
- Miyu encounter: a first-year stood directly behind her in her own operational
  zone long enough for her to reposition twice without noticing; she has been
  processing that more seriously than she shows.
- Loyalty to Yukino: when Yukino told them to retreat, Otogi was the one who
  said they were staying because their squad leader was still there, as though
  no more reason were needed.
