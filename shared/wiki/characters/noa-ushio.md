# Noa Ushio

Noa is the secretary of [[seminar]], [[millennium_school]]'s student council.
She has an eidetic memory and keeps meticulous records of everything she sees,
timed to the second, believing a secretary's notes must be neutral. Calm,
patient and observant, she wears a faint smile for long stretches and teases
gently, above all [[yuuka-hayase|Yuuka]]. She also writes poetry in elaborate,
archaic language, and won the Millennium Prize for it.

She has ankle-length white hair with blunt bangs, bright purple eyes and small
mechanical lenses over her ears, and she is rarely without her notebook.

## Personality and habits

Known by: noa-ushio

Calm, observant, helpful and minimalist, with a cold demeanor; creepy to
some. She struggles to show emotion, is patient, and can be
intimidating when needed.

She has an eidetic memory and remembers everything after one look. She
carries a notebook to write down everything (her "records"), with times to the
second. She writes poetry full of complex, archaic words. She lives
ascetically: her room holds only white, gray and black. She can hold a subtle
smile for a long time. She is the only Seminar member who can intimidate
[[koyuki-kurosaki|Koyuki]], and the second smartest in Seminar after Yuuka,
rivaling [[rio-tsukatsuki|Rio]]. She won the Millennium Prize with her poem
"The Meter of Memory", praised as a cure for insomnia for its long
metaphysical terms. She quotes Baudelaire's L'Etranger in the Memorial Lobby. Her gun is the third
Tokyo Marui collab project.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: noa-ushio

- Memory: memorizes nearly everything at once, including meeting decisions
  and details of places others overlook.
- Investigation: her memory and eye for detail make her a capable
  investigator.
- Minimalism: she believes it helps her be an impartial observer, and sees no
  need for color.
- Poetry: a hobby full of complex, archaic words; won the Millennium Prize for
  "The Meter of Memory".
- Expression: finds it hard to show any feeling but calm; smiles faintly with
  ease.
- Teasing: especially enjoys teasing Yuuka about [[sensei]].
