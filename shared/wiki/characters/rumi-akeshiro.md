# Rumi Akeshiro

Rumi is the president of the [[xuanwu_merchant_association]] and head chef of
the [[shanhaijing_promenade]], the commercial and culinary heart of
Shanhaijing's gourmet district. She runs the Promenade the way she cooks, with warmth, confidence
and a smile, and handles supply crises, Genryumon inspections and difficult
customers without raising her voice. Food is how she looks after people, and
she feeds anyone who looks hungry whether they asked or not. Her experimental
recipes are famous for landing anywhere between extraordinary and inedible.

- **Year:** 3rd year

- **Interests:** cooking, food product development
- **Looks:** Rumi has straight buff-brown hair fading lighter toward the ends

- **Outfit:** She wears a white shirt under a red blazer with gold buttons and a

  Over a red wrap skirt with gold trim she wears a black apron, both reaching
  her ankles, with black tights, a left thigh strap, and ankle-high black

- **Weapon:** Rumi carries a Type 64 silenced submachine gun with the receiver
  painted in a flame gradient. Quiet, controlled, and effective at close range,
  which suits someone who operates primarily in the crowded corridors of a
  restaurant district. She has also demonstrated a willingness to throw hands
  when circumstances call for it, and her uppercut is, by all accounts, clean.

## Outlook

Known by: rumi-akeshiro

- Rumi leads the Promenade the way she cooks: with warmth, confidence, and a
  willingness to push further than anyone around her is entirely comfortable
  with. She handles supply crises, hostile customers, Genryumon inspections and
  the occasional dine-and-dasher with the same steady, smiling practicality. She
  does not raise her voice in the dining room. She does not need to. Her
  presence alone tends to resolve situations before they escalate, and on the
  rare occasion it does not, she resolves them outside the dining room, where
  the restaurant's reputation is not at stake.

- Cooking is not something Rumi does as part of her role. It is the reason she
  took the role in the first place. She thinks about flavour constantly, turning
  over what works, what is missing, what could be pushed further. She develops
  new recipes through relentless experimentation that the association's members
  have learned to be cautious about, because the results range from genuinely
  extraordinary to completely inedible with very little warning. She considers
  this an acceptable cost of progress.

- She feeds people the way she breathes. Food is how she takes care of people,
  and she takes care of people constantly and without being asked.

- Beneath the warmth is someone who carries the weight of being president with
  genuine seriousness and genuine tiredness. She said once that being in charge
  feels less like reward and more like recurring relief, and she said it like
  someone who had been thinking it for a long time and had never quite found the
  right person to say it to.
