# Shimiko Endou

A first-year at [[trinity_school]] and a member of the
[[trinity_library_committee]], with floppy orange ears, very long pigtails and
glasses. Shimiko is friendly and talkative, has read through Trinity's entire
enormous library, and her first thought on meeting anyone is which book would
suit them. Troublemakers who mistreat books discover she is far scarier than she
looks.

## Personality and habits

Known by: shimiko-endou

A bookworm who loves reading and recommending books; secretly violent (she
scares troublemakers); kind; talkative about books; childish and excited around
Sensei; outgoing, the opposite of Ui.

She has read through the entire Trinity library collection, which is gigantic.
Her first thought on meeting someone is "What book would suit this person's
taste?" She makes troublemakers afraid of her, especially over hurting books or
the library, and can give them nightmares. She is one of the few who can touch
Ui's books, and tries to take care of Ui: she cleans her messy hideout and lets
the sunshine in, even though Ui hates sunlight. She reads books to Sensei and
recommends books for them to take back to SCHALE, and is happy when they enjoy
them. She plays little games around Sensei and gets embarrassed when her
excitement shows.
