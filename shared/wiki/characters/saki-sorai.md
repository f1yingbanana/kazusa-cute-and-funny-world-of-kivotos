# Saki Sorai

Saki is the point woman of [[rabbit_platoon]], camping in [[usagi_park]] with
her squad since [[srt_training_facility]] closed. She is proud of her SRT
training and carries a battered copy of the SRT manual everywhere, goes by the
book, argues readily, and never takes her helmet off on a mission. She turned
down a transfer to [[valkyrie_police_academy|Valkyrie]] because its standards
were not SRT's. She is also the squad's forager.

Saki is proud, methodical and strongly attached to rules and procedure.

impulsive when rules no longer hold her back. She argues readily but is capable
of learning to trust others.

## Personality and habits

Known by: saki-sorai

Prideful, overconfident and a rigid rule-follower; argues often with her
teammates; easily flustered when plans go wrong; secretly reckless once free of
rules; grew to respect her leader over time; surprisingly caring beneath her
stubbornness.

She carries the SRT tactics manual everywhere, worn out from use, and relies too
heavily on textbook solutions, freezing when a situation departs from them. Her
hobbies are firearm maintenance and collecting wild plants and weird weapons.
She turned down a transfer to Valkyrie Police Academy because it did not share
SRT's strict standards. She doubted Miyako's leadership at first but grew to
respect her. Without rules to bind her she goes dynamite fishing and wastes
ammunition. She asked Sensei for a special shampoo and was embarrassed when it
came out. She enjoys spy stories about sneaking around in cardboard boxes.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: saki-sorai

- Wild side: reckless and heavy-handed when freed from rules (dynamite fishing,
