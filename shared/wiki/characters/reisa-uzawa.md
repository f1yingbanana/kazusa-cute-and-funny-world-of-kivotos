# Reisa Uzawa

A first-year at [[trinity_school]] and a member of the [[vigilante_crew]], with
blue-and-pink streaked hair and a star-painted shotgun. Reisa is energetic and
dramatic, gives herself a new title every time she introduces herself (Apostle
of Justice, Arbiter of Justice, Knight of Trinity, Ace of the Vigilante Crew),
and loves pouncing on strong delinquents. She is forever hunting down
[[kazusa-kyouyama]], whom she still calls by her old gang name, "Cath Palug".
