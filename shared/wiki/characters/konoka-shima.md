# Konoka Shima

Konoka is the deputy director of the [[valkyrie_public_peace]], second to
[[kanna-ogata]], whom she calls "Sis" half as a joke. She wears her
[[valkyrie_police]] uniform as loosely as she can get away with, takes as many
breaks as she can get away with, and passes work to whoever is nearest. When she
does get started she is quick and effective, until it gets troublesome.
