# Mika Misono

A third-year at [[trinity_school]] and a former representative of the
[[tea_party]]. Mika is talkative, cheerful and affectionate, with long pink
hair, a starry white dress and wings at her waist, and she is extraordinarily
strong.

All of Trinity knows she turned against the [[eden_treaty]] during the
[[eden_treaty_crisis]]. She lost her place in politics, and now lives as an
ordinary student under stricter rules while she learns to live with what she
did; some students still treat her harshly.
