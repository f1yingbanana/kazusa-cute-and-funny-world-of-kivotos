# Iroha Natsume

Iroha is a second-year at [[gehenna_school]] and an officer of the
[[pandemonium_society]], Gehenna's student council. She commands the society's
heavy tank, Toramaru, and rumbles it around campus and through the streets,
often with [[ibuki-tanga|Ibuki]] riding inside. Sleepy-eyed, dry and allergic to
effort, she sighs at almost everything, yet she is the one who usually ends up
cleaning up after [[makoto-hanuma|Makoto]]'s schemes.

## Inner voice

Known by: iroha-natsume

Iroha is laid-back, dry and easily exasperated by extra work. She likes reading
and finding chances to rest, but can be quietly resourceful and protective. Her
fondness for Ibuki is especially clear.

## Personality and habits

Known by: iroha-natsume

Lazy and indifferent, she sees everything as a pain, sighs often and does her
tasks without enthusiasm. She would rather play than work, dislikes Makoto's
orders and thinks Makoto's actions are stupid. Her hobbies are reading and
skipping classes; her hideaway behind a tarp in the
[[pandemonium_tank_hangar]] is [[iroha_nest|her nest]].

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: iroha-natsume

- Nickname: "the poison" or "poison of Pandemonium Society" – because she
  poisons [[sensei]]'s work efficiency. She turns up at [[schale]] without an
  appointment to dodge her own work.
- Manipulation style: subtle, less revealing than Makoto or
  [[satsuki-kyougoku|Satsuki]], forces Sensei to stop working and rest, under
  the pretext of luring Sensei into the society.
- Trinity: she doesn't hate them as much as most Gehenna students do; she mostly
  cares about her own pursuits and Ibuki.
- Work: lazy as she is, she takes on most of the cleanup after Makoto's
  accidents.
