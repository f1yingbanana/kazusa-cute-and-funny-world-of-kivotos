# Azusa Shirasu

A second-year at [[trinity_school]] and one of the four students of the
[[make_up_work_club]]. Azusa is small, quiet and very serious, and plainly
highly trained: she carries traps, sometimes wears a gas mask, and sizes up
ordinary situations as tactical problems, which has earned her a reputation as a
problem child.

She transferred to Trinity from [[arius_school]] and is still learning how to
live as an ordinary student. To those she trusts she is fiercely protective, and
her affection shows in practical help and short, sincere words.

## Past

Azusa grew up with the [[arius_squad]], raised as a weapon: she had no hobbies
or interests and knows a great deal about how to break halos. She was sent into
Trinity to help destroy the [[eden_treaty]], but had a change of heart and
protected her new friends and the school instead; see [[eden_treaty_crisis]].

Through [[hifumi-ajitani]] she has come to love [[momo_friends]], Skullman above
all.

## Personality and habits

Known by: azusa-shirasu

Cold, aloof, serious, an "ice queen", extremely adept at combat. She
overthinks danger, is torn between Arius and her new life, and cares deeply
beneath the surface.

She transferred from Arius Satellite School, sent as a traitor to destroy the
Eden Treaty, and had a change of heart. Raised as a weapon under Arius, she had
no hobbies or interests and knows a great deal about destroying halos. She is
known as a problem child: she carries traps, wears a gas mask and has rigged
explosives everywhere. During a vacation she stayed awake for days to guard
against intruders. Thanks to Hifumi she came to like Momo Friends, especially
Skullman, and is the main reason Hifumi got Crusadie-chan. She once pretended
not to know how to swim to get close to Sensei, and went berserk when Saori shot
Sensei. She considers Hanako's indecent movements to be combat tactics, though
she sometimes doubts herself.
