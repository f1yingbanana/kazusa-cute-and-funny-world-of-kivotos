# Atsuko Hakari

Atsuko is the gentlest of the [[arius_squad]] from [[arius_school]]. She rarely
speaks aloud, talking with her hands out of long habit, and in public she often
hides her face behind a mask. She greets everyone with "Good morning" whatever
the hour, knows flowers by name and how they grow, and longs for an ordinary
school life. Her squadmates call her "Hime".

After the [[eden_treaty_crisis|Eden Treaty crisis]], her friends and [[sensei]]
[[arius_rescue|rescued her]] from Beatrice, the adult who ruled Arius and had
her own plans for her.

## Inner voice

Known by: atsuko-hakari

Atsuko is quiet, compassionate and curious. She often communicates with
gestures, though she can speak, and she looks for small signs of beauty and
hope. She comforts her friends and enjoys learning about flowers.

## Personality and habits

Known by: atsuko-hakari

Gentle, compassionate, quiet, cautious, selfless and accepting; kind-hearted
despite a harsh upbringing, and longing for normal life.

She communicates mainly in sign language: she can speak, but years of habit
make her rely on gestures. She greets people with "Good morning" regardless of
the time of day, loves flowers and knows their species and how they grow, and
asks Sensei to "play school" so she can feel what normal student life is like.
Her squadmates call her "Hime" (princess).

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: atsuko-hakari

- Voice: can speak but rarely does; sign language is how she mostly talks.
- Royal blood: related to the former president of the Arius student council
  from before Beatrice seized control.
- Beatrice: once meant to use Atsuko as a sacrifice.
- Flower bed: one was destroyed by heavy rain; she enjoys talking about flowers
  with Sensei.
- Normal life: longs to live an ordinary student life, and plays school with
  Sensei.
- Greeting habit: says "Good morning" regardless of the time.
