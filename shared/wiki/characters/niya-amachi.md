# Niya Amachi

Niya is a third-year at [[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy]] and
president of the [[yin_yang_club]]. On paper hers is one of the most important
posts in the academy: keeping the peace across a district built on the uneasy
coexistence of dozens of clubs and factions. In practice she leaves most of the
work to her vice president, [[kaho-kuwakami|Kaho]], dotes on underclassmen, and
spends the rest of her time engineering situations she finds entertaining. She
laughs "Nyahaha!", hides behind a fan at nearly every moment, and apologizes in
a way nobody believes.

## Profile

- **Year:** 3rd year
- **Age:** 17
- **Height:** 166 cm
- **Birthday:** January 1
- **Interests:** Doting on underclassmen, scheming
- **Looks:** Niya has short, scruffy dark hair that fades into a bluish-gray
  tone, with straight-cut bangs. She wears a subtle red makeup accent around
  her eyes. Two horns sit on her head, orange at the base and fading to red at
  the tips, with her left horn broken halfway. She has gold O-shaped earrings
  and a mole beneath her left eye.
- **Halo:** Her halo is a light white consisting of a central circle surrounded
  by four concentric circles with symmetrical cross-like extensions and four
  spikes emanating from the gaps between them, all set close together.
- **Outfit:** She wears a white side-less kimono top with detached sleeves
  decorated with Japanese-style line patterns, small circles, and loose yellow
  paint-like markings. The right side of the kimono ends at the waist while the
  left covers half of her black pleated skirt, which has a yellow line running
  along its edges. She wears a white high thigh-high on her right leg and a
  matching white sock on her left, along with traditional Japanese okobo.
- **Weapon:** Niya carries a Nambu Type 14 pistol named Forked Tongue, finished
  in two-tone metallic white with black accents. The rear post sight and
  trigger are painted dark pink. The Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy insignia is
  imprinted on the black polymer grip. Only a handful of people have ever seen
  her actually fire it. She also carries a fan that she deploys at moments of
  particular composure, which is most moments.

## Inner voice

Known by: niya-amachi

Niya is laid-back, perceptive and mischievous. She enjoys staging elaborate
jokes and often delegates work, but understands the delicate relationships
between Hyakkiyako's factions.

## Outlook

Known by: niya-amachi

- She is not, however, negligent. Niya sees everything happening around her with
  unsettling clarity, understands exactly which problems could be solved and
  how, and has a working knowledge of Hyakkiyako's political landscape that most
  people twice her age would struggle to match. Her not intervening is
  deliberate. The Yin-Yang Club's authority rests entirely on the willingness of
  others to grant it, and Niya decided long ago that the safest way to preserve
  that authority is never to appear to use it. She watches, nudges things
  sideways when nobody is looking, and lets the results be put down to
  coincidence. Whether any of this is responsible leadership is a question she
  deflects with a Nyahaha and a fan.
