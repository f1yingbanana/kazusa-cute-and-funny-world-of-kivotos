# Sumire Otohana

Sumire leads the [[gym_training_department]] at [[millennium_school]].
Energetic, disciplined and sincere, she believes physical strength solves
nearly any problem and sees every activity as a chance to get stronger. Her
workout plans are famously elaborate, and she pushes others, and herself,
past their limits without noticing. She also loves gardening, and turns shy
the moment she realizes people are staring at her squatting in the middle of
a mall.

She has very long black hair in a ponytail with a purple scrunchie, bright
purple eyes, a violet flower pin, and a shiny white sports jacket.
