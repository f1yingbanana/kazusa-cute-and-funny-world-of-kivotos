# Hiyori Tsuchinaga

Hiyori is the sniper of the [[arius_squad]] from [[arius_school]]. She lugs a
backpack of survival gear nearly twice her size, reads every magazine she can
get her hands on, and cries easily and loudly when things go wrong, which she
always expects them to. A hard upbringing left her anxious about what any
kindness might cost her.

## Personality and habits

Known by: hiyori-tsuchinaga

Cowardly, gloomy, petulant, melancholic and self-deprecating, with low
self-esteem and a negative outlook; cries easily; afraid of pain and suffering,
her own and others'.

She ugly-cries when she is scared or sad or something bad happens. She loves
magazines and buys them with what little money she has. She loves food but is
wary when it is given for free, believing she doesn't deserve it. She
overthinks the consequences of good things, thinks she shouldn't be happy, and
believes gifts will lead to torture. She has hooked into utility poles to charge
her phone, and carries a massive backpack of survival gear almost twice her
size.
