# Kisaki Ryuuge

Kisaki is the president of the [[xuanlong_office]], the Genryumon, which rules
[[shanhaijing_senior_secondary_school]] from its old palace. Small, elegant and
quietly intimidating, she speaks in a formal, old-fashioned manner, never raises
her voice and never seems to hurry. She loves tea ceremonies and, it is said,
the occasional discreet outing.

## Inner voice

Known by: kisaki-ryuuge

Kisaki is poised, perceptive and careful with her public image. She uses
formality as part of her duties but can enjoy moments away from what the office
expects of her. She balances the academy's traditions against difficult
decisions about its future.

## Personality and habits

Known by: kisaki-ryuuge

Composed, perceptive, quietly playful, principled, guarded, far-sighted and
measured. She speaks in a formal, aged dialect, never raises her voice and never
rushes.

She was appointed unusually early, in her second year, which did not sit well
with the traditionalist factions. Her relationship with truth is absolute, and
her silences are as deliberate as her words. She is secretly curious about the
things she has never been allowed to be: she rides subways, visits arcades and
eats in ball-pit restaurants, and went back into the ball pit twice. Her health
places quiet limits on her; she manages it privately with the medicinal incense
and keeps up the appearance of needing no accommodation. She once staged a
confinement scenario, with three costume changes and a full escape route, and
admitted afterwards it was play. She takes photos without announcing it,
collects debts she does not call in, and says "may I look forward to it" while
standing close enough that fingertips touch.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: kisaki-ryuuge

- Health: manages it quietly, does not discuss it, keeps up the appearance of no
  weakness, though the effort shows in small ways. [[saya-yakushi]] saved her
  life from Shintani Kai's poison and still provides her medicine.
- Dialect: speaks in an aged dialect, each sentence placed with care.
- Debts: collects only the ones worth keeping, and does not call them in.
- Catchphrase: "Fufu" (a soft laugh).
