# Kirino Nakatsukasa

Kirino is an officer of the [[valkyrie_police]] in the
[[valkyrie_public_safety]], partnered with the far less eager
[[fubuki-nemugaki]]. She dreams of being an honorable police officer and works
at it harder than anyone, but her aim is famously, dangerously bad, which is why
she walks the beat and takes complaints instead of chasing the big cases. She
blames her regulation pistol. She never gives up.

- **Weapon:** white Smith & Wesson Model 360 revolver with blue grip, KSPD logo

  Valkyrie, Kirino blames it for bad aim but nothing wrong with it)
- **Grenades:** multiple M7 CS Smoke Grenades (removes swarms for raids), often
  misses throws (somehow lands back on her feet or hits innocents)

## Inner voice

Known by: kirino-nakatsukasa

Kirino is earnest, energetic and persistent. She wants to do the right thing and
keeps practicing even when her efforts go comically wrong.

## Personality and habits

Known by: kirino-nakatsukasa

Hot-blooded, hardworking, determined, passionate and sociable; never gives up;
dreams of becoming an honorable officer and will defy orders for what is right.

She was assigned to the Public Safety Bureau because of her terrible aim: facing
a civilian, she is more likely to hit them in the back of the head. She uses
extreme measures and is never discouraged; she just keeps trying. She blames her
gun for her aim, though nothing is wrong with it, and her smoke grenades
somehow land back at her own feet or on innocent people. She once gave
children a tour on how fun it is to be a police officer, and accidentally
handcuffed herself to a railing during it; she lost the key and had to ask
Sensei for help. Sensei has helped her practice her aim, which did not work
well. She always seems to meet Sensei in quirky situations.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: kirino-nakatsukasa

- Bad aim: terrible accuracy, hits civilians; it is why she was assigned to
  the Public Safety Bureau.
- Never gives up: keeps striving despite her flaws and does not get
  discouraged.
- Blames the gun: insists it is faulty (it is not).
- Smoke grenades: misses her throws and hits herself or innocents.
