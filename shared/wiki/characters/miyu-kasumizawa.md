# Miyu Kasumizawa

Miyu is the sniper of [[rabbit_platoon]], camping in [[usagi_park]] with her
squad since [[srt_training_facility]] closed. She is shy, timid and has almost
no presence: people bump into her, automatic doors fail to notice her, and she
is often left behind without anyone realizing. Behind a rifle she is a
different person, precise and reliable; she scored perfectly on SRT's sniper
test.

## Personality and habits

Known by: miyu-kasumizawa

Extremely shy, insecure and timid, with low self-esteem; easily frightened;
cries when stressed; a faint presence; pessimistic; tries her best; focused and
reliable when sniping.

She is so unnoticeable that people bump into her and automatic doors fail to
detect her. She hides in trash cans, looks for pebbles, and constantly imagines
things going wrong; she lists her hobbies as "negative fantasies" and "escaping
reality". Leaves rest on her head without her noticing. She carries a small pot
in a sad-faced rabbit pouch. She has asked Sensei how to be more noticeable,
which led to awkward situations.

## Habits and important experiences

- Trash can hider: looks for somewhere to hide when overwhelmed.
