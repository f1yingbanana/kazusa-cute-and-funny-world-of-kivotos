# Midori Saiba

Midori is a first-year at [[millennium_school]] and the illustrator of the
[[game_development_department]], drawing its sketches and retro-style sprites.
She is the twin of [[momoi-saiba|Momoi]] and shares her love of games, but
where Momoi is loud, Midori is quiet, cautious and a little pessimistic, until
something excites or annoys her and she bursts out. She likes to seem more
grown-up than she feels.

She looks much like her sister: shoulder-length strawberry blonde hair with
small pigtails tied in blue ribbons, lime eyes, a cat-eared headset in neon
green, and a white and green hoodie.
