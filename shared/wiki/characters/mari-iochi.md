# Mari Iochi

A first-year at [[trinity_school]] and a Sister of the [[sisterhood]], with
fox-like ears, long orange hair under a black veil and a soft, constant smile.
Mari is humble, gentle and sincere, and students come to her at
[[trinity_sisterhood]] to confess or simply to talk through their worries. She
tries to help anyone who needs her, often past her own limits.

prayer and thinking.
