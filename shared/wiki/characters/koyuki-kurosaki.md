# Koyuki Kurosaki

Koyuki is a first-year member of [[seminar]], [[millennium_school]]'s student
council, and a natural codebreaker who cracks encryption by what she calls
guessing. She is also arguably the most irresponsible student in Kivotos: her
pranks and schemes land her in the [[millennium_self_reflection_room]] so
often that she has made it her own. Cheerful and impulsive, she laughs
"Nihahaha!" and never admits she was wrong.

She is small, with pink twintails, sparkling magenta eyes, Seminar's white
oversized overcoat, and a pink machine gun slung over her shoulder.

## Personality and habits

Known by: koyuki-kurosaki

Irresponsible and mischievous, never owns a mistake and thinks she is always
right; harmless and friendly, if obnoxious to some.

She has a natural talent for deciphering codes, which she treats as funny
pranks, and cracked [[sensei]]'s codes by "guessing". Her nickname is "White
Rabbit", and her laugh is "Nihahahaha!" or "Niha!". She is sent to the
Self-Reflection Room often; she has decorated it and sneaks games inside. She
is on friendly terms with [[cleaning_and_clearing|C&C]] and calls them
"Senpai". She once felt guilt over adult magazines. She thinks of Sensei as her best friend.
