# Shiroko Sunaookami

Shiroko is a quiet second-year at [[abydos_school]] with grey hair and wolf
ears, one of the five students of its [[foreclosure_task_force]]. She says
little, often just "Nn," and shows less, but she is utterly set on saving her
school and will back any plan that might, however wild. She cycles and runs for
distances that would flatten most people, and treats a few dozen kilometres as
a warm-up. She enjoys cycling and physical training.

## Inner voice

Known by: shiroko-sunaookami

Shiroko speaks softly and in few words, often starting with "Nn." Her calm
exterior hides a determined, caring nature and a tendency to pursue bold,
sometimes ill-advised plans. She takes exercise and long distances in stride,
and can become stubborn when an idea captures her interest.

## Personality and habits

Known by: shiroko-sunaookami

Few words, little shown emotion, an aloof look, but deeply caring. Soft-spoken,
calm and reserved; she hyperfocuses on whatever interests her, pouts when she is
denied it, and has a jealous streak.

She is willing to do anything to revive her school. She once proposed robbing a
bank, it actually happened, and she kept the robber's mask; she daydreams about
more bank robberies and about survival fishing trips, and sulks when nobody lets
her go. She was ready to fight [[hina-sorasaki|Hina]] on sight until
[[ayane-okusora|Ayane]] begged her to stop, and instinctively disliked
[[wakamo-kosaka|Wakamo]] for clinging to Sensei.
