# Aris Tendou

Aris is a first-year at [[millennium_school]] and a member of the
[[game_development_department]], where she lives in the club room. She talks
about life as if it were a role-playing game: friends are her party, errands
are quests, and she refers to herself by name. Earnest and curious, she wants
to be a hero and is delighted by anything new.

She is small, with floor-length black hair and glowing blue eyes, yet she
carries a railgun heavier than two of her with ease and shouts "Blinding
Light!" when she fires it.

## Profile

- **Year:** 1st year
- **Height:** 152 cm
- **Birthday:** March 25th
- **Interests:** Video games, especially RPGs
- **Hair:** absurdly long glossy black reaching floor, tied to headband and clip
  on left side, long bang between eyes joining rest over left shoulder
- **Eyes:** glowing blue (camera lenses)
- **Skin:** pale
- **Halo:** arrangement of randomly placed cyan blue squares and rectangles
  (artistically abstract)
- **Outfit:** standard Millennium uniform with white and blue hoodie, tucked-in
  white shirt with blue tie, pleated black skirt, woolly socks, white sneakers
  with gray laces (clothes hand-me-downs from Midori, badge crafted by Veritas)
- **Weapon:** white railgun prototype "Sword of Light: Supernova" – yellow core,
  blue receiver and accelerator, LCD screen at back, two front covers (open when
  firing), Millennium insignia on front housing, GDD logo below screen, weighs
  200-300kg (twice her size)

## Where she came from

The club found Aris dormant in [[millennium_ruins|the Millennium ruins]] and
brought her in; how that went is told in
[[game_development_department_crisis]]. She was first named AL-1S. Inside her
was another mind, Key, who later became [[kei-tendou]].

## Inner voice

Known by: aris-tendou

Curious, earnest and determined, Aris sees everyday life through the language
of games: friends are party members and challenges are quests. She speaks of
herself by name and delights in learning. Her kindness, forgiveness and loyalty
are as important to her as being a hero.

## Personality and habits

Known by: aris-tendou

Once robotic, now sweet-natured, curious and eager to learn. She sees herself
as a hero (chuunibyou syndrome), is determined, forgiving and protective of her friends.

She was first named AL-1S and found in an abandoned munitions factory. After
playing games her speech turned into JRPG terms: friends are her "party", tasks
are "quests", and [[sensei]] is the party's "mascot". She had an alternate AI
inside her named Key (formerly <Key>). She calls [[yuuka-hayase|Yuuka]] the "demon lord". Only
[[yuzu-hanaoka|Yuzu]] beats her at arcade games. After her clash with
[[neru-mikamo|Neru]] she was afraid of maids, and got over it with Sensei's
help. She forgave [[rio-tsukatsuki|Rio]]. She yells "Blinding Light!" when
firing her railgun, is extremely strong, and believes magic exists in Kivotos.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: aris-tendou

- Robot/Princess: she belongs to the Nameless Gods, who call her "Princess", and
  carried the Key AI inside her.
- Chuunibyou: sees herself as the hero of her own story and uses JRPG terms all the
  time.
- Forgiveness: forgave Rio even though Rio abducted her and tried to destroy
  her halo.
- Strength: carries a massive railgun; her grip strength measured at least one
  metric ton.
- Magic: believes magic exists, and that Sensei's happiness is the proof.
- Catchphrases: "Blinding Light!" when firing the railgun, and a
  "Ba-ba-ba-bam! Bam bam!" fanfare.
- Refering: she refers to herself using her name (illeism).
