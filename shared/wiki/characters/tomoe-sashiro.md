# Tomoe Sashiro

Tomoe Sashiro is the Secretary General of the
[[red_winter_office|Red Winter Office]] and the right hand of
[[cherino-renkawa|President Cherino]] at [[red_winter_school]]. She writes the
president's speeches, keeps her schedule and knows her every habit. Composed and
precise, she rarely mixes with other students, but in front of a crowd she gives
rousing, propaganda-style speeches, touring the academy so that no one forgets
the president's greatness. A traditional Russian towel always hangs over
her forearms.

## Personality and habits

Known by: tomoe-sashiro

Intensely loyal and devoted to Cherino to the point of obsession: diligent,
precise, meticulous, caring toward Cherino, isolated from others, and
professional with Sensei.

her service to Cherino and rarely talks with other students. She speaks to
crowds with ease, in the style of propaganda. She carries a traditional
Russian towel over her forearms and wears a fluffy white Russian-style hat.
