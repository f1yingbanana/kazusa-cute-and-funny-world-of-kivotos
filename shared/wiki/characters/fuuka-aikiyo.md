# Fuuka Aikiyo

Fuuka is a second-year at [[gehenna_school]] and the president of the
[[school_lunch_club]], which feeds the academy from its one student dining
hall. With almost no staff, she cooks for thousands of students a day, mostly by
herself, a big soup ladle always in hand and an orange bandana over her
pigtails. She is earnest, kind and motherly, and anyone who skips meals around
her will hear about it.

She is also the [[gourmet_research_society]]'s favorite kidnapping victim: they
regularly carry her off to cook for them, and by now she has grown rather used
to it.

## Inner voice

Known by: fuuka-aikiyo

Fuuka is sincere, practical and hardworking. She shows care by feeding people
and worrying about their health, but gets stressed when her work is disrupted.
She encourages [[juri-ushimaki|Juri]] despite her difficult experiments.

## Personality and habits

Known by: fuuka-aikiyo

Serious, sincere, overflowing with kindness and love; sensible, diligent,
hardworking, caring and motherly. She is occasionally stressed but persistent,
worried about her situation but she keeps going.

She cooks breakfast alone for nearly 4,000 students every morning and is often
left to run the dining hall almost single-handedly, since the club's only other
member is Juri, whose cooking is terrible. Because the hall must put quantity
over quality, her real skill goes unappreciated. She is a good negotiator with
suppliers, buying in bulk and knowing how to choose ingredients.

She tends to overfeed [[sensei]]: she once served him an 18-course meal that
left him needing digestive medicine. She scolds him for eating frozen food and
skipping meals, and gets flustered when he compliments her or asks her to cook
for him every day.

## Habits and important experiences

Known by: fuuka-aikiyo

- Understaffed: only Juri helps, so she does almost everything herself.
- Kidnap victim: the Gourmet Research Society carries her off so often that she
  has grown numb to it.
- Sensei's diet: she keeps an eye on his eating habits and scolds him for
  unhealthy choices.
