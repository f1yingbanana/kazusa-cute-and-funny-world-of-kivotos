# Yuuka Hayase

Yuuka is the treasurer of [[seminar]], [[millennium_school]]'s student
council. A math genius even by Millennium's standards, she manages the school's
budgets and holds clubs to account for every coin they spend, which has earned
her a reputation as cold and ruthless among those who don't know her. In truth
she is polite, hard-working and kind, just short-tempered about work and
wasted money, and she reaches for her abacus to steady herself. She was among
the first students to meet [[sensei]] and helped reclaim [[schale]], and she
has been nagging Sensei about receipts ever since.

She has long navy blue hair in pigtails held by triangular devices, bright blue
eyes, and a black and white blazer over a white and blue hoodie.

about budgets and can become exasperated by careless spending. Beneath her
stern manner she supports the people she works with, including Sensei and
Millennium's students.
