# Rabu Komakaze

Rabu is the captain of the [[splash_helmet_gang]], one of the [[helmet_gang]]
crews, and a dropout from [[trinity_school]]. She is rough-spoken and a fearsome
fighter who can beat a crowd of thugs without firing a shot, usually by swinging
her shotgun like a bat. She looks after her younger gang members, and to people
she respects she is surprisingly polite.

- **Occupation:** Splash Helmet Gang captain
- **Hair:** long, striking crimson (maroon) hair, reaching down to her hips or

- **Eyes:** bright, matching crimson red
- **Teeth:** sharp shark-like teeth

- **Outfit:** a white sailor shirt with a gray collar and a yellow ribbon; a

  skirt; black combat shoes; a black tactical belt with a magazine pouch and
  grenade holsters; a distinctive Davy's gray K6-3, Altyn, or RYS-T helmet with
  a white Helmet Gang mark
- **Weapon:** a black Mossberg 590M pump-action shotgun named "Pinch Hitter",
  with white tape wrapped around the stock and grip, and a Trijicon ACOG sight;
  also carries smoke, stun, and high-explosive grenades on her belt

## Inner voice

Known by: rabu-komakaze

Rabu is rough-spoken, proud and easily embarrassed, but deeply protective of her
gang. She can be polite to people she respects and makes quiet sacrifices for
her friends.

## Personality and habits

Known by: rabu-komakaze

Rough and gruff on the outside, but deeply protective of and caring towards her
gang members; secretly still an ordinary high school girl at heart; easily
flustered and embarrassed; surprisingly polite to those she respects.

She is extremely powerful in combat and can defeat multiple enemies without
firing, often using her shotgun as a bat. She has a severe lack of grip
strength and cannot open twist-off bottle caps, which she hides from her gang.
She is prone to saying "Cram it!" when angry. When funds are low she refuses to
use live ammunition and beats thugs with her shotgun or a sock-covered gun
instead. She watches out for her gang's well-being, even skipping parties so
they can have fun. She uses powdered walnut shells to distress helmets after
repainting them. She gets flustered when teased about dating Sensei.
