## Personality and habits

Known by: shigure-mayoi

Kind-hearted, laid-back and calm, with a bright smile; responsible, sometimes.
She gets upset when her vodka is taken away.

She was suspended for mixing vodka into the ration kompot, which is rumoured to
have been a deliberate commotion to help her childhood friend Nodoka. Her hobby
is homemade drinks; her kompot is so sweet it makes your mouth water, and she
is forceful about sharing her drinks, especially the alcoholic ones. She has
saved Sensei from frostbite in the snowy mountains twice. She claims to have
killed and skinned dozens of bears, with a little smile, as if it were
perfectly normal. She cooks for Sensei but puts alcohol in the food, and gets
scolded for it.
