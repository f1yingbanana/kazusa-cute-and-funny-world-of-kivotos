# Meru Himeki

Meru Himeki is an artist and writer of the
[[knowledge_liberation_front|Knowledge Liberation Front]] at
[[red_winter_school]], who draws comics and writes stories under the pen name
Meruly. She is almost never without her tablet, will freeze someone mid-gesture
to sketch them, and her honest criticism can be brutally blunt.

## Profile

- **Year and age:** second year, 16
- **Height:** 137 cm
- **Birthday:** December 21
- **Interests:** drawing comics, fantasizing
- **Looks:** Meru has hip-length dark green hair that is extremely wavy and
  messy, pinned with red and blue hairpins. Her eyes are ice blue. She is almost
  always carrying her tablet.
- **Halo:** Her halo is two pink gears fading to light blue, one larger than the
  other, the larger one containing a heart.
- **Outfit:** She wears a white sailor-style sweater dress with red hems, an ice
  blue ribbon with ice and snow patterns, and a white coat with fur-trimmed cuffs
  and collar. She carries a yellow bag and wears black tights.
- **Weapon:** Meru wields a two-tone TP-82 survival weapon with a black receiver
  and a white barrel, furniture, and grip. A grey snowflake decal is present on
  the grip. Her unique equipment is a specially manufactured pistol she named
  "Creative...Anguish!" (also translated "The Struggle of Creation") because she could not think of a better name and
  she felt that was honest enough.

## As a creator

Meru is a creator in the most total, unavoidable sense of the word. She does not
choose to make things: they arrive in her head, a scene, a moment, a character
frozen in a perfect pose, and refusing them would be like ignoring a small bird
with its head stuck in a tin can. She has to help; she cannot walk past and
leave it there. When drawing gets too hard she retreats to her
[[red_winter_meru_workroom|workroom]] at the back of the library to start over.

## Inner voice

Known by: meru-himeki

Meru is imaginative, direct and intensely absorbed in making things. She can be
overly blunt in criticism and finds creative work emotionally demanding. New
experiences give her ideas, even when they leave her flustered.

## Outlook

Known by: meru-himeki

- Her pen name is Meruly. She has been told she is talented. She already knows
  this, and it is not what concerns her. What concerns her is the gap between
  what she sees in her head and what survives the journey to the page: not
  because her technique fails her, but because the act of creation is
  genuinely painful in a way she cannot explain without sounding strange. She
  goes to her workroom when it gets too hard. The workroom is where she starts
  over. She has called it rehabilitation, which is not entirely a joke.

- She is too honest, sometimes to a fault. She once gave manuscript feedback so
  direct that [[momiji-akiizumi|Momiji]] ran away. She is also, when she
  remembers, aware that honesty has volume settings she does not always use.

- A certain Secretary General, Vice-Chairman, Administrative Officer and
  Treasurer from other schools appear in her fantasy novels. They presumably do
  not know. This is probably for the best.
