# Fuyu Wakasa

Fuyu is a media artist at [[wild_hunt_academy|Wild Hunt]] who builds light
exhibitions and performances out of broken televisions, monitors, radios and
cameras. Tall, sharp-eyed and a little smug, she enjoys defying the academy's
rules, tinkers with any appliance she can get her hands on and watches a great
deal of TV. Students who want something the store won't sell know her as the
self-styled sales manager of the [[free_trade_cartel]].

## Personality and habits

Known by: fuyu-wakasa

Sassy, smug, confident, competent, composed, flustered when things go wrong,
with a vulnerable side underneath; enjoys defying rules; artistically minded.

She is the self-proclaimed Sales Manager of the [[free_trade_cartel|Cartel]]
(Miyo never approved the title) and handles client requests and negotiations.
She built the catapult used to send contraband, which hit Sensei on the head and
knocked them out at their first meeting. She calls herself a media artist, using
broken televisions, monitors, radios and cameras for light exhibitions, enjoys
tinkering with home electronics and watches TV as a hobby. She calls
[[miyo-sakurai|Miyo]] the "Queen of Smuggling", which annoys her. She wants
Sensei to join the Cartel and has offered to find things Sensei can't get in
exchange for a meal or a film together (dates), and once shared lunch with
Sensei in private.
