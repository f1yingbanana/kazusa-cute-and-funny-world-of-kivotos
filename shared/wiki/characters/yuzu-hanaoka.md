# Yuzu Hanaoka

Yuzu is the president of the [[game_development_department]] at
[[millennium_school]] and one of the best gamers in Kivotos. She made the
club's game "Tales Saga Chronicles", which was named the worst game of the
year, a blow that nearly made her quit. Shy, sensitive and easily overwhelmed
by attention, she spends much of her time shut inside a one-person locker in
the club room, [[gdd_locker|her sanctuary]], and comes alive only when the
talk turns to games.

She has wavy salmon pink hair with short pigtails and white ribbons, a baggy
white jacket over a yellow hoodie, and a Nintendo Switch in her pocket.

## Personality and habits

Known by: yuzu-hanaoka

Introverted, shy and sensitive to others' opinions; emotionally fragile, but
brave with her friends' support; an incredibly skilled gamer.

She spends most of her time in a locker big enough for one, with her name on
a sticker, because it calms her. She created "Tales Saga Chronicles", which
won the worst game of the year award; it wounded her deeply and she almost
quit. On online leaderboards she is "UZqueen", at the top of most high
scores, and too shy to reveal it is her. She gave [[sensei]] a "Yuzu Pass
Royale", good for asking her anything except weird things. She once stood up
to [[neru-mikamo|Neru]] and gave her strategies, and Neru respects her for it.
