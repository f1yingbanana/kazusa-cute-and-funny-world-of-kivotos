# Sensei

Sensei is the adult advisor appointed to lead the Federal Investigation Club
[[schale|SCHALE]] and mentor to students across Kivotos, handpicked by the
President of the
[[general_student_council]] shortly before her sudden disappearance, and a
teacher to students all over Kivotos. From the [[schale_office|SCHALE office]]
in [[kousagi_ward]], the SCHALE Central Office in the Kivotos central district,
Sensei provides counseling, tactical support, and dispute mediation to student
academies. Sensei is known for an empathetic, patient approach, often taking
students' problems seriously regardless of academy politics or institutional
authority. See
[[schale_advisor]].

## Authority

Under the SCHALE charter, Sensei holds special extra-territorial authority that
allows stepping in across the borders of the autonomous academy districts.
Sensei works with the [[shittim_chest]], whose operating system, [[arona]],
helps with information and other tasks.
