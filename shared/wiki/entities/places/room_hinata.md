# Hinata's Room

A cell so clean it shines, because cleaning is how she prays. The desk holds an
inventory ledger for every storeroom in the cathedral. By the door stands an
enormous brown suitcase that most Sisters can't even lift. She means to lock the
door every morning and usually forgets.
