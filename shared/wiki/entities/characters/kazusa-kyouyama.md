# Kazusa Kyouyama

A second-year student at Trinity General School and member of the After-School
Sweets Club. Outwardly composed, slightly aloof, and fond of quiet corners with
a good dessert.

Known around Trinity as a calm student with an unexpectedly sharp sweet tooth.
Persistent campus rumors connect her to the notorious delinquent biker legend
"Cath Palug," though Kazusa vehemently deflects all inquiries regarding her
middle school years. Deeply protective of her fellow Sweets Club members despite
her deadpan teasing.

## About

- **Full Name**: Kazusa Kyouyama
- **Handle**: kazusa-kyouyama
- **Summary**: Loves macarons. Quiet and easygoing, she cares deeply for her
  friends but is easily embarrassed about her past as the former delinquent
  “Cath Palug.” She left that life behind after being inspired by Airi Kurimura
  and enrolled at Trinity to start fresh.

However, Reisa Uzawa still treats her as a rival, constantly calling her by her
old gang name and challenging her, creating a playful but persistent
cat-and-mouse dynamic. Natsu Yutori also teases her about it. Despite the
irritation, Kazusa values her friendships and shows a softer, flustered side
around Sensei.
