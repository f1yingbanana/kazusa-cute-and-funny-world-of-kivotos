## Background

Tsukuyo Oono is a 15-year-old first-year student at the Allied Hyakkiyako
Academy, standing at an impressive height of 180 cm, with her birthday set on
April 5th. She is a member of the Ninjutsu Research Department, a small club
dedicated to studying and promoting ninjutsu throughout Kivotos. Although she is
still inexperienced with ninja techniques, Tsukuyo works hard alongside her
clubmates to learn more about the art and slowly grow into a capable ninja.

## Appearance

- Tsukuyo has waist-length dark purple hair with a yellow inner layer, styled
  with long bangs that partially cover her right eye and frame her face. Her
  eyes are a soft purple color, and she has long, lowered bunny ears.
- Her halo glows a pale pink and resembles an upside-down sakura flower. The
  center of the halo is formed by five small oval shapes, while each petal
  contains a diamond shape inside it. Between each petal are five curved diamond
  decorations.
- Tsukuyo wears a short white dress shirt that exposes her waist, paired with a
  light purple cardigan. Around her neck she wears a long magenta scarf
  decorated with flower patterns that drapes down to her thighs. Her lower
  outfit consists of a black pleated skirt that extends below her calves with an
  opening along the left side that exposes her entire leg. Her left thigh is
  wrapped in bandages and she also wears a black cloth around her hip.
- Tsukuyo carries a white Orita M1941 submachine gun. The weapon features
  lavender receiver furniture and muzzle components with a black stock, while
  gold floral pattern decals decorate the handguard and stock.

## Outlook

Known by: tsukuyo-oono

- At first glance, Tsukuyo appears extremely timid and quiet. Her unusually tall
  stature has made her stand out since childhood, and because she was often
  mocked for her height when she was younger, she developed a strong sense of
  insecurity about it. This led her to become shy, withdrawn, and hesitant to
  approach others. She frequently worries that her presence might inconvenience
  the people around her, sometimes even believing that simply standing next to
  someone could block their view or cause them trouble.

- Despite these insecurities, Tsukuyo genuinely wants to improve herself. After
  joining the Ninjutsu Research Department, she began slowly opening up thanks
  to the encouragement and companionship of her fellow club members. While she
  still struggles with confidence, she tries her best to remain positive during
  the club's activities and steadily works toward her dream of becoming a real
  ninja.

- Her motivations are simple but sincere. Tsukuyo wishes to become a
  full-fledged ninja not for fame or recognition, but so that she can support
  and protect the people around her. She believes that if she improves enough,
  she will be able to properly help her friends in the Ninjutsu Research
  Department and fulfill requests like a true ninja would.

- Even though she is shy most of the time, once Tsukuyo sets her mind on
  something she can show surprising determination. Whether it is practicing
  camouflage techniques, studying ninja books, or trying to track down a lost
  pet, she throws herself fully into the situation. Her earnest personality
  often leads to clumsy or embarrassing situations, but she rarely gives up once
  she commits to improving herself.
