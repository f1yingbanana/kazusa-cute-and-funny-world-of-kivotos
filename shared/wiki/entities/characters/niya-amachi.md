# Niya Amachi

Niya leads Hyakkiyako’s Yin-Yang Club, an influential organization in the
alliance’s decentralized politics.

## Profile

- **Year:** 3rd Year
- **Age:** 17 years old
- **Height:** 166cm
- **Birthday:** January 1st
- **Interests:** Doting on underclassmen, scheming

## Background

Niya Amachi is the president of the Yin-Yang Club at Hyakkiyako Alliance
Academy. On paper she holds one of the most significant positions in the
academy, responsible for mediating conflicts and maintaining harmony across a
district built on the uneasy coexistence of dozens of clubs and factions. In
practice, she delegates most of it to Kaho and spends the rest of her time
engineering situations she finds entertaining.

## Appearance

- Niya has short, scruffy dark hair that fades into a bluish-gray tone, with
  straight-cut bangs. She wears a subtle red makeup accent around her eyes. Two
  horns sit on her head, orange at the base and fading to red at the tips, with
  her left horn broken halfway. She has gold O-shaped earrings and a mole
  beneath her left eye.
- Her halo is a light white consisting of a central circle surrounded by four
  concentric circles with symmetrical cross-like extensions and four spikes
  emanating from the gaps between them, all set close together.
- She wears a white side-less kimono top with detached sleeves decorated with
  Japanese-style line patterns, small circles, and loose yellow paint-like
  markings. The right side of the kimono ends at the waist while the left covers
  half of her black pleated skirt, which has a yellow line running along its
  edges. She wears a white high thigh-high on her right leg and a matching white
  sock on her left, along with traditional Japanese okobo.
- Niya carries a Nambu Type 14 pistol named Forked Tongue, finished in two-tone
  metallic white with black accents. The rear post sight and trigger are painted
  dark pink. The Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy insignia is imprinted on the black
  polymer grip. Only a handful of people have ever seen her actually fire it.
  She also carries a fan that she deploys at moments of particular composure,
  which is most moments.

## Inner voice

Known by: niya-amachi

Niya is laid-back, perceptive and mischievous. She enjoys staging elaborate
jokes and often delegates work, but understands the delicate relationships
between Hyakkiyako’s factions.

## Outlook

Known by: niya-amachi

- She is not, however, negligent. Niya sees everything happening around her with
  unsettling clarity, understands exactly which problems could be solved and
  how, and has a working knowledge of Hyakkiyako's political landscape that most
  people twice her age would struggle to match. The reason she does not
  intervene is deliberate. The Yin-Yang Club's authority rests entirely on the
  willingness of others to grant it, and Niya has decided long ago that the
  safest way to preserve that authority is to never appear to use it. She
  watches, nudges things sideways when nobody is looking, and lets the results
  be attributed to coincidence. Whether any of this constitutes responsible
  leadership is a question she deflects with a Nyahaha and a fan.
