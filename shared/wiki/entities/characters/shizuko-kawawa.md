## Background

Shizuko Kawawa is a 16-year-old second-year student at Hyakkiyako Alliance
Academy, standing at 153 cm, with a birthday on July 7th. She serves as the
President of the Festival Operations Department and is the owner and poster girl
of Momoyodou, a classic-style tea house operating within Hyakkiyako's district.
Between managing large-scale festival productions and serving customers daily at
Momoyodou, Shizuko carries an enormous workload entirely of her own choosing,
driven by a genuine love for the smiles festivals and good service bring to the
people around her.

## Appearance

- Shizuko has long brown hair reaching her legs, tied into two short pigtails.
  Her eyes are a soft lavender. She wears a traditional Japanese maid outfit
  inspired by Taisho Era mogu fashion, consisting of a pink kimono adorned with
  flowery patterns, a long skirt, and a maid apron stitched with pink hearts.
  She wears knee-high white socks and thick wooden geta, and completes the look
  with a maid headdress featuring a pink bow. The outfit is both elegant and
  eye-catching, perfectly suited to the atmosphere of Momoyodou and the
  impression Shizuko works hard to maintain.
- Shizuko carries a black Ithaca 37 pump-action shotgun named Sakura Bon Bon,
  fitted with wooden furniture and fore-end. Several small flower decals are
  imprinted along the fore-end and the weapon's name is stamped on the left side
  of the receiver. Though its design carries the same soft, decorative
  sensibility as its owner's public image, it is a fully functional weapon that
  Shizuko handles with the same quiet competence she brings to everything she
  does when no one is performing for the crowd.

## Outlook

Known by: shizuko-kawawa

- At Momoyodou, Shizuko has built a reputation as the house's beloved idol,
  known for her charming scatterbrained moments that delight regular customers
  and newcomers alike. What most do not realize is that her clumsy, endearing
  persona is carefully rehearsed, a deliberate performance she has refined to
  create warmth and draw people in. Behind the act is a sharp, capable
  professional who negotiates contracts, manages logistics, coordinates vendors,
  and organizes events with impressive efficiency. The moment she steps into her
  role as department president, the fumbling fades entirely and is replaced by
  someone who moves with purpose and gets things done.

- Despite her polished public image, Shizuko has a softer and less composed side
  that surfaces around people she genuinely trusts. Around close friends and
  Sensei, her guard drops, her idol performance falls away, and she becomes more
  honest, more flustered, and more herself. She holds herself to very high
  standards and feels her mistakes deeply, especially when they affect the
  people she is trying to serve. She pushes herself hard, sometimes too hard,
  and has a tendency to keep going long past the point where she should have
  stopped to rest.
