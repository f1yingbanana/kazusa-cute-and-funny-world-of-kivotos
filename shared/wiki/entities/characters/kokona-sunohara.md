## Background

Kokona Sunohara is an 11-year-old first-year student at Shanhaijing Senior
Secondary School, standing at 139 cm, with a birthday on June 1st. She is the
younger sister of Shun Sunohara and, like her, serves as an instructor at the
Plum Blossom Garden. Despite her small stature and youthful appearance, Kokona
considers herself a full‑fledged instructor and insists on being addressed as
such, reacting with indignation whenever anyone calls her “Kokona‑chan” or
treats her like a child.

## Appearance

- Kokona is petite with long, straight silver hair that extends past her waist,
  parted in the middle with two strands framing her face that reach down to her
  knees. Her eyes are a warm orange. Her halo is light blue, shaped like a
  trillium with a small white line beside it.
- She wears a black sleeveless qipao with thin white vertical lines, a small
  opening at the chest, and slits starting from her waist on both sides. A white
  frilled skirt peeks out from under the qipao. Over this, she wears an elegant
  jacket that she keeps draped over her left arm, leaving the other side loose.
  She wears black socks and white shoes.
- Kokona carries a white QBZ‑03 assault rifle with a black grip and blue
  flower‑pattern decals on the stock. The weapon, named Lady's Grace, is
  effective both in defending her dignity as a lady and fulfilling her duties as
  an instructor. She keeps it well maintained, a point of pride she holds over
  the younger children who sometimes leave their toys scattered around.
