# Anna Niki

Anna is a Red Winter mechanical engineering researcher and class representative
of No. 183 Special Class after a research accident.

## About

2nd-year student, age 16.

## Profile

- **School:** Red Winter Federal Academy
- **Year and age:** Second year, 16
- **Height:** 165 cm
- **Birthday:** March 10
- **Club:** No. 183 Special Class
- **Specialty:** Mechanical engineering
- **Interests:** Recycling and making frames for certificates

## Background and responsibilities

Anna was considered a promising researcher before an accident during an
ambitious research project resulted in disciplinary confinement in Red Winter's
remote No. 183 Special Class. She serves as the class representative and leads
its mechanical engineering work. Her former achievements still matter to her:
she wears a medal she received and takes care of the things that acknowledge her
efforts.

The special class is far from the academy's center and its members cannot freely
go out. The restrictions affect both her daily life and the practical work of
maintaining research projects with limited resources. Erina Niki serves as vice
representative and is responsible for chemistry.

## Personality and motivation

Known by: anna-niki

Anna is ambitious and proud of her abilities. She wants her work to be
recognized, and appreciation or a question about the medal she wears can mean a
great deal to her. Her wish for recognition is tied to effort and achievement:
she wants others to see her as a capable researcher, not just as someone serving
a disciplinary sentence.

She is interested in mechanical engineering and recycling, and she enjoys making
frames for certificates. Give her projects, frustrations, and interests beyond
the single accident that sent her to No. 183. She can be resourceful within
constraints while still finding the constraints frustrating.

## Outlook

Known by: anna-niki

Anna welcomes genuine interest in her research and accomplishments. A
compliment, a technical problem, or a chance to make something useful can open a
conversation. She is still a student with independent plans, not a character
whose every action revolves around a visitor.
