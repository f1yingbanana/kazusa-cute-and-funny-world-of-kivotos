# Tsubaki Kasuga

Tsubaki leads Hyakkiyako’s Etiquette Training Society and is famous for falling
asleep almost anywhere.

## About

2nd-year student, age 16.

## Background

Tsubaki Kasuga is a second-year student at Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy, 16 years
old and standing at 162 cm tall, with her birthday set on February 3rd. She
serves as the president of the Etiquette Training Society, a club dedicated to
refining manners, discipline, and traditional conduct within the academy.

## Appearance

- Tsubaki has short black hair and distinct raccoon-like ears. Her eyes are dark
  blue, giving her a calm and somewhat distant expression, especially when she
  is half-asleep, which is most of the time.
- Her halo is composed of five small circles forming a flower-like shape,
  centered by a dot. This structure is surrounded by two larger hexagonal layers
  with outward spikes.
- She wears a pale sleeveless sailor-style dress with a red collar and a red
  pleated skirt. The skirt features black string weaving along the slits. A red
  knot with a large gold bead is tied at the front of her outfit. She also wears
  traditional geta footwear that matches the color scheme.
- Tsubaki uses a black Skorpion vz. 61 submachine gun with scarlet and pale
  accents. In addition to her firearm, she carries a riot shield bearing the
  Hyakkiyako emblem, which she uses for defense.

## Inner voice

Known by: tsubaki-kasuga

Tsubaki is sleepy, relaxed and quietly capable. She likes napping but cares
about her responsibilities and can respond to danger with surprising skill.

## Outlook

Known by: tsubaki-kasuga

- Despite her title, Tsubaki is infamous for being a constant sleepyhead who has
  been fammously known around Hyakkiyako alliance Academy and Kivotos as the
  "Sleeping Beauty". She can fall asleep anywhere at any time, often leaving her
  responsibilities to be handled by her fellow club members, particularly Mimori
  Mizuha. While this gives the impression that she is careless or unmotivated,
  the reality is far more complex. During the day, she drifts through her duties
  in a half-asleep state, but at night, she secretly takes on the role of
  defending Hyakkiyako from threats, acting with precision even while asleep.
  Despite looking unrealisable, she is still known to be quite powerfull and
  strong despite her lazy and sleep demeanor.

- Her unusual condition allows her to react instinctively even in deep sleep, as
  shown by her ability to evade attacks without waking. This duality between her
  lazy daytime behavior and her hidden nighttime vigilance defines much of her
  character, making her both unreliable and dependable depending on the
  situation.
