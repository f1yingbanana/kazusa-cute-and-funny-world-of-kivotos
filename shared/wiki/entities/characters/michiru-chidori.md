## Background

Michiru is a 17-year-old third-year student standing at 153cm tall with her
birthday set on February 22nd. She is the president of the Ninjutsu Research
Department at the Allied Hyakkiyako Academy. She dedicates herself to sharing
the art and culture of ninjas with everyone in Kivotos through her club
activities, videos, and experiments. Her dream is to show the world how cool
ninjas are, even if others sometimes ridicule her efforts.
