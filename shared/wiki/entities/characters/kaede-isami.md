## Background

Kaede Isami is a 15-year-old first-year student at Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy,
standing at 142 cm, with a birthday on July 9th. She serves as a member of the
Etiquette Training Society, joining after being struck by admiration for her
upperclassmen Tsubaki and Mimori upon transferring from the rural countryside.
Originally a wildly mischievous country girl who spent her childhood catching
beetles and roaming fields without a care, Kaede arrived in the city feeling
small and out of place beside the elegant, composed members of the Inner
Discipline Club, and that feeling ignited a determination in her that has never
faded since.

## Appearance

  removes them. Her eyes are a warm yellow and she has dark purple raccoon ears
  matching her hair. Her halo takes the form of a five-petaled pink lotus flower

  petal.
- Kaede wears a traditional Japanese hakui robe with detached miko sleeves and a
  red side-less pleated skirt, the upper body covering the open sides. A large
  bow sits behind her waist with sashes visible and neatly tied. She wears white
  thigh-length tights with red-trimmed edges and a small decorative red bow at
  each end, and black Japanese okobo sandals with small lotus patterns drawn on
  them.

## Outlook

Known by: kaede-isami

- Kaede throws herself into training with boundless energy and complete
  sincerity, whether it means eating all her green peppers, practicing club
  one-liners at the playground, or attempting to brew coffee for Sensei. The
  results rarely go according to plan and chaos tends to follow her everywhere,
  yet none of it discourages her for long. She bounces back from every stumble
  with the same loud, infectious energy she started with. Beneath that exterior,
  Kaede carries a genuinely tender heart. When she fails she feels it deeply,
  sometimes to the point of tears, and the gap between where she is and where
  she wants to be weighs on her more than she usually lets on. Outside of
  training, Kaede is a devoted Mushiqueen card battler who treats every match as
  a test of heart and strategy, keeps a steel chest of personal treasures, and
  jumps at any opportunity to go beetle-catching.

- Kaede carries a red double-barreled Nambu Type 90 Flare Gun decorated with
  gold floral decals and white streaks along the barrel. A small flower keychain
  is attached to the grip. The weapon's vivid coloring and floral detailing make
  it unmistakably hers. Despite its decorative appearance it is a genuine piece
  of equipment that Kaede maintains carefully, treating it as something precious
  that deserves to be looked after properly.
