## Background

Kaho Kuwakami is a 17-year-old third-year student at Hyakkiyako Alliance
Academy, standing at 173 cm, with a birthday on October 9th. She serves as the
vice president of the Yin-Yang Club and the Lead Strategist of Hyakkiyako's
Tourism and Culture Public Relations Support Team. In a club known for its
eccentric and unhurried membership, Kaho stands out as the one person keeping
everything functioning, regularly handling responsibilities that extend well
beyond her title and earning quiet recognition from students across the academy
as someone who feels more like a president than the actual one.

## Appearance

- Kaho has feet-length blonde hair with bangs covering her right eye, tied at
  the end with a large yellow bow. Her eyes are a warm amber and she has a small
  mole beneath her lips. Atop her head sit fennec-like ears, soft and rounded. A
  yellow headband with a decorative hairpin sits in her hair.
- Her halo consists of two yellow circles, the larger one thicker in form while
  the smaller one features three hollow leaf shapes with inner leaves and three
  surrounding dots.
- She wears a white and yellow side-less kimono top and a below-knee-length
  white and yellow kimono skirt, with a black obi and a large yellow bow tie
  around her waist.
- Kaho carries a two-tone Howa Type 64 battle rifle with a white receiver,
  handguard, barrel, and stock, and a black pistol grip, buttplate, bipod, and
  muzzle brake. The Hyakkiyako insignia is stamped on the receiver. The rifle
  was custom-made by Kaho to reflect the beauty of Hyakkiyako and is effective
  both as a promotional statement and as a practical tool for managing the
  school's more disruptive elements. Her framed certificate of appreciation from
  Hyakkiyako students sits in the Yin-Yang Club break room and is polished
  without fail during every cleaning session, treated with more consistent care
  than most people give to anything.

## Inner voice

Known by: kaho-kuwakami

Kaho is responsible, meticulous and passionate about Hyakkiyako. Her composure
can vanish when discussing the things or people she admires, and she is hard on
herself when she makes mistakes.

## Outlook

Known by: kaho-kuwakami

- Her dedication to Hyakkiyako is genuine and runs deep. She does not promote
  the district out of obligation but out of real love for everything it
  represents, the festivals, the culture, the merchandise, the flash mobs, the
  street haiku battles, and the modest internet channels that only she and a
  handful of others follow. Every part of it matters to her because she sees
  Hyakkiyako as the product of people dreaming together, and she wants as many
  people as possible to understand why that is worth caring about. This passion
  occasionally overrides her composure entirely, sending her into full
  presentation mode with charts and industry vocabulary before checking whether
  her email was ever sent, or collapsing in front of a limited merchandise
  display she was supposed to walk past.

- Beneath the professionalism is someone who holds herself to an exceptionally
  high standard and feels her mistakes sharply. When things go wrong she is
  quick to apologize and slow to forgive herself, often reaching for more formal
  gestures of accountability than the situation requires. Sensei's simple,
  consistent reassurance tends to cut through that cycle more effectively than
  anything else, and she is not entirely unaware of it, which occasionally makes
  her more flustered than she would prefer.
