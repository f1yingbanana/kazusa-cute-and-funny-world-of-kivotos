## Background

Izuna is 15 years old, a first-year student from the Allied Hyakkiyako Academy.
She stands at 155 cm tall and was born on December 16th. She is a member of the
Ninjutsu Research Department and dreams of becoming the greatest ninja in all of
Kivotos. Unfortunately, most people see her dream as unrealistic, and her
obsession with ninjas often makes others treat her as strange or childish.
Because of that, Izuna often ends up being something of a lone fox, continuing
her training even when others doubt her.

## Appearance

- Izuna has short dark brown hair styled into fur-like tufts tied with magenta
  knots, along with a small kitsune hair clip. She has fox ears and a large
  fluffy fox tail, giving her a distinct fox-like appearance. Her eyes are amber
  with soft pink eyeshadow, and she almost always carries a bright and energetic
  smile.
- Her halo is pink and features a hollow five-petal cherry blossom pattern
  inside it.
- Izuna wears a cropped white sailor uniform top with a navy blue collar and a
  pink/magenta neckerchief tied at the front, paired with a short navy blue
  pleated skirt, over which she drapes a wide-sleeved floral kimono haori jacket
  in rich pink and red tones decorated with cherry blossom patterns in pink,
  white, and light blue that flows dramatically behind her like a cape. At the
  same time, her legs are dressed asymmetrically with fishnet stockings on one
  side and a dark stocking on the other, both accented by a thigh strap holding
  several kunai throwing knives. Her feet are clad in dark brown loafers, her
  hands covered in black fingerless gloves, all topped off with a flowing pink
  scarf, a glowing pink floral halo above her head, fox ears adorned with a
  small white kitsune hair ornament, a large fluffy fox tail behind her, a
  decorated sniper rifle in hand, and a skull and blade charm dangling at her
  side.
- Izuna carries a Type 100 submachine gun "Izuna-Style Super Ninja Tool" with
  its entire body covered in a similar flowery patterns as her kimono, with
  shinobi-style accessories attached at the base of the stock.

## Outlook

Known by: izuna-kuda

- Despite that, Izuna remains incredibly cheerful and optimistic. She approaches
  everything with enthusiasm and rarely loses her smile, even in difficult
  situations. To her, every moment is another opportunity to train and improve
  her ninjutsu. Whether it’s practicing stealth, studying ninja techniques, or
  performing dramatic “escort missions,” she throws herself into the role
  completely.

- Izuna takes her ninja role very seriously, even if her actions sometimes come
  across as playful or exaggerated. She constantly studies ninjutsu and proudly
  declares herself a proper shinobi in training. Even when others doubt her or
  treat her dreams lightly, she continues moving forward with determination,
  believing that one day she will truly become the best ninja in Kivotos.
