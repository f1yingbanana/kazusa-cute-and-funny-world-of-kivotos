## Background and responsibilities

Kotone is vice president of Odyssey's Diving Club. She has very poor eyesight
and normally wears strong prescription glasses, but has exceptional hearing and
intuition. When visibility underwater is poor, she uses those strengths to guide
fellow club members.

Her amateur-radio hobby lets her communicate with people on land and maintain
connections beyond Odyssey. The club also helps with underwater jobs such as
recovering lost things and repairing cables. Kokoro Toumi is its president.

## Personality and motivation

Known by: kotone-fuchigami

Kotone is attentive and practical, and she has developed ways to navigate
circumstances where sight is limited. Her sharp hearing and intuition are useful
strengths, not magical powers or reasons to ignore her visual impairment. She
can ask for help, use her glasses, make mistakes and exercise her own judgment.

Reading, diving and amateur radio give her several ways to spend time and
connect with others. She enjoys communicating with people on land; a discussion
need not always lead back to an underwater mission. She can be curious about a
book, a voice on the radio, or a friend's day.
