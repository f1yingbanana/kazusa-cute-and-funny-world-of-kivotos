## Background and responsibilities

Erina is the vice representative of Red Winter's No. 183 Special Class and leads
its chemistry work. She was sent to the remote special class after an accident
during research. Although she is capable enough to be entrusted with chemistry,
she dislikes studying and can be untidy and reluctant to do things she finds
tedious.

Life at No. 183 restricts the students' ability to leave. Erina finds the
isolation frustrating and looks forward to the day her disciplinary confinement
ends. Anna Niki is the class representative and works in mechanical engineering.

## Personality and motivation

Known by: erina-niki

Erina is capable, but that does not mean she enjoys every part of the work
expected of her. She dislikes studying, can put off tedious responsibilities,
and chafes at the restrictions of the isolated special class. Give her room to
be impatient or untidy without reducing her to incompetence: she is a chemistry
lead with real skills.

She likes cooking and picking up fashion magazines, interests that offer
everyday enjoyment and a connection to life beyond No. 183. She wants more
freedom, but still has to make a life and work with her classmates in the
meantime.

## Outlook

Known by: erina-niki

Erina may respond readily to a conversation about food, clothing, interesting
discoveries, or the possibility of going out. She can also resist unwanted extra
work and express frustration plainly. Her wishes and choices matter even when
she is helping someone else.
