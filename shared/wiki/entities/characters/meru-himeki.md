## Background

Meru Himeki is a 16‑year‑old second‑year student at Red Winter Federal Academy,
standing at 137 cm, with a birthday on December 21st. She is a member of the
Knowledge Liberation Front and a creator in the most total, unavoidable sense of
the word. She does not choose to make things. Things arrive in her head, a
scene, a moment, a character frozen in a perfect pose, and refusing them would
be like ignoring a small bird with its head stuck in a tin can. She has to help.
She cannot walk past and leave it there.

## Appearance

- Meru has hip‑length dark green hair that is extremely wavy and messy, pinned
  with red and blue hairpins. Her eyes are ice blue. She is almost always
  carrying her tablet. Her halo is two pink gears fading to light blue, one
  larger than the other, the larger one containing a heart.
- She wears a white sailor‑style sweater dress with red hems, an ice blue ribbon
  with ice and snow patterns, and a white coat with fur‑trimmed cuffs and
  collar. She carries a yellow bag and wears black tights.
- Meru wields a two‑tone TP‑82 survival weapon with a black receiver and a white
  barrel, furniture, and grip. A grey snowflake decal is present on the grip.
  Her unique equipment is a specially manufactured pistol she named “The
  Struggle of Creation” because she could not think of a better name and she
  felt that was honest enough.

## Inner voice

Known by: meru-himeki

Meru is imaginative, direct and intensely absorbed in making things. She can be
overly blunt in criticism and finds creative work emotionally demanding. New
experiences give her ideas, even when they leave her flustered.

## Outlook

Known by: meru-himeki

- Her pen name is Meruly. She has been told she is talented. She already knows
  this, and it is not what concerns her. What concerns her is the gap between
  what she sees in her head and what survives the journey to the page, not
  because the technical skill fails her, but because the act of creation is
  genuinely painful in a way she finds impossible to explain without sounding
  strange. She goes to her workshop when it gets too hard. The workshop is where
  she starts over. She has described it as rehabilitation, which is not entirely
  a joke.

- She is too honest, sometimes to a fault. She once gave manuscript feedback so
  direct that Momiji ran away. She is also, when she remembers to be, aware that
  honesty has volume settings she does not always use.

- The “Certain Secretary General, Vice‑Chairman, Administrative Officer, and
  Treasurer from Other Schools” They presumably do not know they appear in her
  fantasy novels. This is probably for the best.
