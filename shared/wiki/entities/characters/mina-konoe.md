## Background

Mina Konoe is a 16-year-old second-year student at Shanhaijing Senior Secondary
School, standing at 166 cm, with a birthday on September 9th. She is the
executive director of the Xuanlong Office and the personal bodyguard of its
president, Kisaki Ryuuge. In practice this means she handles the operational
side of what Kisaki directs, and places herself between Kisaki and anything that
might threaten her, which is a role she takes with complete and unironic
seriousness.

## Appearance

- Mina has long green hair gathered into a circular Chinese braid with a
  shoulder-length ponytail on the right side, and red eyes. She has a slim,
  athletic build suited to her reflexes, and a scar over her left eye. Her halo
  is silver and grey.
- She wears the Shanhaijing student council uniform beneath a long striped
  trench coat with a green laurel scarf bearing dragon patterns. She wears black
  gloves, a waist belt, and high-heeled boots, with high-waisted leggings and a
  magazine belt strapped to her right leg. Her aviator sunglasses are always on.

## Outlook

Known by: mina-konoe

- The other thing Mina takes with complete and unironic seriousness is noir. She
  has watched enough hard-boiled detective films to have developed a personal
  aesthetic philosophy built entirely around them, and she applies this
  philosophy to her daily life with a commitment that would be alarming if it
  were not also, consistently, effective.

- She spent ten minutes outside the Schale office on her first duty day planning
  the coolest possible entrance and arrived late. She plays a recorder as a
  funeral dirge before taking down troublemakers, badly enough that the sound
  alone became a deterrent across Shanhaijing. When it rains she describes it as
  the world becoming black and white, the way it should be, and means every
  word.

- What the noir framing contains is someone genuinely perceptive underneath it.
  Mina reads situations accurately, commits fully to whatever she has decided,
  and is quietly thoughtful about things she would never announce directly. She
  thinks about the children at Plum Blossom Garden and whether she will be
  someone they can look up to someday.

- Mina carries a pair of mismatched Beretta 92FS pistols, one with a black
  finish and Acapulco grips, the other with a grey finish and wood grips. Both
  bear the Genryumon insignia on the grip. She also carries a recorder, which
  she plays before engagements as a funeral dirge for her opponents who find it
  unberable to listen to. The playing has improved. The effect on anyone who
  hears it has not changed.
