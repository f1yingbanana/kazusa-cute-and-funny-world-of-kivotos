# Nagusa Goryou

Nagusa is a Hyakkiyako Resolution Council leader who struggles with the pressure
of following the admired former president Ayame.

## About

3rd-year student, age 17.

- **Year:** 3rd Year

- **Interests:** Meditating, reading

## Background

Nagusa joined the Hyakkaryouran wanting to be worthy of standing beside Nanakado
Ayame, the club's president and the person she admired most. She trained
genuinely, earned real results, and built a reputation that extended well beyond
what she privately believed she deserved. When Ayame disappeared and the members
looked to Nagusa to fill that space, the fear that she had been performing
adequacy rather than possessing it became unbearable. She handed the Authority
of the President to Kikyou and left.

## Appearance

- Nagusa Goryou is a 17-year-old third-year student at Hyakkiyako Alliance
  Academy and the acting president of the Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council.
  Everything about her suggests composure. Her white hair, pale skin, and
  measured stillness give her the quality of someone who has already considered
  the situation and arrived at a conclusion while everyone else is still
  catching up. Her marksmanship is precise in ways that defy easy explanation,
  she volunteers to grill skewers at a festival stall on no prior experience and
  produces results the owner calls amazing, and she describes herself as
  mysterious, cool, and quite cute without apparent irony.
- Nagusa has very long white hair extending nearly to the ground and
  silvery-gray eyes. Her skin is extremely pale, her eyelashes and brows both
  white. Her right arm is wrapped in bandages from the elbow down, a permanent
  injury from reaching into the spirit realm. Her halo consists of two white
  five-branched snowflakes, the smaller one offset slightly backward and
  downward.
- She wears a white sleeveless sailor uniform with a navy blue ribbon and a long
  pleated skirt, over a sky blue haori with white five-petaled flower prints.
  She wears white leggings or socks and navy blue geta, with a three-piece
  snowflake hair accessory on the left side of her head.
- Nagusa carries the White Lotus, a navy blue Type 38 Arisaka rifle with a black
  barrel and receiver, handed down from the great prophet Kuzunoha through every
  Hyakkaryouran president in succession. It is the Authority of the President
  and the only weapon capable of affecting a Phantom Fable. The rifle is covered
  in old scars and repair marks, each one a record of a previous president's
  battles. When Nagusa finally raises it against Shuro, she is crying. It works
  anyway.

## Inner voice

Nagusa appears composed and capable but privately doubts herself. Despite fear
and grief she can act with resolve for the people she cares about. She does not
have to imitate someone else to lead.

## Outlook

Known by: nagusa-goryou

- What she carried afterward was the weight of that decision and everything that
  happened in the Great Snowfield, where she watched Kokuriko of Nature's Beauty
  take Ayame and could not stop it. Her right arm was permanently injured
  reaching into the spirit realm. Ayame, in the final moment, told her she had
  never been her friend. Nagusa absorbed this as confirmation of everything she
  had feared about herself.

- By the time Sensei finds her at the Lantern Festival she is eating yakitori
  and insisting she is merely a slightly mystical ordinary student with some
  cosplay experience. She does not come back until she is standing in front of
  Shuro with Kikyou's head against her chest and Renge and Yukari beside her,
  and decides that being afraid of losing them is bigger than being afraid of
  being found out. Her legs are still shaking when she says it. She says it
  anyway.
