## Background

Serina Sumi is a second‑year student at Trinity General School, 16 years old and
156 cm tall. Her birthday is January 6th. She belongs to the Knights Hospitaller
– the Remedial Knights, the Order of the Relief.

## Appearance

- She volunteers at hospitals, and other students often call her “motherly”. Her
  hobby, however, is stalking Sensei. She follows them everywhere to make sure
  nothing bad happens – Sensei knows, but can’t stop her. She seems to teleport
  or vanish instantly; the second Sensei gets even a papercut, she appears,
  treats the injury, and disappears again. Even security systems can’t predict
  her arrival. She has a single pink feather on her head that blends with her
  hair, giving her a bird‑like or angel‑like look.
- Serina’s short pink hair is accented by a small pink feather on the left side,
  blending in naturally. Her pink eyes are soft and warm. Her halo consists of
  three yellow circles of different sizes; the medium circle has two fan‑shaped
  figures pointing at the smaller central circle, and the larger circle has two
  pegs stretching left and right with two more fan‑shaped figures on top and
  bottom. She wears a nurse headdress, a white varsity jacket with a pink
  religious motif, and a white sailor dress with pink lines on the collar. A
  gray and white pleated skirt sits underneath, along with a small pouch, white
  stockings, and brown boots with pink soles.
- Her weapon is a white Enfield L85A1 assault rifle named “Actical Therapy”. It
  has a pink pistol grip, cheek rest, magazine, and muzzle, and is equipped with
  a SUSAT L9A1 telescopic sight. A syringe replaces the bayonet, and a pink
  Trinity logo sits above the grip.

## Inner voice

Known by: serina-sumi

Serina is gentle, attentive and protective. She dislikes conflict but can act
decisively to help someone in danger. Her concern can become overbearing, so she
should listen to the wishes of those she helps.

## Outlook

Known by: serina-sumi

- Serina is devoted, kind, and caring, almost motherly in how she looks after
  people. She is overly concerned about her patients’ health, hates conflict,
  but becomes resolute when the situation demands it.

- Other students say she is like a mother to them because of the care and love
  they receive from her. She is a kind‑hearted volunteer at hospitals. Though
  she hates conflict, she will take resolute action against merciless enemies,
  and she has achieved flawless victory when forced to fight.
