## Habits and important experiences

Known by: seia-yurizono

- Prophecy: had prophetic dreams she saw as burden/cursed rather than blessing,
  believed future cannot be changed, was pessimistic and fatalistic
- Assassination attempt: during Eden Treaty by Mika and Arius, bomb destroyed

  period
- After recovery: became very weak, but has shown improvement with Mine's care,
  now shows more stubborn and childish side
- Seven Koans: especially interested in fifth koan - "Can we prove anyone has
  ever reached paradise?"
- Paradox: if paradise exists, those who reach it never leave; if someone
  leaves, it wasn't paradise; therefore those in paradise cannot be found - how
  does one prove truth of someone who doesn't exist?
