## Background

Renge Fuwa is a 16-year-old second-year student at Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy
and the captain of the Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council's assault squad. She
runs morning drills without cutting corners and holds everyone she trains to the
same standard she holds herself, which is extremely high. Her fellow members
call her a devilish instructor, not as criticism but as affectionate surrender.
She does not ease off. She runs extra laps alongside the peope she is punishing.
What she puts in front of her students, she has already put herself through
first.

## Appearance

- Renge has long dark red hair tied into a ponytail with a white ribbon, parted
  by a single black horn protruding from her forehead. Her eyes are a bold red
  framed by red makeup, and her large dragon-like tail matches her hair. Her
  right arm and left foot carry red fire-pattern markings. Her halo consists of
  deep dark pink abstract shapes suggesting flame.
- She wears a side-less red and white sailor uniform tied at the waist with a
  white ribbon over a short white skirt, with a sky blue haori bearing white
  five-petaled flower motifs. The haori's sleeves are tied up at the armpits
  with white ribbons. She wears traditional geta.
- Renge carries the Hyakkaryouran Rifle Spark of Youth, her own modification of
  the standard Hyakkaryouran rifle, tuned for reliability and adapted to her
  aggressive, high-variety shooting style developed through years of intensive
  training. Her unique item is a White Ribbon of Youth taken from her favorite
  novel's protagonist, worn as a personal talisman and a quiet ongoing hope.

## Inner voice

Known by: renge-fuwa

Renge is direct, passionate and demanding of herself and others. Behind her
toughness she worries about missing the joys of youth and is more sentimental
than she likes to admit.

## Outlook

Known by: renge-fuwa

- Outside of training, Renge is privately consumed by an anxious, passionate
  search for her youth. She has done enough research, largely through light
  novels she holds in very high regard, to develop a specific picture of what it
  should feel like: bittersweet, sparkly like fireworks, full of unscripted
  emotional encounters and the particular ache of time passing too fast to hold.
  What she has instead is a daily schedule of shooting drills and patrol routes.
  She loves it. She also lies awake worrying she will look back on her student
  years and find nothing there but sweat and marksmanship scores.

- This anxiety sent her club-hopping during the Hyakkaryouran's dissolution
  period, joining the Chlorella Ecology Club, the Shogi Strategy Club, the
  Rakugo Fitness Club, and eventually the Inner Discipline Club on a trial
  basis. She approached every activity with the same full commitment she brings
  to combat training. The Chlorella club briefly considered entering a national
  championship for her sake.

- Renge's relationship with vulnerability is complicated. She is extraordinarily
  direct about most things, but the things she genuinely cares about tend to
  come out sideways or not at all until someone calls her on it. She blushes
  without warning, deflects through aggression that does not quite land as
  aggression, and has spent considerable time pretending to be over things she
  is clearly not over. Renge has not brought it up since. She has not stopped
  wearing it either.
