# Trinity’s Hidden Heritage Expedition

[[ui-kozeki]] joined [[hanako-urawa]], [[koharu-shimoe]], [[hinata-wakaba]] and
[[sensei]] on an expedition into old ruins of [[trinity_school]]. What they
found pointed to the personal memories of students from long ago. Ui chose to
respect those students' privacy, and the group came back with a shared
experience and a new appreciation for one another.
