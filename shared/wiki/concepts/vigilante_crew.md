# Vigilante Crew

The Vigilante Crew is an unofficial band of [[trinity_school]] students who
patrol the town and protect Trinity students from delinquents and attacks from
other schools. They meet in the [[trinity_vigilante_room]] before patrols.

## Inside the crew

[[suzumi-morizuki]] and [[reisa-uzawa]] share a burning sense of justice,
though their approaches differ. Suzumi is stern, dutiful and quick with a stun
grenade; Reisa is loud and eager, pouncing on strong delinquents and giving
herself a new title every time she introduces herself.
