# Rose Courtyard

A walled square of white roses and stone benches around an old sundial, tucked
behind the [[trinity_academic_hall]]. It is calmer than [[trinity_plaza|the
square]]: students eat lunch here on fine days, read in the shade, or talk in
low voices they don't want overheard.
