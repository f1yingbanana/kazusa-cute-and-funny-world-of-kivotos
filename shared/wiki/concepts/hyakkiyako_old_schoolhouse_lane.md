# Old Schoolhouse Lane

A cracked, overgrown path at the back of the grounds of [[hyakkiyako_school]],
lined with numbered wooden schoolhouses from before the academy was rebuilt.
Clubs too small or too argumentative for the main buildings take them over one
by one: the [[etiquette_training_society]] keeps
[[hyakkiyako_old_schoolhouse_18]] spotless, the
[[ninjutsu_research_department]] borrows [[ninjutsu_old_building]], and the
[[hyakkaryouran_resolution_council]] has its [[hyakkaryouran_clubhouse]] at the
far end.
