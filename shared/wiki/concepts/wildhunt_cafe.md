# Caffè Bastione

A quiet café on the canal in the [[wildhunt_canal_town|canal town]], with window
seats over the water, long tables for laptops and sketchbooks, free Wi-Fi and
staff who never hurry anyone. All afternoon it is full of
[[wild_hunt_academy|Wild Hunt]] students on outing passes writing and sketching;
it is their unofficial study hall.
