# Valkyrie Dorm 4 Common Room

Sofas facing a television, a kitchenette with a kettle, a microwave and a fridge
full of labelled lunches, a vending machine and a rack of the day's papers.
Officers coming off late shifts eat here when the
[[valkyrie_cafeteria|cafeteria]] has already closed, which is most nights.
