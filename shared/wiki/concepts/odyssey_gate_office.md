# Terminal 3 Gate Office

A white prefab gatehouse at the land end of the terminal where visitors trade
their ID for a pass and deliveries pile up for collection. A duty sailor of
[[odyssey_maritime_high_school|Odyssey]] with a logbook decides who goes aboard.
After 22:00 there is only a bell, and a long wait.
