# Game Development Department Club Room

The [[game_development_department]]'s room in the [[millennium_clubhouse]], and
every surface is covered: retro consoles, dev PCs, tangled controllers, snack
bags and design notebooks. The red sofa is where the club argues about
scenarios. In one corner, under an enormous railgun leaning against the wall,
is a nest of blankets beside a CRT looping an old RPG, a "hero's inventory"
shelf and a bag of cat treats. A tall locker with a name sticker stands against
the back wall.

## The club's corners

The nest is where [[aris-tendou|Aris]], the club's youngest member, sleeps; the
railgun is hers. The locker is [[gdd_locker|Yuzu's]]: [[yuzu-hanaoka|Yuzu]] hides in it to work
and to hear the room without being seen.

## Money and Seminar

The club owes [[seminar]] money, and everyone in the building knows it. The club
once faced being shut down for good, the start of
[[game_development_department_crisis]].
