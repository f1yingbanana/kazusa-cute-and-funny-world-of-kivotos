# Valkyrie Public Peace Bureau

A long top-floor office with case boards strung in red thread, a coffee machine
that is never off, and a folding cot in the corner for nights that run late. The
director's desk faces the door. Its investigators are feared across D.U. and
have the most complaints filed against them, sometimes by the Security Bureau
downstairs. Visitors come in only with an escort.

## The director

The desk facing the door is [[kanna-ogata|Kanna Ogata]]'s.
