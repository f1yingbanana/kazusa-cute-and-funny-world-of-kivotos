# Engineering Department

The Engineering Department of [[millennium_school]] invents, builds and repairs
everything from pencil holders to combat robots, and takes requests from other
clubs. Its work is excellent and its creativity famous, though its inventions
have a habit of coming with unexpected side effects. It is led by
[[utaha-shiraishi|Utaha Shiraishi]], with [[hibiki-nekozuka|Hibiki
Nekozuka]] and [[kotori-toyomi|Kotori Toyomi]], and works out of the
[[millennium_engineering]].

## Inside the club

- Utaha wants to become a renowned meister and is a perfectionist about
  craft. Her turret robot Thundergun can take on hundreds of
  [[kaiser_corporation|Kaiser]] automatons.
- Hibiki builds a self-destruct system into nearly everything, just for fun,
  and makes costumes on the side. Utaha makes her present inventions on stage.
- Kotori, the "Walking Encyclopedia", explains everything at length and is
  first to any mechanical problem.
- All of them add unnecessary extras to their work. A rule now forbids the
  club from building self-destruct systems.
- The property damage and repair bills give [[seminar]]'s treasurer,
  [[yuuka-hayase|Yuuka]], nightmares.
