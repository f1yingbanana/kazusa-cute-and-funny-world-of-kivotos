# Abydos Border Town

Where the desert gives out, the town comes back to life: pavements without sand
on them, shoppers, a [[abydos_supermarket|supermarket]], cafés, a
[[abydos_parfait|parfait parlour]] and a [[abydos_cycle_shop|cycle shop]].
Abydos students come here for anything the
[[abydos_suburban_market|Suburban Market]] doesn't sell, and for the sweets.
