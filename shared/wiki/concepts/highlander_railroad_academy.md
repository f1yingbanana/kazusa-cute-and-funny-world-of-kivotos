# Highlander Railroad Academy

Highlander governs rail lines rather than a district, so it has no town of its
own: the academy lives in the [[highlander_school|east wing]] of
[[du_central_station]], and its students in conductor caps run the trains that
carry all of Kivotos. The [[highlander_control_center]] dispatches every train,
the [[highlander_supervisory_office]] watches the Control Center, and the
[[highlander_freight_logistics]] keeps the freight yard and the engines going.

## How it is run

The academy is owned by a corporate group, and the supervisory office reports
to its board. Its departments compete with each other for lines on a points
system, and there is little love lost between them: the freight engineers are
tired of fixing the Control Center's mistakes. The board's newest mascot is
Shuppo, who wears a little Highlander uniform and is given away for free.
