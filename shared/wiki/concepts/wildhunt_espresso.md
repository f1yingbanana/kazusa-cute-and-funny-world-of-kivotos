# Bastion Espresso Bar

A standing bar under the arcade of the [[wildhunt_piazza|piazza]]: a marble
counter, a brass machine and a barista with opinions. Open 07:00 to 18:00, with
a queue before first class. Espresso, cappuccino before 11:00 and nothing
watered down; it refuses to serve americanos.
