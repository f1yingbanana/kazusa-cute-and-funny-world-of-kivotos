# Araba Beach

A wide beach of pale sand south-east of [[gehenna_district|town]], with a beach
shack, rental umbrellas and a campground behind the dunes. The [[prefect_team]]
once held a summer camp here; local troublemakers treat it as theirs the rest of
the year.
