# Arius Branch Schoolhouse

The Gothic main building of [[arius_school]]: pointed arches, cracked stained
glass and a bell that no longer rings. One wing has new glass, wiring and
heaters, and holds the [[arius_council_room|council room]] and the
[[arius_study_hall|junior study hall]]; the rest is sealed with tape and
warning signs until the crews reach it.
