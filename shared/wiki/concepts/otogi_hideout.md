# Pier 7 Inspection Walkway

A grated catwalk running under the deck of the [[kousagi_viaduct|viaduct]]
between two piers, meant for bridge inspectors who rarely come. Someone has
strung a hammock across it, stacked puzzle boxes in the corners and wired a
radio into an inspection lamp. Small chalk symbols on the pier lead the way up
for anyone who can read them.

## Whose it is

It is [[otogi-tenjinyama|Otogi]]'s, a place to slip away to at night from her
[[room_otogi|room at SCHALE]].
