# Phantom Fables and Artifact Spirits

[[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako]] tells many stories of spirits born from rumors
and fears. The lesser [[artifact_spirits]] appear as objects come to life; the
far more powerful Phantom Fables, such as Kurokage and the One-Eyed One, are the
old tales themselves made real. Both have walked the district during its
Lantern Festival crises ([[hyakkaryouran_crisis]]).

## Fighting them

Ordinary guns do nothing to a Phantom Fable. The only weapon that can touch one
is the [[hundred_lotus]], the rifle handed down from [[kuzunoha]] to every
president of the [[hyakkaryouran_resolution_council]] as the Authority of the
President.

The members of [[natures_beauty]] called up and steered these spirits with
strange tomes ([[phantom_tomes]]), whose power works through stories, fears and
desires.
