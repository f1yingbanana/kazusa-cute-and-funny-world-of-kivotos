# Valkyrie at the Water Park

Kirino, Fubuki and Kanna served as lifeguards at a water park. Kanna struggled
with public-facing work but rescued a child during an incident involving a
runaway mechanical crocodile. The experience reminded her of her wish to help
citizens directly.
