# Valkyrie at the Water Park

One summer [[kirino-nakatsukasa]], [[fubuki-nemugaki]] and [[kanna-ogata]] of
the [[valkyrie_police]] served as lifeguards at [[aegir_water_park]]. When a
runaway mechanical crocodile caused an incident, Kanna rescued a child.

## What it meant to Kanna

Kanna, used to paperwork and interrogations, struggled with the public-facing
work. The rescue reminded her of why she had joined: to help citizens directly.
It was her deputy, [[konoka-shima]], who had sent her there.
