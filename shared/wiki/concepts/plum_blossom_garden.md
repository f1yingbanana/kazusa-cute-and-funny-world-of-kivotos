# Plum Blossom Garden

The Plum Blossom Garden is where [[shanhaijing_senior_secondary_school]] raises
and teaches its youngest children. Its instructors are students themselves, and
everyone in Shanhaijing respects them. It keeps a schoolhouse among plum trees,
the [[shanhaijing_plum_garden]], with a playground behind and a staff house
beside it.

Its best-known instructors are the Sunohara sisters: [[shun-sunohara]], who has
taught there for longer than anyone likes to ask, and her younger sister
[[kokona-sunohara]], who insists on being called Instructor Kokona.

## Inside the Garden

The instructors live in the [[plum_garden_staff_house]] next to the
schoolhouse. Under the old climbing frame in the [[plum_garden_playground]],
generations of Garden children have kept a secret base.
