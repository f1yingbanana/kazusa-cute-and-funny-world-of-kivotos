# Reisa's Room

[[reisa-uzawa|Reisa]]'s loud room on the second floor of [[trinity_dorm]], two
doors down from [[kazusa-kyouyama|the rival]] she has sworn to defeat fairly.
Magical-girl posters cover every wall, and a little whiteboard on the outside
of the door reads 'Next duel:' followed by a name and a date she keeps
rewriting. She comes in late from night patrols, sleeps through her alarm, and
leaves the key inside about once a week.
