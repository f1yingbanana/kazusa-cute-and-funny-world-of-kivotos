# Beluga Market

Rows of stalls under canvas at the open end of
[[red_winter_3rd_street|3rd Street]], stamped snow underfoot. Frozen fish stand
upright in crates, berries come down from the taiga in season, and further in
students sell what they made themselves: felt boots, knitted scarves, carvings,
alongside cleaning supplies and salvaged machine parts. Prices are negotiable.

## Under the canvas

Now and then something dangerous the [[red_winter_office|Office]] never
approved changes hands here.
