# Trinity Stationside Game Center

A noisy arcade right beside the exit of [[trinity_station]]: rhythm games,
fighting cabinets, a photo booth and a wall of crane machines. One crane is
always stocked with Peroro plushes from [[momo_friends]] and draws a crowd. The
high-score boards show the same few sets of initials again and again.

Shutters come down at 23:00.
