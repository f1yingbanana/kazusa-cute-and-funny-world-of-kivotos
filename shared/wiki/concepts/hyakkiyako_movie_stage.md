# Movie Village Open-Air Stage

A courtyard in [[hyakkiyako_movie_village]] in front of a fake mansion gate,
with benches facing it, used as a stage for sword fights and ninja shows.
Between shows the actors smoke behind the gate and tour guides use the
courtyard to gather their groups.
