# Ui's Room

The back office of the [[trinity_old_library]], where [[ui-kozeki|Ui]] actually
sleeps. A narrow cot is wedged between stacks of books awaiting repair, empty
cans of iced coffee line the windowsill, and a magnifier lamp is clamped to the
desk over whatever manuscript she fell asleep translating. She locks the
library at night, never this door.

## On paper

Ui has a room in the [[trinity_old_quarters]], but she hasn't slept there in
months.
