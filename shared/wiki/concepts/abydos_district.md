# Abydos District

Abydos was once the richest district in Kivotos. Advancing desertification and
sandstorms have buried whole neighbourhoods, and most residents and students
have left. A little life remains: some residential and commercial areas are
still active, in the [[abydos_town|town]] and out in
[[abydos_suburban_town|Suburban Town]], including
[[shiba_seki|Shiba Seki Ramen]], and the remaining students work to protect
[[abydos_school|Abydos High School]] and keep it open. Beyond the town
lies the [[abydos_desert|desert]], much of it owned by
[[kaiser_corporation|Kaiser]].
