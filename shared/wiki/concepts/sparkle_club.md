# Sparkle Club

The Sparkle Club, also called the Twinkle Twinkle Club, is a small, new club at
[[gehenna_school]] whose activities are whatever its members feel like:
chatting, following trends, window shopping and wandering the city. It is made
up of two close friends, [[kirara-yozakura|Kirara Yozakura]] and
[[erika-hatami|Erika Hatami]].

## Inside the club

Kirara founded it to leave the "Go-Home Club", calling it the "totally
heart-pounding, super twinkling club", and would like [[sensei]] as its
advisor. Erika usually plans their outings. The club has quietly taken over
[[gehenna_old_classroom_7|Old Classroom 7]] as its clubroom.
