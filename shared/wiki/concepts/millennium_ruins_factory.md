# Millennium Ruins Factory

A factory building deep in the [[millennium_ruins|ruins]], its roof caved in,
guard machines still moving through the dark halls. A stairway leads down to a
sealed chamber where a sleeping girl was once found among dead machines.
Explorers still come looking for whatever else is down there.

## What was found here

The girl was [[aris-tendou|Aris]], found by the
[[game_development_department|Game Development Department]] when they ventured
into the ruins to save their club, the start of
[[game_development_department_crisis]].
