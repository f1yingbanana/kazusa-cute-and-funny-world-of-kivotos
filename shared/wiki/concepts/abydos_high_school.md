# Abydos High School

Abydos High School once held considerable influence, but desertification and
debt have left only a small number of students. The Foreclosure Task Force works
to preserve the surviving campus and the wider district. Much of Abydos land
came under Kaiser Corporation control as the school struggled with debt.
