# Nodoka and Shigure's Room

An old classroom of the [[red_winter_spec227|Former Institute]], shared by
[[nodoka-amami|Nodoka]] and [[shigure-mayoi|Shigure]] and chosen because its
stove still draws. The desks are pushed to the walls, and two cots with patched
blankets sit close to the stove. A big telescope stands at the window on a
tripod, with star charts taped over cracked plaster and a jar of picked-up coins
beside it; along the other wall are shelves of jars of fermenting fruit and
kompot, and a flask on a hook. They bar the door at night against draughts and
bears.
