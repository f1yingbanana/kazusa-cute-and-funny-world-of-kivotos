# Arius District

Broken stone streets and Gothic ruins under Kivotos, where no sun ever reaches.
Work lamps strung by the repair crews light the patched roofs and the one busy
street, [[arius_lantern_street|Lantern Street]]; beyond them the ruins fall
away into dark. Behind a high wall stands [[arius_school]], and at the
district's far end the roped-off [[arius_basilica|Basilica]].

This is the home of Arius, split by a civil war a decade ago and ruled for years
by an adult who taught its children that everything is in vain. Since Arius was
put on parole, [[trinity_school|Trinity]] and
[[valkyrie_police_academy|Valkyrie]] crews have been repairing it street by
street.

## Getting in and out

The only way in or out is the marked route through the
[[trinity_catacombs|Trinity Catacombs]], about fifty minutes on foot. On
weekdays a column of Arius students walks it each morning to exam classes at
the [[trinity_old_school_building]] and back each evening.

## Who lives here

The remaining students live in the [[arius_barracks|Nicomedia Barracks]] inside
the school grounds; the paroled former [[arius_squad]] keeps a
[[arius_shelter|shelter]] of its own nearby. Old records wait in the
[[arius_porta_pasis|Porta Pasis Archives]], and on a good afternoon people sit
on the [[arius_aqueduct_steps|aqueduct steps]] to watch the light come in.
