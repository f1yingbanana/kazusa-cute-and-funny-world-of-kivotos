# Nagisa's Room

[[nagisa-kirifuji|Nagisa]]'s suite in the [[trinity_tea_residence]] is elegant,
but its real centrepiece is a wall of tea tins, each labelled by estate and
harvest. Herbs grow in a window box. A guard is posted down the hall, and she
locks the door, checks it, and sometimes checks it again.

## The writing desk

[[tea_party]] paperwork is piled on the desk, hidden under a silver tray when
guests come.
