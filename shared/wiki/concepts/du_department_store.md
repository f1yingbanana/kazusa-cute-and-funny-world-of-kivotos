# Kivotos Central Department Store

The city's flagship department store, eight floors over the south exit of
[[du_central_station]]. Cosmetics and accessories on the ground floor, clothes
and school-uniform tailoring above, a large bookshop and a stationery floor,
then a whole floor for the [[du_momo_friends_shop|Momo Friends flagship shop]].
The [[du_food_hall|food hall]] is in the basement, and the rooftop food court is
a favourite meeting spot for students from different schools.
