# Millennium Central Cafeteria

The ground floor of the [[millennium_lecture_building|Lecture Building]] and the
one place on campus where every club eats at once. Long tables, a set-meal line,
and a bakery counter whose fresh bread starts a small stampede at the lunch
bell. Behind it is a student kitchen with rentable hobs, where the careful ones
cook their own bento for the week.
