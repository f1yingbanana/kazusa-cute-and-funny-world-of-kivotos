# Aru Rikuhachima's Room

[[aru-rikuhachima|Aru]]'s room: an executive desk too big for it, a fake-leather
chair, a stack of management books with highlighted titles, and a rented kimono
on a hanger that she never found the money to return. On late nights she sleeps
at the [[problem_solver_office|office]] instead.
