# Millennium Study Area

The academic half of the [[millennium_school|campus]], quiet paths built around
the [[millennium_monorail_station|monorail stop]]: the
[[millennium_library|42nd Millennium Library]], the
[[millennium_study_center|Study Center]] and its terrace café, the
[[millennium_training_center|Training Center]], and behind fences the
[[millennium_data_center|Data Center]] and
[[millennium_power_plant|Power Plant 1]] that keep the whole school running.
