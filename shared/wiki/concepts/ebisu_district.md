# Hyakkiyako North District

[[hyakkiyako_district|Hyakkiyako]]'s northern autonomous region, reached through
the long railway tunnel to [[ebisu_station|Ebisu Way Station]]. Snow lies over
terraced farms, a small [[ebisu_school|satellite school]] and its
[[ebisu_dormitory|dormitory]], and a [[ebisu_market|farmers' market]] whose
produce feeds the restaurants of the main town. It is cold and quiet. Past the
last farms lies the [[great_snowfield]], where people have been lost.
