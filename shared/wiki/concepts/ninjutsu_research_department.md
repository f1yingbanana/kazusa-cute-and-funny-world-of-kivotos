# Ninjutsu Research Department

The Ninjutsu Research Department studies ninja culture at
[[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako]] and tries to show all of Kivotos how cool ninjas
are, through training, experiments and videos on its president's channel,
[[michiruchi_channel]]. Its president [[michiru-chidori|Michiru Chidori]] leads
two first-years, the eager [[izuna-kuda|Izuna Kuda]] and the shy, very tall
[[tsukuyo-oono|Tsukuyo Oono]]. The club borrows the drafty
[[ninjutsu_old_building]], held only "temporarily". Plenty of people laugh at
them; they carry on.

## Inside the club

By Michiru's own admission the club is not quite official yet. She fears the
[[hyakkaryouran_resolution_council|Hyakkaryouran]] might raid the clubroom one
day and that the [[yin_yang_club]]'s commotions will reach it, so she keeps her
favorite manga at [[schale_office|SCHALE]]. The three track well: their
directions led [[sensei]] straight to [[nagusa-goryou|Nagusa Goryou]] when she
was hiding.
