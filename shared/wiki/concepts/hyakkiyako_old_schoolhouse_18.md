# Old Schoolhouse No. 18

An old schoolhouse on [[hyakkiyako_old_schoolhouse_lane]], scrubbed until the
wood shines. Here the [[etiquette_training_society]], also called the Inner
Discipline Club, practises manners, flower arranging, cooking and cleaning, and
visitors are served tea in the tatami room. A sleeping cushion lies in one
corner of it that nobody is allowed to move.
