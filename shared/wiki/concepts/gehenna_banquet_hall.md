# Gehenna Banquet Hall

A gilded hall facing [[gehenna_central_square|Central Square]], with
chandeliers, a stage and a grand piano. The [[pandemonium_society|council]]
books it for parties and receptions; on quiet days the caretaker lets someone
practise on the piano.
