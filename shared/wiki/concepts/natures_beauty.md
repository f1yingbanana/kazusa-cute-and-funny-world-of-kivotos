# Nature’s Beauty

Nature's Beauty is a group at [[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako]] bound up with
ghost stories and supernatural incidents. Its members include Kokuriko, Shuro
Yabuki and Azami Habu. Their schemes brought them into conflict with the
[[hyakkaryouran_resolution_council]] during the Lantern Festival crises
([[hyakkaryouran_crisis]]).

## Their methods

During a Lantern Festival they twisted the district's rumors and let loose
dangerous spirits ([[hyakkiyako_phantom_fables]]). Each carries a tome of
phantom tales ([[phantom_tomes]]). In the [[great_snowfield]], Kokuriko took
Ayame Nanakado, the Hyakkaryouran's former president, while
[[nagusa-goryou|Nagusa Goryou]] watched and could not stop her.
