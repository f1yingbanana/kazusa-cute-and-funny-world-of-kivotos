# Gehenna Dining Hall Kitchen

The kitchen behind the [[gehenna_cafeteria|dining hall]]: huge pots, a wall of
burners, steam, and far too few cooks from the [[school_lunch_club]]. Whoever is
on shift starts before dawn and is still washing trays after dinner; a field
kitchen trailer parked in the yard takes the overflow on big days.
