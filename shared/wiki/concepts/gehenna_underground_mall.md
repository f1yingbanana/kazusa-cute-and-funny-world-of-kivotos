# Gehenna Underground Mall

A tiled underground arcade beneath [[gehenna_station|the station]]: boutiques
such as [[gehenna_atelier_lilith|Atelier Lilith]], accessory stalls, snack
counters and a catalogue stand. The Gehenna Subway leaves from its far end for
[[gehenna_school|the academy]], climbing out into daylight for the last stretch.
