# Arius Repair Depot

Scaffolding poles, cable drums, generators and a site office in a shipping
container, run by [[valkyrie_police_academy|Valkyrie]] and
[[trinity_school|Trinity]] crews. The board outside lists day jobs that pay:
hauling, clearing rubble, holding a lamp while someone else rewires a
building.
