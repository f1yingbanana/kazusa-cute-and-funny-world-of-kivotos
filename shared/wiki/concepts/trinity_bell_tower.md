# Trinity Bell Tower

A narrow spiral stair climbs from the top floor of the
[[trinity_academic_hall]] to an open gallery beside the bells. From up here you
can see the whole of Trinity, from the station to the outskirts, and anyone
crossing [[trinity_plaza|the square]] below.

The bells ring at 08:30, 12:00 and 15:20. Students aren't supposed to come up,
which is exactly why some do.
