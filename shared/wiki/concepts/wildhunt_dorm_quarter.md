# Wild Hunt Dormitory Quarter

Eight dormitories stand round a courtyard in one bastion of the fortress, behind
an arch watched from the
[[wildhunt_supervisory_post|Dorm Supervisory Unit's post]]. The courtyard fills
with students after class and empties fast before curfew at 22:00; the room
check follows half an hour later. Rooms are assigned by the Arts Council of
[[wild_hunt_academy|Wild Hunt]] and cannot be swapped on request, so neighbours
are a matter of luck. Among the dormitories are the old [[wildhunt_dorm4|No. 4]]
and the newer [[wildhunt_dorm5|No. 5]].
