# Trinity District

The town around [[trinity_school]], one of the oldest and grandest districts in
Kivotos: white stone, church spires, rose gardens and cobbled streets running
down toward the river and, further off, the sea.

[[trinity_station]] sits at the foot of [[church_street]], the shopping street
that climbs toward the campus on its hill. [[trinity_sisterhood|Trinity
Cathedral]], seat of the [[sisterhood]], stands between the street and the
campus, and the dormitories sit among gardens in the
[[trinity_residential|Residential Quarter]] on the river side. Beyond the town
lie the [[trinity_outskirts|outskirts]], with fields, old annexes, the
[[trinity_tea_residence|Tea Party's residence]] and the ruined
[[trinity_old_cathedral]], and a bus ride away, the [[trinity_seafront]]. Local
buses come only every 30 minutes or so, and a short subway line runs under the
town, so most students walk or cycle.
