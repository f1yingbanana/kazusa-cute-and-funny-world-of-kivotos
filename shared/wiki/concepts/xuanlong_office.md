# Xuanlong Office

The Xuanlong Office, called the Genryumon, is the student council of
[[shanhaijing_senior_secondary_school]]. It governs from the
[[shanhaijing_rikkagaku]] with the ceremony of an old dynasty: petitions,
audiences and inspections, guards at every arch and at the Inner Gate. Its
president is [[kisaki-ryuuge]], with [[mina-konoe]] as executive and her
personal bodyguard.

Under Kisaki the office keeps Shanhaijing's guarded ways while trying,
carefully, to open the school a little to the outside world. Its inspectors are not loved
on the Black Tortoise Promenade, and its interests often run against the
[[xuanwu_merchant_association]].

## Inside the office

Kisaki was made president unusually early, in her second year, and the
traditionalist factions never quite forgave it. Her reach for outsiders has met
resistance before, as in the [[moonlight_dream_exchange]]. Students who fall out
of the council's favour can end up in the [[shanhaijing_school_prison]].
