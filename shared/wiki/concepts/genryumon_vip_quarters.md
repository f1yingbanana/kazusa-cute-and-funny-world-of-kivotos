# Old Palace VIP Quarters

A secluded court of lacquered rooms deep inside the palace, reached by a covered
walkway with guards at its gate. It is comfortable, quiet and very hard to leave
without being seen.

## Who lives here

The council's president, [[kisaki-ryuuge]], lives here in [[room_kisaki]], and
[[mina-konoe]] keeps the guard room by the gate, [[room_mina]], so that nobody
passes without her knowing.
