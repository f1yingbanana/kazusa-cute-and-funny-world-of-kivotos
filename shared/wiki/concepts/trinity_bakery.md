# Boulangerie Lumière

A narrow bakery on [[church_street]] that smells of butter from halfway down
the street. Baguettes, sandwiches and cream buns fill the shelves in the
morning, and the bread is so good that they empty at every mealtime; regulars
know to come early or go hungry.

## Cakes to order

The owner also bakes cakes to order for anyone who asks a day ahead. It is the
best-kept secret on the street.
