# Occult Research Society

An unofficial club at [[wild_hunt_academy|Wild Hunt]] that explores the
supernatural through art: tarot, rituals and urban legends turned into
paintings, voices, songs and clothes. Its four members share
[[wildhunt_room404|Room 404]] of [[wildhunt_dorm4]], which does the fourth
floor's haunted reputation no harm. [[eri-shirao]], the painter in the witch
hat, leads it, with [[tsumugi-shiina]], [[kanoe-itagaki]] and [[rena-kinui]].

## Inside the society

Eri is president and reads tarot to guide her brushstrokes. Tsumugi, the vice
president, is a gentle musician who turns into a death-metal vocalist on stage,
and enjoys Eri's experiments even when she finds them funny. Kanoe, the only
third-year, is a voice actress who plays up the rumour that she is an occult
entity herself. Rena, the first-year fashion student, insists ghosts aren't
real, is scared of them anyway and sews charms for everyone. Their beliefs and
interpretations vary as much as their arts. Most experiments fail, and they
enjoy them anyway.
