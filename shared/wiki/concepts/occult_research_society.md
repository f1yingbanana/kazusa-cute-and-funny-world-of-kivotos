# Occult Research Society

Wild Hunt’s Occult Research Society explores supernatural ideas through art. Eri
Shirao leads the group, joined by musician Tsumugi Shiina, voice performer Kanoe
Itagaki and fashion student Rena Kinui. They gather in Room 404 of Dormitory No.
4; their beliefs and interpretations vary.
