# Trinity Central Library

The great library of [[trinity_school]], facing [[trinity_plaza]]: a hushed
reading room under a painted dome, long oak tables with green-shaded lamps, and
shelves tall enough to need rolling ladders. The [[trinity_library_committee]]
runs the issue desk and the reference service; a member can match almost any
reader with the right book.

Talking above a whisper earns a look from the desk. The doors lock at 20:00,
though a lamp is sometimes still burning in the stacks long after.
