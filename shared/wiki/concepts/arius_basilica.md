# Arius Basilica Ruins

A vast church at the district's far end, its roof partly fallen in and its nave
heaped with rubble. Cold air moves through it. The rites once held here are why
most Arius students won't come near it, and it is where the
[[arius_rescue|rescue]] that ended the Eden Treaty crisis took place.
[[trinity_school|Trinity]] keeps it roped off, investigators visit now and then,
and patrols pass at night.
