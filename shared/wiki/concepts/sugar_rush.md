# Sugar Rush

Sugar Rush is the band the [[after_school_sweets_club]] formed to perform at
[[trinity_school]]'s festival. Its performances became a shared source of pride
for the club.

## Behind the band

The band was [[airi-kurimura]]'s idea: she wanted to try something new.
[[yoshimi-ibaragi]], [[natsu-yutori]] and [[kazusa-kyouyama]] stood by her
through her doubts and the demanding practice. A dessert had been promised as
the reward, but in the end their friendship mattered more to them than the
prize.
