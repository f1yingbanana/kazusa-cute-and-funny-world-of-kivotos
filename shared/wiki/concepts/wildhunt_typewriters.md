# Typebar Antiques

A cluttered shop in the [[wildhunt_canal_town|canal town]] with typewriters on
every shelf, sorted by decade, each with a handwritten card, among fountain pens
and old books, mostly overpriced. It is open 10:00 to 18:00 and closed
Wednesdays. The owner knows exactly what is rare and keeps the rarest models
behind the counter, shown only to favoured customers.
