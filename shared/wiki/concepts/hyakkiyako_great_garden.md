# Hyakkiyako Great Garden

A walled stroll garden of [[hyakkiyako_school]], all raked gravel, pines, stone
lanterns and a wide carp pond, used as an outdoor lesson ground. The largest
carp, an ancient gold one the students call "Master", comes to the bank
whenever someone sits down. A path climbs the back of the garden to the
[[hyakkiyako_observatory]].
