# Momoyodou Resort Plan

[[shizuko-kawawa|Shizuko]] set out to expand [[momoyodou|Momoyodou]] with a
beach house and got caught up in a fraudulent resort-rights scheme. Students
from [[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako]] and [[abydos_school|Abydos]] helped her
face the competition that followed.

## Behind the scheme

[[kaiser_corporation|Kaiser]] was involved in the scheme. A Mushiqueen
tournament aboard the [[golden_fleece_ship|Golden Fleece]] was part of the
affair. In the end the experience brought the groups together around the idea
of genuine hospitality.
