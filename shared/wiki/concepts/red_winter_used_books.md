# Samovar Used Books

Three narrow rooms on [[red_winter_3rd_street|3rd Street]] of secondhand books
stacked to the ceiling, with a samovar on the counter for customers who stay
long, which is most of them. The back room has boxes of old fanzines and Red
Bear back issues. Library volunteers from the
[[knowledge_liberation_front|Knowledge Liberation Front]] and critics from the
[[publishing_department|Publishing Department]] argue here over who saw a rare
edition first.
