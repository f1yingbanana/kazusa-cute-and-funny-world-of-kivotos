# Angel 24, Gehenna Main Gate Branch

A bright corner store of the [[angel_24]] chain across the road from the main
gate of [[gehenna_school]]. It never closes, and its shot-out window has been
repaired so many times it's mostly tape. Students raid it before class; after
midnight its customers are patrol officers, on-call medics and people avoiding
both.
