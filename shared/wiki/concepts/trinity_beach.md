# Tea Party Beach

A long, clean beach of white sand on the [[trinity_seafront]], with changing
huts and a beach shack. It used to be the private beach of the [[tea_party]];
since it was opened to every student, it is packed in summer. Out of season it
is a good place to collect shells and be alone.
