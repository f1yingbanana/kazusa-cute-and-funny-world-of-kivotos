# Azusa Shirasu's Room

[[azusa-shirasu|Azusa]]'s room in the [[trinity_magnolia_east|East Wing]] is
almost bare: a cot made with military corners and equipment laid out in rows.
At night she strings a tripwire across the doorway out of habit; she doesn't
lock the door, she traps it. The single exception to the bareness is a shelf of
[[momo_friends]] plushes, Skullman in the middle, each one a present.
