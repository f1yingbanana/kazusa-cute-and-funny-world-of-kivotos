# Splash Helmet Gang

The Splash Helmet Gang is one of the many crews of the [[helmet_gang]], led by
its captain [[rabu-komakaze]]. Its members have made a home of the
[[slumpia_haunted_mansion|old haunted-house ride]] in
[[du_abandoned_park|Slumpia]], and they do not get along with the other thugs
who use the park.

## Inside the gang

Rabu is rough with everyone and protective of her own: she skips the gang's
parties so the others can have fun, and when money is short she fights with the
butt of her shotgun instead of spending live rounds. The gang repaints its
helmets often and scuffs them up again with powdered walnut shells so they do
not look new.
