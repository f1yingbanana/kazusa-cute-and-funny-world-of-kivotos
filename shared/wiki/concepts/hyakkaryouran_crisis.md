# Hyakkaryouran and the Lantern Festivals

During a Lantern Festival in [[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako]],
[[natures_beauty]] spread rumors and unleashed dangerous spirits. The
[[hyakkaryouran_resolution_council|Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council]], with
[[sensei|Sensei]]'s help, protected the festival. A later crisis brought the
council into conflict with Nature's Beauty again, and with the mystery of its
missing former president.

## The council's rift

The council had fallen into disarray after its former president, Ayame,
disappeared and [[nagusa-goryou|Nagusa]] withdrew, leaving the
[[hundred_lotus|Hundred Lotus]] with [[kikyou-kiryuu|Kikyou]].
[[yukari-kadenokouji|Yukari]] tried to bring it back together, while
[[renge-fuwa|Renge]] and Kikyou struggled with their own fears and
disagreements. During the first festival crisis, the members finally
acknowledged the hurt between them and reunited, and Nagusa took up the Hundred
Lotus again.

## Ayame

In the later crisis Nagusa confronted her own expectations and reached out to
Ayame, who had been lost in the [[great_snowfield]]. The council carried on
together after the danger passed, and Nagusa stayed beside her recovering
friend.
