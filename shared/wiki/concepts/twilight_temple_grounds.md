# Twilight Temple

A small temple standing in perpetual dusk somewhere in the
[[great_snowfield|Great Snowfield]], hard to find and harder to describe. Nobody
can give directions to it; those who arrive say they simply kept walking.
Stories tie it to [[kuzunoha|Kuzunoha]] and to the history of the
[[hyakkaryouran_resolution_council|Hyakkaryouran]].

## Its founder

The temple is connected to the founder of the Hyakkaryouran, and through her to
the council's authority.
