# Trinity Cathedral

A vast white cathedral between [[church_street]] and the campus of
[[trinity_school]], its spires visible from anywhere in town. It is the
headquarters of the [[sisterhood]], heir to the old Justina Council of Saints.
Anyone may come in to pray or sit in the nave, and many students do; services
are at 07:00 and 18:00.

## Behind the altar

Behind the altar are the Sisters' [[trinity_vestry|vestry]] and offices; behind
the cathedral, the [[trinity_cathedral_cloister|cloister]] where Sisters live
and a [[trinity_cathedral_garden|walled garden]] that only they may enter.
Older halls beyond the storerooms are closed to everyone.
