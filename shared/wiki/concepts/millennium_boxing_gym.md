# Faraday Boxing Gym

A one-room gym at the edge of [[millennium_euler_heights|Euler Heights]], with a
ring, a row of heavy bags patched with tape, rusty weights and a radio that only
gets one station. Only a handful of regulars come, and the old owner leaves a
key under the mat for the ones who train at dawn.
