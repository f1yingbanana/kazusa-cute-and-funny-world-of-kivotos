# Wild Hunt Painting Studios

Long studios in the [[wildhunt_art_center|Art Center]] lit through tall north
windows, with rows of easels, a sink crusted with every colour and racks of
canvases drying between critiques. It smells of turpentine. Classes run 09:00 to
15:00 and students paint on until 21:00. Fine-art students have their practicals
here, and the instructors' comments stay chalked on the board for days.
