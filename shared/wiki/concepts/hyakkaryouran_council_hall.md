# Hyakkaryouran Council Hall

A long tatami room with a low table covered by a hand-drawn map of the town's
streets, patrol routes inked across it. Duty rosters hang on the sliding doors,
a kettle sits gone cold by the table, and a hanging scroll fills the alcove.

## Council business

The [[hyakkaryouran_resolution_council]] meets here at 07:00 and 17:00 and keeps
someone on watch through the night. The scroll in the alcove dates from the
council's founding.
