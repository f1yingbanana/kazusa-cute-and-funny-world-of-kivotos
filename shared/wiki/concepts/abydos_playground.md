# Abydos Playground

A running track you can still trace under the sand, goalposts leaning with the
wind, a rusty bench and a single faucet whose pipe needs fixing every few
weeks. Dumbbells left by some long-gone club lie by the fence. Old sports gear
turns up when the wind shifts the dunes, which is why people sometimes dig here
for treasure.
