# Gehenna Academy Library

Two floors of the [[gehenna_second_building|Second School Building]] given to
high shelves, green-shaded lamps and long tables that are scorched only at the
edges. Business, engineering and philosophy are better stocked than anyone
expects, and the window carrels are where people go when they don't want to be
found studying.
