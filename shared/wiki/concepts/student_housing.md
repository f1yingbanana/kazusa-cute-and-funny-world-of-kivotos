# Where Kivotos Students Live

Most students live in their academy's dormitories, a short walk or bus ride
from campus. Dorm doors lock at night, there are curfews and, at the stricter
schools, a nightly roll call; breaking curfew is common, and so are the
punishments. Rooms are small, the shared kitchens and laundries are where
neighbours actually meet, and every dorm has its legends.

Some exceptions everyone knows about: [[rabbit_platoon|RABBIT Platoon]],
refusing to leave D.U. after SRT closed, camps in tents in
[[usagi_park|Kousagi Park]]. Highlander's crews sleep in
[[highlander_crew_quarters|quarters]] at D.U. Central Station. Red Winter's
Labor Party crews bunk beside their work sites in D.U., and
[[odyssey_maritime_high_school|Odyssey]]'s students live aboard their school's
ships.

## By school

- **Trinity**: several dormitories, such as [[trinity_magnolia_hall|Magnolia
  Hall]] and [[trinity_dorm|Willow Hall]], with curfews and roll call from the
  top floor down. Some are grand; the [[trinity_old_quarters|Old Living
  Quarters]] are nearly empty, thin-walled, and have rats.
- **Gehenna**: dormitories as loud as the rest of Gehenna, such as
  [[gehenna_dorm13|Dormitory No. 13]]; curfews are announced and ignored in
  equal measure.
- **Millennium**: the [[millennium_dormitory|Millennium Dormitory]], with long
  hallways and a laundry that never closes. Some students practically live in
  their club rooms.
- **Red Winter**: the [[red_winter_first_dormitory|First Shared Dormitory]],
  cheap and famous for rarely having hot water. Special classes live apart, in
  facilities on the outskirts.
- **Valkyrie**: assigned dormitories with numbered rooms, such as
  [[valkyrie_dorm_4|Dormitory 4]].
- **Wild Hunt**: strictly managed dormitories in the
  [[wildhunt_dorm_quarter|Dormitory Quarter]]; the Arts Council assigns rooms
  and roommates, the Dorm Supervisory Unit enforces the rules, and leaving the
  fortress needs an outing pass.
- **Hyakkiyako**: boarding houses and dorms in town, such as
  [[hyakkiyako_tsukimi_dorm|Tsukimi-sou]], and the
  [[ebisu_dormitory|Ebisu Dormitory]] in the north.
- **Abydos**: no dormitory any more. The few remaining students live in the
  half-empty residential blocks of [[abydos_town|the town]].
