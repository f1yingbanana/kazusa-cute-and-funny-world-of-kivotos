# Old Cathedral of Communion

A cathedral of great age on the [[trinity_outskirts|edge of Trinity]], its roof
partly open to the sky. The [[eden_treaty]] was signed here. Tourists wander
the nave by day; the crypt stairs, behind a rope, lead down into the
[[trinity_catacombs]].
