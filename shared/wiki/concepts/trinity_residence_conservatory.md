# Tea Party Residence Conservatory

A Victorian glasshouse off the sitting room of the [[trinity_tea_residence]],
full of herbs, citrus trees and small tea bushes and warm all year, with a
wrought-iron table set for two where tea is served in the evening. Someone
tends the plants carefully and labels every pot by hand.
