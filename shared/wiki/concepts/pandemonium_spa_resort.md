# Pandemonium Spa Resort

A large, gaudy inn in the [[gehenna_hot_spring_village|hot-spring village]], run
by the [[pandemonium_society|council]], with open-air baths looking over the
valley, a lounge of massage chairs and a gift shop of council-branded
merchandise. Guests are treated like royalty as long as they pay.
