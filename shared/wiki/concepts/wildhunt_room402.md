# Dormitory No. 5, Room 402

A three-bed room on the fourth floor of [[wildhunt_dorm5]], always locked, with
a towel along the door crack. It is crowded: one side is a tangle of modded
televisions under a catapult blueprint, a desk holds a row of vintage
typewriters and campus maps criss-crossed with routes, and under another bed are
chisels and rock samples, with a half-carved stone on the windowsill.

## Whose it is

Home and headquarters of the [[free_trade_cartel]]: [[miyo-sakurai|Miyo]],
[[fuyu-wakasa|Fuyu]] and [[ritsu-usuba|Ritsu]]. The televisions and the
blueprint are Fuyu's; the typewriters, the route maps and a resignation letter
she has never handed in are Miyo's; the chisels and the stone are Ritsu's.
