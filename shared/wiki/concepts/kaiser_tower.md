# Kaiser Corporation Tower

The black-glass headquarters of [[kaiser_corporation]], the largest corporation
in Kivotos. A marble lobby with a reception desk, a branch of Kaiser Loan and
armed security robots at the gates to the lifts. Students with debts know the
loan counter too well.
