# Shanhaijing Valley Trip

Students connected to Genryumon, the Black Tortoise Promenade and Plum Blossom
Garden visited a valley resort while investigating a business dispute. They
helped local merchants facing trouble and returned with a better understanding
of the tensions between their groups.
