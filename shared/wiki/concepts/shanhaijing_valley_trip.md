# Shanhaijing Valley Trip

Students from the [[xuanlong_office]], the [[xuanwu_merchant_association]] and
the [[plum_blossom_garden]] went together to a valley resort to look into a
business dispute. They ended up helping local merchants out of trouble, and came
back understanding a little better the tensions between their own groups.
