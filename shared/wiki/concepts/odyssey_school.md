# Odyssey Fleet Berth, Port Terminal 3

Dozens of ships moored along Port Terminal 3 of [[kanesaki_port]], gathered
around the flagship [[odyssey_olympus|Olympus]]: this is
[[odyssey_maritime_high_school]], a flotilla rather than a campus. Its only land
is the terminal itself, rented from the port. Sailors cross the gangways at all
hours, every gangway is guarded, and visitors sign in at the
[[odyssey_gate_office|gate office]] before anyone lets them aboard.

## Life afloat

The school is run by its "Island" leadership aboard the flagship. Students live,
study and work afloat, follow sailors' taboos to the letter, and brew kelp where
others brew tea. Every two years the flotilla casts off for about a year at sea
with no contact but the [[general_student_council]].
