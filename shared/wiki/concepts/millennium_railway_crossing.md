# Euler Heights Railway Crossing

Red lights, bells, and barriers that drop for the school's trains every few
minutes in the morning. Students heading from
[[millennium_euler_heights|Euler Heights]] to the
[[millennium_school|campus]]'s south gate bunch up here waiting, which is how
half of Euler Heights knows the other half.
