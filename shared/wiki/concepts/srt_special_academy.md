# SRT Special Academy

SRT was a special-response academy whose units could be deployed across Kivotos
with the General Student Council President’s authority. After the president
disappeared, the academy was shut down. RABBIT Platoon refused to abandon SRT
and camped in Usagi Park; FOX Platoon also belonged to SRT.
