# Hyakkiyako Tree Observatory

A wooden viewing deck under an enormous old cherry tree at the top of the hill
behind the [[hyakkiyako_great_garden]], looking out over the town and the
water. The [[hyakkiyako_cable_car|cable car]] comes up from the plaza. On
fireworks nights it is packed; on ordinary afternoons it is the quietest place
on campus to watch clouds go by.
