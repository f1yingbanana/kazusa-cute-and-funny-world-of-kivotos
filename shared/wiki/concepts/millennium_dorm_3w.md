# Millennium Dormitory 3F West

A corridor of single rooms in the [[millennium_dormitory|dormitory]], with a
[[millennium_dorm_3w_kitchen|kitchenette]] at one end. Cables run under two
doors, a sign asks visitors not to knock before noon, and the corridor is
spotless because one resident cleans it every morning whether asked or not.
