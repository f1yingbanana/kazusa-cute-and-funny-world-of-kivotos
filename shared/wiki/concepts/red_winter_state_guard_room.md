# State Guard Duty Room

A narrow room off the entrance hall of the
[[red_winter_secretariat|Office Building]] where the State Guard keeps its rifle
rack, a samovar that is never cold and the duty roster. Officers coming off
shift thaw out here.

## The Guard

Their loyalty runs to whoever is president this week.
