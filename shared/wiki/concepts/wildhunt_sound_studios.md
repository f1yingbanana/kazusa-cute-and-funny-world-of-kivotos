# Wild Hunt Sound Studios

Soundproofed recording booths, a mixing room and rehearsal rooms padded with
foam, in the [[wildhunt_art_center|Art Center]]. Music and voice-acting students
book them by the hour from 08:00 to 21:00, and the corridor hums with muffled
scales. One booth's lock has been broken for years, and it gets used at odd
times by people who didn't book it.
