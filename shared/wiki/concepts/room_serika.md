# Serika's Room

Everything in [[serika-kuromi|Serika]]'s small flat is about work: uniforms
from three jobs on hooks, a calendar with shifts in three colours, flyers to
hand out tomorrow. The coin jar on the shelf says DEBT in marker. The bathtub
is her one luxury after a late shift.

## Under the bed

A cheap keyboard lives under the bed for when she misses the piano.
