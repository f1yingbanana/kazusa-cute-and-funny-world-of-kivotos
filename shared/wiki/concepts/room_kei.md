# Kei's Room

[[kei-tendou|Kei]]'s room is spotless, with a rack of hair ribbons sorted by
colour, fashion magazines squared on the desk, and a framed photo of her and her
sister [[aris-tendou|Aris]] in matching poses. A handheld console hidden under
the pillow is, she will say, confiscated property.
