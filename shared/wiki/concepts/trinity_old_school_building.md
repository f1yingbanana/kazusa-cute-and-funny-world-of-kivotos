# Trinity Old School Building

An old three-storey school building on the [[trinity_outskirts]], emptied years
ago and reopened for students of the [[arius_school|Arius school]] preparing
for Trinity's entrance exam. The classrooms have mismatched desks, fresh paint
over old graffiti and a timetable taped to the door. Trinity volunteers teach
some of the lessons.

## How it came to be

[[nagisa-kirifuji|Nagisa]] arranged the space during the
[[arius_reconstruction|reconstruction of Arius]], so that its students could
study for the comprehensive exam.
