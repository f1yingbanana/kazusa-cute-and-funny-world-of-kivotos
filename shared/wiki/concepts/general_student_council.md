# General Student Council

The General Student Council governs Kivotos as a whole: it handles matters that
cross [[academy_districts|district]] lines, from [[gsc_headquarters|Sanctum
Tower]] at the centre of [[du_downtown|D.U.]] Its President brought
[[sensei|Sensei]] to Kivotos and founded [[schale]], then disappeared, leaving
the council and the academies to get through a long period of uncertainty
without her.

## Offices

The council works through offices, each responsible for one area of
government. The Transportation Office, for one, runs the D.U. Metro (see
[[kivotos_transit]]).
