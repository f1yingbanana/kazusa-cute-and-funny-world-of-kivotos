# Hideout No. 86

Behind the boiler of the [[gehenna_old_bathhouse|old Matsu-no-yu bathhouse]]:
geological survey maps pinned over every wall, a box of detonators, a futon and
a row of spare slippers. [[kasumi-kinugawa|Kasumi]] moves between hideouts, but
this is the one she returns to.

## Under the blanket

Engineering textbooks, hidden where nobody would look.
