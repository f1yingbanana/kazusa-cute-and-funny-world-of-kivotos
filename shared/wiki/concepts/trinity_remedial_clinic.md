# Remedial Knights Central Ward

The campus infirmary of [[trinity_school]], run by the
[[knights_hospitaller|Remedial Knights]]: a row of white beds behind curtains,
a treatment room that smells of antiseptic, and a front desk where a Knight is
always on duty. Students come in with sprains, bullet grazes and the occasional
imaginary illness. The Knights are famously thorough; some say too thorough,
since patients have been known to leave with more bandages than injuries.

## Upstairs

The Knights' own club room: a sofa for whoever just came off night duty, the
rota board that splits them between the ward, [[trinity_hospital]] and patrols,
and a good tea set. The captain, [[mine-aomori|Mine]], hosts tea there most
weekdays at 15:30. Supplies are kept in the
[[trinity_knights_storage|storage room]] behind the ward.
