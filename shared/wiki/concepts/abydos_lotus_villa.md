# Lotus Court Villa

A merchant's house from the district's richest years, restored and spotless,
on the last street in Abydos with anything green on it. It is the only house
with working air conditioning and a gardener's hedge, and the gate is locked at
night. [[nonomi-izayoi|Nonomi]] leases it and lives here alone, though it is
much too big for one person, so the
[[foreclosure_task_force|Task Force's]] parties tend to end up in its
[[lotus_villa_parlour|parlour]].

## The family

Nonomi chose to live apart from her wealthy family. A family butler still
calls now and then to see that she has everything she needs.
