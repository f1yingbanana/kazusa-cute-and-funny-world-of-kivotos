# 12th Millennium Stadium

An all-weather stadium at the far end of the [[millennium_school|campus]],
with retractable seating and a big screen, used for athletic festivals and
inter-school games. On game days part-time sellers climb the stands with drink
tanks on their backs. The [[millennium_baseball_grounds|baseball grounds]], the
[[millennium_fitness_center|Fitness Center]] and the
[[millennium_infirmary|infirmary]] cluster behind it.
