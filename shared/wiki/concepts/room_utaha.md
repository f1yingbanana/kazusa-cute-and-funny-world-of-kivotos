# Utaha's Room

[[utaha-shiraishi|Utaha]]'s room is half bedroom, half workshop: a pegboard of
tools she would not let anyone else touch, bins of robot parts, a lab coat with
proud grease stains, and a second charging dock in case the big robot visits.
The bed is often unslept in; the [[millennium_engineering|hangar]] keeps her
late.
