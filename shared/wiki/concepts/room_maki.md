# Maki's Room

Every wall of [[maki-konuri|Maki]]'s room is a mural, painted over twice
already after inspections. Spray cans stand in rainbow order on a shelf, a
console and a phone are always charging, and a sketch of the [[veritas]] emblem
is pinned above the bed.
