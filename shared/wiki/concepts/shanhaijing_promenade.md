# Black Tortoise Promenade

A street of lanterns and steaming baskets in every window, hawkers calling out
the day's dishes over the crowd. Once a middling shopping street, it is now the
best eating in Kivotos. The [[xuanwu_merchant_association]] runs it from the
[[shanhaijing_main_restaurant]] at its heart; the [[shanhaijing_noodle_house]],
the [[shanhaijing_sweets_cafe]] and, at the far end, the
[[shanhaijing_dawn_market]] line the rest of it.

## Between the stalls

Genryumon inspectors are never welcome here, and their inspections are a running
feud with the [[xuanlong_office]]. When one goes sideways, it is usually
[[reijo-kayama]] who settles it.
