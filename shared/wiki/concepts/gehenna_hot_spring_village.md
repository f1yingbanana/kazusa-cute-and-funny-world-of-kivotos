# Hinnom Hot Spring Village

Steep lanes of inns, foot baths and souvenir shops on the flank of
[[hinnom_volcano]], selling spring-steamed eggs and towels, with steam rising
from the gutters. The [[pandemonium_society|council]] owns a share of it,
including the [[pandemonium_spa_resort|Pandemonium Spa Resort]], and says so on
every sign.
