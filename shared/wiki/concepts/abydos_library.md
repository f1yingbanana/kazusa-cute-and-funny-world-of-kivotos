# Abydos School Library

Tall shelves from the days when Abydos had thousands of students, now read by a
handful. Nobody sits at the desk, sand gathers on the sills, and a reading
table by the window catches the afternoon light.

## Among the shelves

The old student council's records and minutes are kept here, going back
decades, alongside district records nobody else has copies of. Some of the
books might be worth something to a collector; a few already have price slips
tucked inside, waiting for a trip to [[abydos_antiques|the antiques dealer]].
