# Hotaru Building

A narrow, stained building in the [[gehenna_backstreets|Zone 32 backstreets]],
with a pawnshop on the ground floor, a small office on the second and rented
rooms on the third and fourth. The stairs creak, the landlord is rarely seen,
and rent is due on the first.

## Tenants

The office is [[problem_solver_office|Problem Solver 68's]], and the agency's
members, [[aru-rikuhachima|Aru]], [[mutsuki-asagi|Mutsuki]],
[[kayoko-onikata|Kayoko]] and [[haruka-igusa|Haruka]], rent the rooms upstairs
and share the [[gehenna_hotaru_washroom|washroom]] on the landing.
