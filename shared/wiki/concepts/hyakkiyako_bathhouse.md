# Sakurayu Bathhouse

A tiled public bath in the [[hyakkiyako_shirakawa_quarter]], fed by a hot
spring, with a cherry tree painted across the wall above the tubs. Students
drift in after club, the tenants of the [[hyakkiyako_kawabata_house]] next door
bathe here every night, and the bench by the milk fridge is where the day's
gossip gets settled.
