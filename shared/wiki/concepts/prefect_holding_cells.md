# Prefect Team Holding Cells

A row of barred cells in the basement of
[[prefect_headquarters|Prefect Team Headquarters]], with benches, a guard desk,
and repair patches on the walls where someone dug, blasted or melted their way
out. Regulars have favourite cells.
