# Millennium Dormitory 2F West Kitchenette

The [[millennium_dorm_2w|2F West]] corridor's kitchenette: two hobs, a
microwave, a rice cooker left on warm for midnight noodles, and a fridge half
full of ice-cream tubs and energy drinks. The sink is never quite empty, and the
corridor argues about whose turn it is.
