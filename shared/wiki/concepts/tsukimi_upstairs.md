# Tsukimi-sou Upstairs Corridor

A polished wooden corridor upstairs in the [[hyakkiyako_tsukimi_dorm]], lined
with the sliding doors of residents' tatami rooms, with the
[[tsukimi_dining_room|dining room]] off it. After the 22:30 roll call it goes
quiet.

## Residents

The rooms belong to [[room_chise|Chise]], [[room_kaede|Kaede]],
[[room_kaho|Kaho]], [[room_mimori|Mimori]], [[room_niya|Niya]] and
[[room_tsubaki|Tsubaki]].
