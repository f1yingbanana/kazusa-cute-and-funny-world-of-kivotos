# Dorm Supervisory Unit Post

A guardroom in the arch of the [[wildhunt_dorm_quarter|Dormitory Quarter]],
where the Dorm Supervisory Unit keeps the curfew log, a radio and a shelf of
things taken off students. Night patrols leave from here on the half hour
between 21:30 and 06:00.
