# Highlander Central Control Center

The Central Control Center is the part of [[highlander_railroad_academy]] that
runs the trains and handles much of the railway's coordination and
administration: its dispatchers watch every Highlander line from the
[[highlander_control|operations floor]] in the academy's wing of
[[du_central_station]], around the clock. Its officers also drive trains and
take tickets. The [[highlander_supervisory_office]] upstairs audits everything
it does.

## Inside

Among its officers are the twins [[hikari-tachibana]] and
[[nozomi-tachibana]], who are good at the work when they bother to do it and
better at slipping away from it. Mistakes made on the operations floor tend to
land on the [[highlander_freight_logistics]], whose engineers are tired of
cleaning up after the Control Center.
