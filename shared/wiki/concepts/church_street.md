# Trinity Street

Trinity's shopping street, sometimes still called Church Street: cobblestones,
striped awnings and flower boxes, running uphill from [[trinity_station]]
toward the campus of [[trinity_school]]. At the station end are the
[[trinity_game_center|game center]] and the [[trinity_galleria]] mall; halfway
up are [[trinity_patisserie]], [[trinity_bakery|a bakery]], a
[[trinity_parfait|24-hour parfait parlour]] and the covered
[[trinity_market]]; at the top, the dorm end, the [[angel_24_trinity|Angel
24]].

Students flood it after class and on weekends, and the [[vigilante_crew]] walk
it at night. Most shops close at 20:00, but the Angel 24 and the parfait
parlour stay open all night.
