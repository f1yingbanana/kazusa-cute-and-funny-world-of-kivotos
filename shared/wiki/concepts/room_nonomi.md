# Nonomi's Room

[[nonomi-izayoi|Nonomi]]'s airy bedroom upstairs in the villa: overstuffed
cushions, a vanity, a cleaning caddy, shopping bags she hasn't opened yet and a
reversi board left mid-game. The framed photo on the nightstand is of the five
of the [[foreclosure_task_force|Task Force]] crowded onto the club room sofa.

## The drawer

A gold card sits in the drawer. She is not allowed to use it on the debt.
