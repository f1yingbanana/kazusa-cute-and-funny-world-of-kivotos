# Gehenna Second School Building

A squarer, newer building of [[gehenna_school]] beside the
[[gehenna_first_building|first]], with lifts that work half the time. Third-year
classrooms fill the upper floors; the [[gehenna_library|library]] takes the
second, and the [[gehenna_infirmary|infirmary]] has its own side door where
stretchers come in at a run.
