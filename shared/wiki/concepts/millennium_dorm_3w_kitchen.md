# Millennium Dormitory 3F West Kitchenette

The [[millennium_dorm_3w|3F West]] corridor's kitchenette: two hobs, a kettle
that is always warm, a tea caddy labeled in careful handwriting, and a fridge
stacked with energy drinks. It is the cleanest kitchenette in the building,
wiped down every morning before anyone else is up.
