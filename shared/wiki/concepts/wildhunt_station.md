# Wild Hunt Station

A small Italianate station by the canal at the end of the Western Line, its
waiting room hung with student paintings for sale. Trains leave for
[[du_central_station|D.U. Central]] every 15 minutes from 05:30 to 23:30. From
here the quay leads into the [[wildhunt_canal_town|canal town]] and on to the
fortress of [[wild_hunt_academy|Wild Hunt]].
