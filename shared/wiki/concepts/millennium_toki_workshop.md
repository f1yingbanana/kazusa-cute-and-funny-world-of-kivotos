# C&C Equipment Bay

A walled bay off the floor of the [[millennium_engineering|Engineering
Hangar]], kept clinically tidy: a charging rig for a powered suit, labeled parts
drawers, a maintenance bench, and a folding chair with a tablet propped on it,
paused on some streamer's channel.

## Whose bay it is

It belongs to [[cleaning_and_clearing|C&C]], and in practice to
[[toki-asuma|Toki]], who keeps the only key and maintains her powered suit
here.
