# Gehenna Lawn Square

A broad lawn at the heart of [[gehenna_school]], ringed by stone paths and
colonnades, with a fountain in the middle and a row of vending machines under
the arcade. Everyone crosses it; students eat, nap, argue and occasionally shoot
each other here.
