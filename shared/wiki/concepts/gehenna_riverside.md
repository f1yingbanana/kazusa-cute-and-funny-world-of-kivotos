# Gehenna Riverside Cherry Path

A long riverside path under cherry trees behind [[gehenna_school]], with
benches, a grassy bank and a footbridge, and the
[[gehenna_teahouse|Yūgure Teahouse]] at its quiet end. It is one of the few calm
places in Gehenna, and people come here to breathe, nap or wait for something to
happen.
