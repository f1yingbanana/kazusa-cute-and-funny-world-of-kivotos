# Storehouse Loft on Kousagi Hill

The loft of an old shrine storehouse up an overgrown path on [[kousagi_hill]],
reached by a ladder; no one has visited in years. Fox masks hang from the
rafters, a parasol leans by the round window, and that window looks straight
across the park at the [[schale_office|SCHALE office]].

## Whose it is

[[wakamo-kosaka|Wakamo]] sleeps here between appearances.
