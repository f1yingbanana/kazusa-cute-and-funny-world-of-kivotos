# Kawabata Upstairs Corridor

A narrow upstairs corridor in the [[hyakkiyako_kawabata_house]] whose boards
creak under every step, with rented tatami rooms along it and the
[[kawabata_kitchen|shared kitchen]] at one end. Lights go out at 23:00.

## Tenants

The rooms belong to [[room_izuna|Izuna]], [[room_michiru|Michiru]],
[[room_nagusa|Nagusa]], [[room_pina|Pina]], [[room_tsukuyo|Tsukuyo]] and
[[room_umika|Umika]].
