# Slumpia Amusement Park

A theme park on the edge of [[shiratori_ward|Shiratori]] that closed years ago:
a stalled [[slumpia_ferris_wheel|Ferris wheel]], a carousel under tarps, ticket
booths with smashed windows. The fence has more holes than wire, and everyone
comes in by the [[slumpia_back_gate|back gate]]. Gangs and thrill-seekers use
it, and stories say there is something underneath.

## Who lives here

The [[splash_helmet_gang]] has made its home in the old
[[slumpia_haunted_mansion|haunted-house ride]].
