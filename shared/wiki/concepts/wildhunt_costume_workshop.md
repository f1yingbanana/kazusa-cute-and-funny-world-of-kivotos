# Wild Hunt Costume Workshop

A bright room in the [[wildhunt_art_center|Art Center]] with twelve sewing
machines, a cutting table and dress forms wearing half-made outfits. Pins are
everywhere, fashion and costume students work here from 09:00 until 20:00, and
the machines are rarely all quiet. The fabric shelves hold only what the academy
store approves, so students bring in their own ribbon and trim.
