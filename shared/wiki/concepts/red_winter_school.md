# Red Winter Federal Academy

Red Winter Federal Academy is the school of the snowbound
[[red_winter_district]], a huge academy that runs itself like a small state.
Its student government, the [[red_winter_office]], rules by decree under
President [[cherino-renkawa|Cherino Renkawa]], and is overthrown so often that
coups are almost part of the timetable; somehow Cherino keeps coming back.
[[tomoe-sashiro|Tomoe Sashiro]] assists her, and
[[marina-ikekura|Marina Ikekura]] leads the Security Committee.
Students it suspends are sent away to special classes on the edge of the
district.

Arriving, you pass State Guard sentries at a walled gate into a campus of heavy
stone buildings under a permanent crust of snow. The spire of the
[[red_winter_main_building|Main Building]], where classes are held, towers over
everything and can be seen from anywhere in the district; in front of it the
[[red_winter_secretariat|Office Building]] faces
[[red_winter_revolution_plaza|Red Mustache Revolution Plaza]] and the
president's statue.
Shovelled paths lead between the buildings; everything else is knee-deep.

## The campus

Around the plaza sit the [[red_winter_library|Federal Library]], the
[[red_winter_cafeteria|Third Communal Cafeteria]], the Labor Party's converted
boiler house, the [[red_winter_labor_office|Workforce Office]], the
[[red_winter_broadcasting|Red Bear radio tower]] and the
[[red_winter_parade_ground|parade ground]]. The main gate is manned from 06:30
until it is chained at 22:00.

## How the academy is run

The Office governs through decrees read from the Office Building's balcony and
broadcast over Red Bear radio. The Security Committee keeps order and the State
Guard guards the government, loyal to whoever is president that week. Science
and engineering students who cause trouble go to
[[red_winter_special_class_183|No. 183 Special Class]] at a remote facility;
other suspended students are kept at the Former Institute as
[[special_class_227|No. 227 Special Class]]. Clubs and departments such as the
[[publishing_department|Publishing Department]], the
[[red_winter_public_works|Public Works Department]] and the
[[knowledge_liberation_front|Knowledge Liberation Front]] live on what budget the
Office allows them.
