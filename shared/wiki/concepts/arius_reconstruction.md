# Arius Reconstruction and the Comprehensive Exam

After the [[eden_treaty_crisis|Eden Treaty crisis]], some [[arius_school]]
students began studying at [[trinity_school|Trinity]] while others stayed in
the [[arius_district]]. [[sensei]], the [[arius_squad]] and Trinity's
representatives arranged lessons and a chance to sit a comprehensive exam.
[[nagisa-kirifuji|Nagisa]] arranged space for the Arius students at Trinity,
and they now take exam classes in the [[trinity_old_school_building]]. The
Arius Squad was granted parole with support from Sensei and other schools.

## The crisis at Porta Pacis

[[subaru-kakehashi|Subaru Kakehashi]] and the students of the
[[nicomedia_troop]] struggled with distrust of outsiders and the weight of
Arius's history. A crisis at the [[arius_porta_pasis|Porta Pacis]] archives
brought those tensions to a head. Maia, the Squad and Sensei reached out to
Subaru, and students from several schools helped care for those involved.
Afterwards the students went on rebuilding Arius and studying together.
