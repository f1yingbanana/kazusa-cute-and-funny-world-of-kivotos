# Gehenna Dormitory No. 2

A turn-of-the-century mansion in [[gehenna_zone3|Zone 3]] converted into a
dormitory, with a carved staircase, big windows and rooms twice the size of
anyone else's. The [[pandemonium_society|council]] pays for most of its rooms
and treats it as its own.

## Who lives here

[[makoto-hanuma|Makoto]], [[ibuki-tanga|Ibuki]], [[chiaki-motomiya|Chiaki]] and
[[satsuki-kyougoku|Satsuki]] of the council, and [[haruna-kurodate|Haruna]] of
the [[gourmet_research_society]]. They meet in the
[[gehenna_dorm2_parlor|parlour]].
