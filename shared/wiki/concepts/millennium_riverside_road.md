# Millennium Riverside Road

A long embankment with the river on one side and the
[[millennium_district_park|park]] on the other, lined with streetlights,
benches and a few food carts. Good for running, for walking home slowly, and
for sitting on the grass slope watching the trains cross the far bridge.
