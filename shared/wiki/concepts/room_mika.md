# Mika Misono's Room

[[mika-misono|Mika]]'s room is a cramped attic at the very end of the top floor
of the [[trinity_old_quarters]], reached up the emergency stairs. The ceiling
slopes, the walls are thin and something scratches inside them at night. She
has covered every surface with accessories and jewellery, filled a jar with
seashells, and strung fairy lights over the damp patches. A copy of her
probation terms is pinned by the door.
