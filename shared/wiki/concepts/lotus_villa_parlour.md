# Lotus Court Villa Parlour

A sitting room with deep sofas and a big table, joined to a kitchen far bigger
than one person needs. The fridge is kept full of snacks for whoever drops by,
one sofa has a dent where a certain upperclassman naps, and a reversi set lives
on the coffee table.
