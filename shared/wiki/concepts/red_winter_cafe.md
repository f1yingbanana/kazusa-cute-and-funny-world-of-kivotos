# Café Matryoshka

Shelves of painted nesting dolls, steamed windows and a long counter of blini
and cocoa. The window seats look straight onto
[[red_winter_3rd_street|3rd Street]], so it is the place to watch who goes by
with whom; sketchers and gossips take those seats first.
