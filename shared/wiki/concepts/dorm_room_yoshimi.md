# Yoshimi's Room

[[yoshimi-ibaragi|Yoshimi]]'s room on the top floor of [[trinity_dorm]]: a
handheld console always charging by the bed, arcade tokens in a jar, and
handwritten dessert rankings pinned to the wall. She means to lock the door and
often forgets; the key is usually somewhere under the snack wrappers.
