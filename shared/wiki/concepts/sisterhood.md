# Sisterhood

The Sisterhood is [[trinity_school]]'s influential religious order. Based at
[[trinity_sisterhood]], its Sisters lead prayer, hear confessions and offer
spiritual guidance and care to students. They dress in veils and dark nun-like
habits.

It is led by [[sakurako-utazumi]].

## Inside the Sisterhood

- [[mari-iochi]] is a young Sister many students go to with their worries.
- [[hinata-wakaba]] manages the cathedral's supplies and does its heavy
  lifting.
- The Sisterhood became more directly involved in Trinity's affairs during the
  [[eden_treaty_crisis]], and now helps the [[tea_party]] and the
  [[knights_hospitaller]] run the school, not always smoothly.
- It once tried to recruit [[hanako-urawa]], who kept it at a distance.
