# Festival Operations Department

The Festival Operations Department organizes the festivals that fill
[[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako]]'s calendar: contracts, vendors, stages and
stall placements. Between festivals it runs the [[momoyodou]] tea house. It has
three members: its president [[shizuko-kawawa|Shizuko Kawawa]], who is also
Momoyodou's owner and poster girl, the film-loving
[[pina-asahina|Pina Asahina]], and the festival researcher
[[umika-satohama|Umika Satohama]].

## Inside the department

Shizuko holds the department to its codes and ethics and does most of the
negotiating herself. Pina likes meetings opened with a ceremonial taiko
drumroll and quotes her movies at every turn. Umika's research proposals
regularly feature tomato catapults, armored racing vehicles or live fire on
stage; Shizuko has learned to wait for the idea that comes after, which is
usually the right one.

Shizuko's plans to expand Momoyodou once led the department into the
[[momoyodou_resort_plan]].
