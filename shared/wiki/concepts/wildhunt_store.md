# Wild Hunt Academy Store

The academy's shop off the [[wildhunt_piazza|piazza]], open 08:00 to 19:00:
paper, brushes, inspected paints, snacks and toiletries. Everything on its
shelves has passed the approved-materials list, which is why the selection is
thin and the demand for anything else is high.
