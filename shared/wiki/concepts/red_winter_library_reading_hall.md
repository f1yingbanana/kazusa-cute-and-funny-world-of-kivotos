# Federal Library Reading Hall

Long oak tables under green lamps, the circulation desk by the door with a stamp
and a stack of loan cards, and a hand-lettered sign about treating books kindly.
The only heater in the [[red_winter_library|library]] stands at one end, so
readers crowd that end.
