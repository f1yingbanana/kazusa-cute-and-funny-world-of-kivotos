# Shittim Chest Classroom

A quiet classroom surrounded by shallow water reaching to the horizon. A desk,
chairs, and [[arona|ARONA]]'s familiar umbrella make it feel like home. This is
an enclosed digital space inside the [[shittim_chest]], apart from the streets
and buildings of Kivotos.

## No way out

There is no door and no road from here to anywhere else. ARONA stays here and
helps [[sensei|Sensei]] through the Shittim Chest's console without leaving.
Changing how she appears, or opening this room to the outside world, is not a
trip she can take on her own: only Sensei can ask for it, deliberately.

The Shittim Chest itself is kept in a room in
[[schale_basement|SCHALE's basement]]; that is where this classroom is, as far
as the city is concerned, but it has no door there. Placing it there on the
map does not describe a physical classroom door or permission for ARONA to
leave.
