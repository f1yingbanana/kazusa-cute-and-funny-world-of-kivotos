# Angel 24, Red Winter 3rd Street Branch

A bright corner store of the [[angel_24|Angel 24]] chain at the station end of
[[red_winter_3rd_street|3rd Street]] that never closes. Late at night it is the
only warm, lit room on the street: students coming off the last train, dorm
residents after curfew and night-shift guards all meet over the hot water
dispenser.
