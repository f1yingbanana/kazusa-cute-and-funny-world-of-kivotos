# Shiroko's Room

[[shiroko-sunaookami|Shiroko]]'s fourth-floor flat is spare and practical: a
road bike hung on the wall, a pull-up bar in the doorway, running shoes by the
door and a whiteboard covered in cycling routes and suspiciously detailed floor
plans of banks.

## Kept things

A robber's balaclava lies folded in a drawer. The scarf she was given the
winter she was found hangs on its own hook.
