# Valkyrie Headquarters Building

The oldest building on campus, four floors of offices around a stairwell papered
with wanted posters and duty rosters, with a light on somewhere at every hour.
[[valkyrie_public_safety|Public Safety]] has the ground floor nearest the
street, [[valkyrie_public_peace|Public Peace]] the top floor nearest the coffee,
and the Security Bureau sits in between and makes sure both know it. Rival
bureaus share the lifts.
