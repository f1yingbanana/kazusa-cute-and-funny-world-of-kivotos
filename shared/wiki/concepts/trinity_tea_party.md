# Tea Party Chamber

A white hall with a domed roof on the highest point of the campus of
[[trinity_school]], seat of the [[tea_party]], the student council that governs
Trinity. A round council chamber with three chairs takes up the centre, offices
and archives the wings. Guards at the door turn away anyone without an
appointment.

Business is done over tea. The [[trinity_tea_terrace]] and the Tea Party's
private [[trinity_tea_library|library]] open off the chamber.
