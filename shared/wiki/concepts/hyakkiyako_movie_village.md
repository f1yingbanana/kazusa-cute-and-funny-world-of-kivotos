# Movie Village

An old period-drama film set kept as a street of samurai houses, fake
storefronts and watchtowers. Actors stage sword fights at set hours on the
[[hyakkiyako_movie_stage|open-air stage]], and tour guides lead flag-following
groups past the [[hyakkiyako_museum]], the
[[hyakkiyako_crafts_workshop|crafts workshop]] and the old
[[hyakkiyako_cinema|cinema]]. A gate opens onto the
[[hyakkiyako_shopping_district]], and the tour route runs on to
[[hyakkiyako_plaza]].

## Back alleys

Behind the fake storefronts, the narrow back alleys are the best place in town
to lose someone chasing you.
