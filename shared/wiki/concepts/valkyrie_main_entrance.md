# Valkyrie Main Entrance

A brick gatehouse with a glass front desk, a flagpole and a bench for people
waiting to file complaints. Visitors sign in here and lost property is handed
over; school groups on field trips are met here and shown the patrol bikes. At
the 08:30 shift change the gate fills with officers in both directions.
