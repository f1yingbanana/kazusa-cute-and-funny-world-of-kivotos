# Live House Gargoyle

A low brick cellar under the [[wildhunt_canal_town|canal town]] with a small
stage and a bar. Shows start at 19:00 on Fridays and Saturdays: student bands,
touring acts, and a monthly metal night that shakes the canal. Tickets are cheap
and it is loud. [[wild_hunt_academy|Wild Hunt]] students crowd the early sets
and leave at the interval in a rush, because the gate shuts at 22:00 whether the
encore has started or not; outsiders stay for the last train to D.U. at 23:30.
