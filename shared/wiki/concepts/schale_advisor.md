# SCHALE Advisor

[[sensei|Sensei]] is the adult advisor of [[schale|SCHALE]], appointed by the
[[general_student_council|General Student Council]] President before she
disappeared. Students from every academy come to Sensei for guidance and for
help with problems anywhere in Kivotos.
