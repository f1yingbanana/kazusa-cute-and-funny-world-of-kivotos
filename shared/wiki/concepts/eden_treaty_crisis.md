# Eden Treaty Crisis

[[arius_school|Arius]] attacked the signing of the [[eden_treaty]]. Students
from [[trinity_school|Trinity]] and [[gehenna_school|Gehenna]] rallied with
[[sensei|Sensei]]'s help and fought the attack off, and the crisis was
eventually brought to an end. It left Trinity's leadership shaken.

## The Make-Up Work Club

The [[make_up_work_club]] was formed while [[nagisa-kirifuji|Nagisa]] feared an
infiltrator would sabotage the treaty. [[hifumi-ajitani|Hifumi]],
[[azusa-shirasu|Azusa]], [[koharu-shimoe|Koharu]] and [[hanako-urawa|Hanako]]
studied together under Sensei and became friends despite the pressure. Azusa,
who came from Arius, chose to protect her friends and Trinity rather than carry
out the plan she had been sent for. The club's friendships outlived its
original purpose.

## Arius and Beatrice

The crisis exposed how Beatrice of [[gematria]] had been manipulating Arius. It
left [[mika-misono|Mika]], Nagisa and [[seia-yurizono|Seia]] with difficult
personal and political consequences. Later [[saori-joumae|Saori]] sought
Sensei's help to save [[atsuko-hakari|Atsuko]] from Beatrice, and Mika and the
[[arius_squad|Arius students]] helped bring the crisis to its end.
