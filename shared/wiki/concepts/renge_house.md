# Renge's House

A narrow two-room house at the end of a riverside lane in the
[[hyakkiyako_shirakawa_quarter]], which [[renge-fuwa]] has to herself.
Shelves of school-romance light novels, dumbbells under the bed, a white ribbon
tucked into the mirror frame and a "youth to-do list" pinned to the wall with
half the items ticked.

## The key

The door is kept locked, with a spare key under the planter.
