# No. 227 Bathhouse

One stone tub under a wooden roof behind the
[[red_winter_spec227|Former Institute]], renovated and steaming, with changing
rooms and staff quarters behind and a [[red_winter_milk_bar|milk bar]] by the
exit. Evening visitors come out on the shuttle train, soak, and sometimes miss
the last one back.

## What is left

It is all that remains of a grand [[red_winter_hot_springs|hot spring resort]]
that was built on the class's spring and then blown up.
[[special_class_227|No. 227 Special Class]] runs what is left.
