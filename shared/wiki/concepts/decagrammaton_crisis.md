# Decagrammaton and the Steel Continent

Decagrammaton was an AI that set out to prove its own divinity through
machines it called prophets, while a continent of steel spread outward from the
ruins. Students of [[millennium_school]], with [[sensei]], confronted it and
defeated it. Afterward [[kei-tendou|Kei]] had a body of her own, and
[[rio-tsukatsuki|Rio Tsukatsuki]] came back to Millennium.

## The investigation

Millennium's Super Phenomenon Task Force, the
[[paranormal_affairs_department]], investigated first:
[[himari-akeboshi|Himari]], [[eimi-izumimoto|Eimi]] and
[[toki-asuma|Toki]] followed the trail through the ruins with Sensei. There
they met three engineers, Ein, Sof and Ohr, and their sister Malkuth. The
engineers had created the ever-expanding Steel Continent.

## Kei and the fight

Rio and Himari helped restore Kei, who joined [[aris-tendou|Aris]], the
[[game_development_department]] and the other students against the danger.
Kei took her body from the Netzach facility on the Steel Continent. Along the
way the students met prophets with histories of their own, among them
Tiphareth, which had cared for a seed vault. They helped free Malkuth and
defeated Decagrammaton.

## Afterward

Kei joined her friends in a body of her own, and still hunts the remaining
prophets. Malkuth was offered time and shelter to decide what she wanted. Rio
returned to Millennium and faced the consequences of her earlier actions.
