# Red Winter Punch Card Registry

A cold basement hall under the [[red_winter_secretariat|Office Building]]. Red
Winter's student records were never digitised: they live here on punch cards in
rows of steel cabinets, read by a machine that clatters for minutes per query.

## The records

Suspensions, transfers and medals are all punched in by hand, which is why some
of them get lost.
