# Shimiko's Room

In [[shimiko-endou|Shimiko]]'s room in the [[trinity_magnolia_west|West Wing]],
every wall is a bookshelf, and the books have started on the floor too.
Handmade bookmarks hang drying on a string across the window, and a reading
lamp by the bed stays on all night, its glow showing under the door.
