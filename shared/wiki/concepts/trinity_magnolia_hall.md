# Magnolia Hall

The largest dormitory at [[trinity_school]]: a four-storey stone building
wrapped around a magnolia garden in the [[trinity_residential|Residential
Quarter]], closest to the [[school_gate|campus gate]]. Hundreds of students
live here. The ground floor has the entrance hall, the dorm mother's office and
the [[trinity_magnolia_dining|dining room]]; the residents' corridors go by
wings, and the hallways are loud every morning from 07:30.

Roll call is at 22:30, floor by floor. Two corridors are well known: the
second-floor [[trinity_magnolia_east|East Wing]], where the
[[make_up_work_club]] lives side by side, and the third-floor
[[trinity_magnolia_west|West Wing]], home to several [[justice_task_force]]
officers.
