# Trinity Shooting Range

An outdoor range behind the [[trinity_gymnasium]]: twelve lanes backed by high
earth berms, a wooden hut that sells ammunition and rents ear protection, and a
line of targets the groundskeeper repaints every Monday. At dawn it is so quiet
you can hear the birds between shots.
