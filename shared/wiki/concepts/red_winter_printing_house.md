# Red Bear Printing House

A brick works behind [[red_winter_3rd_street|3rd Street]] where the
[[publishing_department|Publishing Department]] prints the Red Bear newspaper,
magazines and licensed books. The presses thunder from early morning, rolls of
paper stand in the yard, and a newsstand at the street front sells the day's
issue. Upstairs is the [[red_winter_editorial_room|editorial room]].
