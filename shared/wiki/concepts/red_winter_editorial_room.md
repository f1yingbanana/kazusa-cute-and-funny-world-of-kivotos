# Red Bear Editorial Room

Desks under a skylight above the [[red_winter_printing_house|presses]], buried
in galley proofs and business proposals. The corner of
[[yakumo-aramaki|Yakumo]], head of the department, has rail timetables pinned
over the desk; the corner of the critic [[takane-miyoshi|Takane]] has a stack of
books waiting for verdicts.

## Deadlines

Writers who miss deadlines are reminded firmly, sometimes with a pistol.
