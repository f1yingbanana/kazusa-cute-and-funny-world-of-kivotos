# Kurumi Takakura's Room

[[kurumi-takakura]]'s room is the cleanest in the building, and she would like
that noticed. A cleaning caddy stands by the door, the bunk has hospital
corners, the mini-fridge holds strawberry, banana and melon milk in rows, and
her shield leans on its own rack by the bed.

## Under the pillow

A sticker album lives under the pillow, where nobody will see it.
