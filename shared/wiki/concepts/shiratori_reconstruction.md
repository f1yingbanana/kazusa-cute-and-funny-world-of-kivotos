# Shiratori Reconstruction Area 5

A block of [[shiratori_ward|Shiratori]] still being rebuilt after the last
crisis: cranes, scaffolding, generators and site huts behind a fence. Hired
crews from across Kivotos work here in shifts, most of them from
[[red_winter_school|Red Winter]]'s [[red_winter_public_works|Labor Party]], who
sleep in the [[labor_party_bunkhouse|bunkhouse]] next door.
