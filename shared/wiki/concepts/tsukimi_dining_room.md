# Tsukimi-sou Dining Room

The dining room of the [[hyakkiyako_tsukimi_dorm]]: a long low table, a rice
cooker the size of a drum, and a kitchen behind it where the landlady cooks
breakfast and dinner.

## After hours

Residents may use the stove after 20:00, once the landlady has finished for the
day.
