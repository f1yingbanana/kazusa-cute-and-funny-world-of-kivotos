# Kronos School of Journalism

Kronos School of Journalism is the academy of reporting and broadcasting in
Kivotos. Its students cover public events, conflicts and developments in every
academy district, and its [[kronos_news_club|News Club]] broadcasts from the
[[kronos_broadcasting|Kronos Broadcasting Center]].
