# Make-Up Work Club Annex

An old two-storey school annex on the [[trinity_outskirts|edge of Trinity]],
the base of the [[make_up_work_club]]. Inside are a study hall with desks
pushed together and mock exams pinned up, bedrooms upstairs and, in the yard,
an outdoor pool and a laundry line that still has the members' pegs on it. The
place is clean and lived in, though it stands empty most of the week.

## The boot camp

The club was sent here for a boot camp during the
[[eden_treaty_crisis|Eden Treaty crisis]]. The members cleaned it from top to
bottom, scrubbed the pool and lived in its bedrooms until they passed. They
kept the key, and still come out after class to study, and on weekends to swim,
drill or just be together, then walk back to the dorm before roll call.
