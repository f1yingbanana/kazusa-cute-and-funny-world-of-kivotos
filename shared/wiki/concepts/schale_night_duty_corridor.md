# SCHALE Night-Duty Corridor

A short carpeted corridor at the back of the residence wing: a
[[schale_night_duty_room|duty room]] with a bunk and a phone, a shower room, and
a few small bedrooms. Quiet hours start at 23:00.

## Who sleeps here

[[fox_platoon|FOX Squad]] live here and cover SCHALE's night duty:
[[niko-yoshino|Niko]], [[kurumi-takakura|Kurumi]] and [[otogi-tenjinyama|Otogi]]
each have a room off the corridor.
