# Ichika's Room

[[ichika-nakamasa|Ichika]]'s room in the [[trinity_magnolia_west|West Wing]] is
full of things she meant to get into: a guitar gathering dust in the corner,
beginner manuals for knitting and photography, and boxes of flea-market finds
she hasn't unpacked. It looks relaxed, as she does, until you notice everything
is exactly where she left it.
