# Nicomedia Troop

A group of [[arius_school]] students founded by
[[subaru-kakehashi|Subaru Kakehashi]] to organise and protect the younger
students as Arius rebuilds. They live in the
[[arius_barracks|Nicomedia Barracks]] under her eye, and she leads the patrols
that keep order in the district.

## Inside the troop

During the [[arius_reconstruction|reconstruction]], Subaru and the troop
struggled with distrust of outsiders and the weight of Arius's history. Those
tensions came to a head in a crisis at the
[[arius_porta_pasis|Porta Pacis]] archives, when the Arius Squad and Sensei
reached out to Subaru. Since then the troop has kept rebuilding and studying
alongside the rest of Arius.
