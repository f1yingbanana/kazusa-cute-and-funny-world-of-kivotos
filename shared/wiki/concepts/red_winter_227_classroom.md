A ground-floor classroom of the [[red_winter_spec227|Former Institute]] where
[[special_class_227|No. 227 Special Class]] is meant to do self-study and sign a
daily register. Its textbooks are years out of date and its blackboard holds
assignments nobody marks. Breath fogs here even at noon.
