# Abydos Main Building Basement

Pipes, fuse panels, a water pump and a diesel generator that needs coaxing,
under one dim bulb. There is no budget for contractors, so every outage is
repaired from here with spare parts and a well-thumbed manual. A clipboard by
the door logs each repair.
