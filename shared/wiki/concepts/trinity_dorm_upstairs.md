# Willow Hall Upper Corridor

The carpeted corridors on the two upper floors of [[trinity_dorm]], lined with
residents' doors, some decorated, some locked tight, one with a duel
whiteboard. The [[dorm_kitchenette|kitchenette]] opens off the corridor, and
after 22:00 the floor is meant to be quiet.
