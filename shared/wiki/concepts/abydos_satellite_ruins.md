# Abydos Satellite Wing Ruins

The [[abydos_school|school's]] satellite wing, abandoned when the sand reached
it. Desks still stand in rows in roofless rooms, half filled with sand, and the
bell tower is broken off at the top. Almost nobody comes out here.

## The winter find

One winter, [[hoshino-takanashi|Hoshino]] and [[nonomi-izayoi|Nonomi]] found a
girl here with no memory of her past:
[[shiroko-sunaookami|Shiroko]].
