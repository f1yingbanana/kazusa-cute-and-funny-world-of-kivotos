# Akane Murokasa's Room

[[akane-murokasa|Akane]]'s room is so clean it squeaks. A tea set on a lace
cloth, aprons folded into exact squares, a glasses-polishing cloth on the desk,
and a locked case of detonators whose labels look like a spice rack: paprika,
cumin, C4.
