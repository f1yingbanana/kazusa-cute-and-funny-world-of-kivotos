# Dormitory No. 5

A newer dormitory beside [[wildhunt_dorm4|No. 4]] in the
[[wildhunt_dorm_quarter|Dormitory Quarter]], with shared rooms. Its ground-floor
[[wildhunt_dorm5_mailroom|mailroom]] is where the Dorm Supervisory Unit inspects
every parcel. Even so, its residents have a reputation for always having things
the academy store doesn't sell.
