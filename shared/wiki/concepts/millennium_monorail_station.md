# Millennium Study Area Monorail Station

An elevated stop in the [[millennium_study_area|Study Area]], with glass screens
along the platform and a [[millennium_kiosk|kiosk]] at the foot of the
escalator. The driverless monorail shuttles between here and
[[millennium_station|Millennium Station]]; the last car leaves at midnight.
