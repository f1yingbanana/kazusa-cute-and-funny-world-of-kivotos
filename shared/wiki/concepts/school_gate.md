# Trinity Main Gate

Tall wrought-iron gates crowned with the crest of [[trinity_school]], between
stone pillars and a guard booth. Inside lies [[trinity_plaza]]; outside, paths
lead down the hill to [[church_street]] and across the gardens to the
dormitories.

It is the natural meeting point before and after school. In the morning a
[[justice_task_force]] officer with a clipboard takes the names of latecomers.
