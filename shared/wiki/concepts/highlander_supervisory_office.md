# Highlander Supervisory Office

A quiet, glass-walled office one floor above the
[[highlander_control|operations floor]], so its supervisors can look down on the
dispatch desks. Visitors wait at the door with an appointment.

## What it does

The office reports to the academy's corporate board and reviews every incident
report the [[highlander_control_center|Control Center]] files, and several it
doesn't.
