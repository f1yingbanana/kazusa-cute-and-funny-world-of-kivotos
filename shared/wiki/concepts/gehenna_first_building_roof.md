# Gehenna First School Building Roof

The gravel roof of the [[gehenna_first_building|First School Building]], with a
rusted water tank, a broken bench and a low parapet. Getting up means climbing
out of the fourth-floor storeroom window, so almost nobody does, which is
exactly why a few people treat it as their own.
