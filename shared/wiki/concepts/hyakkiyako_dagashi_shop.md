# Komachi Dagashi Shop

A narrow old sweets shop off the
[[hyakkiyako_shopping_district|shopping street]], crammed with jars of penny
candy, ramune bottles in a cooler, capsule-toy machines and a box of scratch
lottery cards. The old shopkeeper remembers every regular's favourite.
