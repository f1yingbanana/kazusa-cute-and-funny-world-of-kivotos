# Shanhaijing District

Tiled roofs, pavilions and gardens spread behind a long wall at the foot of
Kunlun Mountain. Cooking smells drift from the restaurants by the station, and
beyond them a second, guarded wall closes off the inner district. This is the
home of [[shanhaijing_senior_secondary_school]], and it keeps its doors mostly
closed.

Visitors may go only as far as the [[shanhaijing_entrance]] by the station: a
long strip of restaurants, tea houses and the [[shanhaijing_promenade]]. Going
through the [[shanhaijing_inner_gate]] needs a pass. Inside are the
[[shanhaijing_school|Central Special Zone]], the Genryumon's
[[shanhaijing_rikkagaku|old palace]] and the student courtyards of the
[[shanhaijing_lotus_court]].

## Beyond the walls

Bamboo forest, terraced tea fields and the trails up Kunlun Mountain lie around
the district: the [[shanhaijing_bamboo_forest]], the [[shanhaijing_tea_garden]]
and the [[shanhaijing_kunlun_foot]].
