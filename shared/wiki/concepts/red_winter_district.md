# Red Winter District

A vast, snowbound district on the far northern edge of Kivotos. Stern apartment
blocks and onion-domed halls stand among the taiga, and snow-plough trams keep
the main streets open between [[red_winter_station|Central Station]], the
[[red_winter_3rd_street|3rd Street]] shops, the
[[red_winter_school|academy campus]] and the
[[red_winter_first_dormitory|dormitory]]. Beyond the last tram stop the road
climbs into the [[red_winter_snow_mountains|mountains]]: the
[[red_winter_spec227|Former Institute]] on the outskirts, and further up,
forestry depots, security posts and the remote
[[red_winter_facility_183|No. 183 facility]].

The academy's government changes often enough that statues are built to be
swapped out, and the [[red_winter_broadcasting|radio station]] is the first
building every coup seizes. Warmth of any kind, a working radiator, a hot bath,
a thaw, is treated as a small festival.
