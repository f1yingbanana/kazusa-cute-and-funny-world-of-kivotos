# Outlaw Beach

A long, scruffy beach past [[kanesaki_port|the port]]'s far breakwater, where
three jurisdictions' maps fail to agree and so nobody patrols. Every summer the
[[helmet_gang|Helmet Gangs]] throw their own festival here: food stalls, cheap
fireworks, games with prizes nobody checks. Students from respectable schools
come for the thrill, and some take jobs at the stalls. Out of season it is
driftwood, gulls and the occasional bonfire.

## Last summer

[[arius_beach_festival|Saori of the Arius Squad worked a stall here]] while her
friends came to the festival.
