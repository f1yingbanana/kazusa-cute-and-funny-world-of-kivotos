# Trinity General School

Trinity General School is one of the three colossal great powers of Kivotos,
renowned for its rich history, Christian-aesthetic architecture, immaculate rose
gardens, and traditional aristocratic culture. Trinity prizes
elegance, poise and scholarly decorum above all else; it is governed by the
[[tea_party]], and its rivalry with [[gehenna_school]] is
[[gehenna_trinity_rivalry|old and famous]].

The campus spreads over the hill above [[church_street]]: white marble paths,
clipped rose hedges and wrought-iron benches, and between periods a stream of
pale uniforms, bookbags on one shoulder and rifles on the other. The
[[school_gate|main gate]] opens onto [[trinity_plaza]], where everything
starts.

## Governance

### The Tea Party

The ruling student council, historically composed of three rotating faction
leaders representing the Pater, Filius and Sanctus branches. The Tea Party
oversees overall school policy, diplomatic relations with rival academies like
Gehenna, and major academic ceremonies, from the [[trinity_tea_party]].

### The Sisterhood

A solemn, discreet religious order based in
[[trinity_sisterhood|Trinity Cathedral]]. While maintaining outward
appearances of prayer, hymns, and charitable works, the [[sisterhood]] operates
Trinity's most capable internal intelligence apparatus and guards ancient
Kivotos secrets.

### The Justice Task Force

Trinity's official peacekeeping force and defense committee. The
[[justice_task_force]] is responsible for security across campus grounds,
policing rowdy students, and responding to external incursions or delinquent
outbursts, from its [[trinity_justice_hq]].

## Student life

Between classes, Trinity students frequent the manicured campus courtyards, the
Victorian-style dormitories of the [[trinity_residential|Residential Quarter]]
and the patisseries lining Church Street. Afternoon tea and freshly baked
confections are an essential cultural ritual across all social tiers.

## The campus

Everything starts at Trinity Square: the main gate, the
[[trinity_academic_hall]], the [[school_library|Central Library]] and the
[[school_cafeteria|dining hall]] face it. Further out stand the Tea Party
Chamber, Justice Task Force Headquarters, the
[[trinity_remedial_clinic|Remedial Knights' Central Ward]], the
[[trinity_gymnasium|gymnasium]], the club building called the [[west_annex]]
and, in a forgotten corner, the [[trinity_old_library]].

The campus is big; a busy student can cross between ten buildings in a day.
