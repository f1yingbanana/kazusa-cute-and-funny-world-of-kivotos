# Atsuko's Room

[[atsuko-hakari|Atsuko]]'s room is more greenhouse than bedroom: potted
hydrangeas and rose cuttings on every surface, a grow lamp clipped to the shelf
and seed packets sorted by season. A proper school desk with a pencil case and
a notebook stands in the middle, ready for a lesson.
