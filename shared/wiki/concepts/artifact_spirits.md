# Artifact Spirits

Artifact Spirits are the lesser spirits of [[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako]]'s
ghost stories: supernatural entities born of rumors and the tales people tell,
sometimes taking the form of animated objects, ordinary things come alive. They appeared during a Lantern Festival crisis
([[hyakkaryouran_crisis]]). They are far weaker than the
[[hyakkiyako_phantom_fables|Phantom Fables]].

## Where they came from

During that Lantern Festival, [[natures_beauty]] twisted the district's rumors
and let loose dangerous spirits, and the
[[hyakkaryouran_resolution_council]] had to put them down to protect the
festival.
