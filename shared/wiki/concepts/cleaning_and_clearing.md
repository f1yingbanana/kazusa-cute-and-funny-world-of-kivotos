# Cleaning & Clearing

Cleaning & Clearing, C&C for short, is [[millennium_school]]'s agent unit. Its
members dress as maids and do serve as maids, but their real work is
"cleaning up": the sensitive operations the academy needs handled, carried out
with [[seminar]]. They are known for winning, and for the damage they leave
behind.

The unit is led by [[neru-mikamo|Neru Mikamo]], codename Double O, one of the
strongest fighters at Millennium.

## The agents

Every agent goes by a number:

- **00, Double O:** Neru, the leader, who claims a perfect mission record.
- **01, Zero-One:** [[asuna-ichinose|Asuna Ichinose]], the veteran who wins on
  instinct and uncanny luck.
- **02, Zero-Two:** [[karin-kakudate|Karin Kakudate]], the sniper on the back
  line.
- **03, Zero-Three:** [[akane-murokasa|Akane Murokasa]], infiltration and
  demolition, who keeps Neru at her work.
- **04, Zero-Four:** [[toki-asuma|Toki Asuma]], the newest, once
  [[rio-tsukatsuki|Rio]]'s personal bodyguard, with a powered exoskeleton.

## Where they work

They gear up in the [[millennium_cc_prep_room]], behind a door marked for
cleaning supplies, and Toki maintains her powered gear in the
[[millennium_toki_workshop]]. [[koyuki-kurosaki|Koyuki]] of Seminar is on
friendly terms with them and calls them "Senpai"; they once had to chase her
down, in [[bunny_chasers]].
