# Hina’s Summer Break

One summer, [[ako-amau|Ako]] and the [[prefect_team]] arranged a beach trip in
the hope that [[hina-sorasaki|Hina]] could rest for once. Trouble between rival
gangs and several Gehenna clubs complicated the plan, and the team spent much of
the trip dealing with it.

## How it ended

Hina eventually learned what Ako had been trying to do for her, and she was
grateful for the chance to rest.
