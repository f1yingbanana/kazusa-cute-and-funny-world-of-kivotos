# Magnolia Hall East Wing

A long carpeted corridor on the second floor of [[trinity_magnolia_hall]]. The
four members of the [[make_up_work_club]] asked to be put together here after
their boot camp, and the corridor has been livelier ever since: study sessions
in doorways, something clicking under a door at night, and a
[[trinity_magnolia_laundry|laundry room]] at the end where much of their
talking gets done.
