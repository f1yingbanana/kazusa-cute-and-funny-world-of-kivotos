# Trinity West Annex

An old brick building at the western edge of the campus of [[trinity_school]],
handed over to small clubs. The stairs creak, the corridor is lined with
hand-lettered signs, and something sweet is usually baking upstairs in the
[[sweets_club_room]]. Also upstairs is the [[trinity_cantata_room]], where
bands rehearse; downstairs, the [[vigilante_crew]] borrow a
[[trinity_vigilante_room|storeroom]].

After classes it fills with club members; at 20:00 the caretaker walks the
corridor rattling her keys and locks the front door.
