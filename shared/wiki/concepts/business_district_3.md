# Business District 3

Glass towers and narrow service streets next to [[tri_square]].
[[kaiser_corporation]]'s logo is on every third building and its
[[kaiser_tower|headquarters]] is the tallest, the banks keep strict hours, and
office workers fill the cafés at noon. The
[[kronos_broadcasting|Kronos studios]] are a block away. By night the district
empties except for security guards.
