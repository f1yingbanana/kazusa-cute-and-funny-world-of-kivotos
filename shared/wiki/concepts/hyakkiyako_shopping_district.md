# Hyakkiyako Shopping District

The town's main street, wooden shopfronts hung with lanterns and banners
running from the [[hyakkiyako_station|station]] gate up toward the castle.
Tourists and students share it all day, queuing at [[momoyodou]] and browsing
the shops; in the evening the food stalls take over
[[hyakkiyako_yatai_row]], and [[hyakkiyako_shopping_23]] branches off as the
merchandise street.

## Along the street

By the station stand the [[hyakkiyako_bus_terminal]] and the
[[hyakkiyako_angel24|Angel 24]], the only shop lit late at night. Further up
are [[hyakkiyako_bookstore]] and the [[hyakkiyako_dagashi_shop]].
