# Yin-Yang Club Tatami Lounge

A cosy tatami room behind the [[yin_yang_meeting_room]]: a kotatsu, a kettle
that is always warm, and cardboard boxes of promotional goods waiting to be
sorted. Someone from the [[yin_yang_club]] is usually napping under the
kotatsu; it is where the club goes to avoid the meeting room.
