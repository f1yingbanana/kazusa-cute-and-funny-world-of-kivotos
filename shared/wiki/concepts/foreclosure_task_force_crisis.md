# The Foreclosure Task Force Crisis

When [[kaiser_corporation|Kaiser]] moved openly against
[[abydos_school]], [[sensei]] of [[schale]], the
[[foreclosure_task_force]] and allies from other schools fought back and
rescued one of Abydos's own. The school survived, though its
[[abydos_debt|debt]] did not go away.

## How it went

It began when [[ayane-okusora|Ayane]] wrote to SCHALE asking for help and
Sensei came to Abydos. Together they fought off the [[helmet_gang|Helmet
Gang]], rescued [[serika-kuromi|Serika]], and found links between the gang and
Kaiser. Their digging brought in [[hifumi-ajitani|Hifumi]] and
[[problem_solver_68]], and led to the [[masked_swimsuit_gang]]'s raid on the
Stygian Bank. Then [[hoshino-takanashi|Hoshino]] accepted an offer meant to
relieve the school's debt, and Kaiser used it to move against Abydos. Sensei,
the Task Force and their allies brought her back. The debt remained, and the
students carried on together.
