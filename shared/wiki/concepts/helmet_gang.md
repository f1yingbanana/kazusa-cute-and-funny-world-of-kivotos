# Helmet Gang

The Helmet Gang is not one gang but a loose collection of delinquent groups
across Kivotos, recognizable by the helmets they wear. The
[[splash_helmet_gang|Splash Helmet Gang]] led by [[rabu-komakaze|Rabu]] is one
of them; the Kata-Kata Helmet Gang keeps a [[abydos_helmet_camp|camp]] in the
Abydos desert. Behind the helmets, their members have ordinary lives and
friendships as well as their gang business.
