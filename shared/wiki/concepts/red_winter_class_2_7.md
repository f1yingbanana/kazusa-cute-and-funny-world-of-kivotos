# Red Winter Class 2-7

A second-year homeroom where the seating chart is mostly crossed out: several of
its students are serving time in the special classes. The back row is where the
Security Committee chair [[marina-ikekura|Marina]], the manga artist
[[meru-himeki|Meru]] and the book critic [[takane-miyoshi|Takane]] argue across
the aisle.
