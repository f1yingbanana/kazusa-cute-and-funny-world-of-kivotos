# Aegir Water Park

Slides, lazy rivers and a giant wave pool on the coast beside
[[kanesaki_port|the port]]. In summer [[valkyrie_police_academy|Valkyrie]] lends
lifeguards and the queues stretch to the car park. Out of season only the domed
indoor wave pool opens, on weekends.
