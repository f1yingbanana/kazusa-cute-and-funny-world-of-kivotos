# Koharu's Room

[[koharu-shimoe|Koharu]]'s room in the [[trinity_magnolia_east|East Wing]] is
messy, with a [[justice_task_force]] armband pinned proudly above the bed and a
stack of textbooks that hardly look opened. She always locks the door and
checks it twice.

## Under the mattress

A pile of magazines she insists she confiscated as evidence. They are why the
door gets checked twice.
