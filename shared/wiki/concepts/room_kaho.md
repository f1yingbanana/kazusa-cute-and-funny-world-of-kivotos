# Kaho's Room

The tidy room of [[kaho-kuwakami]] in the [[hyakkiyako_tsukimi_dorm]], next
door to [[room_niya|the president's]] so she can knock in the morning. Shelves
of Hyakkiyako mascot goods, festival posters arranged by year, a whiteboard of
tourism campaigns, and a framed thank-you certificate from students that she
dusts every day.
