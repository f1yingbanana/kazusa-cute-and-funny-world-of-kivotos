# Eden Treaty

The Eden Treaty was meant to bring peace between [[trinity_school|Trinity]] and
[[gehenna_school|Gehenna]] after their long
[[gehenna_trinity_rivalry|rivalry]]. [[arius_school|Arius]] attacked the signing
ceremony, setting off the [[eden_treaty_crisis]]. [[sensei|Sensei]] and students
from both schools worked to stop the attack and keep the treaty's purpose alive.

## The negotiations

The treaty had opponents on both sides, and its negotiations were manipulated
from within. Fear of an infiltrator in Trinity shaped how the
[[tea_party|Tea Party]] prepared for the signing.
