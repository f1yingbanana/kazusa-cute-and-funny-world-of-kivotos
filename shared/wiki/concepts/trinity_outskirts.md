# Trinity Outskirts

Where the town of [[trinity_district|Trinity]] thins into fields, hedges and
old estates. The walled [[trinity_tea_residence]] looks over
[[trinity_bird_park]]; down the lane, the [[make_up_work_club]] keeps its
[[trinity_makeup_room|annex]], and Arius students take lessons in the
[[trinity_old_school_building]]. [[trinity_glebe_field]], the school's own
farmland, is where students sentenced to community service pull weeds. The road
ends at the ruined [[trinity_old_cathedral]].

Buses barely come out here; most people walk or cycle.
