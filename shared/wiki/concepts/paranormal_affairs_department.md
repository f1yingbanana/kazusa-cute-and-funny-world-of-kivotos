# Paranormal Affairs Department

The Paranormal Affairs Department, also called the Super Phenomenon Task
Force, investigates unusual phenomena for [[millennium_school]]. It is headed
by [[himari-akeboshi|Himari Akeboshi]], the self-proclaimed super genius, with
[[eimi-izumimoto|Eimi Izumimoto]] doing the fieldwork at her side. It works
out of the [[millennium_paranormal_office]].

## Inside the department

Eimi was its first member; Himari joined afterward at
[[rio-tsukatsuki|Rio]]'s demand. Himari feels the cold and Eimi feels the
heat, so the thermostat is a daily fight. The department led the
investigation into [[decagrammaton_crisis|Decagrammaton]], and
[[kei-tendou|Kei Tendou]] joins its hunts for the prophets that remain.
