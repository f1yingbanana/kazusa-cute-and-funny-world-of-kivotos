# Labor Party Workforce Office

The old campus boiler house, still warm from the pipes, taken over by the
[[red_winter_public_works|Public Works Department]]. A job board lists
construction crews across Kivotos and who is short of hands; placards are
stacked by the door and megaphones charge on a shelf. Students from bankrupt
schools come here looking for work, and anyone who needs urgent hands sent
somewhere asks here first. Thursday nights it fills for strike meetings and
arguments about pudding.

## Behind the curtain

Behind a curtain at the back, a row of camp cots by the warm pipes is where
organisers sleep when they are home between contracts.
