# Shirakawa Riverbank

A grassy riverside path under willows in the
[[hyakkiyako_shirakawa_quarter]], with stone steps down to the water and a
rowboat rental shed by the bridge. People come here to read, to train, to
think, or to sit somewhere they are sure to be found.
