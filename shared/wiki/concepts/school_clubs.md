# School Clubs in Kivotos

Clubs are central to student life in Kivotos. Some gather students around a
shared interest such as games, books or sweets; others run their school's
affairs, provide services or deal with threats. How they are organized depends
on their school and their purpose, and a student's club often matters to her as
much as her school does.
