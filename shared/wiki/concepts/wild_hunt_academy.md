# Wild Hunt Academy of Arts

Wild Hunt is the arts academy of Kivotos, a whole school given over to painting,
sculpture, music, literature, fashion, media art and the rest. It lives inside a
star fortress ringed by moats in the [[wildhunt_district]], and its campus is
the [[wildhunt_school]]. Everything a student does there is meant to serve her
art, and the rules are famously strict: one inspected [[wildhunt_gate|gate]], an
outing pass to leave, dormitories assigned by the Arts Council and a 22:00
curfew kept by the Dorm Supervisory Unit.

Officially there are no clubs and no bands, only artistic activity. In practice
friends gather in dorm rooms and quiet corners, and some of these gatherings,
like the [[occult_research_society]] and the [[free_trade_cartel]], are known
well beyond their members.

## How it is run

The Arts Council governs the academy's affairs and assigns every student her
room in the [[wildhunt_dorm_quarter|Dormitory Quarter]]; rooms cannot be swapped
on request. The Dorm Supervisory Unit enforces the dormitory rules from its
[[wildhunt_supervisory_post|post]] at the quarter's arch: curfew, the 22:30 room
check, parcel inspections and night patrols.

## Outing passes

Leaving the fortress needs an outing pass, stamped out and back at the gate.
Passes come more easily with an art-related reason, such as buying marble for a
sculpture or staging a performance outside. Literature students have it hardest:
they keep mandatory cloister hours. Activities with friends outside the walls
need separate permission.
