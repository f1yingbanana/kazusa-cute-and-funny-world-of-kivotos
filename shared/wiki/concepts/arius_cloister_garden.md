# Arius Cloister Garden

A cloister walk around a square of broken paving that has been dug up and
planted. With no sun this deep, the beds grow under lamps a repair crew rigged
up, seedlings sit in tins beneath them, and somebody waters everything every
morning without fail. Hydrangeas do best; the roses are a work in progress.
