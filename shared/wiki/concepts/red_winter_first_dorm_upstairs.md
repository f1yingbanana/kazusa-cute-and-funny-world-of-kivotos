# First Shared Dormitory Upper Floor

Narrow stairwells climb past landing after landing of cold corridors, each lined
with identical doors to residents' rooms. The radiators knock and the walls
between rooms are thin. The shared
[[red_winter_dorm_washroom|washroom]] is along the corridor.
