# Angel 24, Hyakkiyako Branch

An [[angel_24]] dressed in dark wood to match the rest of the
[[hyakkiyako_shopping_district|shopping street]], near the
[[hyakkiyako_station|station]] end: onigiri and bento in the cooler, ammunition
by the register. After the stalls close it is the only lit shop between the
station and the castle, so anyone out late ends up under its sign.
