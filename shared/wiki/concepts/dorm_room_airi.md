# Airi's Room

[[airi-kurimura|Airi]]'s room in [[trinity_dorm]]: neat shelves of tea tins and
recipe notebooks, a carefully kept planner on the desk, and a lot of mint
green. She locks up carefully when she goes out, but she rarely turns away a
friend who knocks.
