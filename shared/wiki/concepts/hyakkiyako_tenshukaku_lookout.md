# Tenshukaku Lookout

A bare wooden gallery at the very top of the keep of the
[[hyakkiyako_main_building]], wind blowing through the open shutters. All of
Hyakkiyako lies below it, down to the river and the mountains in the north.

## After hours

The stairs are roped off after 16:00, but students slip up them at night
anyway.
