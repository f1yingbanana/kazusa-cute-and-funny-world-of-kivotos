# Seminar

Seminar is the student council of [[millennium_school]]. It oversees the
academy's budgets, clubs and research, so its decisions reach every department,
and club heads queue outside its [[seminar_office|office]] before budget day.
Its president is [[rio-tsukatsuki|Rio Tsukatsuki]], its treasurer
[[yuuka-hayase|Yuuka Hayase]] and its secretary [[noa-ushio|Noa Ushio]]. Its
members wear a white field-style overcoat.

## The members

- Rio, the president, built the [[amas]] defense network. She was long absent
  after the trouble with Aris, and returned after the
  [[decagrammaton_crisis|Steel Continent]].
- Yuuka, the treasurer, runs the budget and chases every club about spending.
- Noa, the secretary, remembers everything and records it all.
- [[koyuki-kurosaki|Koyuki Kurosaki]], the codebreaker, spends much of her
  time in the [[millennium_self_reflection_room]].

## How it works

Seminar meets and holds its quarterly budget hearings in the
[[millennium_conference_room]], and fences off the [[millennium_ruins]]. It
works with [[cleaning_and_clearing]] on the academy's sensitive operations,
and expects club presidents at its meetings, whether or not they hide in a
locker.
