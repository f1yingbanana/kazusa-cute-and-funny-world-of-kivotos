# Promenade Dawn Market

Trestle tables under awnings at the end of the [[shanhaijing_promenade]], set up
before dawn: mountain vegetables, dried mushrooms, medicinal roots in sacks and
a single stall that sells cheese. The cooks come at five; the alchemists come
for the roots.
