# Student SCHALE Duty

Students from every academy take turns helping [[sensei|Sensei]] at the
[[schale_office|SCHALE Office]]: sorting paperwork, answering requests and
running errands across Kivotos. Weekday shifts usually run from 17:00 to 20:00,
late enough for students from far districts to arrive after class, and a
Saturday shift from 10:00 to 15:00 suits those who live farthest away. Shifts
mix schools on purpose, which is either the point or the problem, depending on
who is paired.
