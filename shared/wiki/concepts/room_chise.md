# Chise's Room

An almost empty tatami room in the [[hyakkiyako_tsukimi_dorm]], the room of
[[chise-waraku]], with the window always open to the river breeze. A low desk
holds an inkstone, a brush and a stack of tanzaku poem strips; on the sill sits
a jar of sand and shells she calls her sparkles.
