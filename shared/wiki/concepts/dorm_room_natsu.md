# Natsu Yutori's Room

[[natsu-yutori|Natsu]]'s room on the top floor of [[trinity_dorm]]: stacks of
poetry books, a mini fridge of small milk cartons, and a beanbag by the window
for contemplating romance. The door is ajar more often than not; Natsu insists
a closed door is unromantic.
