# Millennium Data Center

Behind a fence at the edge of the [[millennium_study_area|Study Area]], rows of
server racks hum in cold halls, the school's network and a good part of the
district's running through them. Badge doors, cameras, and a temperature that
makes visitors regret their short sleeves.
