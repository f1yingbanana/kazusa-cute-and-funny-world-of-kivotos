# Problem Solver 68

Problem Solver 68, also called Handyman 68, is an independent odd-jobs agency
that grew out of [[gehenna_school]]. It takes nearly any job that pays, while
its self-proclaimed boss, [[aru-rikuhachima|Aru Rikuhachima]], chases her image
of a stylish outlaw. Its finances and reputation are often shaky. Its other
members are [[mutsuki-asagi|Mutsuki Asagi]], [[kayoko-onikata|Kayoko Onikata]]
and [[haruka-igusa|Haruka Igusa]].

## Inside the agency

Aru dreams of making it a feared criminal organization like those in the
movies, and keeps asking [[sensei]] to be its management consultant. Mutsuki,
her childhood friend and chief of staff, brings the pranks and the bag of
bombs; Kayoko, the section chief, is the reasonable one who cleans up
afterward; and Haruka, who owes Aru everything, is the most dangerous of them,
with her explosives and her unpredictability.

The agency works from a small [[problem_solver_office|office]], where the
client sofa doubles as the boss's bed on late nights.
