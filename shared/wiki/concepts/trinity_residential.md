# Trinity Residential Quarter

The river side of [[trinity_district|Trinity]], where most of its students
live: hedged lanes, weeping willows and gardens around the dormitories.
[[trinity_magnolia_hall]], the biggest dorm, is closest to the campus gate;
small [[trinity_dorm|Willow Hall]] sits behind its willows nearer the shops;
the [[trinity_old_quarters]] stand at the far end beside
[[trinity_hospital|Saint Teresa General Hospital]]. [[trinity_linden_park]] is
in the middle of it all.

Quiet by day, the lanes stream with students on their way to class and back.
A bus stops at the corner of the quarter about every 30 minutes; most students
walk.
