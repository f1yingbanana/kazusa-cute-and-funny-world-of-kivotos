# Shiratori Station

A clean, busy Metro station beneath [[tri_square|Tri-Square]] in
[[shiratori_ward|Shiratori Ward]], its exits opening straight onto the square's
fountains. The Shiratori Line runs to D.U. Central and
[[kanesaki_port_station|Kanesaki Port]], and the outer arc to
[[kousagi_station|Kousagi]].
