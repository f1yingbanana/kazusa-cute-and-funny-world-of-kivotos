# Gehenna Zone 3 Residential Area

Narrow streets of dormitories, apartment blocks, laundromats and tiny shops
right across the road from the main gate of [[gehenna_school]], among them
[[gehenna_dorm13|Dormitory No. 13]], [[gehenna_dorm2|Dormitory No. 2]] and the
[[gehenna_kamadoyu|Kamado-yu]] bathhouse. [[prefect_team|Prefect]] officers
patrol it at night, and delinquents leave traps in it for them.
