# Hyakkiyako District

A tourist town of wooden shopfronts, lanterns, shrines and cherry trees,
gathered around the castle keep of [[hyakkiyako_school]]. Rickshaws and tour
buses carry visitors from [[hyakkiyako_station]] along the
[[hyakkiyako_shopping_district]] to [[hyakkiyako_movie_village]] and
[[hyakkiyako_plaza]], and there is a festival on most weeks.

The [[hyakkiyako_shirakawa_quarter]] along the river behind the castle is where
most students live. The railway runs north from the station through a long
tunnel to the snowy farmland of the [[ebisu_district]].

## Festivals and fables

The academy's [[festival_operations_department]] organizes the town's
celebrations, from the [[hyakkiyako_water_festival]] to the Lantern Festival on
[[hyakkiyako_lantern_road]]. The town is also full of old stories of
[[hyakkiyako_phantom_fables|phantom fables and artifact spirits]], and not all
of them are only stories.
