# Millennium Exhibition Hall

A vast column-free hall with a curved roof in the
[[millennium_research_park|Research Park]], near the station. During the
Millennium EXPO, game shows and artwork exhibitions it fills with booths, demos
and queues out the door; the rest of the time its doors are shut.
