# Dormitory No. 13 Second Floor

A linoleum corridor on the second floor of [[gehenna_dorm13]], where three
[[prefect_team|Prefect]] officers live side by side, a fourth room sits mostly
dark, and a gourmand's door at the end smells of whatever she cooked last.

## Who lives here

The officers are [[ako-amau|Ako]], [[iori-shiromi|Iori]] and
[[chinatsu-hinomiya|Chinatsu]]. The dark room is [[room_hina|Hina's]]; she
sleeps at her office most nights. The gourmand is [[izumi-shishidou|Izumi]] of
the [[gourmet_research_society]].
