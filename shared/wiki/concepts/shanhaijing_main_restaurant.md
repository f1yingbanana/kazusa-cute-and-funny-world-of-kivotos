# Black Tortoise Main Restaurant

A three-storey restaurant with a carved tortoise over the door. The ground floor
seats two hundred and is rarely less than full; the second floor is kept for
banquets. It is the flagship and headquarters of the
[[xuanwu_merchant_association]], and on mass dumpling days the queue shuts the
street.

## Upstairs

The association's president, [[rumi-akeshiro]], lives on the top floor above
the test kitchen, in [[room_rumi]].
