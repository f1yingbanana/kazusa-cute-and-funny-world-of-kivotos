# Tsukimi-sou Dormitory

A rambling two-storey wooden dorm by the river in the
[[hyakkiyako_shirakawa_quarter]], with sliding doors and tatami rooms. A strict,
kind landlady cooks breakfast and dinner in the
[[tsukimi_dining_room|dining room]] and counts heads at 22:30. There is a small
bath downstairs, and the [[tsukimi_veranda|veranda]] on the river side is for
moon viewing.
