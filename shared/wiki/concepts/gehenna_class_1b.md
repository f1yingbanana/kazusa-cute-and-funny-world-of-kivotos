# Gehenna Class 1-B

A ground-floor classroom in the
[[gehenna_first_building|First School Building]], with a cracked blackboard, a
teacher's desk bolted to the floor after the last riot, and windows onto the
[[gehenna_lawn_square|Lawn Square]]. The front row is where the diligent sit;
the back row is where people sleep.
