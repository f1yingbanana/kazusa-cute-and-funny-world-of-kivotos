# Villa Lagrange

A neat white complex around a small courtyard in
[[millennium_euler_heights|Euler Heights]], with an auto-lock front door,
bicycle racks, and [[millennium_coin_laundry|a laundromat]] on the corner that
never closes. Most of the tenants are Millennium students.

## Residents
