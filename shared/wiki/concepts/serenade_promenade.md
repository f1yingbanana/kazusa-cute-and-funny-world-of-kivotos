# Serenade Promenade

The Trinity Carnival held an idol event and filled
[[trinity_school|Trinity]] with stalls, shows and visitors from across
Kivotos. [[mari-iochi|Mari]] led the idol group Antique Seraphim with
[[sakurako-utazumi|Sakurako]] and [[mine-aomori|Mine]].

## Preparing the idol event

Mari helped Sakurako and Mine prepare by learning from students across
Kivotos, and they invited her to lead their group. Other participants included
students from [[abydos_school|Abydos]] and [[utaha-shiraishi|Utaha]], backed by
the [[engineering_department|Engineering Department]].

## Around the carnival

The [[after_school_sweets_club|After-School Sweets Club]] tried a haunted-house
café, [[valkyrie_police_academy|Valkyrie]] students performed a safety
promotion, and [[tsurugi-kenzaki|Tsurugi]] stepped in for an actor in a
[[justice_task_force|Justice Task Force]] play. [[nagisa-kirifuji|Nagisa]]
exhibited art, the [[gourmet_research_society|Gourmet Research Society]]
advised the food vendors, and [[rabu-komakaze|Rabu]]'s group enjoyed the
festival with [[sensei|Sensei]].
