# Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy

The way in is through [[hyakkiyako_castle_square]], past the tour groups, to
a timber main building crowned by a castle keep, with club buildings around it
each flying their own banners. The grounds sit at the heart of
[[hyakkiyako_district]], and the town's traditional architecture, shopping
streets and festivals are the academy's pride.

Hyakkiyako has no conventional student council: its clubs and committees share
authority as an alliance. The [[yin_yang_club]] and the
[[hyakkaryouran_resolution_council]] are the most influential, alongside the
[[festival_operations_department]], the [[etiquette_training_society]] and the
[[ninjutsu_research_department]].

## The campus

The [[hyakkiyako_main_building]] with its keep holds the homerooms. The
[[yin_yang_building]] is where clubs bring their disputes and permits, the
[[hyakkiyako_great_garden]] serves as an outdoor lesson ground below the
[[hyakkiyako_observatory]], and students eat at
[[hyakkiyako_dining_hall_7]]. At the back of the grounds,
[[hyakkiyako_old_schoolhouse_lane]] is a row of old numbered schoolhouses that
small clubs squat in.
