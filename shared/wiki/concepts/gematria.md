# Gematria

Gematria is a group of adults from beyond Kivotos who research its mysteries.
They have been behind several of the city's major crises, though its members'
aims and methods often differ.

## Members

Its known members have included Black Suit, Maestro (see
[[maestro_mimesis]]), Golconde and Beatrice, who manipulated Arius during the
[[eden_treaty_crisis]].
