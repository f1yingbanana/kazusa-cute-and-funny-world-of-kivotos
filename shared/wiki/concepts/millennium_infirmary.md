# Millennium Infirmary

Behind the [[millennium_stadium|stadium]], beside the
[[millennium_fitness_center|gym]]: six curtained beds, a nurse's desk and a
cabinet of bandages and fever patches. After a field mission or an athletic
festival every bed is taken.
