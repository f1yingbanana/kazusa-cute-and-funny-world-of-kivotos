# Hanako's Room

[[hanako-urawa|Hanako]]'s room in the [[trinity_magnolia_east|East Wing]]: tidy
and elegant, a top student's room, which it is. A swimsuit hangs on the
wardrobe door like a trophy, and exam papers with perfect scores, some of them
third-year papers, are used as coasters. She leaves the door unlocked to see
who will be scandalised by walking in.
