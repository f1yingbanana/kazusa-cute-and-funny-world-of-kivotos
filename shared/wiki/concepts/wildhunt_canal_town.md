# Wild Hunt Canal Town

Narrow streets of pastel houses between the moat and [[wildhunt_station]], with
a bridge every hundred metres. The town lives off
[[wild_hunt_academy|the academy]]: galleries hang student work, shops sell
everything the [[wildhunt_store|store]] inside won't, and on weekends students
with outing passes crowd the cafés. Along the canals are
[[wildhunt_cafe|Caffè Bastione]], [[wildhunt_fabric|Bobbin & Bolt Fabrics]],
[[wildhunt_typewriters|Typebar Antiques]],
[[wildhunt_gallery|Gallery Kinescope]], the [[wildhunt_crepes|crepe stand]] on
the main bridge and the [[wildhunt_live_house|Live House Gargoyle]] in a cellar.
Most shops close between 19:00 and 21:00. Past the last bridge the north moat is
reeds and an old [[wildhunt_boathouse|boathouse]].
