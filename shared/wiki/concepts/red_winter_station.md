# Red Winter Central Station

A cavernous, cold station with a mosaic ceiling and a stove in the waiting hall,
at the far end of the Northern Trunk. Arrival boards show delays more often than
times. Outside wait the snow and the trams of the
[[red_winter_district|Red Winter District]].
