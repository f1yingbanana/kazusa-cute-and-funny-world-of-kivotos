# Trinity Market

An iron-and-glass market hall just off [[church_street]]. Stalls sell fruit and
vegetables, loose tea from every school in Kivotos, cut flowers and seed
packets, cheese, and hot snacks. Saturday mornings are crowded, and the
[[vigilante_crew]] patrol it because pickpockets love a crowd.
