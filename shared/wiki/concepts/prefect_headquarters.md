# Prefect Team Headquarters

A grim stone building near the main gate of [[gehenna_school]], with a flagpole,
a motor pool and a sign warning delinquents not to bother. The [[prefect_team]]
runs much of Gehenna from here in practice: patrols, raids, bounties and the
paperwork that follows.

## Inside

The [[prefect_office|team office]] and [[hina_office|the chairwoman's office]]
are upstairs; the [[prefect_holding_cells|holding cells]] are in the basement.
