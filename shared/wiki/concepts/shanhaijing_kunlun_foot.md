# Kunlun Mountain Trailhead

A ranger hut and a stone gate where the trails up Kunlun Mountain begin. Rare
herbs grow on its slopes, among them the famous ten-thousand-year ginseng, which
may not leave Shanhaijing, so packs are searched on the way down.
