# Mari's Room

[[mari-iochi|Mari]]'s cell in the [[trinity_cathedral_cloister]] is tidy, with a
prayer corner under the window, a kneeler worn smooth, and a stack of letters
from the students she counsels. She keeps it locked, and the light on too late.

## Under the bed

A small box of ribbons and hair clips she bought and has never worn, and tells
herself she shouldn't keep.
