# Tsukimi-sou Moon-Viewing Veranda

A wide wooden veranda on the river side of the [[hyakkiyako_tsukimi_dorm]],
wind chimes along the eaves and cushions on the boards. On clear nights
residents sit out here until the landlady calls curfew.
