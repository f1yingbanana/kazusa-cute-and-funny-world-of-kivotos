# Sunshine Girls’ Serenade

For Gehenna's Special Party, [[makoto-hanuma|Makoto]] enlisted [[sensei]] and
arranged for [[hina-sorasaki|Hina]] to give a piano recital. Hina accepted the
challenge and prepared for it despite her duties as head of the
[[prefect_team]].

## Behind the scenes

The rivalry between the [[pandemonium_society]] and the Prefect Team
complicated the party preparations throughout.
