# Labor Party Bunkhouse

Two storeys of prefab rooms beside the
[[shiratori_reconstruction|reconstruction site]], a
[[labor_party_bunkhouse_canteen|canteen]] with long tables, boots drying by the
door and union posters on every wall. Crews from
[[red_winter_school|Red Winter]]'s [[red_winter_public_works|Labor Party]] live
here while they work in D.U.
