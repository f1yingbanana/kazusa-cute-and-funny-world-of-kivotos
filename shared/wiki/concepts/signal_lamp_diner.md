# Signal Lamp Diner

A narrow counter diner just outside the [[highlander_rail_yard|yard]] gate, an
old red signal lamp over the door. Set meals come fast and cheap, the owner
knows every crew's order, and the counter seats eight elbow to elbow. Nobody
eats alone here, whether they like it or not.
