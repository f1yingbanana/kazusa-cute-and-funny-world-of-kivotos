# Seminar Conference Room

Near the top of [[millennium_tower|Millennium Tower]]: a long white table, a
projector, and windows that show the whole campus and streak beautifully when it
rains. [[seminar]] meets here, and club presidents are called in for the
quarterly budget hearings, which most of them describe as interrogations.
