# Abydos Resort Trip

The [[foreclosure_task_force]] was promised a resort holiday and got an
abandoned island resort instead. They spent the trip repairing and cleaning it,
fighting off trouble, and still found time to share meals and enjoy themselves
before heading home.

## What happened

The resort's old defences were still active and had to be dealt with. The
students clashed with the [[splash_helmet_gang]] and with
[[wakamo-kosaka|Wakamo]]. Between the fights they cooked, ate and spent time
together.
