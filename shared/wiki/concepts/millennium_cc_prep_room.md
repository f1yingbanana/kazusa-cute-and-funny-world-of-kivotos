# Cleaning & Clearing Prep Room

To anyone passing in the [[millennium_clubhouse|clubhouse]], it's a janitor's
room. Inside is the ready room of [[cleaning_and_clearing|Cleaning & Clearing]]:
a rack of maid uniforms pressed and numbered, mops and buckets, a locked cabinet
of charges and ammunition, a mission board with a long unbroken column of
successes, and a small round table with a proper tea set. A bomber jacket is
always thrown over the back of the one armchair.

## The team's room

The armchair and the bomber jacket are [[neru-mikamo|Neru]]'s. The team works
with [[seminar]], and the jobs it takes go up on the mission board.
