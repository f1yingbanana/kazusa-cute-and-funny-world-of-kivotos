# Abydos School Store

A counter, a bread rack and a drinks fridge that hums too loudly. The bread
comes from the [[abydos_suburban_market|Suburban Market]] twice a week; the
sweets are whatever was cheap in bulk. Outside staffed hours, a tin by the till
takes the money.
