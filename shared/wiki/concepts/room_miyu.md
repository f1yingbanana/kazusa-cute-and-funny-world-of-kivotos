# Miyu's Tent

[[miyu-kasumizawa]]'s tent sits a little apart from the rest, draped in netting
and leaves so well that delivery drivers walk past it. Inside are a thermos of
sweet tea, a rifle wrapped in a blanket like something asleep, and rows of plain
pebbles in an egg carton, each compartment labelled with a mood.
