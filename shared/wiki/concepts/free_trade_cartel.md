# Free Trade Cartel

Ask around [[wild_hunt_academy|Wild Hunt]] for something the
[[wildhunt_store|academy store]] won't stock and sooner or later someone points
you to the Free Trade Cartel. It is not a club, since Wild Hunt allows none, but
a small unofficial business that gets restricted goods past the
[[wildhunt_gate|gate]] inspection and delivers them, for a price. Its sales
manager does the talking; nobody is quite sure who does the planning.

## Members

Three roommates in [[wildhunt_room402|Room 402]] of [[wildhunt_dorm5]].
[[miyo-sakurai]] is its president and the planner of every operation, though she
never wanted the job and would disband the Cartel if she had the nerve: she
works out the drop-off points, the safest routes and the escape paths.
[[fuyu-wakasa]] calls herself its Sales Manager, a title Miyo never approved,
and handles clients and negotiations. [[ritsu-usuba]], small, strong and very
fast, does the running and climbing and enjoys every minute of it.

## How goods get in

Orders come over the walls rather than through the gate. Fuyu built a catapult
for the purpose, kept folded in the [[wildhunt_launch_loft|loft]] of the old
[[wildhunt_boathouse]] on the north moat, with a map of drop points along the
[[wildhunt_rampart|rampart]].
