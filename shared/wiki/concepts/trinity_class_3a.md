# Trinity Class 3-A

A top-floor classroom in the [[trinity_academic_hall]], with the best view on
campus, over the rooftops toward the cathedral. By tradition the members of the
[[tea_party]] are enrolled here, and the whole school seems to watch who sits
next to whom.
