# Mansion of the Beyond

A rotting old mansion at the edge of the [[great_snowfield|snowfield]]. Its
windows are boarded, no light shows inside, and someone on the roof is always
watching the road. Beyond the door, a cold ballroom is heaped with festival
props that plainly belong to someone else.

## Who holds it

The troublemakers of [[natures_beauty]] took the mansion over as their base. The
props in the ballroom were stolen from Hyakkiyako's festivals.
