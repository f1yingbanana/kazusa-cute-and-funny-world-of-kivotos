# Gehenna Old Dining Hall

The closed old dining hall of the
[[gehenna_abandoned_building|abandoned building]]: stacked chairs, a long
serving counter and a kitchen whose gas line someone quietly reconnected. A
tasting table stands in the middle under a single lamp, with a notebook of
scores beside it.

## The lab

It is the [[gourmet_research_society]]'s unofficial food lab, used on evenings
and weekends for the experiments they can't run in their
[[gehenna_gourmet_clubroom|clubroom]].
