# Masked Swimsuit Gang

A gang in improvised masks and swimsuits that hit the Stygian Bank in the
[[black_market_district|Black Market]]. Their leader went by the name Faust.

## Who they were

The gang was the [[foreclosure_task_force]] with [[hifumi-ajitani|Hifumi]], who
went by Faust. They were after the bank's records, which tied the payments on
[[abydos_debt|the Abydos debt]] to criminal activity.
[[shiroko-sunaookami|Shiroko]] proposed the raid and still keeps her mask.
