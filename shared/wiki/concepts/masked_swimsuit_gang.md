# Masked Swimsuit Gang

The Masked Swimsuit Gang is a name used during an operation involving Abydos
students and Hifumi. Wearing improvised disguises, they sought records linking
debt payments to criminal activity at the Stygian Bank. Hifumi used the name
Faust during the incident.
