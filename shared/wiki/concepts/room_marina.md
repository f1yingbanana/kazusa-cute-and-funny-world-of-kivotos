# Marina's Room

[[marina-ikekura|Marina]]'s room in the
[[red_winter_first_dormitory|dormitory]], bare and scrubbed spotless: she is an
expert cleaner, thanks to all the punishments. A district map over the bed is
stuck full of pins and arrows correcting where she thought she was. A can of cat
repellent stands by the door, just in case. Across the corridor sleeps the
Secretary General, [[tomoe-sashiro|Tomoe]].

## The key

Marina locks the room and then has to hunt for the key. Tomoe notices every
night it goes missing.
