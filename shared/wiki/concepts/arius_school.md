# Arius Satellite School

Arius is the school that broke away from the schools that became
[[trinity_school|Trinity]] and hid in the [[arius_district]] beneath Kivotos.
For years an adult ruled it and trained its students for war, and it was Arius
that attacked the [[eden_treaty|Eden Treaty]] signing. Since then it has been
on parole, rebuilding with Trinity's help.

Its grounds lie behind a high stone wall. The repaired wing of the Gothic
[[arius_schoolhouse|schoolhouse]] is in use again, the
[[arius_barracks|barracks]] are full, and the [[arius_drill_yard|drill yard]]
where children were once drilled for war now has a football net at one end.

## The campus

Inside the wall are the schoolhouse, with the
[[arius_council_room|interim council's room]] and the
[[arius_study_hall|junior study hall]] in its repaired wing; the Nicomedia
Barracks; the drill yard; and the [[arius_cloister_garden|cloister garden]].
Patrols led by a senior student, [[subaru-kakehashi|Subaru Kakehashi]] of the
[[nicomedia_troop]], walk the wall at night.

## Its past

Arius split from the schools that became Trinity and went its own way
underground. A civil war divided it about a decade ago, and in its aftermath
Beatrice, an adult of [[gematria]], took control. She taught its children that
everything is in vain and used the school and the [[arius_squad]] for her
violent plans in the [[eden_treaty_crisis|Eden Treaty crisis]]. After her
defeat the students faced the hard work of rebuilding their lives and
relationships, and the school was put on parole under an interim council (see
[[arius_reconstruction]]).
