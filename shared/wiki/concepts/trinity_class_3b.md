# Trinity Class 3-B

A top-floor classroom in the [[trinity_academic_hall]], beside the stairs to
the [[trinity_bell_tower]]. Its third-years are mostly club officers who arrive
late from morning duties and leave early for afternoon ones, so the seats are
often half empty.
