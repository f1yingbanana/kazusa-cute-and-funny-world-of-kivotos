# Kepler House

A plain concrete walk-up at the edge of
[[millennium_euler_heights|Euler Heights]], with an outdoor stairwell, a shared
bike rack and a view of the rougher streets past the edge of the district.
Cheap rent, thin mailboxes, quiet neighbours.

## Residents
