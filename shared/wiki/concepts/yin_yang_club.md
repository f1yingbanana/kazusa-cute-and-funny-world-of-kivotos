# Yin-Yang Club

The Yin-Yang Club is an influential [[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako]]
organization concerned with the district's affairs, the nearest thing the
academy has to a student council. The academy is an alliance of dozens of clubs and factions, and
the club mediates and arbitrates between them: every dispute, festival permit and
complaint ends up at the [[yin_yang_building]] sooner or later. Its president is
the laid-back [[niya-amachi|Niya Amachi]]; its vice president
[[kaho-kuwakami|Kaho Kuwakami]] keeps it running and promotes Hyakkiyako's
tourism; the haiku poet [[chise-waraku|Chise Waraku]] is its third member.

## Inside the club

The club's authority rests entirely on the willingness of others to grant it,
so Niya takes care never to be seen using it: she watches, nudges things
sideways when nobody is looking and lets the results pass for coincidence,
while Kaho does most of the actual work. Niya once staged a fake plan to take
over the world, video and army included, purely to see [[sensei]]'s face; the
invitation to join the club that came with it was real.
