# Prefect Team

The Prefect Team keeps what order there is at [[gehenna_school]]: it patrols,
raids delinquent clubs, posts bounties and fills the holding cells, all amid the
academy's endless disturbances. It is chaired by
[[hina-sorasaki|Hina Sorasaki]], feared across Kivotos as one of its strongest
students. Its officers wear red armbands, and its stern headquarters stands near
the main gate.

## The team

Under Hina, [[ako-amau|Ako Amau]] runs the administration,
[[iori-shiromi|Iori Shiromi]] leads the field work, and
[[chinatsu-hinomiya|Chinatsu Hinomiya]] is the team's medic; many other
students serve as officers. The team works out of its
[[prefect_headquarters|headquarters]] and [[prefect_office|office]], and keeps
troublemakers in the [[prefect_holding_cells]].

The work never ends and nearly all of it lands on Hina, who sleeps in her office
more than in her dorm. The team's relationship with Gehenna's student council,
the [[pandemonium_society]], is a constant source of tension; its president
Makoto delights in mocking the prefects. Regular targets include the
[[hot_spring_development_department]] and the
[[gourmet_research_society]].
