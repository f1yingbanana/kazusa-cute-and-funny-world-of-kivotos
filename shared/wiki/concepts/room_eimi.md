# Eimi's Room

[[eimi-izumimoto|Eimi]]'s room is the coldest in the building. The air
conditioner runs flat out, a fan points at the pillow, headphones and celebrity
magazines lie on the bed, and a mini-freezer holds a tub of ice cream with a
spoon already in it. The door stands open for the draft, which the corridor
does not appreciate.
