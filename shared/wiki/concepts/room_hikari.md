# Hikari's Room

[[hikari-tachibana]]'s narrow crew bedroom, with a model railway on a board
above the bed and an ant farm on the windowsill that she watches longer than she
sleeps. A shelf of curry spices stands in plain view. Knocking on the wall
reaches [[nozomi-tachibana|her sister]] next door.

## Behind the spices

The candy is hidden behind the curry spices, out of the dentist's sight.
