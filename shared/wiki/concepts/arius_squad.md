# Arius Squad

A four-person unit from [[arius_school]]: [[saori-joumae|Saori Joumae]], its
leader, with [[atsuko-hakari|Atsuko Hakari]],
[[misaki-imashino|Misaki Imashino]] and [[hiyori-tsuchinaga|Hiyori
Tsuchinaga]]. Raised and trained together under the adult who ruled Arius,
they fought on her side in the [[eden_treaty_crisis|Eden Treaty crisis]]. They
have since been paroled and live together in the [[arius_shelter]].

## Their story

The four grew up together under Beatrice's control. [[azusa-shirasu|Azusa]]
shared that past before she chose a life at Trinity. After the attack on the
treaty signing, Saori turned to [[sensei]] for help to save Atsuko from
Beatrice ([[arius_rescue]]). Afterwards the students began finding their own
paths: Saori left to find hers, and
the others drifted between abandoned buildings, hiding from trackers, though
their bonds stayed important to them. They reunited one summer at
[[arius_beach_festival|a beach festival]], helped with the
[[arius_reconstruction|reconstruction]], and were granted parole with support
from Sensei and other schools.
