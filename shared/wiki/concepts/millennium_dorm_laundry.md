# Millennium Dormitory Laundry Room

The [[millennium_dormitory|dormitory]]'s basement laundry: ten washers, ten
dryers, a folding table and a vending machine. The app says which machines are
free; it is usually wrong. Around 01:00 the night owls gather here with their
baskets.
