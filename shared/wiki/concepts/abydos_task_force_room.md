# Abydos Foreclosure Task Force Room

The [[foreclosure_task_force]]'s base and the real heart of the school. A long
table fills the middle, a sofa that is rarely free sits under the window, and a
whiteboard tracks the monthly interest payment against this month's earnings.
On the wall, a district map shows [[kaiser_corporation|Kaiser's]] holdings in
red.

## Club business

Meetings, money-making schemes and long arguments about them all happen here.
Ammunition is counted out on the long table before every job. A cabinet holds
antiques and old books waiting to be appraised, and the snack shelf is
restocked out of [[nonomi-izayoi|Nonomi's]] allowance. Someone usually naps on
the sofa through lunch.
