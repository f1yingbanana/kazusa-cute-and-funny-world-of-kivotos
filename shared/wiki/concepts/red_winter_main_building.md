# Red Winter Main Building

A stepped tower with a star on its spire, far larger than the student body
needs: whole floors are shut and unheated. Homerooms such as
[[red_winter_class_1_4|Class 1-4]], [[red_winter_class_2_7|2-7]] and
[[red_winter_class_3_1|3-1]] are on the lower floors near the boilers. The two
lifts are slow and the staircases are cold, so the cloakroom on the ground floor
is jammed with students shedding coats and boots just before the 08:30 bell.
