# Berthing Ship Calypso

A broad, slow former ferry converted to cabins, moored two berths down from the
[[odyssey_olympus|flagship]]. Narrow corridors, bunks bolted to the walls, a
small [[calypso_galley|galley]] and a [[calypso_radio_room|radio room]] on the
upper deck. A sailor stands gangway watch at night and the lights go out at
22:30, sea or harbour.
