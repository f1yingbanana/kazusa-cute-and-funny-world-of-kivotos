# Songbird Park

A quiet park on the [[trinity_outskirts]], thick with hedges and old trees,
where sparrows, finches and tits live in huge numbers. Feeders hang along the
paths and the noise at dawn is extraordinary; at night it is still and dark.
The walls of the [[trinity_tea_residence]] run along one side, with a gate the
residents use after dusk.
