# Kikyou's Room

A side room off the [[hyakkaryouran_council_hall]] that has slowly become
[[kikyou-kiryuu]]'s: a futon folded in the corner, a cushion where the sun comes
in, a stack of military histories and a loop of cat's-cradle string.

## Under the futon

There is a battle map of the town under the futon.
