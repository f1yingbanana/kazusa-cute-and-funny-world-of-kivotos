# Hyakkiyako Museum of Traditions

A former magistrate's office in [[hyakkiyako_movie_village]], filled with
festival floats, demon and fox masks, armour, old school records and scrolls
from the warring-districts era before the academy. The [[yin_yang_club]] lends
it pieces and checks the labels.
