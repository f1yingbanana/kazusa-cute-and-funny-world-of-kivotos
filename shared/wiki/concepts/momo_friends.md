# MomoFriends

MomoFriends is one of the most popular character and merchandise franchises in
Kivotos. Its mascots include Peroro and Skullman, and its goods sell at shops
such as the [[du_momo_friends_shop|flagship shop]] in D.U. and
[[trinity_momo_store|an automated store]] in Trinity.

## Fans

[[hifumi-ajitani|Hifumi Ajitani]] is a devoted Peroro fan, and
[[azusa-shirasu|Azusa Shirasu]] is fond of Skullman.
