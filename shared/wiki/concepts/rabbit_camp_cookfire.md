# RABBIT Camp Cookfire

A blue tarp stretched over an oil drum cut into a stove, a folding table, a
cooler and four ammunition crates for chairs. The whiteboard on the tarp pole
holds the duty roster and the day's operation name.

## What's for dinner

Dinner is whatever the hill, the locals or the late-night discount shelf at the
[[angel_24_kousagi|station Angel 24]] provided: wild greens, gifted vegetables,
near-expiry bento warmed in a pot.
