# Shittim Chest

The Shittim Chest is a device [[sensei|Sensei]] uses. [[arona|ARONA]] serves as
its operating-system assistant, and Plana, an assistant from another timeline,
later joined her. Both help Sensei with information and other tasks. What the
device really is, and the limits of what it can do, remain a mystery.

## Where it is kept

The room that holds the Chest is in the [[schale_basement|SCHALE basement]].
Within the device itself lies [[shittim_classroom|ARONA's classroom]], a quiet
digital space with no door to the rest of Kivotos.
