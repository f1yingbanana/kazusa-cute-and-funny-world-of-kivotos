# Abydos Suburban Town

When the centre emptied, the shops that survived moved out here, near the
station and the cheaper rents. It is a few streets of low buildings with
awnings against the sun: the covered [[abydos_suburban_market|market]], the
[[shiba_seki|Shiba Seki]] ramen stall, the district's only 24-hour store, an
[[abydos_antiques|antiques dealer]] and a small [[abydos_suburban_park|park]].
Flyers for part-time jobs paper every lamppost. The local bus to the
[[abydos_border_town|border town]] and the desert bus to the
[[black_market_district|Black Market]] both leave from the
[[abydos_station|station]] forecourt.
