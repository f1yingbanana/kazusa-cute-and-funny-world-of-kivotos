# Millennium Clubhouse Annex

A narrow back wing joined to the [[millennium_clubhouse|clubhouse]] by a single
corridor. Its rooms are registered to clubs that dissolved years ago, so the
doors stay shut, the dust stays put and the corridor lights flicker. Almost
nobody has a reason to come here.
