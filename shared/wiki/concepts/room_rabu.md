# Rabu's Room

The ride operator's booth above the ballroom, where [[rabu-komakaze]] sleeps,
with a window over the gang's bunk mats so she can see them all. A cot, a rack
of helmets buffed to a shine and a jar of coins fished from under vending
machines.

## In the drawer

A rubber jar-opening grip is hidden in the desk drawer, where none of her girls
will ever find it.
