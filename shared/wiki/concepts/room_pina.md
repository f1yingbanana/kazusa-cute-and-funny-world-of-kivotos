# Pina's Room

The room of [[pina-asahina]] by the stairs of the
[[hyakkiyako_kawabata_house]], its door usually open. Gangster film posters
cover every wall, a calligraphy scroll reads "chivalry", there is a set of sake
cups she only ever fills with tea, and a tour-guide flag and megaphone lean in
the corner.
