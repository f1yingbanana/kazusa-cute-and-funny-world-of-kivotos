# Great Snowfield

A white plain beyond the last farm of the [[ebisu_district|North District]],
flat and silent all the way to the mountains. Storms close in without warning
and erase every track. A derelict [[beyond_mansion|mansion]] stands at its near
edge; past that there is no shelter at all.

## What it hides

A former Hyakkaryouran president, Ayame, was lost here (see
[[hyakkaryouran_crisis]]). The stories about what lies hidden in the snow are
older than the academy, and the [[twilight_temple_grounds|Twilight Temple]] is
said to stand somewhere within it.
