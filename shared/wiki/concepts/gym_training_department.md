# Gym Training Department

The Gym Training Department of [[millennium_school]] is devoted to physical
conditioning and runs the [[millennium_fitness_center]]. It is led by
[[sumire-otohana|Sumire Otohana]], who believes strength solves nearly any
problem and designs workout plans few can follow.
[[rei-nomasa|Rei Nomasa]], a baseball lover, trains with the department.

## Inside the club

Rei joined on her seniors' advice to improve at baseball, and finds Sumire's
long endurance drills and muscle work far harder than she expected. She is
often worn out but keeps going. Sumire has also taken [[sensei]] on as her
"Trainer", though she is the one who does the training.
