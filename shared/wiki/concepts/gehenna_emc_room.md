# Emergency Medicine Club Room

A ground-floor room of the [[gehenna_clubroom_building|clubroom building]], with
shelves of meticulously labelled medicine, folded stretchers, a cot for the
night shift and a radio that relays district alerts. A battered ambulance waits
by the back door with its keys on a hook.
