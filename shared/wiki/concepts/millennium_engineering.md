# Millennium Engineering Hangar

A former aircraft hangar given to the
[[engineering_department|Engineering Department]]: printers humming in a row,
lathes and welding bays, pegboards of polished tools, and robots in every stage
of assembly. A charging dock for a large robot sits near the entrance, a yellow
line on the floor marks where visitors stop, and the sign by the door reminds
them not to press anything.

## The department's habits

Every invention leaves the hangar with one feature nobody asked for. At the far
end is a walled bay lent to [[cleaning_and_clearing|C&C]], the
[[millennium_toki_workshop|C&C Equipment Bay]].
