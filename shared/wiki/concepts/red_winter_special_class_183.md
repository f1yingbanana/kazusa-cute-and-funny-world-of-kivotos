# No. 183 Special Class

No. 183 Special Class is where Red Winter sends science and engineering students
the Office has sentenced. They live and work at
[[red_winter_facility_183|Facility No. 183]], a research station high in the
mountains, and cannot leave without permission. The class representative is
the engineer [[anna-niki|Anna Niki]] and her vice representative is the chemist
[[erina-niki|Erina Niki]]; both were sent there after research accidents.

## Life in the class

The class does commissions for the [[red_winter_office|Office]] in exchange for
the fuel and food a supply truck brings up twice a week. Its pride is a tank it
is building in the [[red_winter_183_workshop|workshop]]. Disagreements between
the representatives are settled by wrestling on a mat in the workshop corner.
