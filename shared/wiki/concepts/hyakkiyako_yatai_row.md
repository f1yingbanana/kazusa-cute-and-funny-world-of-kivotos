# Hyakkiyako Yatai Row

A side lane off the [[hyakkiyako_shopping_district|shopping street]] where the
yatai carts set up every evening: yakitori smoke, takoyaki griddles, a ramen
cart with three stools, shaved ice in summer. Students eat here more than they
admit, and on festival days it runs from noon.
