# Abydos School Annex

A two-storey wing that once held the specialist rooms, now mostly shut. The
chemistry lab is locked with its cabinets empty, and sand drifts along
corridors nobody has time to sweep. The [[abydos_music_room|music room]] at the
end is the only room anyone still visits.
