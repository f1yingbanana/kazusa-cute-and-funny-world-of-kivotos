# Miyako's Tent

[[miyako-tsukiyuki]]'s tent, pitched at the head of the square as befits the
platoon leader. Her bedroll is folded to SRT standard and a laminated card of
the academy's ideals is clipped to the ridge pole. The hutch by the entrance
belongs to Bun Bun, her rabbit, who gets the dry corner when it rains.
