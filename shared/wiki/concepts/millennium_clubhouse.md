# Millennium Clubhouse

Three storeys of club rooms on the [[millennium_school|Millennium]] campus, one
long corridor per floor, every door plastered with posters, warnings and
recruitment flyers. The [[game_development_room|Game Development Department]],
[[veritas_clubroom|Veritas]] and the
[[millennium_paranormal_office|Paranormal Affairs Department]] all have rooms
here, along with the [[millennium_student_lounge|student lounge]] and, at the
back, an older [[millennium_clubhouse_annex|annex]] of rooms nobody uses. Noise
complaints and power-draw warnings are a weekly event.
