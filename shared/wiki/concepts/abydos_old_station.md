# Old Desert Line Station

The original Abydos Central Station, out in districts the desert has since
swallowed. The platform clock stopped long ago, the ticket hall is full of sand
and the rails run off half buried into the dunes. When the drifts covered the
approach, the line was cut back to the present [[abydos_station|station]] by
Suburban Town and this building was left to the desert. Survey crews from
[[highlander_railroad_academy|Highlander]] still come out now and then to check
the rails.
