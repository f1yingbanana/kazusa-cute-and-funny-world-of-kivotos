# Justice Task Force

The Justice Task Force, formally the Justice Realization Committee, is
[[trinity_school]]'s armed disciplinary body. It answers threats to the school,
arrests troublemakers and enforces discipline, working out of the
[[trinity_justice_hq]]. Its members wear black uniforms with red armbands
reading "Justice", and many have black wings.

Its president is [[tsurugi-kenzaki]], feared as Trinity's secret weapon; its
vice president is [[hasumi-hanekawa]].

## Inside the Task Force

- Hasumi handles most of the planning and formal business, and makes many
  decisions for Tsurugi.
- [[ichika-nakamasa]] is the officer closest to the rank and file and is
  charged with keeping Tsurugi from running wild, with limited success.
- [[mashiro-shizuyama]] is its earnest young sniper, who looks up to Hasumi.
- [[koharu-shimoe]] is a member, once posted to guard the contraband room,
  currently assigned to the [[make_up_work_club]] for her grades.
- Many in the Task Force share a long hostility toward [[gehenna_school]],
  Hasumi above all; see [[gehenna_trinity_rivalry]].
