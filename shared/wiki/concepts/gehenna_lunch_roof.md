# School Lunch Club Rooftop

The flat roof of the [[gehenna_lunch_building|School Lunch Club Building]], with
laundry lines of kitchen towels, a bench, and planters of herbs kept alive
against the odds. From here the whole campus is visible, and none of it can see
you.
