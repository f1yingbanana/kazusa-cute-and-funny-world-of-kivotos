# Knowledge Liberation Front

The Knowledge Liberation Front keeps the [[red_winter_library|Federal Library]]
of [[red_winter_school]], a cavernous library that hardly anyone visits. It has
almost no budget and few members: the librarian
[[momiji-akiizumi|Momiji Akiizumi]], who guards and hunts down books, and the
artist [[meru-himeki|Meru Himeki]], who draws and writes stories.

## Inside the Front

The Front files book-purchase requests with the Office with great regularity,
and has them rejected just as regularly. An earlier generation of the Front made
a fanzine that became a legend among its members; Momiji searched Red Winter
for it until she found it. Meru keeps a [[red_winter_meru_workroom|workroom]] at
the very back of the closed stacks, where she goes when drawing gets too hard.
