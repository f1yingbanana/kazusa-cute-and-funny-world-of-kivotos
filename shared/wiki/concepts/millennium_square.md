# Millennium Square

The middle of campus, at the foot of [[millennium_tower|Millennium Tower]]. A
fountain that runs light shows on festival nights, benches under young trees,
screens with [[seminar]] notices, and a row of food stalls and carts at
lunchtime selling yakisoba, crepes and skewers. The
[[millennium_campus_store|convenience store]] is just off it. If two Millennium
students agree to meet somewhere, it's here.
