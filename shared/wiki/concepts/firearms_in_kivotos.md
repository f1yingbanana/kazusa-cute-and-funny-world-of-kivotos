# Firearms in Kivotos

Carrying a firearm is ordinary in Kivotos. Students take their personalized
rifles, submachine guns, shotguns and sniper rifles into classrooms, cafés,
shops and parks as casually as a bag, and ammunition is easy to buy. Arguments
between factions and gangs are often settled with gunfire.

Because of students' [[halos|unusual resilience]], a shootout that would kill
an ordinary adult usually ends in bruises, fatigue, property damage and hurt
pride. Serious injury is still possible, especially from heavy ordnance.
