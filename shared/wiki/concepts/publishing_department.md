# Red Winter Publishing Department

The Publishing Department of [[red_winter_school]] writes, prints and sells the
Red Bear newspaper, magazines and licensed books from the
[[red_winter_printing_house|Red Bear Printing House]] on 3rd Street. It is led
by [[yakumo-aramaki|Yakumo Aramaki]], who is always drafting another business
proposal, and its critic [[takane-miyoshi|Takane Miyoshi]] has an opinion on
just about everything.

## Inside the department

Yakumo runs it as a business. A good share of what it prints is cheap licensed
material from outside, and she is always chasing ventures beyond publishing,
railways above all. The staff work from the
[[red_winter_editorial_room|editorial room]] above the presses, where writers
who miss deadlines are reminded firmly, sometimes at gunpoint.
