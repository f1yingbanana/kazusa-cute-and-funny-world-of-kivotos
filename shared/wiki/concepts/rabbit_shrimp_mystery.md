# RABBIT Squad’s Shrimp Mystery

[[rabbit_platoon|RABBIT Platoon]] investigated a shrimp shortage in
[[yatoura_village|a coastal fishing village]].

## The investigation

Their first suspicions, drawn from local legends, proved misleading. By helping
the residents and examining the evidence, they uncovered a smuggling operation,
and the grateful villagers later shared their shrimp with them.
