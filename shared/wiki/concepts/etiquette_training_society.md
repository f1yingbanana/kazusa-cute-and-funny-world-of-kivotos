# Etiquette Training Society

The Etiquette Training Society, also called the Inner Discipline Club, practises
manners, flower arranging, cooking, cleaning and traditional conduct at
[[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako]], out of the spotless
[[hyakkiyako_old_schoolhouse_18]]. Its president,
[[tsubaki-kasuga|Tsubaki Kasuga]], is famous for falling asleep anywhere, so
vice president [[mimori-mizuha|Mimori Mizuha]] runs most of its days, with the
first-year [[kaede-isami|Kaede Isami]] training hard beside them.

## Inside the club

Mimori keeps the club to its schedule and mentors Kaede, who joined out of
admiration for her two seniors when she transferred from the countryside. The
sleeping cushion in the corner of the tatami room is not to be moved.

[[renge-fuwa|Renge Fuwa]] of the Hyakkaryouran trained with the club on a trial
basis while her own council was dissolved.
