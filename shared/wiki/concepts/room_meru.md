# Meru's Room

[[meru-himeki|Meru]]'s room in the [[red_winter_first_dormitory|dormitory]]:
sketchbooks in drifts, screentone scraps stuck to the floor and convention
badges on the lampshade. A corkboard on the wall is hidden behind a curtain. The
wall she shares with the Publishing Department's critic
[[takane-miyoshi|Takane]] next door is thin enough to hear a typewriter pass
judgement at midnight. She locks the door whenever she leaves.

## Behind the curtain

Under the curtain is a chart of real students paired off for stories, which no
one is allowed to see.
