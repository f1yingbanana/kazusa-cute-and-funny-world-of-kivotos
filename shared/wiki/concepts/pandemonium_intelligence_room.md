# Pandemonium Intelligence Room

A dim room in [[pandemonium_headquarters|Pandemonium Society Headquarters]],
with monitors, unlabelled folders and an oddly large hypnotism poster. Most
intelligence gathered here comes from gossip, and the comfortable chair gets
more use than the equipment.

## Its head

The room is [[satsuki-kyougoku|Satsuki]]'s, who heads the council's information
work; when she is out shopping, the door stays locked.
