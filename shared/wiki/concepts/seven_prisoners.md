# Seven Prisoners

The Seven Prisoners is the name given to a set of notorious students once held
by the Corrections Bureau in the [[valkyrie_correctional]].
[[wakamo-kosaka|Wakamo Kosaka]], the Fox of Calamity, is one of them. They are
known for very different activities and are not a unified organization; the
name is what they have in common.
