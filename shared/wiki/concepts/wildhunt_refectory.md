# Wild Hunt Refectory

The dining hall of the whole academy, off the [[wildhunt_piazza|piazza]]: long
oak tables, a painted ceiling of hunting scenes and a serving line that moves at
its own pace. Breakfast is 07:00 to 08:30, lunch 12:00 to 13:30 and dinner 18:00
to 20:00, and it is packed at noon. Dorm groups claim the same tables every day.
