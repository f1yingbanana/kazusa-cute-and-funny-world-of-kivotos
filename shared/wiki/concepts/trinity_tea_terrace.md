# Tea Party Terrace

A marble terrace beside the [[trinity_tea_party]], with a view over the campus,
roses climbing its balustrade and a white table always laid with fine china.
The [[tea_party]] holds its meetings and receives guests here in good weather.
An invitation to this table is one of the highest honours at Trinity, and one
of the most nerve-racking.
