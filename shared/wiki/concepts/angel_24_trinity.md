# Angel 24, Trinity Street Branch

A bright convenience store at the top of [[church_street]], the end nearest the
dormitories, with a door that chimes as you come in. The narrow aisles hold
bottled tea, flavoured milk cartons, bento, strawberry sandwiches, instant
ramen, manga magazines and ammunition, and a window counter is where students
eat on the go.

Students drift in and out until the dorms lock. After 23:00 a single clerk
keeps the place, and the only customers are [[vigilante_crew|night
patrollers]] buying coffee and the odd student sneaking back by the side gate
of [[trinity_dorm]].
