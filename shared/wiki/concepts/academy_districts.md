# Academy Districts

Kivotos is made up of many academy districts, each with wide autonomy. A major
school governs its own territory: its streets, its security, its clubs and its
rules. Matters that cross district lines fall to the [[general_student_council]]
in [[du_downtown|D.U.]], the capital at the centre of the city. Academies and
their clubs vary considerably in culture and administration, and so do the
districts themselves; [[kivotos_geography|the lay of Kivotos]] describes where
each one lies.
