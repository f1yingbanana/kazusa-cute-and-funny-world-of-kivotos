# Gallery Kinescope

A narrow rented gallery in the [[wildhunt_canal_town|canal town]], one long room
lit only by its exhibits: stacked salvaged televisions and projectors playing
loops of light and static. It opens on weekends from 11:00 to 19:00, or whenever
the artist is in. The space is rented by a [[wild_hunt_academy|Wild Hunt]]
student, and its hours depend on her outing passes.
