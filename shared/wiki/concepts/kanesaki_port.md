# Kanesaki Port

The capital's harbour, where cargo ships unload into long rows of warehouses and
merchant guilds keep their offices. The Carpaccio Family handles security on the
piers. Past the far breakwater a beach curves away, [[outlaw_beach]], not the
kind anyone expects. [[odyssey_maritime_high_school]]'s ships berth at
[[odyssey_school|Port Terminal 3]], and the [[aegir_water_park|water park]]
stands on the coast beside the port.
