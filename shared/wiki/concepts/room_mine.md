# Mine's Room

[[mine-aomori|Mine]]'s ground-floor room in the [[trinity_old_quarters]].
Glass-fronted cabinets fill the walls, packed with medicine bottles, bandages
and old medical instruments she rescued from being thrown away. A good tea set
sits on a tray, and her riot shield leans against the bed, ready for the dawn
patrol she never misses.
