# Millennium Tower

The tallest building on the [[millennium_school|campus]] and the seat of
[[seminar]], Millennium's student council. The lobby is open to anyone with
business, usually a club head with a budget request clutched to her chest.
Above it are the [[seminar_office|Seminar Office]] and the records, the
[[millennium_conference_room|conference room]] near the top, and a floor of
locked rooms, the [[millennium_self_reflection_room|Self-Reflection Room]]
among them, where the guards at the stairwell take their job seriously. A
scorch mark on one upper window frame is from a rocket that was not supposed to
go that way.
