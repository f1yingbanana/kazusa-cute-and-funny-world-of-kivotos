# First Shared Dormitory Fireplace Lounge

The only truly warm room in the [[red_winter_first_dormitory|dormitory]]: a
brick fireplace, a samovar kept going all day, armchairs worn to the springs and
a radio tuned to [[red_winter_broadcasting|Red Bear]]. Hockey games are listened
to here in noisy groups, and residents toast bread on forks at the fire when the
cafeteria is closed.
