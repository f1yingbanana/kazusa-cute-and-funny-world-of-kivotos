# Prism Mall

The big mall of the [[millennium_shopping_district|shopping district]]: a glass
atrium with escalators crossing in the middle, fashion and a swimsuit shop on
the lower floors, a tool shop on the fourth with its premium wrenches in a
locked case, supplements and sportswear, and a food court with burgers on the
top floor.
