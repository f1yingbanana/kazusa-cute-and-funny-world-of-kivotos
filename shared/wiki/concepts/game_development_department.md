# Game Development Department

The Game Development Department of [[millennium_school]] makes video games,
mostly retro games inspired by old consoles and pixel art. It is a small,
noisy club that plays as hard as it works, and it has more than once had to
fight for its survival. Its president is [[yuzu-hanaoka|Yuzu Hanaoka]], with
the twins [[momoi-saiba|Momoi]] and [[midori-saiba|Midori Saiba]] and
[[aris-tendou|Aris Tendou]]. Their [[game_development_room|club room]] is
packed with games, consoles and development gear.

## The members

- Yuzu leads development and is one of the best gamers in Kivotos, though she
  hides in a locker in the club room, [[gdd_locker]].
- Momoi writes the scenarios, and joined because she loved Yuzu's game.
- Midori draws the art, sketches and sprites.
- Aris plays, tests and helps; she lives in the club room.
- [[kei-tendou|Kei]] acts as the club's stern big sister, nagging the others
  to look after themselves and guarding their servers.

## Tales Saga Chronicles

Yuzu's game "Tales Saga Chronicles" was named the worst game of the year. It
nearly made her quit, but Momoi loved it, and it brought her to the club.

## Trouble with Seminar

The club has faced closure; how it survived, and how it found Aris, is told in
[[game_development_department_crisis]]. [[seminar]]'s treasurer
[[yuuka-hayase|Yuuka]], whom Momoi calls "The Cruel Arithmetician", keeps
after Yuzu to attend the club presidents' meetings.
