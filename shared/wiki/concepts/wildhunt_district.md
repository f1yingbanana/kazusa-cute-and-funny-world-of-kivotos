# Wild Hunt Integrated Arts District

A star fortress of bastions and Italianate towers rises out of moats and canals:
the [[wildhunt_school|campus]] of [[wild_hunt_academy]], which fills it
entirely. Inside the walls every student lives in a dormitory the Arts Council assigns
and the Dorm Supervisory Unit polices, under a 22:00 curfew. There is one [[wildhunt_gate|gate]], and its inspection is famous:
bags are searched, unapproved pigments and even mugs are confiscated, and nobody
leaves without an outing pass.

Outside the walls, the small [[wildhunt_canal_town|canal town]] of galleries,
cafés and craft shops lives off the academy, and [[wildhunt_station]], the Western Line terminus at
the end of the quay, has trains to [[du_central_station|D.U. Central]] every 15
minutes. Up in the
hills is the academy's [[wildhunt_quarry|marble quarry]]. Things the
[[wildhunt_store|academy store]] won't sell have a way of coming over the walls
anyway.
