# No. 227 Special Class

No. 227 Special Class holds students suspended by the Red Winter Office: a
disciplinary isolation dormitory with difficult living conditions. They live at
the drafty [[red_winter_spec227|Former Institute]] on the edge of town,
with little heat and less money, and are meant to study by themselves. Among
them are the childhood friends [[nodoka-amami|Nodoka Amami]] and
[[shigure-mayoi|Shigure Mayoi]], who look after each other. The class once found
a hot spring and briefly ran a [[red_winter_hot_springs|resort]] on it.

## Life in the class

Nodoka was suspended for stalking with a telescope, and Shigure for mixing vodka
into the ration kompot, some say on purpose to stay with Nodoka. Nobody from the
Office checks on them often. The class gathers firewood, forages, and keeps the
small [[red_winter_227_bathhouse|bathhouse]] that is left of the resort, which
brings in visitors and a little money.
