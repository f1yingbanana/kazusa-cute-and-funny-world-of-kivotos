# Gehenna Hot Springs Worksite

A muddy valley high on [[hinnom_volcano]], littered with drilling rigs, crates
of explosives, tents and half-finished pools. The
[[hot_spring_development_department]] digs here until the [[prefect_team]]
chases it off, then comes back.
