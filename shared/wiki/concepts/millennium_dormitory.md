# Millennium Dormitory

A clean, modern block on the slope above the
[[millennium_shopping_district|shopping streets]], joined to the
[[millennium_school|campus]] by a covered footbridge. Through the
[[millennium_dorm_lobby|lobby]], rooms are singles along corridors by floor,
each corridor sharing a kitchenette, and the
[[millennium_dorm_laundry|basement laundry]] never closes. There is no roll
call: the door system simply logs who comes in after 23:30, and [[seminar]] can
read the log.
