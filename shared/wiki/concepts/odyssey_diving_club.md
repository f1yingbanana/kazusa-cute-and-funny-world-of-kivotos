# Odyssey Diving Club

The Diving Club is who Odyssey students call when something has to be done
under the water: a lost item to bring up, a cable to repair, a hull to look at.
It works from the [[odyssey_harbor|dive pier]] at the end of Port Terminal 3 and
keeps its tanks and wetsuits in the [[odyssey_dive_shed]] beside it.
[[kokoro-toumi]] is its president and [[kotone-fuchigami]] its vice president.

## In the club

Kokoro will not go into the water until every piece of gear she thinks
necessary is ready and checked, which makes the club slow to start and rarely
sorry. Kotone, who can barely see without her glasses, guides the others by
hearing and instinct when the water turns murky. Kokoro also likes to sing, and
sings badly; when she starts, her clubmates tend to find somewhere else to be.
