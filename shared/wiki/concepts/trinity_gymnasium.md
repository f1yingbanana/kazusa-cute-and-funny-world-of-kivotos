# Trinity Gymnasium

A big sports hall on the campus of [[trinity_school]], with a sprung floor, a
weights room, and an equipment store with a door that sticks shut. Behind it
are a 25-metre outdoor pool, drained in winter and fenced off at night, and the
[[trinity_shooting_range]].

Sports clubs take over after 16:00; early mornings belong to the few who train
alone.
