# Trinity Riverside Path

A paved path following the river along the bottom of
[[trinity_district|Trinity]], with willows, iron benches and lamps that stay
lit until 23:00. Steps lead up to [[church_street]] at one end and a lane into
the [[trinity_residential|Residential Quarter]] at the other. Runners use it at
dawn, families picnic on its grassy banks at weekends, and in the evening it is
quiet enough to think. On fine days the [[trinity_gelateria|gelato stand]] is
open.
