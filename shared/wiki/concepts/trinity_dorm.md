# Willow Hall

A peaceful three-storey Gothic dorm in the
[[trinity_residential|Residential Quarter]], behind tall hedges and weeping
willows, small enough that everyone knows everyone. The ground floor is a
lounge with upholstered armchairs and a fireplace, which everyone coming or
going passes through; upstairs, carpeted corridors lead to the rooms. Its
residents include the [[after_school_sweets_club]] and two of the
[[vigilante_crew]]'s night patrollers.

The dorm mother locks the front door at 22:00 and takes roll call from the top
floor down. The side gate is the quick way to the shops at the top of
[[church_street]]; the bus from the corner of the lane comes about every 30
minutes, which is why people usually walk.
