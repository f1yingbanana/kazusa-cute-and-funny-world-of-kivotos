# Valkyrie Police School, Shiratori Branch

[[valkyrie_police_academy]], the police academy of Kivotos, keeps its D.U.
campus in a leafy corner of [[shiratori_ward|Shiratori]]: low brick buildings
among old trees, a parade ground, and patrol bicycles racked along every wall.
Officers come and go at all hours, so the [[valkyrie_main_entrance|front desk]]
never closes, but the [[valkyrie_cafeteria|campus cafeteria]] keeps short hours
and everyone complains about it.

## The bureaus

Students here are trainees and serving officers at once, sorted into bureaus:
[[valkyrie_public_safety|Public Safety]] takes civil complaints and walks the
beats, [[valkyrie_public_peace|Public Peace]] investigates the serious cases,
and the Security and Investigation Bureaus keep their own floors of the
[[valkyrie_hq_building|headquarters building]] (and their own grudges). The
Corrections Bureau runs the [[valkyrie_correctional|prison]] on downtown's edge.
