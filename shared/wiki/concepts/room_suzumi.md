# Suzumi's Room

[[suzumi-morizuki|Suzumi]]'s simple room at the foot of the back stairs of
[[trinity_dorm]], chosen so she can slip out on patrol without waking anyone.
Glow-in-the-dark stars are stuck all over the ceiling, a headset hangs on the
bedpost, and a crate of flash grenades is pushed under the bed. A framed photo
from a fan convention is the only thing on the wall.
