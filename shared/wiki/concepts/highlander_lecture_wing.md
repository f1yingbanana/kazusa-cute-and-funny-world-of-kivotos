# Highlander Lecture Wing

A corridor of classrooms on the third floor of the
[[highlander_school|station campus]], one per year, where students study
signalling, timetabling, rail law and engineering between shifts. The windows
look over the platforms, which does nothing for concentration, and crews back
from night runs nod off at the back.
