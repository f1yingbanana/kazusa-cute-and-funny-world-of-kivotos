# Tri-Square

A wide triangular square at the centre of [[shiratori_ward|Shiratori]], with
fountains, shade trees and benches. Food trucks park along one side at lunch,
buskers take the other, and students from all over Kivotos use the clock on its
corner as a meeting point. The Metro station is underneath.
