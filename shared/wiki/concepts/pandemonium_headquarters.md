# Pandemonium Society Headquarters

A baroque hall of gilt and red velvet on the [[gehenna_school]] campus, too
large for the little work done in it, with banners of the council's crest and a
[[pandemonium_tank_hangar|hangar]] out back for its tanks. Guards stand at the
door mostly for show.

## Inside

Past the guards are [[pandemonium_executive_office|the president's office]], the
[[pandemonium_intelligence_room|intelligence room]] and the
[[pandemonium_break_room|break room]].
