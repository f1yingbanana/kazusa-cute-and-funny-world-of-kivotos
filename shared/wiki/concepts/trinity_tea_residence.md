# Tea Party Residence

A stone manor behind high walls on the [[trinity_outskirts]], home to the
members of the [[tea_party]] during their term. A guard post stands at the
gate, a car waits for the morning drive to the [[trinity_tea_party]], and the
windows on the east side overlook [[trinity_bird_park]].

## Since the attempt

Since the assassination attempt on [[seia-yurizono|Seia]], security is tighter,
and a [[knights_hospitaller|Remedial Knight]] calls daily on her as she
convalesces upstairs. One of the three suites has stood empty since its
occupant, [[mika-misono|Mika]], lost her privileges.
