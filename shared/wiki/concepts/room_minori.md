# Minori's Room

[[minori-yasumori]]'s narrow upstairs room in the prefab bunkhouse, the same
size as every crew member's. A hard hat hangs on the bedpost, rolled protest
placards are stored under the bunk, and a thick binder of union meeting minutes
sits beside a stack of dog-eared political novels full of her own margin notes.
