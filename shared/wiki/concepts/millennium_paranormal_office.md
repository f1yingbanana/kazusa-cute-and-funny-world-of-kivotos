# Super Phenomenon Task Force Room

A former storeroom in the [[millennium_clubhouse|clubhouse]], refitted as the
operations room of the
[[paranormal_affairs_department|Paranormal Affairs Department]]: a wall map of
Kivotos stuck with pins for unexplained events, a terminal, a reclining
"sickbed" chair, horoscope magazines, and a cooler of ice cream. The room is
either freezing or stifling, depending on who touched the thermostat last.

## Inside the department

The terminal has more access to the school's systems than it should. The
recliner is [[himari-akeboshi|Himari]]'s. The thermostat gets set to arctic by
one member and to sauna by another, several times a day.
