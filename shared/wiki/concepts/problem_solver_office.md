# Problem Solver 68 Office

An office on the second floor of the
[[gehenna_hotaru_building|Hotaru Building]]: pale green walls, a desk with
paperwork and a laptop, a sofa for clients that is also
[[aru-rikuhachima|the boss]]'s bed on late nights, and a framed emblem of the
agency. A dusty old dress hangs in one corner; the floor is swept every dawn.
