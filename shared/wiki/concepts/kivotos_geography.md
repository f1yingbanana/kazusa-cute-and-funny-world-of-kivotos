# The Lay of Kivotos

[[du_downtown|D.U.]], the [[general_student_council|General Student Council]]'s
capital, sits at the centre of Kivotos with [[gsc_headquarters|Sanctum Tower]]
over it. The great academies' districts surround it, each a city of its own,
joined by [[highlander_railroad_academy|Highlander]]'s railways (see
[[kivotos_transit]]).

- **South-west**: [[trinity_district|Trinity]], white stone and spires running
  down to the sea. Its border with Gehenna is the most argued-over line in
  Kivotos; [[borderland_amusement_park]] sits right on it.
- **South**: [[gehenna_district|Gehenna]]'s sprawling numbered zones, hot
  springs and [[araba_beach|Araba Beach]]. Past Gehenna's south end lies the
  [[black_market_district|Black Market District]], an unauthorized district no
  school polices, and beyond it the desert.
- **South-east**: [[abydos_district|Abydos]], once the richest district in
  Kivotos, now a town the desert is swallowing.
- **East**: [[haruhabara|Haruhabara Electric Town]] on the way to
  [[millennium_district|Millennium]], the newest of the great districts. Past
  Millennium's outskirts is a restricted zone of [[millennium_ruins|ruins]].
- **North-west**: [[hyakkiyako_district|Hyakkiyako]]'s lantern streets, its
  snowy [[ebisu_district|farmland further north]] around Ebisu, and next door
  the walled district of [[shanhaijing_district|Shanhaijing]] at the foot of
  Kunlun Mountain.
- **West**: [[wildhunt_district|Wild Hunt]]'s star-fortress art academy and its
  canal town.
- **Far north**: [[red_winter_district|Red Winter]], snow and taiga on the edge
  of Kivotos.
- **D.U. itself**: downtown around [[du_central_station]];
  [[shiratori_ward|Shiratori Ward]] with [[tri_square|Tri-Square]], the
  galleries, the river food street and [[kanesaki_port|Kanesaki Port]], where
  [[odyssey_maritime_high_school|Odyssey]]'s ships berth; and
  [[kousagi_ward|Kousagi Ward]] on the south-west outskirts, with
  [[usagi_park|Kousagi Park]] and the [[schale]] building on its hill.

[[valkyrie_police_academy|Valkyrie]] polices D.U. and the shared zones from its
[[valkyrie_school|Shiratori branch]]. Highlander governs its rail lines rather
than a district.
