# Dormitory No. 13 Fourth Floor

The top floor of [[gehenna_dorm13]]: a gyaru duo's doors covered in stickers, a
council member who is rarely home, and a medic whose radio never sleeps.

## Who lives here

The duo are [[kirara-yozakura|Kirara]] and [[erika-hatami|Erika]] of the
[[sparkle_club]]. The council member is [[iroha-natsume|Iroha]] of the
[[pandemonium_society]], and the medic is [[sena-himuro|Sena]] of the
[[emergency_medicine_club]].
