# Millennium Dormitory 2F West

A corridor of single rooms in the [[millennium_dormitory|dormitory]], with a
[[millennium_dorm_2w_kitchen|kitchenette]] at one end. Thin doors, loud games:
controller noise and arguments about art direction leak into the corridor most
nights, along with a draft of very cold air from one open door.
