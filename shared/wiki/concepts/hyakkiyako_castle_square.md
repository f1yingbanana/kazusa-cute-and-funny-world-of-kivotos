# Castle Square

The broad gravel square at the foot of the castle keep of the
[[hyakkiyako_main_building]], where every tour guide in Hyakkiyako stops to
point upward. It is also the front gate of [[hyakkiyako_school]], so students
late for homeroom have to run through the photographers. Stone steps lead down
to the [[hyakkiyako_shopping_district|shops]], the moat path runs round to the
[[hyakkiyako_shirakawa_quarter]], and the east gate opens on
[[hyakkiyako_plaza]].
