# Trinity Library Committee

The Library Committee looks after [[trinity_school]]'s books and library
spaces, from the vast [[school_library]] to the crumbling
[[trinity_old_library]]. It helps readers find what they want, keeps order in
the reading rooms and protects the collection.

It is headed by [[ui-kozeki]], who specializes in old books.

## Inside the committee

- Ui keeps to the Old Library, deciphering and managing antiquarian books, and
  rarely comes out.
- [[shimiko-endou]] is the friendly face at the desks, who has read the whole
  collection and recommends books to everyone. She is one of the few allowed to
  touch Ui's books, and tries to look after Ui.
- Troublemakers who mistreat books learn quickly that librarians are stronger
  than they look.
