# Trinity Seafront

Where [[trinity_district|Trinity]] meets the sea: a promenade of palms and
ice-cream kiosks, the [[trinity_aquarium]], and a long white
[[trinity_beach|beach]] that once belonged to the [[tea_party]]. On clear days
an uninhabited island with old ruins can be seen offshore.
