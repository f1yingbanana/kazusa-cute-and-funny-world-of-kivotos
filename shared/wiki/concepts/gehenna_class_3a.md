# Gehenna Class 3-A

A bright corner classroom on the fourth floor of the
[[gehenna_second_building|Second School Building]], with a view down to the
[[gehenna_lawn_square|Lawn Square]]. Leaders of the
[[pandemonium_society|council]] and of the [[prefect_team]] are both on its
roll, which makes seating a matter of diplomacy and homeroom announcements a
matter of public debate.
