# Oasis View Apartments

A four-storey block from when Abydos was rich, named for a view of the
[[abydos_oasis|Great Oasis]] that dried up long ago. Most doors are empty, the
landlord collects rent by post, and there is no manager on site. Several Abydos
students rent units here because it is cheap and a short walk from
[[abydos_school|school]]. There is a [[oasis_view_laundry|coin laundry]] in the
basement and a water tank on the [[oasis_view_roof|roof]] that everybody climbs
up to sooner or later.

## Tenants

fourth floor, [[serika-kuromi|Serika]] and [[ayane-okusora|Ayane]] on the
second.
