# Dormitory No. 4 Common Kitchen

A small kitchen beside the fourth-floor washroom of [[wildhunt_dorm4]]: a
kettle, two burners and a fridge full of labelled containers. It is busy in the
evenings until curfew. The kettle whistles at night for no reason anyone admits,
and the floor swears there is a ghost here at 02:00.
