# Dormitory No. 4

An old five-storey dormitory in the [[wildhunt_dorm_quarter|Dormitory Quarter]],
with creaking stairs, long corridors and shared rooms of two to four. The front
door is open 06:00 to 22:00. The fourth floor has a reputation for being
haunted, helped along by the residents of one particular room.
