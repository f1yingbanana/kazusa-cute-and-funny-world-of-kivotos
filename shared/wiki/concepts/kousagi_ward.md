# Kousagi Ward

The quiet south-west outskirts of [[du_downtown|D.U.]], where the towers give
way to low apartment blocks, small shops and green hills. [[kousagi_station]] is
the southern gateway out of the capital, the
[[kousagi_station_street|street outside it]] has everything a student needs, and
the [[schale_office|SCHALE]] building stands on the hill above
[[usagi_park|Kousagi Park]].

## Plans and closures

Kaiser Construction once planned to flatten the area into a "Kousagi Town"
development; the plan stalled. The shut-down campus of [[srt_training_facility]]
stands locked in the ward as well.
