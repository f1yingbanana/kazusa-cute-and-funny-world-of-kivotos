# Lantern Street Provisions

A general shop in an old vestry: tins, snacks, soap and phone cards. Everything
comes down the [[trinity_catacombs|catacomb]] route on a cart, so stock is a
week behind the surface. The magazine rack is the newest thing in the district,
and the snacks sell out on payday.
