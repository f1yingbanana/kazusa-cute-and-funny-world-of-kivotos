# Noa's Room

[[noa-ushio|Noa]]'s room: white walls, grey bedding, black desk, nothing in
colour. Shelves of identical notebooks hold her records, dated to the second.
The only thing that doesn't fit is a single air freshener on the windowsill, a
gift she kept. Through the wall she can hear [[room_yuuka|next door]]'s abacus
clicking late at night.
