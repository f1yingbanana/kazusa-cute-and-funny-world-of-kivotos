# Yakumo's Room

[[yakumo-aramaki|Yakumo]]'s room in the
[[red_winter_first_dormitory|dormitory]]. Rail maps cover the walls, every line
pinned with timetables and her own notes on how it could be run better. A shelf
holds empty station bento boxes, washed and kept as souvenirs: the octopus pot,
the train-shaped one. Business proposal binders are stacked under the bed. She
locks up out of habit.
