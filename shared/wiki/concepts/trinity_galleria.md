# Trinity Galleria

A three-storey glass-roofed mall at the station end of [[church_street]].
Clothes shops line the ground floor; upstairs are [[trinity_bookshop]], the
[[trinity_bijouterie]] accessory boutique, a music shop selling guitars and
second-hand records, a fully automated [[trinity_momo_store|Momo Friends
store]], the [[trinity_karaoke|karaoke box]] and, on the top floor, the
[[trinity_cinema|cinema]].

The main doors close at 21:00; the cinema and karaoke stay open later through a
side lift.
