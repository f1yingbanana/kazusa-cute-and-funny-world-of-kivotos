# Valkyrie Police

Valkyrie's students are D.U.'s police while they train: they walk the beats,
take complaints, investigate crimes and run the city's prison, all from the
[[valkyrie_school]] in Shiratori. Officers are sorted into bureaus, which share
the [[valkyrie_hq_building]] and do not always share much else.

## The bureaus

The [[valkyrie_public_safety]] on the ground floor takes civil complaints and
patrols the beats; it is where [[kirino-nakatsukasa]], who badly wants
something more heroic, and [[fubuki-nemugaki]], who does not, work as partners.
The [[valkyrie_public_peace]] on the top floor investigates the serious cases
and is feared across D.U.; [[kanna-ogata]], the "Feral Hound", is its director
and [[konoka-shima]] her deputy. The Security Bureau sits between them, the
Investigation Bureau keeps its own floor, and the Corrections Bureau runs the
[[valkyrie_correctional]], from which several notorious prisoners have escaped.
