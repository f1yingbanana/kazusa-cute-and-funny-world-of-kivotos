# Haruhabara Electric Town

Neon signs stacked six storeys high, speakers blaring anime themes, and
sidewalks crowded with students hunting parts, games and figures. The Electric
Street runs from the [[haruhabara_station|station]] past the
[[haruhabara_multimedia|Multimedia Center]], the
[[haruhabara_video_hall|Video Hall]] and dozens of narrow shops, among them the
[[mandrake_complex|Mandrake Complex]], [[maid_cafe_candy|Maid Cafe
Candy-candy]] and [[kinds_steakhouse|Kinds! Steakhouse]]. It lies between
[[du_downtown|D.U.]] and [[millennium_district|Millennium]], and Millennium
students come here straight after school.
