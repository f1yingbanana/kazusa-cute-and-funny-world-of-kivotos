# Aoba's Room

[[aoba-utsumi]]'s crew bedroom, the one furthest down the corridor from the
[[hikari-tachibana|Tachibana]] twins, on purpose. A workbench corner holds a
toaster, a radio and a fan in various states of repair, and the mug on the desk
has been glued back together three times. Earplugs and motion-sickness pills sit
by the bed, both well used.
