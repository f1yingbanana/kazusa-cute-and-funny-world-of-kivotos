# Kadenokouji Estate

A walled compound on the hill above town, its gate staffed day and night: an
old main house, a moss garden, a family shrine and servants' quarters. This is
the home of the [[kadenokouji_family]], a venerable miko lineage, and the
servants move through it quietly and on schedule.

## The household

The household runs on ritual, schedules and polite silence. A doctor lives on
the grounds, the family's daughters pray each dawn at the
[[kadenokouji_shrine]], and [[yukari-kadenokouji]] has her
[[room_yukari|room]] in the main house. The family keeps a rickshaw for the
hill road down to town.
