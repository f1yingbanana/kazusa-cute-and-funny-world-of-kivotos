# Tree Square

A round square off [[hyakkiyako_plaza]], built around a vast old tree wrapped
in a sacred rope, with benches in its shade. Festival scaffolding goes up here
first, and the Tree of Deities rite is held beneath it; when nothing is on, it
is a place to sit and watch the stalls being built.
