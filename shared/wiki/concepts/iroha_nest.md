# Iroha's Nest

Behind a tarp in the far corner of the [[pandemonium_tank_hangar|tank hangar]]:
an enormous cushion, a lamp, stacks of novels, a console and a crate of snacks.
[[iroha-natsume|Iroha]] spends many class hours here, with her tank Toramaru
parked a few metres away.
