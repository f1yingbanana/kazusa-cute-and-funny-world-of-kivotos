# Tsurugi's Room

Everyone expects chaos in [[tsurugi-kenzaki|Tsurugi]]'s room in the
[[trinity_magnolia_west|West Wing]] and finds it neat and well organised.
Romance novels are shelved by author, movie ticket stubs are pinned in rows,
and a locked chest sits at the foot of the bed.

## The chest

It holds odd keepsakes she won't explain to anyone.
