# Abydos Debt

[[abydos_school]] borrowed heavily from Kaiser Loan, part of
[[kaiser_corporation]], in earlier attempts to hold back the desert. The desert
won anyway, and much of the district's land passed to Kaiser as the debt grew.
The interest and recurring payments are a crushing burden on the few students
left, and the [[foreclosure_task_force]] works to keep the school open while
looking for a way out.

## Paying it down

Every month the Task Force has to find the interest payment, and the whiteboard
in their [[abydos_task_force_room|club room]] tracks it against what they have
earned from jobs and from salvage sold to
[[abydos_antiques|the antiques dealer]]. They found links between Kaiser and
the [[helmet_gang|Helmet Gang]] that harassed the school, and when
[[hoshino-takanashi|Hoshino]] once accepted an offer meant to relieve the debt,
Kaiser moved against Abydos. The debt is still there.
