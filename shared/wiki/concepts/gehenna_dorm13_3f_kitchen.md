# Dormitory No. 13 Third-Floor Kitchen

The [[gehenna_dorm13_3f|third floor]]'s kitchen, the best in the dorm: four
burners, an oven, a rice cooker the size of a drum on loan from one room and a
fire extinguisher on loan from another.
