# Black Market

An unauthorized district beyond [[gehenna_district|Gehenna]]'s south end, run by
no rules but its own. Covered alleys of stalls sell everything from snacks to
restricted weapons, and the people who come here for what they can't buy
anywhere else keep their hands near their guns. Off the main alleys lie an
[[black_market_arms_row|arms row]], a [[black_market_job_board|job board]], a
neon [[black_market_entertainment|entertainment quarter]] and the district's own
[[black_market_bank|bank]].

The Black Market is an open secret in Kivotos: a network of stalls and side
streets where vendors sell both ordinary merchandise and prohibited or
restricted goods. Most of it is concentrated in this district. That something is
on sale here does not make buying it legal, or safe.

## The district

It is about the size of a small academy's district. Dropouts, students on leave
and unregistered clubs live in its blocks, and it keeps its own enforcers. The
bank lends to anyone at terrible rates, and the job board offers work with no
questions asked. A bus runs in from [[gehenna_backstreets|Gehenna's Zone 32
backstreets]], and four desert buses a day come from
[[abydos_district|Abydos]].
