# Red Mustache Revolution Plaza

The academy's ceremonial square, paved under the snow, with the columned
[[red_winter_secretariat|Office Building]] on one side and the
[[red_winter_broadcasting|Red Bear radio tower]] on the other. In the middle
stands a giant floodlit statue of [[cherino-renkawa|the president]] wearing an
outsized mustache. Speeches are given from a wooden podium most lunchtimes, the
Red Winter Book Festival fills the square with stalls once a year, and in
between it is where every crowd of students crossing campus meets.

## The statue

The statue's bolts are kept loose, because it is changed after every coup and
changed back after every counter-coup.
