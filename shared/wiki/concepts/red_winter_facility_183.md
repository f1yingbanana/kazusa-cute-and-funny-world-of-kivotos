# Facility No. 183

An old research station on a ridge high in the snowy mountains, cold and full of
salvaged machinery. The [[red_winter_office|Office]] uses it to hold the science
and engineering students it has sentenced,
[[red_winter_special_class_183|No. 183 Special Class]]. They cannot leave
without permission, and a supply truck grinds up the single track twice a week
with their fuel and food. Off the main corridor are the
[[red_winter_183_workshop|tank workshop]], the
[[red_winter_183_lab|laboratory]] and the
[[red_winter_183_kitchen|mess kitchen]].

## How the class lives

The class pays for its supplies with commissions for the Office, built in the
workshop or brewed in the lab. Its great project is the tank in the garage, which
has been nearly finished for a long time.
