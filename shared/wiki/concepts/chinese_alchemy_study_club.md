# Chinese Alchemy Study Club

A research club of [[shanhaijing_senior_secondary_school]] that studies the old
art of alchemy as experimental science: elixirs, medicines, supplements and the
odd love potion. It works out of the [[shanhaijing_alchemy_lab]], where
something is always bubbling. Its prodigy is [[saya-yakushi]], whose results
range from brilliant to disastrous with very little warning.

## Inside the club

The club makes the medicinal incense that [[kisaki-ryuuge]] relies on. Saya once
saved Kisaki's life from a poisoning, and she still prepares and checks Kisaki's
medicine every day. Neither of them talks about it.
