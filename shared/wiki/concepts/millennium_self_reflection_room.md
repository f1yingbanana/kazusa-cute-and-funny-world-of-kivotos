# Self-Reflection Room

On the guarded floor of [[millennium_tower|Millennium Tower]], a room meant to
be a bare cell where rule-breakers think about what they did. Its most frequent
occupant, [[koyuki-kurosaki|Koyuki]], has made it hers: fairy lights along the
ceiling, a shelf of board games, card decks and dice in a teacup, a snack stash
under the cot, and a pair of soft pajamas. A single coin always sits on the
desk, ready to be flipped. The lock is on the outside; somehow it rarely holds.

## Behind the sentence

The pajamas were bought by a strict friend, who pretends she didn't. The
sentences are [[seminar]]'s to hand out, and so is the key.
