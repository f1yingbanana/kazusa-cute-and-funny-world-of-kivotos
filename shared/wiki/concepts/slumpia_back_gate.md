# Slumpia Back Gate

The real entrance to Slumpia: a peeled-back section of fence behind the
weed-grown parking lot, where the ward bus still stops. Three vending machines
glow beside it, wired to power from who knows where; one of them sells drinks
nobody has heard of. Coins roll under them often, and someone fishes them out
often.
