# Cantata Room

Once the choir's practice room upstairs in the [[west_annex]], now shared by
any club that needs to make noise. Egg-crate foam covers the walls, an old
upright piano stands in one corner and a drum kit in another, and a sign-up
sheet on the door decides who gets the room each evening.
