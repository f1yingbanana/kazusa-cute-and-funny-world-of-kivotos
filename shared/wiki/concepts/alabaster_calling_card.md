# Alabaster Calling Card

A mistaken job offer sent [[game_development_department|the Game Development
Department]] to a mansion where [[toki-asuma|Toki]] was also working. There they
ran into a thief, an illicit art operation and, before long,
[[cleaning_and_clearing|C&C]].

## What happened

The thief was Kiyosumi Akira. Uncovering the art operation threw the department
together with C&C, and left the students with questions about what art is for
and how it should be preserved.
