# Shiorido Used Books

A deep, narrow shop off the [[hyakkiyako_shopping_district|shopping street]],
all tottering shelves: haiku anthologies by the door, military histories at the
back, a wall of light novels and shojo manga in between. The owner reads at the
counter and doesn't mind people standing for hours.
