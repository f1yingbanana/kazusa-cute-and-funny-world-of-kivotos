# Peach Blossom Spring

A carved two-storey teahouse built over a little stream of koi. Downstairs, tea
is poured gongfu style beside baskets of dim sum; upstairs, screened private
rooms line a quiet gallery. Visitors who come all the way to Shanhaijing come
here.

## Upstairs

The private rooms are where deals are made and secrets kept.
