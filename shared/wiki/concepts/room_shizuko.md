# Shizuko's Room

A little room up a steep staircase above [[momoyodou]], where
[[shizuko-kawawa]] sleeps. A futon is squeezed between stacks of account
ledgers and festival schedules, spare kimono aprons hang on a hanger, and an old,
chipped practice tea set sits on the shelf.

## The tea set

The chipped practice tea set is one thing she would never sell.
