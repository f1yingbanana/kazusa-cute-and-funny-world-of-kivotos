# Kata-Kata Helmet Gang Camp

Tents, scrap-metal shacks and a bonfire ring among half-buried buildings, with
armed lookouts on the rooftops. This is the base of the Kata-Kata
[[helmet_gang|Helmet Gang]], loud at night and restless by day; when they are
bored they still raid the town.

## Who paid them

For a long time the gang harassed [[abydos_school]] with someone else's money
behind them. The [[foreclosure_task_force]] found the links between the gang
and [[kaiser_corporation|Kaiser]].
