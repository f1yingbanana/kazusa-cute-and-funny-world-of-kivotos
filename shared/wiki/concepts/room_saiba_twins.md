# Momoi and Midori's Room

A line of tape on the floor divides it. [[momoi-saiba|Momoi]]'s half is a
console tower, tangled controllers, event posters, scenario notebooks and
cod-roe cracker bags; [[midori-saiba|Midori]]'s is a drawing tablet, art books
and claw-machine plushies. On the tidy side's shelf sits an old handheld that
no longer turns on, kept on purpose.
