# Former Matsu-no-yu Bathhouse

A bathhouse in the [[gehenna_backstreets|backstreets]] that went out of business
years ago. The chimney still stands, the painted mountain on the wall is
peeling, and the boiler room door is newer than the rest of the building.

## The boiler room

Behind that door is [[kasumi_hideout_86|Hideout No. 86]], one of
[[kasumi-kinugawa|Kasumi]]'s many hiding places, and the source of the noises at
night.
