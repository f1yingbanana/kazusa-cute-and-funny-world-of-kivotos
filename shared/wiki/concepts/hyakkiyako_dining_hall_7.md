# Student Dining Hall No. 7

A long wooden dining hall on the grounds of [[hyakkiyako_school]], with noren
curtains at the counter and benches by the windows. Lunch is a crush; in the
afternoons the kitchen lets students book a stove for cooking practice, which
is why it so often smells of burnt tamagoyaki.
