# Haruhabara Station

The main entrance to [[haruhabara|Electric Town]]. Costumed mascots wave from
the exit, and the ticket gates are papered with posters for new releases.
Eastern Line trains run to D.U. Central one way and
[[millennium_station|Millennium]] the other.
