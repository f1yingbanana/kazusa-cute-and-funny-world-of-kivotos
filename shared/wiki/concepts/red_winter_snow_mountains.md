# Red Winter Snow Mountains

Forested mountains above the district, cut by a few ploughed roads and the
Northern Trunk line. A mountain road climbs from the
[[red_winter_spec227|Former Institute]] past the
[[red_winter_forestry_storage|forestry depot]] and
[[red_winter_district8_post|District 8's security post]], and further up a
single track leads to [[red_winter_facility_183|Facility No. 183]]. Above a bend
in the railway is the [[red_winter_bear_ridge|Bear Ridge lookout]]. Snowstorms
come in fast, bears are real, and people who leave the road get lost.
