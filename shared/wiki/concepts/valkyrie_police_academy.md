# Valkyrie Police Academy

Valkyrie is the academy that trains Kivotos's police, and its students are
trainees and serving officers at once: in uniform, they are the
[[valkyrie_police]]. Its officers include the Community Safety Bureau partners
[[kirino-nakatsukasa|Kirino Nakatsukasa]] and
[[fubuki-nemugaki|Fubuki Nemugaki]] and the Public Security Bureau officers
[[kanna-ogata|Kanna Ogata]] and [[konoka-shima|Konoka Shima]]. Its D.U. campus is the [[valkyrie_school]], a leafy corner
of Shiratori with brick bureau buildings, classrooms, a shooting range and the
dormitories where most of its students live.

## The school

Students go to class between shifts, and the campus cafeteria keeps such short
hours that everyone complains about it. When [[srt_training_facility]] closed,
its students were offered places at Valkyrie; [[rabbit_platoon]] turned them
down.
