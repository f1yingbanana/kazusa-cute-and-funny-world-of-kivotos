# Clubhouse Annex Room 13

Behind a keypad lock at the end of the [[millennium_clubhouse_annex|annex]]
corridor, the blinds down, is [[himari-akeboshi|Himari]]'s secret hideaway: a
plush recliner with a blanket, a tarot deck laid out on a side table, a record
player stacked with enka, horoscope magazines, and a terminal glowing in the
corner.

## How she keeps it

The room is still registered to a club that dissolved years ago; Himari got it
by quietly editing the school's room registry. The terminal is patched into the
campus systems, the broadcast speakers included. It is the one room on campus
that "Big Sister", [[rio-tsukatsuki|Rio]], was never allowed to see.
