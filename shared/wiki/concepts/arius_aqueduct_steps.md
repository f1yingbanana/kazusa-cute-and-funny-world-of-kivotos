# Arius Aqueduct Steps

The nearest thing Arius has to a riverside: broad stone steps down to a channel
of running water, under a vault cracked open to the surface. For an hour before
evening, real daylight falls through and people sit on the steps to watch it.
Sound carries beautifully here.
