# Frenzy MAX

Frenzy MAX is a caffeinated energy drink sold across Kivotos, a staple for
students working through the night. At [[millennium_school]] its best-known
fan is the engineer [[hare-omagari|Hare Omagari]] of [[veritas]], who is rarely
seen without a can, especially while working late.

## Hare's habit

Hare depends on it. Her favorite flavor is passion fruit; she says the others
taste bitter from the sweetener. When she once tried to give it up for three
days, the headaches and exhaustion beat her, and she swore never to quit
again.
