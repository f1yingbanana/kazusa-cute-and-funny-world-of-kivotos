# Kivotos

Kivotos is a sprawling, autonomous megacity divided into
[[academy_districts|semi-independent academy districts]]. Thousands of
academies live side by side in territories of their own culture, politics and
architecture, from the gothic-cathedral courtyards of
[[trinity_school|Trinity General School]] to the corporate high-rises of
[[millennium_school|Millennium]] and the sprawling desert ruins of
[[abydos_school|Abydos]]. [[kivotos_geography|The lay of Kivotos]] and
[[kivotos_transit|getting around it]] are a subject of their own.

Every student has a [[halos|halo]], and with it a body far tougher than an
ordinary person's; that is why [[firearms_in_kivotos|carrying a gun]] is as
ordinary as carrying a bag, and why most fights end in bruises and property
damage. The [[general_student_council|General Student Council]] governs the
city as a whole from [[gsc_headquarters|Sanctum Tower]] in D.U. Its vanished
President founded [[schale|SCHALE]], the Federal Investigation Club: led by the
adult [[sensei|Sensei]], it can act across every academy's borders to resolve
crises and help students anywhere in Kivotos.

## Halos and resilience

- **Halos**: Every student in Kivotos possesses a unique, glowing halo floating
  above their head. The halo reflects the student's consciousness, vitality, and
  mystic identity. When a student falls asleep or is knocked unconscious, their
  halo disappears until they awaken.
- **Physical Resilience**: Halos grant students immense physical durability.
  Normal firearm rounds, explosions, and impacts that would be fatal to regular
  humans cause little more than bruises, fatigue, or scrapes to students.
  However, heavy ordnance and extreme cumulative damage can incapacitate them.

## Firearms as everyday items

- Carrying firearms is ubiquitous throughout Kivotos. Assault rifles, submachine
  guns, shotguns, and sniper rifles are treated as standard personal
  accessories, carried casually into classrooms, cafes, shopping centers, and
  parks.
- Disagreements between factions or delinquent gangs are frequently settled with
  gunfire, typically resulting in property damage and bruised pride rather than
  mortal casualties.

## Governance and SCHALE

- **General Student Council (GSC)**: The centralized governing body of Kivotos,
  headquartered in Sanctum Tower at the centre of D.U., the capital district.
- **Federal Investigation Club (SCHALE)**: An extra-judicial, city-wide club
  established by the vanished General Student Council President. Led by the
  adult "Sensei", SCHALE possesses authority across all academy boundaries to
  resolve crises, provide mentorship, and support students across Kivotos.
