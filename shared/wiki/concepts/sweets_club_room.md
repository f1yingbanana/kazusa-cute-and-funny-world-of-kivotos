# Sweets Club Room

A sunlit room upstairs in the [[west_annex]], claimed and redecorated by the
[[after_school_sweets_club]]. It smells of vanilla, toasted pastry and melted
chocolate. A long wooden table in the middle is surrounded by mismatched
pastel-cushioned chairs and covered in dessert magazines and tea sets; in the
corner stand a small refrigerator of butter, cream, preserves and milk cartons,
a countertop oven and a baking station.

## The empty chair

The club has never filled its president's chair.
