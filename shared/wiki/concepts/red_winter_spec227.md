# Former Institute

The academy's old school building, abandoned when the new campus was built and
now used to hold students the [[red_winter_office|Office]] has suspended. Most
of it is shut and unheated; snow drifts through broken panes in the upper
corridors. [[special_class_227|No. 227 Special Class]] lives in the few rooms
that still keep heat, and nobody from the Office comes to check on them very
often.

The campus shuttle train runs here from [[red_winter_station|Central Station]]
roughly hourly, late as a rule; the last one back to town leaves at 22:40. The
other way is a long mountain road. Behind the building, a hot spring found by
the class now feeds a small [[red_winter_227_bathhouse|bathhouse]] that town
visitors ride out to in the evenings.
