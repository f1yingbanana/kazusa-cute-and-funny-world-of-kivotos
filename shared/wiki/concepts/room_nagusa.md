# Nagusa's Room

The north room of the [[hyakkiyako_kawabata_house]] that nobody else wanted,
which is why [[nagusa-goryou]] took it: dim and cool even in summer. A
meditation cushion, a stack of paperbacks, an electric fan, a jug of cold water,
and a framed photo lying face-down on the shelf.

## The photo

The face-down photo is of the old [[hyakkaryouran_resolution_council]].
