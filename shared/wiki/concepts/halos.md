# Student Halos

Every student in Kivotos has a glowing halo floating above her head, each one
different. A halo vanishes when its owner falls asleep or is knocked
unconscious, and returns when she wakes.

Students are also far tougher than ordinary people: bullets, explosions and
falls that would kill a regular adult leave them with bruises, scrapes and
fatigue, which is why [[firearms_in_kivotos|guns]] are an everyday part of life
here. That resilience has limits. Heavy ordnance or enough accumulated damage
can still knock a student out or badly hurt her.
