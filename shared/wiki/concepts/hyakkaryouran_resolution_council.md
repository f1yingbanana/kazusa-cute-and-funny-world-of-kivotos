# Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council

The Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council, usually just the Hyakkaryouran, mediates
disputes and protects [[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako]]. Its members are known by
their sky blue haori with white five-petaled flowers, their standard
Hyakkaryouran Type 38 rifles and their uncanny marksmanship. They work out of
the old [[hyakkaryouran_clubhouse]], where the lights are often on past
midnight.

The council was founded by the prophet [[kuzunoha]], and its president carries
the [[hundred_lotus]] as the sign of her authority. It has four members: the
acting president [[nagusa-goryou|Nagusa Goryou]], the operations officer
[[kikyou-kiryuu|Kikyou Kiryuu]], the assault captain [[renge-fuwa|Renge Fuwa]]
and the youngest, [[yukari-kadenokouji|Yukari Kadenokouji]].

## Ayame and the dissolution

The council's former president, Ayame Nanakado, disappeared in the
[[great_snowfield]]. When the members looked to Nagusa to take her place, Nagusa
handed the Authority of the President to Kikyou and left, and the council fell
apart. Renge drifted from club to club; Yukari refused to accept the
dissolution and went looking for everyone. During a Lantern Festival, with
[[natures_beauty]] loosing spirits on the district, the four faced the hurt
between them and came back together ([[hyakkaryouran_crisis]]).

## Daily life

Renge runs the morning drills and holds everyone to her own standard; Kikyou
plans and gathers intelligence; Yukari trains harder than anyone to prove she
belongs. Only the president's rifle can harm a
[[hyakkiyako_phantom_fables|Phantom Fable]], so those fights fall to the
council.
