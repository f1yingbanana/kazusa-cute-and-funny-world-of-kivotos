# Cafe Maidramatic!

Up a narrow staircase above a game shop in the
[[millennium_shopping_district|shopping district]]: frills and pastel lights,
omelettes decorated in ketchup, and a stage for the hourly song. Several of the
maids are Millennium students working evening shifts.
