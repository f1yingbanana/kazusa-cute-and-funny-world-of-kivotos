# Odyssey Maritime High School

Odyssey is a school of ships: dozens of them moored along
[[odyssey_school|Port Terminal 3]] in [[kanesaki_port]], gathered around the
flagship [[odyssey_olympus|Olympus]]. Its students live, study and work afloat,
and the school is run by its "Island" leadership from the flagship's bridge.
Gangways are guarded and visitors sign in at the gate office. Its
[[odyssey_diving_club]] takes on underwater work around the port.

## Life afloat

Odyssey students follow sailors' taboos to the letter and brew kelp where
others brew tea. Every two years the flotilla casts off for about a year at
sea, with no contact with Kivotos but the [[general_student_council]]. The
terminal is the only land the school has, and it is rented from the port.
