# Wild Hunt Art Center

A long Italianate building with a clock tower, where nearly every class at
[[wild_hunt_academy|Wild Hunt]] is taught. Each wing smells of its medium:
turpentine from the [[wildhunt_painting_studios|painting studios]], stone dust
from the [[wildhunt_sculpture_workshop|sculpture workshop]], hot electronics
from the [[wildhunt_media_lab|media lab]], machine oil from the
[[wildhunt_costume_workshop|costume workshop]]. The
[[wildhunt_sound_studios|sound studios]], the
[[wildhunt_lecture_hall|Grand Lecture Hall]] and the
[[wildhunt_literature_room|literature seminar room]] are here too. The corridors
are full from 07:00 to 21:00, and at the end of each month practical exams fill
them with nervous students carrying canvases.
