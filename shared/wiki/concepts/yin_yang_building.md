# Yin-Yang Club Building

An old hall on the grounds of [[hyakkiyako_school]], with a curved tiled roof
and a yin-yang crest above the door. It belongs to the [[yin_yang_club]], and
every club with a dispute, a festival permit or a complaint ends up here sooner
or later, waiting on the bench in the entrance. Inside are the club's
[[yin_yang_meeting_room|meeting room]] and the
[[yin_yang_auditorium|auditorium]].

## Behind the meeting room

Members slip away from the meeting room to the
[[yin_yang_lounge|tatami lounge]] behind it.
