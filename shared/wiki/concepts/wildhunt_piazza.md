# Wild Hunt Statue Piazza

A paved square at the heart of the [[wildhunt_school|fortress]] with an odd
purple statue in the middle whose meaning nobody can agree on; "meet at the
statue" is how half of [[wild_hunt_academy|Wild Hunt]] makes plans. Benches line
the arcades, and the [[wildhunt_refectory|refectory]], the
[[wildhunt_store|academy store]] and the [[wildhunt_espresso|espresso bar]] open
onto it. It is busiest at lunch, 12:00 to 13:30, and after class.
