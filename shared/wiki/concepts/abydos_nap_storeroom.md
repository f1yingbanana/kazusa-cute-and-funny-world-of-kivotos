# Third-Floor Storeroom, Abydos Main Building

An unused storeroom on the empty third floor, its door sticking in the frame.
Behind a wall of stacked desks at the back, someone has built a napping fort:
cushions, a duck-down quilt and an alarm clock with the bell taped silent.
Hardly anyone knows it is here.
