# Millennium Lecture Building

A plain, bright block with wide stairwells and digital boards outside every
door, crowded at 08:20 and again at lunch. The
[[millennium_cafeteria|Central Cafeteria]] fills the ground floor, homerooms
such as [[millennium_class_1c|1-C]], [[millennium_class_2a|2-A]] and
[[millennium_class_3b|3-B]] take up the floors above, and the stairwell keeps
going up to a fenced [[millennium_rooftop|rooftop]].
