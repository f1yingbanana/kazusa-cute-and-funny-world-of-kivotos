# Gourmet Research Society

Gehenna's Gourmet Research Society pursues memorable food, sometimes to chaotic
extremes. Haruna Kurodate leads the club, joined by Akari Wanibuchi, Junko
Akashi and Izumi Shishidou. Their tastes range from elaborate presentation to
enormous portions and unconventional combinations. Their pursuit of meals has
repeatedly brought them into conflict with School Lunch Club cook Fuuka Aikiyo.
