# Gourmet Research Society

The Gourmet Research Society of [[gehenna_school]] pursues memorable food, often
to chaotic extremes. Its president, [[haruna-kurodate|Haruna Kurodate]], is
infamous for blowing up restaurants that fail her standards, and the society's
raids, kidnappings and explosions make the news often enough that most of
Kivotos has heard of it. The other members are
[[akari-wanibuchi|Akari Wanibuchi]], [[junko-akashi|Junko Akashi]] and
[[izumi-shishidou|Izumi Shishidou]].

## Inside the society

Each member eats differently: Haruna cares for ingredients, presentation and
ceremony; Akari for sheer quantity; Izumi for strange combinations; and Junko
simply wants a good meal in peace and rarely gets one. Akari drives the yellow
six-wheeled School Lunch Promotion Vehicle.

When they want home cooking, they kidnap [[fuuka-aikiyo|Fuuka Aikiyo]] of the
[[school_lunch_club]], so often that she has grown used to it. They carefully
avoid asking her clubmate [[juri-ushimaki|Juri]] to cook. The society meets in
its [[gehenna_gourmet_clubroom|clubroom]], furnished like a private dining
room, with a wall map of restaurants marked with stars or crosses.
