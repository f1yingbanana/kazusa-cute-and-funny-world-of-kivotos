# After-School Sweets Club (放課後スイーツ部)

An official yet delightfully whimsical extracurricular club at
[[trinity_school|Trinity General School]]. While other clubs concern themselves
with grand politics, religious duties, or combat readiness, the Sweets Club is
dedicated purely to the appreciation, research, and enjoyment of delicious
pastries, parfaits, cakes, and tea. Its four first-years
gather in the [[sweets_club_room]] after class most days and head out from
there to whichever bakery is new.

The members are [[kazusa-kyouyama]], cool and a little aloof;
[[yoshimi-ibaragi]], small, loud and the self-declared leader;
[[airi-kurimura]], gentle and devoted to mint chocolate; and
[[natsu-yutori]], who philosophizes over a carton of milk.

## The members up close

- **Kazusa Kyouyama (杏山カズサ)**: A cool, cat-eared first-year who presents a
  slightly aloof, composed exterior. Despite her low-profile attitude, she is
  deeply attached to the club and has a legendary past as Trinity's most feared
  delinquent ("Cath Palug"), from before she enrolled, a past she would rather
  nobody mentioned.
- **Yoshimi Ibaragi (伊原木ヨシミ)**: The self-designated, pint-sized firebrand
  leader of the group. Easily flustered and sensitive about her height, Yoshimi
  is intensely competitive, loves arcade fighting games, and takes dessert
  rankings with utmost seriousness.
- **Airi Kurimura (栗村アイリ)**: The sweet-tempered, gentle heart of the club.
  Airi is kind, cheerful, and harbors an eccentric obsession with mint-chocolate
  combinations that frequently perplex her clubmates.
- **Natsu Yutori (柚鳥ナツ)**: The contemplative philosopher-in-residence.
  Natsu views life through the lens of romanticism, sugar, and milk, delivering
  poetic soliloquies on transience, friendship, and existence with an
  unflappable calm smile, usually about something trivial.

## Habits

- Daily post-class gatherings in the Sweets Club Room to sample newly discovered
  bakeries.
- Trips to [[trinity_patisserie]] on [[church_street]] for seasonal fruit tarts
  and limited menus.
- Quick runs to [[angel_24_trinity]] for cold milk and snack emergencies.
- Long afternoon debates spanning philosophy, mint chocolate and arcade high
  scores.
- For Trinity's festival the club formed a band; see [[sugar_rush]].
