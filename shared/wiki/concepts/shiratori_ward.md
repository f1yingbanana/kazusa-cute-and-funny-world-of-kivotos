# Shiratori Ward

A broad ward south of [[du_downtown|downtown]], recently rebuilt after heavy
damage. Office towers give way to galleries on [[plato_avenue]], an electronics
street in [[corgi_town]], a river lined with food stalls in [[ramini_town]], a
large [[shiratori_park|park]] and, at the ward's edge, [[kanesaki_port]].
[[tri_square]] in the middle is where everyone arranges to meet.
