# Kawabata Boarding House

A crooked wooden boarding house in the [[hyakkiyako_shirakawa_quarter]], with
an outside staircase and a shared [[kawabata_parlor|parlour]] downstairs. Rooms
are rented one by one to students who wanted a place of their own; tenants have
a key to the gate, cook in the [[kawabata_kitchen|shared kitchen]] upstairs and
bathe next door at the [[hyakkiyako_bathhouse]].
