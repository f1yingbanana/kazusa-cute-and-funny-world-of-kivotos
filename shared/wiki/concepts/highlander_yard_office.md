# Highlander Freight Yard Office

A cramped two-storey office over the throat of the
[[highlander_rail_yard|yard]], with a window onto every siding. Waybills stack
on clipboards, the radio crackles with shunting orders, and a kettle sits on a
filing cabinet next to a sign about kettles on filing cabinets.
