# Bunny Chasers Aboard

After [[koyuki-kurosaki|Koyuki]]'s unauthorized dealings with money caused
trouble for [[millennium_school]], she fled aboard the
[[golden_fleece_ship]], a luxury ship of games and entertainment.
[[cleaning_and_clearing]] went after her with [[sensei]]. They meant to work
quietly; it turned chaotic. In the end the team brought Koyuki back to the
academy.

## On board

[[asuna-ichinose|Asuna]] earned an "S-rank" card aboard the ship, and with it
freed her teammates. Koyuki, nicknamed the "White Rabbit", went back to
Millennium and, before long, to the self-reflection room.
