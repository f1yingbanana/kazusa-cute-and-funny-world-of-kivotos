# Arius Junior Study Hall

Rows of mismatched desks donated by [[trinity_school|Trinity]], a chalkboard and
a shelf of exam workbooks. The youngest students learn reading, sums and
history here while the older ones walk to the
[[trinity_old_school_building]]; in the evenings anyone can study.
