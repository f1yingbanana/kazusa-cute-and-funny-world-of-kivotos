# Kronos Broadcasting Center

The studios from which the [[kronos_school]] broadcasts news and live streams to
all of Kivotos. Satellite dishes crown the roof, the newsroom never sleeps, and
reporters with microphones stake out the entrance for anyone newsworthy.
