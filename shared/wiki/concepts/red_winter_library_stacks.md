# Federal Library Closed Stacks

Rows of shelves behind a staff-only rope that go back further than anyone has
catalogued: first editions, banned pamphlets, fanzines in protective sleeves.
One bay near the end has been turned into a napping nest of blankets and
cushions, guarded by a sign that says reading only.

## Who comes here

Only the [[knowledge_liberation_front|Knowledge Liberation Front]].
[[momiji-akiizumi|Momiji]] often spends the night in the library instead of the
dormitory.
