# Emergency Medicine Club

Gehenna's Emergency Medicine Club is the academy's ambulance service. When a
fight breaks out, and at [[gehenna_school]] one always does, its members race
in, load the wounded and take them for treatment, firing a shot or two if they
must. The club is led by [[sena-himuro|Sena Himuro]], whose calm, blunt manner
and habit of calling patients "corpses" are known across the academy.

## Inside the club

The club works from the [[gehenna_emc_room|club room]] by the back door, where
its heavy off-road ambulance, Emergency Vehicle No. 11, waits with the keys on a
hook, and it brings patients on to the [[gehenna_infirmary]]. Its members check
that downed students are truly out before moving them, sometimes with a warning
shot.

[[chinatsu-hinomiya|Chinatsu Hinomiya]] used to belong to the club before she
moved to the [[prefect_team]] as its medic, and Sena would like her back.
During the [[eden_treaty]] crisis, Sena helped [[sensei]] when he was near death
after the explosion at the cathedral.
