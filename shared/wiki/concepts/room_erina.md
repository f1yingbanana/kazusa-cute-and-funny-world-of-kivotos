# Erina Niki's Room

[[erina-niki|Erina]]'s room at [[red_winter_facility_183|Facility No. 183]],
never tidy: a chemistry bench that doubles as a cooking stove, piles of fashion
magazines rescued from the rubbish, and tissue boxes everywhere for the cold she
always has. On the windowsill is a row of real snowflakes preserved in resin,
each one different.
