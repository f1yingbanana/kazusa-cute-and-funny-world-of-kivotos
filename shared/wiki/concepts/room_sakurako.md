# Sakurako's Room

[[sakurako-utazumi|Sakurako]]'s cell is a plain whitewashed room at the end of
the [[trinity_cathedral_cloister]] nearest the [[trinity_vestry|vestry]]; she
seldom leaves the cathedral. The confession and event schedule for every Sister
is pinned above the desk, and beside it lies a fashion magazine annotated in
the margins as carefully as scripture.
