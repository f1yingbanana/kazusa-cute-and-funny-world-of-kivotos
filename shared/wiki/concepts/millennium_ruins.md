# Millennium Ruins

Past the last houses of the [[millennium_district|district]], across a strip of
wasteland, a fenced zone of collapsed facilities from before Millennium's time.
Old guard machines still patrol, and [[seminar|Seminar]]'s warning signs are
riddled with bullet holes. Treasure hunters come anyway, most of them to the
half-collapsed [[millennium_ruins_factory|factory]].
