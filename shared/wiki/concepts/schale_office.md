# SCHALE Office

The [[schale]] building stands on a hill in [[kousagi_ward]]: one half offices,
the other a [[schale_residence|residence hall]] for visiting students. The
top-floor office has floor-to-ceiling windows over the city, [[sensei|Sensei]]'s
desk buried in district paperwork and club budget reports, a leather sofa for
visitors, an espresso machine, and a side table that keeps accumulating pastry
boxes brought by students. Students are welcome during the day, and come in the
evenings and on Saturdays for [[schale_student_duty|SCHALE duty]]; at night the
building is locked unless Sensei is working late.

## The rest of the building

Next to the office are the [[schale_cafe|café lounge]], a
[[schale_library|library]] and a [[schale_classroom|classroom]]; below are the
[[schale_training_wing|training wing]], the [[schale_hangar|hangar]] and a
keycard-only [[schale_basement|basement]].
