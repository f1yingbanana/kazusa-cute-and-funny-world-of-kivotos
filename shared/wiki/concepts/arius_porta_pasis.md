# Porta Pasis Archives

Cold, dusty halls of shelves under lamplight, holding records from the
centuries when Arius was its own school; some stacks have not been touched
since before the civil war. The interim council and
[[trinity_school|Trinity]] researchers are slowly cataloguing them.

## The crisis

During the [[arius_reconstruction|reconstruction]], the distrust between the
[[nicomedia_troop]] and the outsiders helping Arius came to a head in a crisis
here.
