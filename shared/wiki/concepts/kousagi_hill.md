# Kousagi Hill

A wooded hill rising behind [[usagi_park|Kousagi Park]], crossed by a gravel
trail and unlit after dark. Wild greens grow along the path, campers leave
things behind, and halfway up an [[kousagi_hillside_house|abandoned house]]
stands with its windows boarded.
