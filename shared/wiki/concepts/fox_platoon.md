# FOX Platoon

FOX Platoon was the senior squad of [[srt_training_facility]]: four third-years
led by Yukino, with [[niko-yoshino]] as platoon sergeant, [[kurumi-takakura]] on
point and [[otogi-tenjinyama]] as sniper. Since the trouble that followed SRT's
closure, FOX works under [[schale]]'s supervision from its
[[fox_squad_hq|headquarters]] in the SCHALE basement.

## After SRT closed

When SRT was shut down, FOX followed Yukino into Kaya's coup against the
[[general_student_council]], for the outcome Kaya promised them. It put
them face to face with their juniors in [[rabbit_platoon]], and with
[[sensei]], though its members also showed concern for their juniors; see
[[srt_closure_crisis]]. Niko was the first to say out loud that
it was not the justice they had signed up for. When Yukino ordered the others to
retreat and leave her behind, they stayed.

## The squad

Niko keeps the squad human and brings inarizushi before every mission, a ritual
Yukino started asking for. Kurumi goes first through every door behind her
ballistic shield. Otogi likes to be seen, on the theory that a target who knows
a sniper is watching has already half lost. Kurumi and Otogi steal each other's
salmon roe inarizushi and each swears the other started it.
