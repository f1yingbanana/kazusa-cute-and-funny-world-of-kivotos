# Kaede's Room

The smallest room in the [[hyakkiyako_tsukimi_dorm]], at the end of the
corridor, where [[kaede-isami]] lives. A beetle terrarium hums by the window,
hero-show posters cover the walls, binders of sleeved Mushiqueen cards fill the
shelf, and a "Lady Lessons" notebook on the desk is full of crossed-out rules.
