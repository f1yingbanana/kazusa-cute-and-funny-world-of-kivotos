# Red Bear Broadcasting Station

A brick tower on [[red_winter_revolution_plaza|Revolution Plaza]] topped with an
aerial and a red star. It carries "Red Bear", the academy's only broadcast, to
every loudspeaker on campus: news on the hour, marching music, weather (snow),
the [[cherino-renkawa|president]]'s addresses and a great deal of praise for the
president. Because whoever holds the microphone holds the academy, the tower is
the first building every coup takes, and its door has been replaced several
times.
