# Gehenna Academy

Gehenna Academy is a huge academy famous for its culture of freedom and for the
unruly clubs that make the most of it. The [[pandemonium_society]] is its
student council, and the [[prefect_team]] tries to keep order. Among its clubs
are the [[school_lunch_club]], the [[emergency_medicine_club]] and the
[[gourmet_research_society]]. Its long [[gehenna_trinity_rivalry|rivalry]] with
[[trinity_school|Trinity]] is what the [[eden_treaty]] set out to settle.

Its campus is palace-sized: pale stone, colonnades and lawns, built for grandeur
and since repainted by every rowdy club that ever seized a wing. The main gate
faces the [[gehenna_zone3|Zone 3]] dormitories, and the Prefect checkpoint there
is something delinquents simply walk around. Ordinary students take classes in
whatever the clubs have left them.

## The campus

The subway climbs out of its tunnel from the
[[gehenna_underground_mall|underground mall]] to a stop at the forecourt, and a
back gate opens onto the [[gehenna_riverside|riverside path]]. Classes are held
in the [[gehenna_first_building|First]] and
[[gehenna_second_building|Second School Buildings]], clubs fight over the
[[gehenna_clubroom_building|clubroom building]], and the
[[gehenna_lawn_square|Lawn Square]] sits in the middle of it all. The council
has its [[pandemonium_headquarters|headquarters]], the Prefect Team its own
[[prefect_headquarters|near the gate]], and the [[school_lunch_club|lunch club]]
its [[gehenna_lunch_building|own building]]; at the edge stands the
[[gehenna_abandoned_building|abandoned building]] nobody is supposed to enter.
