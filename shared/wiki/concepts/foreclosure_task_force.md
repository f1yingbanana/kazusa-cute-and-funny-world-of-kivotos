# Foreclosure Task Force

The Foreclosure Task Force, also called the Countermeasures Committee, is what
is left of [[abydos_school]]'s student body: five students working to keep
their school and district alive against desertification and
[[abydos_debt|debt]]. They take jobs, defend the school from gangs, and have
[[sensei]] from [[schale]] on their side.

## The five

[[hoshino-takanashi|Hoshino Takanashi]] chairs it,
[[ayane-okusora|Ayane Okusora]] is its secretary and
[[serika-kuromi|Serika Kuromi]] its treasurer, with
[[shiroko-sunaookami|Shiroko Sunaookami]] and
[[nonomi-izayoi|Nonomi Izayoi]]. They meet in the
[[abydos_task_force_room|club room]] in the Main Building, and their parties end
up at Nonomi's [[abydos_lotus_villa|villa]].
