# Rescue at Arius Basilica

After the attack on the [[eden_treaty|Eden Treaty]] signing,
[[saori-joumae|Saori]] of the [[arius_squad]] asked [[sensei]] for help to save
[[atsuko-hakari|Atsuko]] from Beatrice, the adult who ruled Arius. With Sensei
and, in the end, [[mika-misono|Mika]], the squad reached the
[[arius_basilica|Basilica]] and Atsuko was rescued.

## What happened

Saori, [[hiyori-tsuchinaga|Hiyori]] and [[misaki-imashino|Misaki]] travelled
toward the Basilica with Sensei. Mika first pursued the squad in anger, then
recognised their shared struggle and helped hold off the attackers. Atsuko was
rescued and the sacrifice Beatrice intended was stopped. Afterwards Saori chose
to step away from the squad to find her own path.
