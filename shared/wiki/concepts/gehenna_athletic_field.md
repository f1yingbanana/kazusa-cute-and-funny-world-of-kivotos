# Gehenna Central Athletic Field

The main sports ground of [[gehenna_school]]: a big oval track around a field,
with concrete stands on one side and craters filled in with fresh sand. PE
classes run here in the mornings, and the field hosts festivals, rallies and
whatever the [[pandemonium_society|council]] has planned.
