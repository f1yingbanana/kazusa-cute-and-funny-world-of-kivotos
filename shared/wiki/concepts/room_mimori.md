# Mimori's Room

The room of [[mimori-mizuha]] in the [[hyakkiyako_tsukimi_dorm]], without a
speck of dust. An embroidery hoop with a half-finished handkerchief sits by the
cushion, her prized apron hangs on its own hook, and a cloth curtain hangs over
one shelf.

## Behind the curtain

The curtain hides a shelf of shojo manga.
