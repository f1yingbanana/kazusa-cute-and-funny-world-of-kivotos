# School Lunch Club Vegetable Garden

Neat raised beds behind the [[gehenna_cafeteria|dining hall]], fenced with wire,
with a tool shed, a rain barrel and a hand-painted sign asking people not to
fight here. What grows ends up on the [[school_lunch_club]]'s plates whenever it
survives.
