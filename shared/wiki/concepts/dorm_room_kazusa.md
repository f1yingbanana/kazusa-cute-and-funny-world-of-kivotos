# Kazusa's Room

[[kazusa-kyouyama|Kazusa]]'s room on the second floor of [[trinity_dorm]]: tidy
and a little dim, the curtains half drawn. A bass guitar rests on its stand
beside the desk, and one drawer is reserved for sweets. It is hers alone; she
locks the door whenever she leaves and does not open it for just anyone.
