# Millennium Student Lounge

The crossroads of the [[millennium_clubhouse|clubhouse]]: a row of vending
machines (the energy-drink one empties first), mismatched sofas, a microwave,
and a whiteboard covered in half-erased diagrams that anyone who stops nearby
will have explained to them at length.
