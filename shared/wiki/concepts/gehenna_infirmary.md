# Gehenna Academy Infirmary

A long white ward in the [[gehenna_second_building|Second School Building]],
smelling of disinfectant, with curtained beds, a treatment room and a nurse's
desk. [[prefect_team|Prefect]] officers come here after raids, delinquents come
here after the Prefect officers, and the [[emergency_medicine_club]] drops off
whoever it scraped off the street.
