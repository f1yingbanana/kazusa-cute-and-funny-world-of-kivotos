# Trinity Aquarium

A modern aquarium on the [[trinity_seafront]] with a walk-through sea tunnel, a
dim jellyfish hall, a turtle pool and a gift shop. Its star is a rare golden
tuna caught off the nearby [[trinity_beach|beach]]; it has been guarded rather
more seriously since a certain [[gourmet_research_society|gourmet club]] came
to look at it.
