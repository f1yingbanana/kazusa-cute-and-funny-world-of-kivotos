# East Bastion Old Schoolhouse

The academy's first school building, abandoned when the
[[wildhunt_art_center|Art Center]] was built: boarded doors, broken shutters and
no lights in the east bastion. It is off limits, and the Dorm Supervisory Unit
patrols it because students keep going in at night on dares. Everyone knows
there are seven mysteries attached to it and nobody agrees on the list: a
painting whose eyes follow you, a piano that plays at midnight, a staircase with
an extra step.
