# School Lunch Club

The School Lunch Club runs the student dining hall of [[gehenna_school]] and
cooks for the whole academy, thousands of meals a day, starting long before
dawn. Its president, [[fuuka-aikiyo|Fuuka Aikiyo]], does most of the work
herself, and [[juri-ushimaki|Juri Ushimaki]] helps while striving to become a
better cook. Students often complain the food is bland; the club has to put
quantity first.

## Inside the club

There are only two members, and Juri's cooking is so bad that Fuuka cooks
nearly everything; she makes breakfast alone for almost 4,000 students. The
club works from the [[gehenna_lunch_building|School Lunch Club Building]] and
its [[gehenna_kitchen|kitchen]], with a mobile kitchen truck for big days and a
[[gehenna_vegetable_garden|vegetable garden]] of its own. Limited resources and
the [[gourmet_research_society]], which regularly kidnaps Fuuka to cook for it,
make the work harder still.
