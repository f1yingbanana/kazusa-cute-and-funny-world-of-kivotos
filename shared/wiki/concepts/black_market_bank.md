# Black Market Bank

A fortress of a bank in the middle of the [[black_market_district|market]], with
armed guards at the armoured doors and tellers behind thick glass. It lends to
people no other bank will touch, and its rates show it. Its name over the door
is the Stygian Bank, and it was once raided by the
[[masked_swimsuit_gang|Masked Swimsuit Gang]].
