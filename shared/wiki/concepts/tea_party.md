# Tea Party

The Tea Party is [[trinity_school]]'s student council. Its leadership draws on
Trinity's three historic factions, Pater, Filius and Sanctus, each with a
representative, one of whom serves as host. It governs through formal meetings
over tea in the [[trinity_tea_party]], a tradition that goes back to the union
of the factions that formed Trinity.

Its leaders are [[nagisa-kirifuji]], the current host, [[seia-yurizono]] and
[[mika-misono]].

## The three

- Nagisa leads the Filius faction and is the most politically skilled of the
  three.
- Nagisa and Mika are childhood friends.
- The [[eden_treaty_crisis]] strained their personal and political ties. Mika
  turned against the treaty and lost her place in politics; Seia was attacked
  and hidden away while she recovered; Nagisa was badly hurt and is still
  mistrusted by some students.
- Since the crisis, the Tea Party runs the school together with the
  [[sisterhood]] and the [[knights_hospitaller]].
