# Ebisu Satellite School

Ebisu is [[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako]]'s northern satellite school, out in
the farmland near the [[great_snowfield]]: a single wooden schoolhouse with a
stove in every room and a yard that is snow nine months of the year. The farms
around it grow the ingredients Hyakkiyako's festivals depend on.

## Self-government

The students run their affairs through a self-government committee. During one
of [[hyakkaryouran_crisis|Hyakkiyako's festival crises]], members of
[[natures_beauty]] acted under the name of the former committee, and the
committee was found to be headed by an impostor.
