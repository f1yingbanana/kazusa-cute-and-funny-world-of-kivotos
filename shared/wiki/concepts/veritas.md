# Veritas

Veritas is the hacking club of [[millennium_school]], whose members specialize
in information security, communications and hacking. It is led by
[[chihiro-kagami|Chihiro Kagami]], its conscience, with
[[maki-konuri|Maki Konuri]], [[kotama-otose|Kotama Otose]] and
[[hare-omagari|Hare Omagari]]; [[himari-akeboshi|Himari Akeboshi]] led it
before her. Its members differ a good deal in how carefully they use their
skills. They keep a server-cooled den, the [[veritas_clubroom]].

## The members

- Chihiro, the most restrained of them, works as a security consultant and
  tries to keep the others from going overboard.
- Maki designed the club's emblem, tags walls with graffiti and installs the
  game "BOOM" on any device with a screen.
- Kotama handles communications and listening devices, and can't resist
  bugging people she wants to know.
- Hare builds the club's devices, including her drone Athena-3, on a steady
  supply of [[frenzy_max]].
- Himari, the former leader, now heads the [[paranormal_affairs_department]]
  and still calls Chihiro "Chii-chan".

## Hacker ethics

Chihiro worries that the younger members hack just for fun and could hurt the
club's name. She holds that "hacker" should mean a security expert, a white
hat, and wishes she had taught the members hacker ethics sooner.
