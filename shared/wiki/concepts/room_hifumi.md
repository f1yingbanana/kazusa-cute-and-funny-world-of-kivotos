# Hifumi's Room

[[hifumi-ajitani|Hifumi]]'s room in the [[trinity_magnolia_east|East Wing]] is,
by any measure, a Peroro shrine: plushes on every shelf, limited-edition bags
hung like art, and a pinboard of upcoming [[momo_friends]] release dates circled
in red. On the desk are tidy study notes copied out for her friends. The key
lives on a Peroro charm she would never lose.
