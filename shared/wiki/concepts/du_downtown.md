# D.U. City

The heart of Kivotos and the [[general_student_council]]'s own district. Glass
and stone towers crowd broad avenues, delivery drones weave between billboards,
and students from every academy pass through on their way somewhere else:
[[du_central_station]] sends trains to every corner of the city.
[[gsc_headquarters|Sanctum Tower]] stands over everything.
[[valkyrie_police_academy|Valkyrie]] officers patrol here, because no single
school polices the capital.

## The wards

Around the centre lie [[shiratori_ward]], busy and newly rebuilt, with
[[tri_square]], offices, galleries and [[kanesaki_port]] at its edge, and quiet
[[kousagi_ward]] on the south-west outskirts, where the [[schale_office|SCHALE]]
building stands on its hill.
