# Red Winter Office Building

A columned building with a balcony over Revolution Plaza, from which the
[[red_winter_office|Office]]'s decrees are read. Mustache sculptures, the symbol
of the president's authority, stand on every landing and over every door.
Petitions, complaints and purchase requests go into a brass box by the door and
are mostly rejected. The State Guard holds the door around the clock.

## Inside

Upstairs is the [[red_winter_presidential_office|Presidential Office]]; below it
the [[red_winter_secretariat_staff_room|staff room]] of the Secretary General
and the Security Committee, the
[[red_winter_state_guard_room|State Guard's duty room]], and the
[[red_winter_punch_card_registry|registry]] where every student's record lives
on punch cards. In a coup this is usually the second building taken, after the
radio tower.
