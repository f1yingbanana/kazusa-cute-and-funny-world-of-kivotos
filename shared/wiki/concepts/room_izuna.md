# Izuna's Room

A small room in the [[hyakkiyako_kawabata_house]] that [[izuna-kuda]] rents
with her own money. Ninja period-drama DVDs are stacked by a little TV, a
shuriken target is pinned to the wall, and a rope ladder hangs out of the
window for proper ninja exits.
