# Gehenna District

A huge district of numbered zones around [[gehenna_school|Gehenna Academy]],
where freedom is the motto and trouble is the weather. The streets are loud, the
food is cheap, and somewhere there is always a fight the [[prefect_team]] will
have to break up.

The campus sits on a rise in the north, with the dormitories of
[[gehenna_zone3|Zone 3]] just outside its main gate. Downhill to the south,
around [[gehenna_station|Gehenna Central Station]] in Zone 32-1, lie
[[gehenna_central_square|Central Square]], the
[[gehenna_shopping_district|shopping district]] and the
[[gehenna_underground_mall|underground mall]]; past them the
[[gehenna_backstreets|Zone 32 backstreets]] fade toward the
[[black_market_district|Black Market]]. A
[[gehenna_riverside|river with a cherry-lined path]] curls along the campus's
east side, and far to the west the steaming slopes of [[hinnom_volcano]] hold
the [[gehenna_hot_spring_village|hot-spring village]].
