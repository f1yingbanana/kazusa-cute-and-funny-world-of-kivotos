# Red Winter Office

The Red Winter Office is the student council of [[red_winter_school]], also
called the Secretariat. It is led by President
[[cherino-renkawa|Cherino Renkawa]], with her devoted Secretary General
[[tomoe-sashiro|Tomoe Sashiro]] and [[marina-ikekura|Marina Ikekura]], chair of
the Security Committee. It works from the
[[red_winter_secretariat|Office Building]] on Revolution Plaza, issues decrees,
hands out demerits and suspensions, and turns down most purchase requests.

Its leadership is famously unstable: a coup comes along almost every week, and
Cherino almost always ends up back in her chair.

## Inside the Office

Tomoe writes the president's speeches, keeps her schedule and tours the academy
giving rallies so that students who have never seen Cherino know of her
greatness. Marina's Security Committee patrols the district and mans posts like
the [[red_winter_district8_post|District 8 Security Post]], when she can find
them. The State Guard holds the Office Building around the clock from its
[[red_winter_state_guard_room|duty room]].

Punishments are the Office's main tool. Small offences earn bathroom-cleaning
duty, which Marina knows well after her own failed coups. Serious ones mean
suspension to [[red_winter_special_class_183|No. 183 Special Class]] or to the
Former Institute as [[special_class_227|No. 227 Special Class]], and the
president does not always remember whom she has suspended. Every record of it
is punched by hand into the
[[red_winter_punch_card_registry|punch card registry]].
