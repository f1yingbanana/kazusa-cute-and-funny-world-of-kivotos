# Ponte Crepes

A crepe cart on the main bridge of the [[wildhunt_canal_town|canal town]], with
a short menu chalked on a board, open 11:00 to 20:00. On Saturday afternoons the
queue reaches the end of the bridge, and the chocolate-banana sells out first.
