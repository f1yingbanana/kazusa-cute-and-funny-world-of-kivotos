# Shiba Seki Ramen

A ramen stall under an awning in [[abydos_suburban_town|Suburban Town]]: a
counter, six stools, a steaming pot and Master Shiba behind it. His ramen has
fed Abydos students for years, often on credit, and students still get the big
portion for the small price. The old shop was blown up; he carried on from the
stall with the same broth.

## Regulars

A student part-timer, [[serika-kuromi|Serika]], works the evening rush most
days. The [[foreclosure_task_force]] eats here whenever there is money to.
