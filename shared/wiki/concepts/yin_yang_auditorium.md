# Yin-Yang Club Auditorium

A wooden hall in the [[yin_yang_building]], with a raised stage and rows of
floor cushions. Assemblies and festival briefings are held here, and clubs that
cannot settle a quarrel are summoned to have it settled in public by the
[[yin_yang_club]].
