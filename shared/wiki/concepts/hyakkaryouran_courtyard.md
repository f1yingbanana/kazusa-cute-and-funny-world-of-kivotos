# Hyakkaryouran Courtyard Garden

A small walled garden with a stone lantern, target posts along one wall and a
wooden veranda that catches the afternoon sun. The ground before the posts is
worn bare; by afternoon someone is usually sunbathing on the boards.

## Drills

The [[hyakkaryouran_resolution_council]] drills here from 06:00 to 07:00. In
summer an inflatable pool goes up in the corner of the garden.
