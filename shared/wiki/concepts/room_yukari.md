# Yukari's Room

A formal room in the main house of the [[kadenokouji_estate]], where
[[yukari-kadenokouji]] sleeps: a small family altar, silk cushions and a scroll
in the alcove, kept exactly as the household likes it.

## The bottom drawer

In the bottom drawer of the writing desk, under a folded kimono, is a stash of
cheap snacks and arcade tokens.
