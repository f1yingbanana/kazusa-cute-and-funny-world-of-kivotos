# The Golden Fleece

The Golden Fleece is a luxury ship belonging to
[[odyssey_maritime_high_school|Odyssey Maritime High School]], given over to
entertainment and games.

## Aboard

Millennium's [[cleaning_and_clearing|C&C]] once chased
[[koyuki-kurosaki|Koyuki]] aboard it. Later, during the
[[momoyodou_resort_plan|Momoyodou resort affair]], it hosted a Mushiqueen card
tournament.
