# Gehenna Dining Hall

A cavernous hall of long tables under iron chandeliers in the
[[gehenna_lunch_building|School Lunch Club Building]], feeding thousands from a
tiny club. The menu is whatever the [[school_lunch_club]] could make in time;
the queue is the most dangerous place on campus at noon, and sometimes the
[[gourmet_research_society]] takes the whole [[gehenna_kitchen|kitchen]]
hostage.
