# Ebisu Dormitory

A long timber dormitory with deep eaves against the snow, home to the
[[ebisu_school|satellite school]]'s students and to any visitor stranded by the
weather. Stoves burn all night, and its [[ebisu_banquet_hall|banquet hall]] is
the biggest room in the North District.
