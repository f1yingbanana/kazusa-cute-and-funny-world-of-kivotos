# Millennium Power Plant 1

At the far edge of the [[millennium_study_area|Study Area]], behind a double
fence: transformers, cooling units and a lit control room, with engineers on
shift around the clock. When something fails at night, it's usually a sleepy
student engineer who gets the call.
