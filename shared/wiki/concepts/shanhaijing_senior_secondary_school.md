# Shanhaijing Senior Secondary School

Shanhaijing is an academy of tiled roofs, gardens and long tradition in the
walled [[shanhaijing_district]] at the foot of Kunlun Mountain. It keeps its
doors mostly closed: outsiders may visit the [[shanhaijing_entrance]] by the
station, but only students and invited guests pass the Inner Gate into the
[[shanhaijing_school]].

The [[xuanlong_office]], better known as the Genryumon, governs the school from
its old palace like an ancient court. The [[xuanwu_merchant_association]] runs
the Black Tortoise Promenade, the gourmet street that makes Shanhaijing's money
and its name, and the two do not always get along. The
[[plum_blossom_garden]] raises and teaches the academy's youngest children, and
the [[chinese_alchemy_study_club]] brews medicines and stranger things in its
laboratory.
