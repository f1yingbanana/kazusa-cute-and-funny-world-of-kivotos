# Remedial Knights Storage Room

A locked supply room behind the ward of the [[trinity_remedial_clinic]],
floor-to-ceiling shelves of bandages, splints, stretchers and oxygen
cylinders. In the back is a museum's worth of old medical equipment the
[[knights_hospitaller|Knights]] keep 'in case'. Taking stock is a dreaded job.
