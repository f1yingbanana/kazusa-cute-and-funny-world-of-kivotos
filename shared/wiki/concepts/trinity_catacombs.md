# Trinity Catacombs

Stone passages lined with niches, opening off the crypt of the
[[trinity_old_cathedral]] and running for kilometres beneath Trinity with no
complete map. Around three hundred entrances are known, nearly all of them dead
ends. One route to the [[arius_district]] is marked with paint and lamps; off
that route, the tunnels still lose people.

## The shifting paths

The old true paths to Arius used to move. Since the Arius district was
reopened, the marked route from this crypt stays put, but everything off it
still shifts.
