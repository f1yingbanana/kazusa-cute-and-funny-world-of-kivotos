# Momo Friends Automated Store

A [[momo_friends]] shop in the [[trinity_galleria]] with no staff at all: robot
arms fetch plushes from glass towers and a machine takes payment. A corner
shelf sells second-hand limited editions at eye-watering prices. On release
mornings, the queue starts outside the Galleria before it opens.
