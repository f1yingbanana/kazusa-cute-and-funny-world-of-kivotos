# Dormitory No. 5 Mailroom

Pigeonholes, a parcel shelf and an inspection table on the ground floor of
[[wildhunt_dorm5]], open 08:00 to 20:00. The Dorm Supervisory Unit opens every
parcel here, and residents collect their post under supervision and watch half
of it go onto the confiscation cart.
