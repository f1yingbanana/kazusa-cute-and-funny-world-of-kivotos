# Glebe Field

A long field of vegetable rows and weeds on the [[trinity_outskirts]], owned by
[[trinity_school]] since before anyone remembers. Students sentenced to
community service work here, pulling weeds by the sackful while a
[[justice_task_force]] officer watches from a folding chair. A tool shed and a
water tap are the only shade.
