# Reijo's Room

A plain room off the courtyard of the [[shanhaijing_lotus_court]], dominated by
a wooden training dummy. Kung fu film posters line the walls beside a rack of
practice staffs, and on the desk lies a notebook of rival restaurants' menus,
each with her rating.
