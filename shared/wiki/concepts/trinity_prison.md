# Trinity Academy Prison

Stone cells in the basement under [[trinity_justice_hq]], where
[[trinity_school]] holds students accused of serious offences until their
trial. A guard sits at the end of the corridor day and night. The cells were
briefly famous when a member of the [[tea_party]] was held here after the
[[eden_treaty_crisis|Eden Treaty crisis]].

## The famous prisoner

The Tea Party member held here was [[mika-misono|Mika Misono]].
