# North Moat Boathouse

A timber boathouse from the days when the moats were used for rowing, left to
rot past the last bridge of the [[wildhunt_canal_town|canal town]]. The jetty
sags into the water, reeds grow all round, and the doors are padlocked. Nobody
comes this way much, and nobody at all after dark.

## The loft

Its [[wildhunt_launch_loft|loft]] has a clear line of sight over the fortress
wall. The [[free_trade_cartel]] uses it as a launch site, and the padlock is
theirs.
