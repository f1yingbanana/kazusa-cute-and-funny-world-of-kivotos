# Highlander Station Campus

[[highlander_railroad_academy]] governs rail lines rather than a district, so it
lives inside its busiest station: the east wing of [[du_central_station]], five
floors of offices, lecture rooms and crew quarters with timetables on every
wall. The floors hum when expresses pass, and students in conductor caps hurry
through the lobby between shifts.

## Who runs what

The [[highlander_control_center|Central Control Center]] watches the whole
network from its [[highlander_control|operations floor]], the
[[highlander_supervisory_office|Supervisory Office]] watches the Control Center,
and the [[highlander_freight_logistics]] keeps everything running from the
[[highlander_rail_yard|freight yard]] down the tracks. The academy is owned by a
corporate group, and its factions compete for lines on a points system.
