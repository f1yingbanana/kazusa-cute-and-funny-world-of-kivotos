# Dormitory No. 4, Room 404

A four-bed room on the fourth floor of [[wildhunt_dorm4]], half studio and half
occult den, behind heavy curtains and locked when empty. A witch hat sits on a
mannequin head over a tarot deck; there are candles, a sewing machine and a
dress form, a recording microphone on the windowsill and a guitar case doing
duty as a suitcase.

## Whose it is

Home and clubroom of the [[occult_research_society]]: [[eri-shirao|Eri]],
[[tsumugi-shiina|Tsumugi]], [[kanoe-itagaki|Kanoe]] and [[rena-kinui|Rena]].
Eri's corner has the witch hat, the tarot deck and a pile of failed practical
sheets. Kanoe's bunk is by the window with the binaural microphone on the sill,
where a crow and a black cat wait that she insists aren't hers. Rena's corner is
the sewing machine, the dress form and a sticky note that says ghosts aren't
real. Tsumugi's guitar case doubles as her suitcase, and her bunk is papered
with ticket stubs.
