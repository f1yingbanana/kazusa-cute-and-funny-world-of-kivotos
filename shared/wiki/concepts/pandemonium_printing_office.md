# Pandemonium Printing Office

A converted warehouse in the [[gehenna_backstreets|backstreets]] full of
clattering presses, printing the [[pandemonium_society|council]]'s posters,
leaflets and weekly magazine, plenty of them mocking the [[prefect_team]].

## The weekly

The magazine is the Weekly Pandemonium Society, which [[chiaki-motomiya|Chiaki]]
writes, edits and publishes herself.
