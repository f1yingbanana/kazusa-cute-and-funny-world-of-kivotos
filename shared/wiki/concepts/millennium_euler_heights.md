# Euler Heights

Narrow streets of low apartment houses and vending machines south of the
[[millennium_school|campus]], with a few student lets and Millennium's own
railway cutting across them at the [[millennium_railway_crossing|level
crossing]]. Most residents walk to school; some of them pick up litter on the
way. After 22:00 the streets are empty.

The student lets include [[millennium_villa_lagrange|Villa Lagrange]], with its
corner laundromat, and plain [[millennium_kepler_house|Kepler House]] at the
edge toward the old outskirts. Nearby is the old
[[millennium_boxing_gym|Faraday Boxing Gym]].
