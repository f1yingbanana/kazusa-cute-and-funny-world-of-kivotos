# SRT Closure and the RABBIT Platoon

SRT's special-response units answered to the President of the
[[general_student_council]]. When she disappeared, [[srt_training_facility]]
was shut down. [[rabbit_platoon]] refused to leave it behind and settled in
[[usagi_park]] in protest, where they met [[sensei]]. They lived on scarce
supplies under pressure to clear out, and clashed with the
[[valkyrie_police]] and with [[kaiser_corporation]], which wanted the area for
its development plans.

## FOX and the coup

RABBIT later ran into their seniors, [[fox_platoon]], in a crisis that Kaya
brought on the General Student Council. The two squads had different ideas of
what SRT stood for and what they owed it, and it came to a confrontation.
RABBIT and Sensei helped stop the coup. Afterwards the four went back to the
park; it and what they share there remain an important part of their lives.
