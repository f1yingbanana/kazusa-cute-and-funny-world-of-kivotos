# SRT Special Academy

SRT was a special-response academy whose units could be deployed anywhere in
Kivotos on the authority of the [[general_student_council]]'s President. After
the President disappeared, the council shut it down. Its compound in
[[kousagi_ward]] now stands behind a locked gate with a council seal, quiet,
with weeds starting on the drill yard.

## After the closure

[[rabbit_platoon]] refused to abandon SRT and camped in
[[usagi_park|Kousagi Park]] in protest; the senior [[fox_platoon]] took another
road, which brought the two teams into conflict. See [[srt_closure_crisis]].

## The campus

Behind the gate lie the training ground, barracks and firing range of the
elite academy, unused since the council closed it.
