# Abydos Desert

Most of Abydos lies under sand. Streets run into dunes, rooftops poke out of
them, and old rail lines vanish under drifts. There is no shade and no water,
and the sandstorms blow up most afternoons. Much of the land was sold to
[[kaiser_corporation|Kaiser]] to pay the school's [[abydos_debt|debts]] and is
fenced and patrolled. Anyone going far takes water, a map and someone who knows
the way back.
