# The Game Development Department Crisis

Facing the closure of their club, the [[game_development_department]] of
[[millennium_school]] went into the school's ruins and found
[[aris-tendou|Aris]]. They brought her into the club, made a game, and won the
right to carry on. Later [[rio-tsukatsuki|Rio Tsukatsuki]], fearing Aris's
link to a dangerous system, took her away to Eridu. [[sensei]] and several
Millennium clubs worked together to bring Aris back and prevent a catastrophe.
Rio withdrew afterward, and Aris returned to her friends.

## The girl in the ruins

The club found Aris dormant in an abandoned munitions factory in the
[[millennium_ruins]], under the name AL-1S. She learned to speak and to be
herself by playing games with the club. Inside her was another mind, Key,
meant to lead the "Princess" to her throne.

## Eridu

Rio abducted Aris to Eridu and tried to destroy her halo, with
[[toki-asuma|Toki]] serving as her bodyguard and the giant machine
[[avant_garde]] in the field. Sensei and the Game Development Department
stopped her. At some point in all this Aris also clashed with
[[cleaning_and_clearing|C&C]]'s [[neru-mikamo|Neru]], and was afraid of maids
for a while after. Key later gave up her existence to save Aris; she returned
as [[kei-tendou|Kei]].
