# Nicomedia Barracks

A long stone building that once housed the squads Arius trained, warm now that
repair crews have put in a stove and hot water. It is home to the students who
stayed, mostly younger ones, under the eye of
[[subaru-kakehashi|Subaru Kakehashi]], the senior who leads the
[[nicomedia_troop]] and sleeps in the room by the door. Most of them sleep in
the [[arius_bunk_hall|bunk hall]] and queue at the
[[arius_barracks_washroom|washroom]] before roll call.
