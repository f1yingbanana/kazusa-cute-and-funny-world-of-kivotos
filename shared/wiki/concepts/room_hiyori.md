# Hiyori's Room

Magazine towers line every wall of [[hiyori-tsuchinaga|Hiyori]]'s room, fished
out of recycling bins and sorted by issue. She still sleeps in the sleeping bag
rather than the bed frame, and the huge backpack by the door is always packed,
just in case. A portable battery someone gave her keeps her phone alive.
