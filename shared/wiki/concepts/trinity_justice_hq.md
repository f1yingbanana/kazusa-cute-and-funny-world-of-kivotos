# Justice Task Force Headquarters

A grey stone block near the [[trinity_gymnasium|gymnasium]], home of the
[[justice_task_force]], the discipline and security committee of
[[trinity_school]]. Inside are a duty desk that never closes, a briefing room
with a map of the district, the chair's office, and racks of black uniforms in
short supply. Suspects are brought in through the side door, and the
[[trinity_prison|cells]] are downstairs.

## Late lights

Reports are filed here at all hours, and the office light of the vice-chair,
[[hasumi-hanekawa|Hasumi]], is usually the last to go out. The chair's office
belongs to [[tsurugi-kenzaki|Tsurugi]].
