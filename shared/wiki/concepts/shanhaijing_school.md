# Shanhaijing Central Special Zone

The heart of [[shanhaijing_senior_secondary_school]], behind its inner walls:
halls with curved roofs, stone courtyards, gardens and the golden roofs of a
palace. Only students and invited guests may come in, and Genryumon guards stand
at the arches.

## Around the zone

The [[shanhaijing_lecture_hall]] holds the classrooms and the
[[shanhaijing_refectory]] feeds everyone. The Genryumon rule from the
[[shanhaijing_rikkagaku]]. The [[shanhaijing_plum_garden]] keeps the youngest
children, the [[shanhaijing_alchemy_lab]] sits at the end of alchemy street, and
the [[shanhaijing_white_tiger_park]] has the open-air stage.
