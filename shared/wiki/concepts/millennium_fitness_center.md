# Millennium Fitness Center

Racks of weights, rowing machines, a padded floor and a short indoor track
behind the [[millennium_stadium|stadium]]. The
[[gym_training_department|Gym Training Department]] runs it and will design a
programme for anyone who makes eye contact. A whiteboard by the door lists the
day's protein targets.
