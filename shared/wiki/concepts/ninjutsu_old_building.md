# Old Schoolhouse No. 38

The last schoolhouse on [[hyakkiyako_old_schoolhouse_lane]], a single drafty
classroom with a leaky roof and a lock that barely works. Inside: a tripod, a
ring light, hand-painted backdrops of castle walls, straw training targets and a
hole in the floor they call a trapdoor. It is the
[[ninjutsu_research_department]]'s room.

## Borrowed

The club holds the schoolhouse only "temporarily", which it takes as a threat.
The tripod, ring light and backdrops are for filming
[[michiruchi_channel]].
