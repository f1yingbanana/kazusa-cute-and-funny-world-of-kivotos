# AMAS

AMAS is an automated surveillance and defense system at
[[millennium_school]], made up of networked machines and drones that watch over
the school and defend it. It was developed by [[rio-tsukatsuki|Rio
Tsukatsuki]], and much of her fearsome reputation at Millennium comes from it:
students know that its eyes are everywhere.
