# Hyakkaryouran Storehouse

A thick-walled kura storehouse behind the clubhouse, its heavy door kept
locked. Ammunition crates, barricade boards and lantern poles fill one side;
boxes of council records going back generations fill the other.

## The key

The key is kept by the council's operations officer, [[kikyou-kiryuu]]. The
barricades and lantern poles come out when the council secures a festival.
