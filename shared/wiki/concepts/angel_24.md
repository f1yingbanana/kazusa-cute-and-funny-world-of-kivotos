# Angel 24

Angel 24 is Kivotos's everyday convenience store: snacks, drinks, bentos and
supplies, with a branch in nearly every district. One stands inside the
[[schale]] building's residence wing, and students across the city stop at their
local branch on the way to and from school.

## Branches

- [[schale_angel24]], in the SCHALE residence wing.
- [[angel_24_kousagi]], by the station SCHALE's visitors use.
- [[angel_24_trinity]], [[angel_24_gehenna]], [[angel_24_abydos]],
  [[hyakkiyako_angel24]] and [[red_winter_angel_24]] in their districts.
- A kiosk at [[du_central_station]] by the south exit.
