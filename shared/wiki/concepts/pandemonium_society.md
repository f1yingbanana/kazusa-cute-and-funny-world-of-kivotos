# Pandemonium Society

The Pandemonium Society is the student council of [[gehenna_school]], led by
its president, [[makoto-hanuma|Makoto Hanuma]]. It celebrates Gehenna's freedom
and chaotic spirit, spends lavishly, and runs the academy more in name than in
practice. It clashes constantly with [[hina-sorasaki|Hina]]'s
[[prefect_team]] over discipline and authority, and floods the campus with
posters mocking the prefects. Its gilded headquarters, guards at the door, and
tanks rolling through the streets are hard to miss.

## The council

Besides Makoto, its core members are [[iroha-natsume|Iroha Natsume]], who
commands the council's tank Toramaru and cleans up after Makoto's schemes;
[[satsuki-kyougoku|Satsuki Kyougoku]], head of information work;
[[chiaki-motomiya|Chiaki Motomiya]], the secretary, who writes and publishes
"Weekly Pandemonium Society"; and little [[ibuki-tanga|Ibuki Tanga]], the
academy's beloved mascot, whom Makoto spoils.

The society works from its [[pandemonium_headquarters|headquarters]], keeps its
tanks in the [[pandemonium_tank_hangar]], prints its posters and magazine at the
[[pandemonium_printing_office]] and runs a resort, the
[[pandemonium_spa_resort]], along with Great Makoto merchandise and plans for a
Makoto Tower. Its aim, as Makoto puts it, is to spread the society's influence
over Kivotos. It keeps trying to recruit [[sensei]] as its "partner", and
Satsuki's hypnosis project, NK Ultra, has yet to work on him.
