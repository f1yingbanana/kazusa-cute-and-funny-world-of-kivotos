# Aurora Pharmacy

A small, warm pharmacy on [[red_winter_3rd_street|3rd Street]] with wooden
drawers behind the counter. In winter it mostly sells cold medicine, throat
lozenges and mustard plasters, and it fills orders sent down from the mountain
posts.
