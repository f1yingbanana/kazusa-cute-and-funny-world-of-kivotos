# Abydos Main Building

A three-storey concrete building with sandbags at the entrance and most of its
rooms closed. The handful of students who remain keep one corridor of it alive:
the [[abydos_task_force_room|Foreclosure Task Force's room]], a single
[[abydos_classroom|classroom]], the [[abydos_library|library]] and a small
[[abydos_school_store|store]], with a generator and the water pump in the
[[abydos_basement|basement]]. The floors are swept every morning. Upstairs,
whole floors of empty classrooms collect dust and the odd napper.
