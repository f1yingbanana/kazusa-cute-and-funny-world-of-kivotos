# Shanhaijing Inner Gate

A massive red gate with a guardhouse on each side. Guards of the
[[xuanlong_office]] check every pass: students show theirs with a sigh, and
outsiders are turned back politely, then less politely. Every morning everyone
going in or out queues here together.
