# Secretariat Staff Room

Two desks facing each other with a cold war down the middle. One, Secretary
General [[tomoe-sashiro|Tomoe]]'s, is immaculate, stacked with speech drafts,
schedules and a folded embroidered towel. The other, Security Committee chair
[[marina-ikekura|Marina]]'s, has a patrol map stuck full of corrected pins and a
crate of cleaning supplies kept from past punishments.
