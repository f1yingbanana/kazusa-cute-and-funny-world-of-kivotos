# Lantern Road

A narrow stone road off [[hyakkiyako_plaza]], roofed with strings of paper
lanterns that are lit every evening. It is the procession route of the revived
[[hyakkaryouran_crisis|Lantern Festival]], and on ordinary nights couples and
late walkers drift along it toward the river.
