# Hyakkiyako Central Plaza

The town's big open square, with an outdoor stage at one end where the
[[festival_operations_department]] makes its announcements and weekend hero
shows draw crowds of children. [[hyakkiyako_lantern_road]] and
[[hyakkiyako_tree_square]] open off it, the [[hyakkiyako_cable_car]] leaves
from its north side, and on festival nights stalls fill the whole square.
