# Tsubaki's Room

The room of [[tsubaki-kasuga]] in the [[hyakkiyako_tsukimi_dorm]]: blackout
curtains, and a futon buried in pillows of every height and firmness. Her
shotgun leans by the door.

## Pillows and rounds

Every pillow has been tested and ranked. The shotgun is ready for the rounds
she makes after everyone else is asleep.
