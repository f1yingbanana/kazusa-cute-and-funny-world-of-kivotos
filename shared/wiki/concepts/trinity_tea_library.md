# Tea Party Library

A small panelled library off the [[trinity_tea_party]], reserved for the
[[tea_party]] and holding books that never reach the shelves of the
[[school_library|Central Library]]. Deep armchairs, a fire grate, and a side
table where a jigsaw is always half finished. The key is kept at the chamber
desk.
