# Arius Reconstruction Canteen

Long tables in a patched former refectory, reopened by
[[trinity_school|Trinity]] volunteers. The food is plain and there is always
enough, which in Arius still feels like news.
[[valkyrie_police_academy|Valkyrie]] crews, Trinity volunteers and Arius
students share the tables, and at supper the hall is loud and crowded.
