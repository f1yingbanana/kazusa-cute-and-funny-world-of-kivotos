# Hundred Lotus

The Hundred Lotus, also called the White Lotus, is a navy blue Type 38 Arisaka
rifle with a black barrel and receiver, handed down through every president of
the [[hyakkaryouran_resolution_council]] since its founder, the prophet
[[kuzunoha]]. It is the Authority of the President: whoever carries it leads the
council. [[nagusa-goryou|Nagusa Goryou]] carries it now.

## The rifle

It is covered in old scars and repair marks, each a record of a former
president's battles. It is the only weapon that can affect a
[[hyakkiyako_phantom_fables|Phantom Fable]].

When the former president Ayame disappeared and Nagusa left the council, she
handed it to [[kikyou-kiryuu|Kikyou Kiryuu]]. Nagusa took it up again during the
Lantern Festival crisis ([[hyakkaryouran_crisis]]) and raised it against Shuro of
[[natures_beauty]], crying as she did. It worked anyway.
