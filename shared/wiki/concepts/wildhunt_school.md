# Wild Hunt Academy Fortress

Past the inspection at the [[wildhunt_gate|gate]], the fortress opens up: studio
buildings with tall north windows round the clock tower of the
[[wildhunt_art_center|Art Center]], the domed
[[wildhunt_arts_info_center|Arts Information Center]], the
[[wildhunt_piazza|piazza]] round its purple statue, and the walled
[[wildhunt_dorm_quarter|Dormitory Quarter]] in its own bastion. The
[[wildhunt_rampart|outer rampart]] runs round everything. Students in uniform
cross between studios carrying canvases, instrument cases and toolboxes; it is
busy from first class until curfew and very quiet after.

In the east bastion stands the [[wildhunt_old_schoolhouse|old schoolhouse]],
boarded up and off limits, which everyone notices and nobody is supposed to
enter. This is the whole campus of [[wild_hunt_academy]].

## Unofficial corners

Clubs are not allowed, so groups of friends meet as informal gatherings in dorm
rooms, empty booths and quiet bastions. The patrols know most of the corners;
students keep finding new ones.
