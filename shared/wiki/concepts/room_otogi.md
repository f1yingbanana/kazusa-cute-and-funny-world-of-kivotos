# Otogi's Room

[[otogi-tenjinyama]]'s room: a bed made in a hurry, a desk covered in a radio
she is repairing for nobody in particular, and a box of small gifts strangers
have pressed on her: keychains, candy, a hand-knitted cup sleeve.

## The window

The window latch has been oiled, which says more than anything else in the room:
at night she is often out, at her [[otogi_hideout|walkway under the viaduct]].
