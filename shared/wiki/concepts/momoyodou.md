# Momoyodou

A two-storey wooden tea house on the
[[hyakkiyako_shopping_district|shopping street]], with a pink noren and
red-felt benches out front. The staff wear kimono aprons and the dango are made
fresh each morning. The [[festival_operations_department]] runs it between
festivals, with [[shizuko-kawawa]] as its owner and poster girl.

## Upstairs

The owner lives in [[room_shizuko|a small room]] above the shop. Shizuko's
ambitions for the tea house once ran as far as the
[[momoyodou_resort_plan]].
