# Subaru's Room

[[subaru-kakehashi|Subaru]]'s narrow room used to be the duty sergeant's, so it
opens onto the corridor where she can hear anyone up at night. Sheet music is
pinned above the bed, three harmonicas sit in a case, and a tin of snacks
labelled FOR THE KIDS is restocked from her café wages. A patrol map of Arius
is tacked inside the door.
