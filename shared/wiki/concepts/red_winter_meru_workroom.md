# Meru's Workroom

The furthest room in the [[red_winter_library|library]], past the end of the
closed stacks, and [[meru-himeki|Meru]]'s. A drawing desk under a daylight lamp,
a tablet on its charger, drifts of screentone offcuts and a wall of reference
photos.

## Starting over

This is where Meru comes to start over when drawing gets too hard; she calls it
rehabilitation.
