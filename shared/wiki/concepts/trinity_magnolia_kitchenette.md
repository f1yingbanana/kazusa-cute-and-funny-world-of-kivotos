# Magnolia Hall West Kitchenette

A narrow kitchenette at the end of the [[trinity_magnolia_west|West Wing]],
with a kettle, a toaster, a shelf of everyone's tea tins and a snack drawer
with a lock that doesn't work. At two in the morning there is often someone at
its little table who couldn't sleep, and sometimes two.
