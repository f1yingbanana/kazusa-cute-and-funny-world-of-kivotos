# Borderland Amusement Park

An amusement park built right across the border between [[trinity_district]]
and [[gehenna_district]]. The main entrance and the Ferris wheel are on the
Trinity side; the rear gate and half the rides are in Gehenna, and a line
painted through the middle of the park marks where one ends and the other
begins. Students from both schools come on weekends, and so do their quarrels.

Jurisdiction stops at that painted line, which makes chasing anyone a headache
for the [[justice_task_force]] and the [[prefect_team]] alike. After closing
the park is dark and silent, and the Ferris wheel is a strange, peaceful place
to be at night.
