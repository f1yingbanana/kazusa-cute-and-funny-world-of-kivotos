# Red Winter Federal Library

A domed hall at the far corner of campus with more shelves than readers. The
[[knowledge_liberation_front|Knowledge Liberation Front]] looks after it with
almost no budget: one heater that works, purchase requests that come back
rejected, and a fierce rule against dog-earing. Past the
[[red_winter_library_reading_hall|reading hall]] the
[[red_winter_library_stacks|closed stacks]] run deep into the building. Other
students mostly come in to hide, nap or escape a coup.

## At the very back

Beyond the end of the closed stacks is a [[red_winter_meru_workroom|workroom]]
only the Front uses, where Meru draws.
