# Old Library

A crumbling stone building among overgrown hedges in a far corner of the
campus of [[trinity_school]], so stuffed with old and rare books that people
call it the Antiquarian Bookstore. Stacks lean against the windows, ancient
manuscripts wait to be translated, and the air smells of dust and paper.

Visitors are rare and not encouraged: they must sign the log, keep their voices
down and keep their drinks far from the books. The chair of the
[[trinity_library_committee]], [[ui-kozeki|Ui]], keeps it, and all but lives
here.
