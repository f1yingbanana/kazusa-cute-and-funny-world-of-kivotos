# Public Works Department

Red Winter's Public Works Department, often called the Labor Party, does
construction and maintenance work and fights for fair treatment of student
workers. Its crews take jobs across Kivotos, and students from bankrupt schools
come to it looking for work. Its leader [[minori-yasumori|Minori Yasumori]]
organizes strikes and demonstrations and is often at odds with the
[[red_winter_office]].

## Inside the department

The department runs from the [[red_winter_labor_office|Workforce Office]], a
converted boiler house on campus, where strike meetings are held on Thursday
nights. Crews on contract sleep at sites such as the
[[labor_party_bunkhouse|Labor Party Bunkhouse]] in D.U.
