# Knights Hospitaller

The Knights Hospitaller, usually called the Remedial Knights, are
[[trinity_school]]'s medical and rescue order. They run the
[[trinity_remedial_clinic]] and go out on aid patrols, treating patients
regardless of affiliation.

They are led by [[mine-aomori]], whose rescues are famous for being direct and
forceful.

## Inside the order

- [[serina-sumi]] is the gentle, motherly nurse who seems to appear wherever
  someone is hurt.
- [[hanae-asagao]] is its cheerful unofficial mascot, whose enthusiasm for
  treatment can outrun her judgment.
- Hanae coined the order's unofficial motto: "Mine destroys, the Knights
  Hospitaller heals."
- Mine collects old medical equipment, kept in the
  [[trinity_knights_storage]].
- During the [[eden_treaty_crisis]], Mine faked [[seia-yurizono]]'s death and
  hid her while she recovered.
