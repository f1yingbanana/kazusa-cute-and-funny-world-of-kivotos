# Kisaki's Room

A lacquered chamber in the [[genryumon_vip_quarters]], with a bronze censer
always smoking and a tea table set for one. Petitions are stacked on the desk,
and guards stand outside the door.

## Under the bed

In a chest under the bed, beneath the formal robes, are disguises and maps of
places far from here.
