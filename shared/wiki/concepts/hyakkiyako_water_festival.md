# Hyakkiyako Water Festival

Hyakkiyako's water festival centers on a competition in the water, entered by
several teams, with the usual festival crowds along the bank.

## The Hyakkaryouran's day

The [[hyakkaryouran_resolution_council]] entered too. When
[[kikyou-kiryuu|Kikyou]], who cannot swim, struggled in the water, the others
came to her help. It gave the four friends a chance to support one another
outside their usual duties.
