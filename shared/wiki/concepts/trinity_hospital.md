# Saint Teresa General Hospital

A white hospital at the edge of the [[trinity_residential|Residential
Quarter]], with wards, an operating floor and a children's ward on the third
floor. The [[knights_hospitaller|Remedial Knights]] volunteer here alongside
the regular staff, doing ward rounds, night shifts and charity events. A
canteen on the ground floor feeds staff and visitors until 20:00.

## Quiet beds

When a patient needs hiding as well as healing, the Knights have been known to
arrange that too.
