# Trinity Square

A wide paved square at the heart of the campus of [[trinity_school]], with a
three-tiered fountain in the middle and the school's notice boards along one
side. The [[school_gate|main gate]] opens onto it, and the
[[trinity_academic_hall]], the [[school_library|Central Library]] and the
[[school_cafeteria|dining hall]] face it, so nearly everyone crosses it several
times a day.

Announcements, club recruitment drives and festival stalls happen here, and
when a rumour spreads, this is where the crowd gathers. After dark it is nearly
empty.
