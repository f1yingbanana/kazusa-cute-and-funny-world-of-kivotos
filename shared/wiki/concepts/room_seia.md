# Seia's Room

[[seia-yurizono|Seia]]'s room in the [[trinity_tea_residence]] is warm and
over-comfortable, kept for someone still treated as an invalid. Books are
stacked in towers around the bed, a jigsaw lies half finished on the blanket,
and a feeder hangs outside the window so the small birds of
[[trinity_bird_park]] come right up to the glass. A
[[knights_hospitaller|Remedial Knight]] looks in every day. She sleeps a great
deal, and dreams more than she would like.
