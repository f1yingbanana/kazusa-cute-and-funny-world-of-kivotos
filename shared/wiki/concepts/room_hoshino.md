# Hoshino's Room

[[hoshino-takanashi|Hoshino]]'s fourth-floor flat has hardly any furniture: a
thin futon on a hard floor, a low table, a whale plush and her shield folded by
the door. When the light goes out, the glow-in-the-dark fish stickers she put
up on the ceiling swim across it.

## The drawer

In the low table's drawer is an old notebook that belonged to the late student
council president. Hoshino shows it to nobody.
