# Valkyrie Lecture Building

A plain three-storey block where trainees study criminal law, report writing,
arrest procedure and first aid between shifts. Each year has one homeroom:
[[valkyrie_class_1|1-A]], [[valkyrie_class_2|2-A]] and [[valkyrie_class_3|3-A]].
Attendance sheets are marked "on duty" as often as "present".
