# Kinema Hyakkiyako

A single-screen cinema in [[hyakkiyako_movie_village]], with hand-painted
posters and creaking velvet seats. It runs old period dramas, chivalry-gangster
films and the odd foreign noir, and its Saturday all-night marathons draw a
small, devoted crowd.
