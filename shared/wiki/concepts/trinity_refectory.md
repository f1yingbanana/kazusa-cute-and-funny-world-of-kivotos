# Sisterhood Refectory

A whitewashed hall off the [[trinity_cathedral_cloister|cloister]] where the
Sisters of the [[sisterhood]] eat: one long table, a serving hatch from the
kitchen, and a reading stand where one Sister reads aloud at supper. Grace comes
first and talk is kept low; the food is plain and the portions careful. A
kettle is always warm for Sisters coming back late from duties.
