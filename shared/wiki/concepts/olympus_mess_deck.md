# Olympus Mess Deck

Long tables bolted to the deck and a galley run by the Cruise Operations
Department, serving fish farmed by the Aquaculture Research Department and kelp
tea in place of anything else. At mealtimes the whole flotilla seems to be here
at once. Whistling at the table is forbidden, like many things aboard.
