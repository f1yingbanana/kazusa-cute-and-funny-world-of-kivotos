# Abydos Suburban Park

A small park behind the market, with a dry fountain, a few benches and flower
beds that are actually green. The town office pays students by the hour to weed
it and keep the beds alive, which is why it is the greenest place in Abydos. At
night it is dark, and only the odd patrol walks through.
