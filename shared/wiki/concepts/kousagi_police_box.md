# Kousagi Station Police Box

A one-room police box with a desk, a map of the ward, a lost-property shelf and
a bicycle outside. [[valkyrie_police_academy|Valkyrie]] officers on beat duty
sit here between patrols; when nobody is in, there is a phone on the wall.
