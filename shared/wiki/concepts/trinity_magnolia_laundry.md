# Magnolia Hall East Laundry

A small room at the end of the [[trinity_magnolia_east|East Wing]] with four
washing machines, one of them temperamental, a line strung across the window
and an ironing board that doubles as a study desk. When the machines run, the
whole corridor rattles. A sign asks residents not to leave wet laundry
overnight; nobody obeys it.
