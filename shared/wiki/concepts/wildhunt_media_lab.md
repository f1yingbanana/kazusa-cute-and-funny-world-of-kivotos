# Wild Hunt Media Arts Lab

A dim lab in the [[wildhunt_art_center|Art Center]] with walls painted black,
projectors on every shelf, monitors stacked on carts and bins of salvaged
cables. Students build installations from screens and light here, open 09:00 to
21:00. Equipment is signed out at the desk, and the ledger is never quite
accurate.
