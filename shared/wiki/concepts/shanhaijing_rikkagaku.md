# Genryumon Old Palace

An old palace of red pillars and golden roofs, seat of the [[xuanlong_office]],
the student council that rules Shanhaijing like an old dynasty. Petitioners wait
in the outer court before the [[genryumon_main_hall]]; guards stand at every
gate, and the inner courts are hushed.

## The inner courts

Behind the main hall lie the guarded [[genryumon_vip_quarters]], where the
council's president lives, and the walled [[genryumon_palace_garden]]. The
[[shanhaijing_school_prison]] stands at the back of the complex.
