# No. 227 Bathhouse Milk Bar

A wooden counter by the exit of the [[red_winter_227_bathhouse|bathhouse]], a
chilled cabinet of glass bottles and a chalkboard menu: fruit milk, coffee milk,
homemade kompot and mors. A member of
[[special_class_227|No. 227 Special Class]] staffs it in the evenings.

## The drinks

They are only sometimes strictly non-alcoholic.
