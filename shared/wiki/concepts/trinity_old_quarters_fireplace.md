# Old Living Quarters Fireplace Room

A panelled common room in the [[trinity_old_quarters]] with the dorm's pride, a
working fireplace, plus worn armchairs, a kettle and a toaster. On winter
evenings the few residents end up here together whether they planned to or
not.
