# Getting Around Kivotos

Kivotos is far too big to walk. Between districts everyone takes the train:
[[highlander_railroad_academy|Highlander Railroad Academy]] runs almost every
railway in the city, and its main lines all meet at
[[du_central_station|D.U. Central Station]] in the capital. Inside D.U. the
Metro is quicker; inside each district students walk, cycle or take the local
buses. Most lines run from about 05:00 to 00:30, and missing the last train
means a night at the station or a very long walk.

A trip from a school to [[schale|SCHALE]] is an outing, not a hop: from Trinity
about forty minutes door to door, from Gehenna or Millennium about fifty, from
Hyakkiyako or Abydos a little over an hour, from Wild Hunt or Shanhaijing longer
still, and from Red Winter most of a morning.

## Highlander's lines

- **Southern Line**: D.U. Central – [[kousagi_station|Kousagi]] –
  [[trinity_station|Trinity]] – [[gehenna_station|Gehenna]]. Through trains run
  all the way, every ten minutes by day.
- **Trans-Desert Railway**: Gehenna – [[abydos_station|Abydos]], and its D.U.
  branch Abydos – Kousagi. Built by Abydos's old student council and now mostly
  abandoned; a train runs every ninety minutes on the surviving track, and
  people plan their day around the timetable pinned at Abydos Central Station.
- **Eastern Line**: D.U. Central – [[haruhabara_station|Haruhabara]] –
  [[millennium_station|Millennium]]. Millennium also runs its own railway and a
  campus monorail.
- **Northwestern Line**: D.U. Central – [[hyakkiyako_station|Hyakkiyako]] –
  [[shanhaijing_station|Shanhaijing]], plus the Hyakkiyako North Line through
  the long tunnel to the snowy farmland at [[ebisu_station|Ebisu]], hourly.
- **Western Line**: D.U. Central – [[wildhunt_station|Wild Hunt]].
- **Northern Trunk**: D.U. Central – [[red_winter_station|Red Winter]]. Every
  forty minutes in theory; in practice, whenever the train arrives. Red
  Winter's own campus shuttle trains are later still.

Highlander trains have assigned seats and conductors who check tickets. Delays
are common, and Highlander's own students are the first to complain about them.
The concourse at D.U. Central sells station bentos and a famous soft-serve, and
the queues are part of the journey.

## The D.U. Metro

The capital's subway is run by the
[[general_student_council|General Student Council]]'s Transportation Office,
not by Highlander; Highlander students get annoyed when people blame them for
Metro fares. It is punctual, quiet and packed at rush hour, and runs
05:30–00:30. Its lines are the Kousagi Line (D.U. Central – Kousagi, the station
for SCHALE), the Shiratori Line (D.U. Central –
[[shiratori_station|Shiratori]] – [[kanesaki_port_station|Kanesaki Port]]) and
the outer arc (Shiratori – Kousagi). [[sensei|Sensei]] commutes on it with a
monthly pass.

## Buses and everything else

Every district runs its own buses. Gehenna runs a short subway from Central
Station to the academy forecourt every six minutes, and Red Winter has trams on
its main streets. Trinity's buses are famously infrequent, about half an hour
apart, though Trinity also has a short subway under its main street. A
cross-border bus runs between Trinity and Gehenna, Gehenna's south end has a
bus into the [[black_market_district|Black Market District]], and four desert
buses a day connect the Black Market with Abydos. Hyakkiyako has rickshaws and
a tourist [[hyakkiyako_cable_car|cable car]]. Plenty of students cycle (some
ride across half of Kivotos for fun), some clubs drive anything from staff cars
to tanks, and helicopters belong to the General Student Council, SCHALE and
anyone rich.
