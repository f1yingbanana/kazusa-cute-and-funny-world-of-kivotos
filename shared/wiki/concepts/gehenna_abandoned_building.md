# Gehenna Abandoned School Building

A cracked, ivy-covered building at the edge of the [[gehenna_school]] campus
that the academy stopped using years ago. Doors are boarded but not very well;
inside are dusty corridors, the closed
[[gehenna_old_dining_hall|old dining hall]] and classrooms where the desks still
stand in rows.

## Who uses it

It is off-limits, which is why it is popular. The [[gourmet_research_society]]
cooks in the old dining hall, and the [[sparkle_club]] has made
[[gehenna_old_classroom_7|Old Classroom 7]] its hangout.
