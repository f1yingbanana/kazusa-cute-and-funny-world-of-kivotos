# Millennium Class 3-B

A third-year homeroom at the top of the
[[millennium_lecture_building|Lecture Building]]. Third-years spend more time in
labs and club rooms than here, so the room has the feel of a waiting lounge: a
coffee maker nobody admits to owning, thesis drafts on the windowsill, and exam
timetables circled in red.
