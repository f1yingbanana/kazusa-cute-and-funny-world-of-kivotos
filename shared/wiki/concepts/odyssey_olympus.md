# Flagship Olympus

The largest ship in the flotilla and the closest thing
[[odyssey_maritime_high_school|Odyssey]] has to a main building: the Island
leadership works from its bridge, lessons run on its middle
[[olympus_lecture_deck|decks]] and the whole school eats on its
[[olympus_mess_deck|mess deck]]. Its long weather deck is the school's parade
ground, and the gangway is always guarded.

## At night

Divers swim beside its hull at night when nobody is supposed to be swimming.
