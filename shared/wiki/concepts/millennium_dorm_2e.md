# Millennium Dormitory 2F East

A corridor of single rooms in the [[millennium_dormitory|dormitory]], with a
[[millennium_dorm_2e_kitchen|kitchenette]] at one end. It is the quietest
corridor in the building, by decree: a printed notice about quiet hours is taped
at both ends, and someone has added the fine for breaking it.
