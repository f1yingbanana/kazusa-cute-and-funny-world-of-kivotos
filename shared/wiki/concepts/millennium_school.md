# Millennium Science School

The academy of science, engineering and technology, and a campus that looks
more like a research park than a school. [[millennium_tower|Millennium Tower]]
rises over the central [[millennium_square|square]]; around it stand laboratories,
research facilities and modern towers, paths lined with charging posts and security cameras, and
delivery drones cross overhead all day.

Its student council is [[seminar]], which holds the purse strings of every club.
Among its many groups are [[veritas]], [[cleaning_and_clearing]], the
[[engineering_department]], the [[paranormal_affairs_department]], the
[[gym_training_department]] and the [[game_development_department]].

## The campus

Around the Tower and the Square stand the
[[millennium_lecture_building|Lecture Building]], the long
[[millennium_clubhouse|Clubhouse]], the [[millennium_study_area|Study Area]]
with its monorail stop, the [[millennium_engineering|Engineering hangar]] and,
at the far end, the [[millennium_stadium|12th Millennium Stadium]]. The north
gate faces the [[millennium_dormitory|dormitory]] footbridge; the south gate
opens toward the [[millennium_railway_crossing|railway crossing]] and
[[millennium_euler_heights|Euler Heights]].
