# Kadenokouji Family

The Kadenokouji are one of [[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako]]'s oldest families, a
venerable miko lineage that runs the district's rituals and events, among them
the miko ceremony of the Lantern Festival. They live in the walled
[[kadenokouji_estate]] on the hill above town, beside their own
[[kadenokouji_shrine|family shrine]].

## The heir

The household runs on ritual, schedules and polite silence.
[[yukari-kadenokouji|Yukari]], the heir, was raised in ceremony, the ritual arts,
academics and music, so sheltered that salty snacks and arcades were new to her.
She chose to follow her own dream into the
[[hyakkaryouran_resolution_council]] instead.
