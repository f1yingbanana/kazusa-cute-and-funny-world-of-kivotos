# Gehenna Central Square

A wide cobbled square outside [[gehenna_station]], with a dry fountain, rows of
food stalls, the district bus terminal along one side and the gilded
[[gehenna_banquet_hall|Banquet Hall]] facing it. Street-food festivals,
[[pandemonium_society|council]] rallies and shootouts all happen here, sometimes
at once.
