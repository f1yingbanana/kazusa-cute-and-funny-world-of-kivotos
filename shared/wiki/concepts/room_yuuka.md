# Yuuka's Room

In [[yuuka-hayase|Yuuka]]'s room everything is labeled and squared to the desk
edge. Binders of ledgers stand in fiscal-quarter order, an abacus sits on the
nightstand for calming down, and a small radio is tuned to the late-night
station she listens to when she can't sleep.

## Next door

On quiet nights the abacus carries through the shared wall to
[[room_noa|Noa's room]]; [[noa-ushio|Noa]] has never once complained.
