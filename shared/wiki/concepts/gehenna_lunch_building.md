# School Lunch Club Building

A plain two-storey building near the [[gehenna_lawn_square|Lawn Square]] that
houses Gehenna's one student [[gehenna_cafeteria|dining hall]], with a
[[gehenna_vegetable_garden|vegetable garden]] behind it. The
[[gehenna_kitchen|kitchen]] lights come on long before dawn, and deliveries
arrive at the loading door every morning by truck or, on bad days, by hand-cart.
