# Niko's Room

[[niko-yoshino]]'s room: a bunk, a desk, and a shelf turned pantry, with a rice
cooker, a hot plate she is not strictly allowed, jars of simmered tofu pouches
and a label maker that has tagged every jar, every drawer and her own pillow.
The door is usually ajar, and visitors rarely leave without something wrapped in
foil.
