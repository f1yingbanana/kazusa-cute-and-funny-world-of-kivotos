# Millennium Research Park

Glass and steel beside [[millennium_station|Millennium Station]]: startups, labs
spun off from [[millennium_school|Millennium]] research groups, and windowless
data centers. The [[millennium_exhibition_hall|Exhibition Hall]] stands at one
end and the residential tower of [[millennium_lumen_court|Lumen Court]] at the
other. Security guards outnumber pedestrians after dark.
