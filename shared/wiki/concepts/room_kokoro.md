# Kokoro's Cabin

[[kokoro-toumi]]'s two-berth cabin aboard the Calypso, which she has to herself.
Her diving gear hangs along the bulkhead in exactly the order she will put it
on, and the pre-dive checklist taped beside it has been laminated twice. A small
frying pan lives under the bunk.

## Under the pillow

A waterproof songbook lives under the pillow, where nobody needs to know about
it.
