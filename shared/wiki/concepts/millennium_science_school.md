# Millennium Science School

Millennium is an academy associated with science, engineering and technology.
Its student council is Seminar. Its many groups include Veritas, Cleaning &
Clearing, the Engineering Department, the Paranormal Affairs Department and the
Game Development Department. Laboratories, research facilities and modern towers
are prominent on campus.
