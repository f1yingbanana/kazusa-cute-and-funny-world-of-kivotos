# Mina's Room

A narrow room by the gate of the [[genryumon_vip_quarters]], placed so that
nobody passes without her knowing. Noir film posters cover the walls, a trench
coat and umbrella hang by the door, a recorder lies on the desk, and the blinds
are kept half-closed for the rain.
