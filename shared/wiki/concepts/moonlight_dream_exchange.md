# Moonlight Dream Exchange

An exchange brought [[cherino-renkawa|Cherino]], [[tomoe-sashiro|Tomoe]] and
[[marina-ikekura|Marina]] from [[red_winter_school|Red Winter]] to
[[shanhaijing_senior_secondary_school|Shanhaijing]].

## The dispute

[[kisaki-ryuuge|Kisaki]]'s efforts to engage with outsiders met resistance from
Kaguya, whose devotion to tradition became tangled up in a challenge to
[[xuanlong_office|Genryumon]]. [[mina-konoe|Mina]] and the visitors were drawn
into the dispute.
