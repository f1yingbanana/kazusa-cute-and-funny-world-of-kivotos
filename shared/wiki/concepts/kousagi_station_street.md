# Kousagi Station Street

A short, lively street running from [[kousagi_station]] toward the hill: an
[[angel_24_kousagi|Angel 24]] on the corner, a
[[kousagi_game_center|game center]], a [[kousagi_karaoke|karaoke box]],
[[loiseau_bleu|a sweets shop with a blue bird on its sign]], a small
[[kousagi_mall|station mall]] and a [[kousagi_police_box|police box]] in front
of the station. Students heading up to [[schale_office|SCHALE]] pass through it,
and many stop.
