# Gehenna and Trinity Rivalry

[[trinity_school|Trinity]] and [[gehenna_school|Gehenna]] have been rivals for
as long as anyone remembers, divided by very different school cultures and
political histories. Their shared border is the most argued-over line in
Kivotos. The [[eden_treaty]] was meant to ease the conflict between them. Not
every student or club shares their school's view of the other.
