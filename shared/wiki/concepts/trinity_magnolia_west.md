# Magnolia Hall West Wing

A quiet corridor on the third floor of [[trinity_magnolia_hall]], where several
[[justice_task_force]] officers live, along with a
[[trinity_library_committee|Library Committee]] member who reads all night.
Boots are lined up outside doors, a duty roster is taped by the stairs, and the
[[trinity_magnolia_kitchenette|kitchenette]] at the end sees more late-night
tea than it should.
