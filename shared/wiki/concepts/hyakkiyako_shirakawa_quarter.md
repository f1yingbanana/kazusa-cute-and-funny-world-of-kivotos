# Shirakawa Quarter

Wooden houses, willow trees and narrow lanes along the river behind the castle,
over an arched bridge from the shops. Most Hyakkiyako students who don't live
with family live here, in the [[hyakkiyako_tsukimi_dorm]] or the
[[hyakkiyako_kawabata_house]], and bathe at the [[hyakkiyako_bathhouse]]. The
morning walk to school along the [[hyakkiyako_riverbank]] is where half of them
meet.
