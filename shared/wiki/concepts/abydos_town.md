# Abydos Residential Area

Once the richest district in Kivotos, now a town the desert is swallowing.
Whole blocks stand empty, sand drifts against shuttered shopfronts, and the
centre has no food shop still open: for groceries, ramen or a late snack,
people walk out to [[abydos_suburban_town|Suburban Town]] by the station. The
surviving [[abydos_school|school]] sits on the north side, a few apartment
blocks still have tenants, and past the last streets the
[[abydos_desert|dunes]] begin. Much of the land beyond them belongs to
[[kaiser_corporation|Kaiser]] now.

Trans-Desert Railway trains leave [[abydos_station]] for Gehenna and for
Kousagi in D.U. every 90 minutes until 21:00. The desert bus to the
[[black_market_district|Black Market]] stops on the station forecourt four
times a day, and a local bus runs out to the
[[abydos_border_town|border town]] every half hour.
