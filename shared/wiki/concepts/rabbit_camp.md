# RABBIT Camp

A hollow on the quiet side of [[usagi_park|Kousagi Park]], screened by azaleas,
where the four members of [[rabbit_platoon]] have lived since
[[srt_training_facility|SRT]] was closed. A rope line with a hand-painted sign
marks where the park ends and the camp begins. Their [[rabbit_tent_row|tents]]
stand in a neat square on pallets, and a tarp over an oil-drum
[[rabbit_camp_cookfire|stove]] serves as kitchen, briefing room and dining
table. Laundry hangs between two trees most days. Everything here was bought
cheap, found on the hill or handed down, and it is kept in better order than
most dorms.

## Around the camp

Stone steps climb from the back of the camp to the [[schale_office|SCHALE]]
building, and a footpath on the east side runs straight up into the woods of
[[kousagi_hill]]. A sentry light stays on until dawn.
