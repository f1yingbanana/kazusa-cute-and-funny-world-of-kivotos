# Old Kettle Noodle House

A narrow shop on the [[shanhaijing_promenade]] where the cook pulls noodles in
the window. A long counter runs to the back, under a shelf of house chili
sauces.

## The regulars' counter

Each counter regular has a chili sauce on the shelf, and one of the sauces was
invented by a customer.
