# Momo Friends Flagship Shop

A whole floor of Peroro and the rest of [[momo_friends]]: walls of plush, a ball
pool for small children, a photo spot with a giant Peroro, and a glass case of
limited editions that sell out within an hour of the Saturday release. The queue
for those starts at seven.
