# Plum Blossom Garden Main Building

A low schoolhouse in a garden of plum trees, behind a painted gate. Inside are
small classrooms with tiny chairs and a kitchen that makes snacks at three, and
the whole place is full of children. This is the home of the
[[plum_blossom_garden]], whose instructors are students themselves.

## Around the schoolhouse

Behind it is the [[plum_garden_playground]]; beside it, the
[[plum_garden_staff_house]] where the instructors live.
