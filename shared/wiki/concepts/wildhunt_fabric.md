# Bobbin & Bolt Fabrics

A shop in the [[wildhunt_canal_town|canal town]] with bolts of fabric to the
ceiling, a wall of ribbon spools in every colour and drawers of thread and craft
supplies. Half its customers are [[wild_hunt_academy|Wild Hunt]] students buying
what they can't get inside, rushing in before the gate closes, and the owner
wraps purchases to survive the gate inspection.
