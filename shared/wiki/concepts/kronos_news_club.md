# Kronos News Club

The News Club of the [[kronos_school|Kronos School of Journalism]] reports on
events across Kivotos. Its students investigate, interview and broadcast live
from every district, working out of the
[[kronos_broadcasting|Kronos Broadcasting Center]]. Its best-known reporters
are Kazemaki Mai and Kawaru Shinon.
