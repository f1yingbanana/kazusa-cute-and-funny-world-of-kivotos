# Hanae's Room

[[hanae-asagao|Hanae]]'s room on the first floor of the
[[trinity_old_quarters]] is one of the few whose old fireplace still works, and
she keeps it lit all winter. Morning glories grow in pots on the sill, pom-poms
hang from the wardrobe, and a radio calisthenics chart is taped beside the bed
she leaves at 05:30 sharp. A first-aid kit the size of a suitcase waits by the
door.
