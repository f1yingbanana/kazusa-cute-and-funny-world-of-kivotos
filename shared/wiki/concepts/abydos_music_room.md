# Abydos Music Room

Sheet-music cabinets, chairs stacked against the wall, a broken metronome and
an upright piano under a dust sheet, with sand in the corners. The piano was
tuned years ago and has mostly held. Anyone who plays can hear the sound carry
across the empty yard.
