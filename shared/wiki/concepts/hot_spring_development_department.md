# Hot Spring Development Department

The Hot Spring Development Department of [[gehenna_school]] searches for hot
springs and develops them, wherever they might be and whoever owns the ground.
Its digging is done with explosives, which has made it enemies all over the
district and put a Gehenna bounty on its president,
[[kasumi-kinugawa|Kasumi Kinugawa]]. The [[prefect_team]] chases it constantly.
[[megu-shimokura|Megu Shimokura]] leads its field work.

## Inside the department

Kasumi plans and negotiates; she took the president's role so that Megu could
just dig. Megu does the blasting, burning and heavy lifting, and can make a hot
spring without any equipment. The department's main dig is the
[[gehenna_hot_springs_worksite]], a muddy valley it returns to every time it is
chased off. Kasumi stays on the move among dozens of secret hideouts.

Those hunting the department include the Prefect Team, the
[[helmet_gang]], arms makers and clubs hurt by its past demolitions.
