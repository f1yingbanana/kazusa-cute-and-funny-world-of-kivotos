# Highlander Freight Logistics Department

The Freight Logistics Department keeps the freight side of
[[highlander_railroad_academy]] moving: it makes up trains in the
[[highlander_rail_yard]] east of [[du_central_station]], repairs engines and
anything else in the [[highlander_repair_shop]], and works out of the
[[highlander_yard_office]] at all hours. It once famously lost a whole train
from the yard.

## Inside

Its engineers spend much of their time fixing problems that started in the
[[highlander_control_center]], and they are not shy about thinking so.
[[aoba-utsumi]] is one of them: quiet, overworked, and the one who gets called
when something on the railway will not start.
