# Gehenna Clubroom Main Building

Five floors of club rooms on the [[gehenna_school]] campus, their doors covered
in stickers, warnings and bullet holes. Rooms change hands by vote, by bribe or
by siege; the [[prefect_team]] seals a few every term and they are reopened
within the month.
