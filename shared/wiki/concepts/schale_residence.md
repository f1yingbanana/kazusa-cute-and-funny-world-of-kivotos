# SCHALE Residence Hall

The other half of the [[schale_office|SCHALE]] building: a residence wing where
students from any school can rest, study, cook and stay over when work runs
late. It has a [[schale_lounge|resting room]], a bookable
[[schale_study_room|self-study room]], a [[schale_cafeteria|cafeteria]] with
practice kitchens, a small [[schale_arcade|arcade]] of retro machines, an
[[schale_angel24|Angel 24]] and a [[schale_garden|vegetable garden]] on the
roof.
