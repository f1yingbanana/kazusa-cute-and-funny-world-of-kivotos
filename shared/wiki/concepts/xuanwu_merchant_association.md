# Xuanwu Merchant Association

The Xuanwu Merchant Association runs the [[shanhaijing_promenade]], the gourmet
street that turned [[shanhaijing_senior_secondary_school]] into the best place
to eat in Kivotos. It works out of the [[shanhaijing_main_restaurant]], where
its president, [[rumi-akeshiro]], is also head chef, and its bodyguard
[[reijo-kayama]] keeps her and the Promenade's people safe.

The association's business often runs against the [[xuanlong_office]], and
Genryumon inspections of the Promenade are a running feud.

## Inside the association

Rumi lives above the main restaurant, where her bedroom has become a test
kitchen; the association's members have learned to be careful with her new
recipes. Reijo has more than once ended a hostile Genryumon inspection on her
own terms, once by threatening to cancel the council's snack deliveries for
good.
