# RABBIT Platoon

RABBIT Platoon is a four-member team from SRT Special Academy: Tsukiyuki Miyako,
Saki Sorai, Moe Kazekura and Miyu Kasumizawa. After SRT closed, they occupied
Usagi Park, also known as Kousagi Park, to protest the decision and continued
living and training there. Sensei has supported the team.
