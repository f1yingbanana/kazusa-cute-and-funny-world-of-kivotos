# RABBIT Platoon

RABBIT Platoon is a squad from [[srt_training_facility]]: four
first-years who would not accept the academy's closure. Instead of transferring
they set up camp in [[usagi_park]] as a protest and have lived there since,
training as if SRT still existed. [[miyako-tsukiyuki]] leads them, with
[[saki-sorai]] on point, [[moe-kazekura]] on equipment and explosives, and
[[miyu-kasumizawa]] as sniper. [[sensei]] has stood by them from the start.

## The squad

They live at the [[rabbit_camp]] in a hollow of the park, four tents and an
oil-drum stove, with the SCHALE building up the stone steps behind them. Food
and supplies are always short, and Saki forages to make up the difference. The
four did not get along at first; Miyako still worries whether she is fit to lead
them. They go by callsigns: Miyako is RABBIT1 and Moe RABBIT3. How they came to
cross their seniors in [[fox_platoon]] is told in [[srt_closure_crisis]], and
their trip to a coastal village in [[rabbit_shrimp_mystery]].
