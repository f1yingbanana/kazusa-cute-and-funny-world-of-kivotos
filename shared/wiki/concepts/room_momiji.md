# Momiji's Room

[[momiji-akiizumi|Momiji]]'s room in the
[[red_winter_first_dormitory|dormitory]]: manga and fanzines in protective
sleeves stacked in careful towers to the ceiling, sorted by a system only she
understands, a reading lamp and a sleeping bag. A binder on the desk holds every
rejected book-purchase request she has filed, neatly dated. She often sleeps in
the [[red_winter_library|library]] instead, and keeps the door double-padlocked.
