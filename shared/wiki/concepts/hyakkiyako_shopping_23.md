# Shopping District No. 23

A side street of souvenir and specialty shops branching off the
[[hyakkiyako_shopping_district]], with the [[hyakkiyako_department_store]] at
its corner. Tourists buy mascot goods here; students come for cards at the
department store, [[hyakkiyako_ninja_goods]], cameras at
[[hyakkiyako_camera_shop]] and the [[hyakkiyako_karaoke]].
