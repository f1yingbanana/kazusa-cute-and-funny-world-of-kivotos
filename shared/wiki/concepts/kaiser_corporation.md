# Kaiser Corporation

Kaiser is one of the largest corporations in Kivotos, with financial,
construction, industrial and private military arms, run from
[[kaiser_tower|Kaiser Tower]]. Kaiser PMC deploys armed personnel and heavy
equipment wherever the company's interests need protecting or pursuing.

## In Abydos

Kaiser's subsidiaries are tangled up in [[abydos_debt|the Abydos debt]] and in
fights over land and authority in the district. Kaiser Loan holds the school's
debt, much of the desert is Kaiser land, and Kaiser PMC runs it from a
[[abydos_kaiser_base|desert base]].
