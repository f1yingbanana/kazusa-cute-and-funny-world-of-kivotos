# Cherino's Room

[[cherino-renkawa|Cherino]]'s small, overheated bedroom behind the
[[red_winter_presidential_office|Presidential Office]], reached through a door
behind the portrait. A canopy bed far too big for its owner, a second pudding
fridge, a rubber duck by the washbasin, and a velvet stand holding a row of
spare fake mustaches, one for every mood.

## In a coup

After a coup the door is the first thing she barricades, or the first thing she
flees through.
