# Ayane's Room

[[ayane-okusora|Ayane]]'s neat second-floor flat. The desk is the club's
accounts office after hours: ledgers, receipts in labelled envelopes, a
calculator worn smooth, her laptop and the drone on its charger. The shelves
hold salvage she hasn't sold yet, each chipped piece tagged with what it might
fetch. A spare pair of glasses sits by the bed, because without them she can't
find the first pair.
