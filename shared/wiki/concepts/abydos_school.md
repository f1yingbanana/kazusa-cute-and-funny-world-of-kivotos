# Abydos High School

Abydos High School once held real weight in Kivotos. Desertification and a
crushing [[abydos_debt|debt]] have emptied it down to a handful of students,
who keep it running themselves as the [[foreclosure_task_force]], working to
preserve the surviving campus and the wider district. Much of the Abydos land
around it came under [[kaiser_corporation|Kaiser Corporation]] control as the
school struggled with its debt.

The campus is a walled compound on the north side of
[[abydos_town|town]], far too big for the few people in it. The gate posts
carry old bullet scars; inside, sand lies on every path, one building is swept
clean and clearly in use, and the rest stand quiet and closed.

## The campus

Abydos was once big enough to get lost in. What is left is the
[[abydos_main_building|Main Building]], which the students sweep every morning,
a mostly closed [[abydos_annex|annex]], a [[abydos_gym|gymnasium]], a cracked
[[abydos_pool|pool]], a [[abydos_playground|field]] the sand is slowly burying
and a condemned [[abydos_old_schoolhouse|wooden schoolhouse]] at the far edge.
The bullet scars on the gate are from the [[helmet_gang|Helmet gangs']] raids.
Delivery trucks and the occasional visitor from [[schale]] are the only
traffic.

## How the school runs

No teaching staff are left. Lessons happen in the one
[[abydos_classroom|classroom]] still in use, from textbooks and correspondence
coursework, on a timetable the students wrote themselves. Everything else, from
repairs in the [[abydos_basement|basement]] to defending the gate and paying
the monthly interest, falls to the Task Force. The old student council's
records and minutes sit in the [[abydos_library|library]].
