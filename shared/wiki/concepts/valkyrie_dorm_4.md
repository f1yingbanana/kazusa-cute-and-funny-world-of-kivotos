# Valkyrie Assigned Dormitory 4

A three-storey brick dorm at the back of campus where officers are assigned
numbered rooms by bureau. The door takes a code day and night; curfew is 23:00,
but the sign-out sheet at the door is thick with night-duty exemptions, and the
house supervisor's 06:30 roll call is taken by knocking. Downstairs are a
[[valkyrie_dorm_4_common|common room]] and a
[[valkyrie_dorm_4_washroom|washroom]].

## Who lives where

[[valkyrie_public_safety|Public Safety]]'s first-years share the second floor;
the third floor is quieter and belongs to
[[valkyrie_public_peace|Public Peace]].
