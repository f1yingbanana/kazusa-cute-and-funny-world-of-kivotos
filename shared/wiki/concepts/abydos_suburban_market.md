# Abydos Suburban Market

A long covered arcade of stalls: vegetables trucked in from the
[[abydos_border_town|border town]], eggs, milk, bread, cheap hardware and spare
parts. Someone is usually handing out flyers at the entrance. Runners stop for
breakfast ingredients at dawn; by early afternoon the shutters are down.
