# The Chroma Crisis

Kivotos faced a city-wide threat when the Chroma and a series of False
Sanctums appeared. Students and organisations from every academy joined
[[sensei]] and the [[general_student_council]] to defend their districts and
confront it, and in the end they won. Kivotos rebuilt afterwards.

## The Ark

The response led to Utnapishtim's Ship, uncovered beneath
[[abydos_district|Abydos]], and to the Ark of Atrahasis above Kivotos.
[[aris-tendou|Aris]] and [[kei-tendou|Key]] helped the expedition reach the
Ark, and Key sacrificed herself in the effort. Those aboard faced Phrenapates
and a Shiroko from another timeline, and the crisis ended through the combined
actions of Sensei and the students. [[arona|Arona]] and the other timeline's
assistant, later named Plana, helped save Sensei. In the aftermath a fragment
of Kei's data was found.
