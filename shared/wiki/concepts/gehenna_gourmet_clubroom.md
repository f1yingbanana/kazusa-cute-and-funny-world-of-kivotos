# Gourmet Research Society Clubroom

A small room in the [[gehenna_clubroom_building|clubroom building]] furnished
like a private dining room: a white tablecloth, good chairs, a tea set, a
camping stove, and a wall map of restaurants marked with stars or with crosses.

## The cupboard

It holds napkins, cutlery and explosives in roughly equal amounts. The crosses
on the map are the restaurants the society has dealt with.
