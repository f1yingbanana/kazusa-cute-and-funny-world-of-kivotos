# Arius Interim Council Room

A former staff room in the [[arius_schoolhouse|schoolhouse]], taken over by
the new interim council of [[arius_school|Arius]]. Parole terms, supply lists
from [[trinity_school|Trinity]] and a wall chart of which buildings the crews
will fix next cover every surface around a long, scarred table. The kettle on
the hot plate is always on.
