# Hyakkiyako Tour Bus Terminal

A covered bus bay and rickshaw stand across the forecourt from
[[hyakkiyako_station]], at the foot of the
[[hyakkiyako_shopping_district|shopping street]]. Guides wave flags over their
groups, and the tourist information desk is staffed by student volunteers.
Pamphlet racks for every festival in Kivotos line the waiting room.
