# Red Winter Class 3-1

The senior homeroom closest to the stairs, so runners from the Office Building
can reach it fastest. The front desks belong to President
[[cherino-renkawa|Cherino]] and her Secretary General
[[tomoe-sashiro|Tomoe]]; the Labor Party's leader
[[minori-yasumori|Minori]] and the head of the Publishing Department,
[[yakumo-aramaki|Yakumo]], sit further back. The loudspeaker over the door
interrupts lessons with decrees.
