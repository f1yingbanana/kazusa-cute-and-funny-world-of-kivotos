# Takane's Room

[[takane-miyoshi|Takane]]'s room in the
[[red_winter_first_dormitory|dormitory]], neat to the point of severity. A
typewriter for reviews sits on a desk beside a camera with a long lens; every
book on the shelf is annotated in red pencil. A hockey pennant hangs over the
bed. Through the thin wall, the manga artist next door, [[meru-himeki|Meru]],
can be heard scratching away at night, which Takane reviews aloud.

## The wardrobe

A hot plate for bean-sprout stir-fries hides in the wardrobe, away from the
warden.
