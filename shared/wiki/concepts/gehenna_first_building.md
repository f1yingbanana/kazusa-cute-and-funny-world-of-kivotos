# Gehenna First School Building

A long four-storey block of [[gehenna_school]] with tall windows and a bell
tower, its plaster chipped by stray rounds. First-years have the lower floors
and second-years the upper ones. The stairwell to the
[[gehenna_first_building_roof|roof]] is chained off with a padlock nobody has a
key to anymore.
