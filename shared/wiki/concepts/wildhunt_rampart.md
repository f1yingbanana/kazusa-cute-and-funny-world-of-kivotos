# Wild Hunt Outer Rampart

A broad grassy walk along the top of the star fortress walls, with benches in
each bastion, a lot of wind and a view over the moats to the
[[wildhunt_canal_town|canal town]]. It is open 06:00 to 21:00 and patrolled
after dark. Things thrown over the wall from outside have a habit of landing in
its quieter corners.
