# Tomoe's Room

[[tomoe-sashiro|Tomoe]]'s room in the
[[red_winter_first_dormitory|dormitory]]: framed portraits of
[[cherino-renkawa|the president]] on every wall, speech drafts in labelled
folders and a megaphone on the windowsill. On the nightstand lies a hand-copied
songbook of lullabies. Tomoe is usually at the Office Building until late and
comes back only to sleep.

## Across the corridor

The door across the corridor belongs to the Security Committee chair,
[[marina-ikekura|Marina]], which makes coup weeks awkward on the second floor.
