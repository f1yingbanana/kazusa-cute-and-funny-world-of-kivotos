# Hyakkaryouran Clubhouse

A single-storey tatami house at the far end of
[[hyakkiyako_old_schoolhouse_lane]], older than the academy itself and patched
where the roof sagged. A wooden gate opens on a walled courtyard scuffed bare by
drills, and lamplight often shows behind the paper screens long after midnight.
This is where the [[hyakkaryouran_resolution_council]] keeps watch over
Hyakkiyako.

## Inside

The [[hyakkaryouran_council_hall]] is the main room, and a side room off it has
slowly become [[room_kikyou|Kikyou's]]. Drills are held in the
[[hyakkaryouran_courtyard]], and the council's weapons and records are locked
in the [[hyakkaryouran_storehouse]] at the back.
