# Tsukuyo's Room

A high-ceilinged room under the eaves of the [[hyakkiyako_kawabata_house]],
the only one in the house where [[tsukuyo-oono]] can stand up straight without
hitting a beam. A rack of tree costumes, ninja manuals with bookmarks, a
fashion-show lanyard she hasn't thrown away, and floor-length curtains to
disappear behind.
