# Wild Hunt Sculpture Workshop

A high-ceilinged workshop in the [[wildhunt_art_center|Art Center]], loud with
chisels and dusty. Marble and sandstone blocks from the academy
[[wildhunt_quarry|quarry]] wait on pallets under a ceiling hoist, and the floor
is gritty with chips. Classes run 09:00 to 15:00 and it stays open until 20:00.
Goggles are compulsory; earplugs are wise.
