# Hyakkiyako Main Academy Building

A wide timber-and-plaster school building whose roof rises into a five-storey
castle keep, the symbol of [[hyakkiyako_school]]. The homerooms are on the lower
floors, with sliding doors and polished wooden corridors that creak; the stairs
up to the [[hyakkiyako_tenshukaku_lookout]] are roped off after hours.

## Homerooms

[[hyakkiyako_class_1a]] is on the ground floor, [[hyakkiyako_class_2a]] on the
first, and [[hyakkiyako_class_3a]] on the top classroom floor under the keep.
