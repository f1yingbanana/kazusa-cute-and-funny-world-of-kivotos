# Millennium District

The youngest of the great districts and the most modern: glass research towers,
data centres humming behind fences, tidy residential streets and level crossings
for Millennium's own railway. Much of Kivotos's technology starts here, at
[[millennium_school]] and in the [[millennium_research_park|Research Park]]
beside [[millennium_station|Millennium Station]].

The campus sits at the heart of it. Across a footbridge is the
[[millennium_dormitory|dormitory]], and below it the
[[millennium_shopping_district|shopping streets]] run down to the station. On
the other side of campus are the quiet streets of
[[millennium_euler_heights|Euler Heights]]; toward the river is the
[[millennium_district_park|District Park]]. Beyond the new town, the outskirts
fade into the restricted zone of the [[millennium_ruins|ruins]].
