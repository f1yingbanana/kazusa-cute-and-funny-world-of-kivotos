# Wild Hunt Marble Quarry

Terraced white rock faces in the hills above the
[[wildhunt_canal_town|canal town]], 25 minutes' walk out, worked by a small crew
from 08:00 to 17:00. The blocks go to the academy's
[[wildhunt_sculpture_workshop|Sculpture Workshop]], and sculpture students come
out on outing passes to choose them. The off-cut pile by the entrance is free
for anyone who can carry it.
