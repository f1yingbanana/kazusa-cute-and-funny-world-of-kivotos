# Wild Hunt Arts Information Center

A domed reading room with galleries of art books, exhibition catalogues and the
portfolios of every graduating class of [[wild_hunt_academy|Wild Hunt]]. Its
reading tables are the quietest place in the fortress, emptiest in the mornings
and full of students cramming before exams. The information desk by the door
handles exhibition bookings and lost outing passes.
