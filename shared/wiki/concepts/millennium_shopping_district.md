# Millennium Shopping District

A grid of clean, busy streets running from the east exit of
[[millennium_station|Millennium Station]] up the hill toward the
[[millennium_dormitory|dormitory]]. The shopfronts are new, the signs are
digital, and the streets are named for scientists. After school it fills with
Millennium uniforms; at night the family restaurant and the karaoke keep it
lit.

There is [[millennium_mall|Prism Mall]], the
[[millennium_family_restaurant|Galileo Family Restaurant]],
[[millennium_karaoke|Karaoke Hertz]], [[millennium_game_center|Game Center
Quantum]], [[millennium_dessert_cafe|Café Parabola]], the
[[millennium_maid_cafe|Cafe Maidramatic!]] and, for groceries,
[[millennium_supermarket|Fresh Mart Ohm]].
