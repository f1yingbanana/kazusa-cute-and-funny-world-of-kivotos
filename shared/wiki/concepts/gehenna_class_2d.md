# Gehenna Class 2-D

A third-floor classroom in the [[gehenna_first_building|First School Building]]
whose roll is a who's-who of trouble: council members who skip, delinquents with
bounties, and the [[prefect_team|Prefect]] officers sworn to arrest them, all
assigned to the same rows. Attendance is a contest of nerve; a seat by the
window has a good view of the main gate.
