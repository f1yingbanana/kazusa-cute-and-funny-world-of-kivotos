# Michiru's Room

The room of [[michiru-chidori]] in the [[hyakkiyako_kawabata_house]], more
studio than bedroom: an editing desk, a camcorder on a tripod, a ring light,
and shelves of ninja figures. The light stays on late while she edits videos
for [[michiruchi_channel]].

## On the top shelf

In a box on the top shelf is an old videotape she would rescue before anything
else.
