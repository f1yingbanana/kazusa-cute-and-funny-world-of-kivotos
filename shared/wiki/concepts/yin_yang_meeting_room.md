# Yin-Yang Club Meeting Room

The working heart of the [[yin_yang_club]], a tatami room in the
[[yin_yang_building]] with a long low table. Shelves of old festival records
line one wall, and one corner of the table is buried under paperwork.

## The president's corner

The buried corner is [[niya-amachi]]'s, the club president's, and the pile of
unsigned permits on it somehow never shrinks.
