# The Opera House Job

[[problem_solver_68|Problem Solver 68]] took a commission concerning Don
Arancino at the Opera House, where [[sensei|Sensei]] also happened to be. The
job descended into a fight between rival operatives.

## How it went

[[saori-joumae|Saori]] was working to protect the same target. As the rival
operatives converged, a misunderstanding turned the Arancino gang against her,
and [[aru-rikuhachima|Aru]]'s team helped her out. The agency never got its
expected payment, though the group and Sensei shared a meal afterwards.
