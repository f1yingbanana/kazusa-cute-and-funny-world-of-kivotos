# Kousagi Park

A hilly public park on the quiet edge of [[kousagi_ward]], with the
[[schale_office|SCHALE]] building on the hill above it and the woods of
[[kousagi_hill]] behind. Paths wind between lawns and azaleas, joggers fill
their bottles at the [[kousagi_park_washhouse|washhouse]] near the centre, and
the south gate leads down to the station corner. In a hollow on the quiet side
stands a tent camp that is very much lived in.

## The camp

After [[srt_training_facility]] closed, the members of [[rabbit_platoon]] chose
to live and protest in this park rather than abandon their academy. Their
[[rabbit_camp|camp]] is theirs; visitors are hailed at the rope line.
