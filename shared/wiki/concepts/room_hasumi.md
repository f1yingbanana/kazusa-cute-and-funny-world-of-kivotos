# Hasumi's Room

[[hasumi-hanekawa|Hasumi]]'s room in the [[trinity_magnolia_west|West Wing]] is
orderly, with a shelf of good tea tins, a bathroom scale turned to face the wall
and a locked notebook on the desk. Her lamp is often still on at 02:00.

## Kept out of sight

A drawer holds an emergency supply of sweets. The locked notebook holds three
years of memories of the [[justice_task_force]].
