# Pandemonium Tank Hangar

A big steel hangar behind
[[pandemonium_headquarters|Pandemonium Society Headquarters]], smelling of
diesel, where the council's tanks sit in a row and crew members tinker in the
mornings. In the far corner, behind the biggest tank, a tarpaulin hides
something cosier.

## Behind the tarp

[[iroha_nest|Iroha's Nest]], where [[iroha-natsume|Iroha]] hides from work.
