# Abydos Great Oasis

Once the pride of the district and the site of the Abydos Sand Festival. The
water is gone, the basin has cracked into plates, the palms around it are dead
and the old festival stalls are rusting where they stood. The abandoned Great
Oasis Station sits on its far side.
