# Wild Hunt Grand Lecture Hall

A raked hall in the [[wildhunt_art_center|Art Center]] with a painted ceiling,
tiered benches and a projector screen. Every year sits art history and general
classes here on weekday mornings before scattering to the studios. The front
rows take notes; the back rows are for sleeping.
