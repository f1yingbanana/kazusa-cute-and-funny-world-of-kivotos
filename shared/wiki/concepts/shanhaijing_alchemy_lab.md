# Eastern Alchemy Society Laboratory

A cluttered workshop of glass flasks, small furnaces and walls of herb drawers
at the end of the inner district's alchemy street, home of the
[[chinese_alchemy_study_club]]. Something is always bubbling, and a sign on the
left-hand counter warns that the samples are alive.
