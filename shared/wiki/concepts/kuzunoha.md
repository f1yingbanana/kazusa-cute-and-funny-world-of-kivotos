# Kuzunoha

Kuzunoha is a legendary figure of [[hyakkiyako_school|Hyakkiyako]], remembered
as a great prophet and as the founder of the
[[hyakkaryouran_resolution_council]]. Stories place her at the
[[twilight_temple_grounds]], hidden somewhere in the [[great_snowfield]].

## Her legacy

The [[hundred_lotus]], the rifle every Hyakkaryouran president inherits as the
Authority of the President, was handed down from her, and with it the power to
strike a [[hyakkiyako_phantom_fables|Phantom Fable]].
