# Trinity Cathedral Vestry

The working heart of the [[sisterhood]], behind the altar of
[[trinity_sisterhood|Trinity Cathedral]]: a robing room, offices where Sisters
keep schedules and records, and two confessional booths where students come to
talk by appointment in the late afternoon.

## The locked halls

Past the offices, a corridor of storerooms leads to older halls kept locked.
The keys hang on one heavy ring, held by the Sister who manages the cathedral.
