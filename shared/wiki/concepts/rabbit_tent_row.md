# RABBIT Tent Row

Olive tents pitched in a straight line behind a low sandbag wall, guy lines
taut, boots lined up outside each flap. Each one is someone's home, so visitors
knock on the pole.

## The fourth tent

The fourth tent is not in the row at all: it hides under netting in the shrubs a
little apart, and belongs to [[room_miyu|Miyu]].
