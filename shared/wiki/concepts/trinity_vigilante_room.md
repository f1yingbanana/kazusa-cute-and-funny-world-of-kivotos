# Vigilante Crew Room

An old ground-floor storeroom in the [[west_annex]], borrowed by the
[[vigilante_crew]]: the Crew isn't an official club, so it has no proper room.
A street map of Trinity covered in coloured pins hangs on the wall, one pin for
every incident, and a kettle and a tin of biscuits sit on a crate. Patrol
routes for the evening are written up on the door each afternoon.
