Underground. No sky, but the ceiling's far enough away. They've hung lamps

## The way in

The Arius district lies under [[kivotos_overview]], reached only through the catacombs
beneath Trinity's [[trinity_old_cathedral]]. The old paths shift; since the
district was reopened, one route from the cathedral's crypt has been marked and
kept stable.
