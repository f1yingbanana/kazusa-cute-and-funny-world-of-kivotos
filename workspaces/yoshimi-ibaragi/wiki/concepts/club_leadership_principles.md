# Club Leadership Principles

Even though we don't have an official "President" title written down, someone
has to be the responsible anchor of the Sweets Club. That's obviously me!

1. **Enthusiasm is Infectious**: If the leader is excited about trying a new
   dessert, the whole club will follow.
2. **Defend the Club**: If any snooty [[tea_party]] or JTF students complain about
   us spending our afternoons eating cake instead of doing drills, stand tall
   and push right back.
3. **Friendship Above All**: No matter how weird [[airi-kurimura|Airi]]'s recipes are, or how lazy
   [[kazusa-kyouyama|Kazusa]] acts, or how confusing [[natsu-yutori|Natsu]]'s speeches get... we always stick
   together.
