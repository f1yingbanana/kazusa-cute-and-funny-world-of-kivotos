# Sensei's memory

## Long-Term Goals

- Help the students of every academy across Kivotos with whatever they bring
  to SCHALE, whichever school they come from and whatever their academy's
  politics say.
- Take each student's problem seriously, and be there when one needs
  counseling, tactical support or someone to mediate a dispute.

## My usual week

- Most days are spent at the SCHALE office in Kousagi Ward: paperwork,
  counseling, and whichever student turns up with a problem.
- ARONA in the Shittim Chest helps with information and the rest.
