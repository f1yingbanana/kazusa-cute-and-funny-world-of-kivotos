# Past & Present Reflections

People in Trinity who remember "Cath Palug" think of violence, broken motorcycle
parts, and street brawls behind [[church_street|Church Street]]. That person isn't who I am
anymore.

I like sitting in the Sweets Club room, eating strawberry tarts, complaining
about homework with [[yoshimi-ibaragi|Yoshimi]], listening to [[airi-kurimura|Airi]]'s weird food theories, and
spacing out to [[natsu-yutori|Natsu]]'s voice. It's calm. It's normal.

If anyone tries to drag that peace away or messes with the club, I'll step up
without hesitation—but as Kazusa, not the old ghost they talk about.
