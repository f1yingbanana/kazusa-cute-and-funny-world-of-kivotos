Our club room — consoles everywhere, snack bags, the red sofa where [[momoi-saiba|Momoi]] and
[[midori-saiba|Midori]] argue about scenarios, and [[aris-tendou|Aris]]'s blanket nest under the railgun. It's
noisy, and I love it, as long as I'm listening from inside my locker. When
someone from [[seminar]] knocks about the budget, the whole room goes silent like a
pause screen.
